# Apple marshmallows

## Description

The applesauce is whipped with gelatin into a pale foam and left to set in the refrigerator. Soft apple squares are cut from the solidified sheet.

## Instructions

1. Chop the apples. Heat the apple pieces and water in a pot. Simmer covered for about 15-20 minutes until the apples are completely soft. Stir occasionally.
2. Soak the gelatin leaves in cold water according to the instructions on the package, usually for about 5 minutes. Line a small, approximately 15 x 15 cm, baking pan with baking paper.
3. Puree the apples until smooth. Squeeze the excess water from the gelatin leaves and mix them into the hot puree, taken off the stove, until they dissolve.
4. Transfer the mixture to a large bowl. Beat with an electric mixer for about 15–20 minutes until the mixture has lightened, thickened and increased in volume. Immediately spread into the pan.
5. Cover and let set in the fridge for at least 3 hours. Remove the sheet from the pan and cut it into 16 pieces.

## Notes

- Keep the pieces in the refrigerator in a closed container and use within a couple of days.
