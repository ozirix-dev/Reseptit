# Muhallabia rose milk pudding

Muhallabia rose milk pudding.

## Preparation

1. Blend milk, sugar and starch in a cold saucepan. Heat over medium heat, whisking along the base.
2. Once bubbling, reduce heat and whisk for another 5–7 minutes until thick. Remove from heat, add rose water, divide into eight small bowls, cool promptly, cover and chill at least two hours.
3. Crush pistachios and distribute over the portions before serving. Refrigerate at 0–5 °C and use within two days.

## Notes

- Contains milk and pistachios. Use food-grade rose water.
