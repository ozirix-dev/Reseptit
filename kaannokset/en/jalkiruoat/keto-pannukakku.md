# Keto pancake

Keto pancake is a familiar Finnish food as a low-carb home version. The recipe has been rewritten to suit your own kitchen and serves four or the stated amount of servings.

## Description

The familiar taste in food is built from simple ingredients. Vegetables and creamy ingredients bring juiciness, and the portion size can be adjusted with the portion counter in the recipe.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C and melt the butter on a baking sheet or in a pan.
2. Beat the eggs, cream, cream cheese, almond flour, psyllium and salt until smooth.
3. Pour the dough onto the hot butter and bake for 20-25 minutes until the surface is risen and golden brown.

## Notes

- Check for doneness and finally season according to your own taste.
