# Keto chocolate chunk cookies

Almond flour, butter and sugar-free dark chocolate make cookies with crisp edges and soft centres.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Beat the butter and erythritol until pale. Add the egg and vanilla.
3. Mix the almond flour, baking powder and salt, then add them to the dough. Fold in the chocolate chunks.
4. Shape the dough into 10 balls, put them on the tray and flatten them slightly.
5. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the edges begin to colour. Leave to cool on the tray before moving the cookies.

## Notes

The cookies are soft when hot and firm up as they cool. The flavour and carbohydrate content of sugar-free chocolate vary by product.
