# Low-carb Finnish mocha squares

## Description

A soft almond cake and coffee-cocoa icing bring the familiar flavour of Finnish mocha squares to a lower-carb version.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C. Line a small baking tin, about 20 × 25 cm, with parchment.
2. Whisk the eggs and erythritol. Add the cream and melted butter.
3. Mix the almond flour, cocoa and baking powder. Fold into the batter and spread in the tin.
4. Bake for 18–22 minutes and let cool completely.
5. Melt the butter for the icing. Mix in the coffee, cocoa and powdered erythritol. Spread over the cooled cake and leave to set.

## Notes

The almond cake is moister and more crumbly than a traditional wheat-flour base. Cut into squares once the icing has set.
