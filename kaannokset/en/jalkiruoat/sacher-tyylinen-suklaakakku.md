# Sacher-style chocolate cake

An airy dark chocolate cake with a thin apricot filling and glossy chocolate glaze.

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 175 °C. Line a 23 cm round pan at least 5 cm deep with parchment and grease pan and paper.
2. Melt the cake chocolate gently. Separate the eggs; blend yolks with melted chocolate, butter and vanilla until smooth.
3. Whip whites with salt, gradually add cake sugar and beat until glossy and firm.
4. Stir about a third of the whites into the chocolate mixture. Fold into the remaining whites, then gently fold in sifted cake flour.
5. Pour into the pan and bake with the door closed for 40–45 minutes, until a tester comes out clean. Rest ten minutes in the pan, turn onto a rack, peel off the paper and cool completely.
6. Sieve jam, split cake into two layers and spread jam between them, leaving about a centimetre at the edge.
7. Heat glaze chocolate, boiling water and sugar in a pan over low heat, stirring for 4–5 minutes until smooth. Beware of the hot glaze. Place cake on a rack over a tray and pour glaze over it. Set for a few hours. Cut into 12 slices.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, milk and egg; check chocolate for soy. This is a Sacher-style cake, not the hotel's secret recipe.
