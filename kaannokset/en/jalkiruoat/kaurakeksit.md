# Crisp oat lace cookies

## Description

These thin oat cookies spread on the tray and crisp as they cool. One batch makes about 30 cookies.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C using top and bottom heat. Line two baking trays with baking paper.
2. Melt the butter and let it cool until lukewarm. In another bowl mix the oats, flour, baking powder and vanillin sugar.
3. Stir the eggs and sugar into the melted butter. Add the dry mixture and stir until evenly moistened.
4. Use two teaspoons to drop about 30 small mounds of dough onto the lined trays. Leave plenty of space between them.
5. Bake one tray at a time on the middle rack for 5–7 minutes. Take the tray out when the edges are golden and the centres are set and no longer look raw.
6. Let the cookies cool on the tray before lifting them off. They harden as they cool.

## Notes

- Contains oats, wheat, milk and eggs. Do not taste the raw dough.
- One serving is one cookie. The yield and total time are estimates.
