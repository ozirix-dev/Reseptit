# Portuguese custard tarts

Portuguese custard tarts.

## Preparation

1. Mix dough flour, salt and water, knead until cohesive, cover and rest 15 minutes. Roll a 25 cm square.
2. Spread one third of butter over two thirds of dough, leaving borders, and fold in thirds. Turn a quarter turn and repeat with another third of butter.
3. Roll to 30 × 35 cm, spread remaining butter, roll from short side and chill covered for at least three hours. Blend custard flour with 50 ml cold milk.
4. Heat remaining 250 ml with vanilla and lemon, combine with flour mixture. Heat syrup sugar, water and cinnamon separately to 100 °C, remove cinnamon, gradually add syrup to milk mixture.
5. Cool slightly, incorporate yolks, strain and keep chilled before baking. Heat oven to 250 °C only if oven and tin are rated for it.
6. Cut log into 12 discs and place in metal muffin cups. Soften ten minutes and press with damp thumbs into bases and sides.
7. Fill three quarters. Start with 15–17 minutes; ensure pastry is cooked and custard centre reaches at least 71 °C, extending as needed.
8. Cool ten minutes; hot filling can burn. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate at 0–5 °C for up to one day.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, milk and egg. 250 °C is a home-oven adaptation; use only an oven and tin rated for it.
