# Finnish hot-water sponge cake

## Description

This traditional sponge starts with eggs and sugar whipped until airy. A thin stream of boiling water makes the sponge light. Use it as a base for berries, jam or cream fillings.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Line the base and sides of a round 24 cm cake tin with baking paper.
2. Beat the room-temperature eggs and sugar for about 7–10 minutes until pale, thick and noticeably airy.
3. Bring the water to a boil just before adding it. Pour the boiling water in slowly in a thin stream, beating continuously. Do not pour it all over the eggs at once.
4. Mix the flour and baking powder. Sift them over the whipped mixture and gently fold with a flexible spatula until just combined. Do not beat again.
5. Pour the batter into the tin and bake on the lower or middle oven rack for about 30–35 minutes. Test the centre with a skewer.
6. Let the cake set in the tin for about 10 minutes, then turn it out and cool completely on a rack before splitting and filling. Allow about an hour for cooling.

## Notes

- Pour in the boiling water slowly while beating. Use a large bowl because the mixture expands considerably.
- The sponge is airy and can crumble when split if it has not cooled completely.
