# Five-ingredient chocolate cottage cheese cake

## Description

Cottage cheese and Greek yogurt are pureed into a soft cake mass. Cocoa gives the baseless cake a chocolate taste and honey rounds out the bitterness of the cocoa. The refrigerator-cold cake can be cut into neat pieces.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 160 °C with constant heat. Line the bottom of a 16 cm springform pan with baking paper.
2. Puree the cottage cheese, yogurt, eggs, cocoa and honey in a blender until the mass is smooth. Pour into a pan.
3. Bake on a medium level for approximately 35–45 minutes. Check the cake for the first time at 35 minutes. The edges should be set and the center should still vibrate a little when moving the pan. Use a thermometer to make sure the center has reached 71°C.
4. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack for about 30 minutes. Transfer to the refrigerator for at least 4 hours. Remove from pan when cold and cut into four pieces.
