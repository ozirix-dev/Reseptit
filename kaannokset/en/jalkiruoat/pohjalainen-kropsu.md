# Ostrobothnian skillet pancake

Light syrup sweetens this thin Finnish pancake baked in a cast-iron skillet.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Melt the butter for the batter. Grease an ovenproof cast-iron skillet roughly 21 cm across.
2. Whisk the egg into the milk. Add the syrup and salt, whisk in the flour a little at a time, then mix in the melted butter.
3. Pour into the skillet and bake for about 30 minutes until the centre has set and the edges brown. Use oven gloves to remove the hot skillet. Cut the pancake into four pieces.

## Notes

- Contains milk, egg and wheat. This skillet batch uses one-quarter of the source recipe.
