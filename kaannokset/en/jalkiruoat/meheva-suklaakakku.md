# Moist chocolate cake

This simple chocolate cake stays soft in the centre and has a thin chocolate glaze. Let the cake cool before glazing it.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C. Grease and flour a roughly 22 cm springform tin.
2. Gently melt the chocolate for the cake with the butter. Leave to cool briefly.
3. Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and foamy. Gently mix in the chocolate and butter.
4. Sift the flour, cocoa powder and baking powder together. Fold the dry ingredients into the batter until just combined.
5. Pour the batter into the tin and bake for 25–30 minutes. The centre can stay slightly moist.
6. Let the cake cool. Heat the cream, take it off the heat and add the chocolate for the glaze. Stir until smooth, let it cool briefly, then spread it over the cake.

## Notes

- Baking time depends on the tin and oven. The cake firms up as it cools, so avoid overbaking.
