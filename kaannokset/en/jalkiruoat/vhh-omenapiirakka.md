# Low-carb apple tart

## Description

A thin apple layer and almond crust make this a lower-carb take on traditional apple tart.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and grease a small tart dish.
2. Rub the almond flour and cold butter together. Add the egg and erythritol, then press the dough over the base and up the sides.
3. Prebake the crust for 8 minutes. Slice the apples thinly and toss them with cinnamon and lemon juice.
4. Mix the quark, egg and erythritol. Spread over the crust and arrange the apples on top.
5. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the filling is set. Let cool before slicing.

## Notes

Apples contain naturally occurring sugar. This tart suits a less strict low-carb diet. Keep portions moderate.
