# Sago gula Melaka with coconut milk

Sago gula Melaka with coconut milk.

## Preparation

1. Boil 1.5 litres water with a knotted pandan leaf. Add small tapioca pearls and stir along the bottom frequently.
2. Cook about 15 minutes or longer as the packet and pearl size require, until translucent without a hard white core. Drain, rinse in clean cold water and remove pandan.
3. Lightly oil six small moulds and wipe off excess. Divide drained pearls among them; cool promptly, cover and refrigerate at 0–5 °C for at least 6 hours.
4. Heat chopped palm sugar with 125 ml water until dissolved; cool. Mix ready-to-eat coconut milk with salt and chill.
5. Unmould into bowls and divide coconut milk and syrup equally. Eat refrigerated portions within 24 hours.
6. Do not leave cooked pearls out for extended periods.

## Notes

- Use small tapioca pearls, not large bubble tea pearls. Check ingredient labels.
