# Gluten-free potato starch sponge cake

## Description

This light four-ingredient cake uses potato starch in place of wheat flour. Bake it in a 22 cm tin and fill only when completely cool. This is a different recipe from the traditional hot-water sponge.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Line the bottom and sides of a springform tin about 22 cm across with baking paper.
2. Beat the eggs and sugar in a large bowl for about 7–10 minutes until very pale and thick.
3. Mix the potato starch and baking powder. Sift the mixture into the whipped eggs a little at a time.
4. Gently fold the dry mixture in with a flexible spatula, reaching to the bottom of the bowl, until no dry starch remains. Do not beat again.
5. Pour into the tin and bake on the middle rack for about 25–30 minutes. Check with a skewer. The centre must be cooked through.
6. Let the sponge cool briefly in the tin. Remove carefully and cool completely on a rack. Split into two layers only once cool.

## Notes

- This sponge uses no boiling water. It is a potato-starch cake.
- Use potato starch and baking powder labelled gluten-free and clean equipment. Dr. Oetker baking powder in the 160 g pack (EAN 5701073066485) is sold as gluten-free. Always check the gluten-free label on the actual pack used. Clean the bowl, tin, utensils and worktop before baking. Contains egg.
- Fillings and toppings are outside the sponge’s ingredient list and nutrition calculation. They require a separate complete recipe.
