# Low-carb almond pancakes

## Description

Small, delicate pancakes with egg, cream cheese and almond meal.

## Preparation

1. Blend eggs, cream cheese, almond meal, cream and erythritol into a smooth batter.
2. Rest the batter for 5 minutes so the almond meal absorbs liquid.
3. Heat a small amount of butter in a frying pan. Pour in a thin layer of batter.
4. Cook over medium-low heat until the edges dry. Turn carefully and cook the other side.
5. Repeat with the remaining batter. Serve plain or with low-sugar berries.

## Notes

The batter is more fragile than ordinary pancake batter. Make small pancakes for easier turning.
