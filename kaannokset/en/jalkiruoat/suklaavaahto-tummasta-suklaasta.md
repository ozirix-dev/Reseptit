# Two-ingredient keto chocolate mousse

A rich mousse made with 90% dark chocolate and pasteurised egg. Makes four small dessert portions.

## Preparation

1. Weigh the pasteurised egg yolks and whites separately. Use only a pasteurised egg-white product the manufacturer confirms can be whipped.
2. Gently melt the dark chocolate over a water bath. Let it cool until just lukewarm so the yolks do not set.
3. Whisk the whites in a clean, grease-free bowl until soft but stable peaks form.
4. Stir the yolks into the lukewarm chocolate. First mix in a little egg-white foam to loosen the mixture.
5. Gently fold in the rest of the foam in two or three additions. Divide between four small serving dishes.
6. Cover and refrigerate for at least two hours. Keep chilled until serving.

## Notes

Whippability varies by pasteurised egg-white brand. This has not been kitchen-tested. 90% chocolate contains some sugar; it is not a sugar-free product.
