# Finnish talkkuna pancakes with blueberries and raspberries

Talkkuna, a toasted grain flour, gives these little pancakes a nutty flavour. Blueberries and raspberries cook into the batter. Serve with vanilla yoghurt.

## Preparation

1. Mix the talkkuna, wheat flour, baking powder, salt, sugar and cardamom. Whisk the milk, melted butter and eggs in another bowl. Stir the liquids into the flour mixture until smooth.
2. Stir the vanilla sugar into the yoghurt and chill until serving. If using fresh berries, check them and rinse gently if needed.
3. Melt a little frying butter in a pan. Spoon about 30 ml of batter per small pancake into the pan. Scatter on blueberries and raspberries and press lightly into the batter.
4. Cook over medium heat for about 2 minutes. Turn and cook for about 2 minutes on the other side, or until golden and cooked in the centre. Cook the rest in the same way. Divide the pancakes and yoghurt between 4 servings.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat, milk and egg. Check the other grains and allergens in the talkkuna blend on its pack. If using imported frozen berries, boil them for 2 minutes and let them cool before adding them to the pancakes.
