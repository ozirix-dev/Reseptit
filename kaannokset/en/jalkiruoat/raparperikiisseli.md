# Rhubarb kissel

## Description

The tart rhubarb softens into a gently sweet, spoonable dessert. Add the starch only once the fruit is tender.

## Instructions

1. Rinse the stalks, discard any leaves and cut the stalks into 1 cm pieces.
2. Bring 7 dl water and the sugar to a boil. Add the rhubarb and simmer for 5–8 minutes until soft.
3. Mix the potato starch with the remaining 1 dl cold water. Take the pan off the heat and pour in the slurry in a thin stream, stirring constantly.
4. Return the pan to the heat. As soon as the first bubble reaches the surface, remove the pan and divide the kissel into bowls.

## Notes

- Use only the stalks. To serve cold, cool the kissel and refrigerate it.
