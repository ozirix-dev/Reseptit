# Gluten-free oven pancake with berries

## Description

A soft tray-baked Finnish pancake made with gluten-free oat flour and a dark gluten-free flour blend, served with strawberries, bilberries and a little cream whipped just before serving. Recipavo adapted the Finnish Coeliac Society recipe to weighed amounts. This adaptation has not been test-baked.

## Preparation

1. Wash your hands and clean the worktops. Check that the deep baking tray, bowls, whisk and other equipment are free of wheat, barley and rye flour and crumbs. Line the tray with clean baking parchment. Preheat the oven to 200 °C using top and bottom heat.
2. Weigh the milk and crack the eggs into a bowl. Beat the eggs lightly, then whisk in the milk and salt.
3. Weigh the dark gluten-free blend and gluten-free oat flour separately. Gradually whisk both into the liquid to break up lumps. Weigh the Keiju liquid spread and mix it evenly into the batter.
4. Let the batter thicken for about 30 minutes. Stir it again before pouring.
5. Pour into the lined deep tray. Bake on the middle rack for about 30 minutes, until the center is set and the surface has browned spots.
6. Let the pancake set for about 10 minutes. Clean the strawberries and bilberries and rinse them if needed. Whip the cream and mix in the measured icing sugar.
7. Cut the pancake into 13 pieces. Divide all the weighed berries and cream whipped just before serving among the 13 servings. Serve and keep any remaining cream whipped just before serving refrigerated.

## Notes

- Use only the flour and oat products specifically labelled gluten-free. Check the current packaging and prevent gluten cross-contact from surfaces, equipment, parchment and hands. Check replacement products individually for gluten-free labelling and possible wheat starch. Oat products must be labelled gluten-free.
- **Allergens:** milk, eggs and oats. Gluten-free does not mean dairy-free or suitable for every grain allergy.
- The original flour volume measures of 1½ and 2 dl did not give a reliable weight. The flour and Keiju spread are specified by weight in this adaptation. The Finnish Coeliac Society has not test-baked these amounts. Review preparation and nutrition before replacing a flour brand by weight.
- The estimate includes the batter and the full batch toppings of 100 g strawberries, 100 g bilberries, 100 g whipping cream and 9 g icing sugar.
