# Pumpkin bua loy

Chewy orange rice dumplings in sweet coconut broth.

## Preparation

1. Knead the squash into the flour, adding only enough dough water for a pliable mixture. Roll into roughly 1 cm balls; cover.
2. Warm the coconut milk with the broth water, palm sugar, salt and optional pandan until the sugar dissolves; remove pandan. Cook the balls in batches in plenty of boiling water.
3. When they float, after about a minute, check one for a fully cooked, non-floury centre; boil longer if necessary. Briefly transfer to clean cold water.
4. Drain and warm in the coconut broth just to a simmer. Serve warm in six bowls.
5. Chill leftovers promptly, ideally within an hour, and refrigerate at 0–5 °C for no more than 24 hours.

## Notes

- Check ingredient labels. The palm sugar amount is reduced from the source; add dough water only as needed, possibly none.
