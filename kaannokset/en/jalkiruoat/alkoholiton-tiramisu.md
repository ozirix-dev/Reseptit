# Alcohol-free tiramisu

Alcohol-free tiramisu.

## Preparation

1. Blend mascarpone with sugar and vanilla until smooth. Whip the cold cream separately until it holds its shape, without becoming grainy.
2. Fold into the mascarpone. Briefly wet both sides of each biscuit in cooled coffee.
3. Arrange about half in a 25 × 18 cm dish and spread half the cream mixture over them. Repeat both layers.
4. Cover and chill at 0–5 °C for at least 4 hours. Sift cocoa over the dessert before serving.
5. Keep refrigerated and use within two days. Ordinary savoiardi contain wheat and egg; check the label.
6. Check that the vanilla flavouring is alcohol-free.

## Notes

- Contains milk; ordinary biscuits also contain wheat and egg. No raw egg in the filling.
