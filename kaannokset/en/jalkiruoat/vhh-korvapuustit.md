# Low-carb cinnamon rolls

## Description

Cheese and almond-meal dough with a butter and cinnamon filling.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Melt mozzarella and cream cheese together in a saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth.
3. Mix almond meal and psyllium. Add the warm cheese mixture and one egg. Knead briefly until smooth.
4. Roll the dough between two sheets of baking paper to about half a centimetre thick.
5. Spread with softened butter. Mix erythritol and cinnamon and sprinkle over the butter.
6. Roll up and cut into eight pieces. Put them cut-side up on the tray and press the middle of each.
7. Brush with the second beaten egg and bake for 12–15 minutes, until golden brown.
8. Cool briefly before serving.

## Notes

The dough rolls more easily while warm. If sticky, warm it briefly and use baking paper to help.
