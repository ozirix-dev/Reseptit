# Kaiserschmarrn, Austrian torn pancakes

Whipped egg whites make the pancake light. Once cooked, it is torn into pieces and browned in butter and sugar.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Separate the eggs. Whisk the yolks with the milk, flour, vanilla sugar and lemon zest until smooth.
2. Beat the whites with the salt to soft peaks. Gradually add 30 ml sugar and beat until the peaks hold their shape. Fold the whites into the batter in two batches.
3. Melt 50 g butter in a large ovenproof frying pan over medium heat. Add the batter and cook until the underside is light brown. Use a spatula to cut it into quarters and turn each piece. Transfer the pan to the oven and cook for about 6–8 minutes. Continue if needed until the thickest part reaches 71 °C and there is no wet batter in the centre.
4. Use oven gloves to take out the pan. Tear the pancake into bite-size pieces with two spatulas. Add 14 g butter and the remaining 15 ml sugar. Cook over medium heat, turning the pieces until the edges are golden. Divide between four plates and dust with icing sugar and cinnamon.

## Storage and safety

Contains egg, milk and wheat. The pan handle stays hot after it comes out of the oven. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
