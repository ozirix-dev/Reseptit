# Blueberry frozen yogurt

## Description

Greek yogurt and frozen blueberries make ice cream flavored with honey and vanilla. Purée the ingredients in a blender and freeze in a shallow pan.

## Instructions

1. Add yogurt, honey, vanilla extract and frozen blueberries to the blender. Puree until smooth. Stop the machine if necessary and scrape off the mass left on the edges before continuing.
2. Spread the mixture into a shallow, freezer-proof dish. Cover and freeze for about 3-4 hours until the mass has solidified. You can stir the mass at the first and second hour so that it freezes more evenly.
3. Let the ice cream soften at room temperature for about 10-15 minutes before serving. After longer freezing, it may need more time.

- Check the origin of the frozen berries. If using foreign frozen blueberries, boil them for two minutes and cool the mixture before pureeing.
