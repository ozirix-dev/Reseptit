# Tallimestari gooseberry kissel with rye crunch

Gooseberries are simmered for their juice, then strained and thickened. Crisp cinnamon rye bread cubes give the smooth kissel a little crunch.

## Preparation

1. Set aside 100 ml of the measured water and keep it cold. Put the gooseberries and remaining 700 ml water into a saucepan. Crush the berries lightly with a masher and simmer for about 20 minutes.
2. Strain the liquid back into the pan and add 50 ml sugar. Mix the potato starch with the cold water. Heat the gooseberry liquid, remove the pan briefly from the heat and pour in the slurry in a thin stream while stirring.
3. Return the pan to the heat and bring it just to the point where the first bubbles rise and the kissel thickens. Take it off the heat at once. Divide between six bowls, cool and refrigerate.
4. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat and let it brown a little. Add 30 ml sugar, the bread cubes and cinnamon. Toss until crisp. Spread out on a plate to cool, then scatter over the kissel just before serving.

## Storage and safety

Contains rye and milk. If using imported frozen berries, boil all the berries for at least 2 minutes before thickening. Cover and refrigerate the kissel and keep the rye cubes separate.
