# Brita cake with almond base, meringue and strawberries

## Description

A layer of meringue and an almond flour base make this Brita cake a light summer dessert. For the filling, whip the cream and add strawberries.

## Ingredients

The quantities are provided by the structured serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C. Line a baking tin approximately 20 × 30 cm with parchment paper.
2. Beat the softened butter and erythritol until light. Add the egg yolks one at a time and beat until smooth.
3. Mix the almond flour and baking powder together. Fold the dry mixture into the whipped butter, erythritol and egg-yolk mixture until evenly combined, then spread the batter thinly across the bottom of the lined tin.
4. Whisk the egg whites until soft peaks form. Gradually add the erythritol and continue whisking until the meringue is glossy.
5. Spread the meringue over the cake base and bake for 25–30 minutes. Allow to cool completely.
6. Cut the cooled cake sheet into two pieces. Whip the cream until soft peaks form, spread it over one piece and add sliced strawberries.
7. Place the second piece on top and serve soon after assembling.

## Notes

The filling will soften the meringue, so it is best to assemble the cake just before serving. Strawberries contain naturally occurring sugars; the serving size affects carbohydrate intake.
