# Thai mango sticky rice

Steamed sticky rice absorbs a sweet coconut mixture and is served with ripe mango and a separate, lightly salted coconut sauce. Arrange it traditionally or fan the mango slices for a restaurant-style presentation using the same ingredients.

## Preparation

1. Rinse 200 g of dry Thai white glutinous rice. Soak in plenty of clean water for at least 4 hours or longer if the package directs. Drain well and discard the soaking water.
2. Bring the steamer water to a boil. Spread the drained rice in a basket lined with a clean, damp steaming cloth so steam can circulate. Steam over boiling water for about 25–35 minutes or longer if needed. The rice is ready when it is cooked through and no grain has a hard centre. Add water to the bottom of the steamer only if needed.
3. Warm the 180 g coconut milk, 60 g sugar and 2 g salt for the rice just until the sugar dissolves. Pour all of the warm mixture into the freshly steamed hot rice. Stir gently, cover and let it absorb for about 20 minutes. Fold the rice over gently and rest it covered for another 15–20 minutes, until no free liquid remains.
4. Mix 5 g rice flour with 20 g cold water until smooth. Add to a saucepan with 100 g coconut milk and 1 g salt. Bring to a boil while stirring and continue cooking until the flour is cooked and the sauce is evenly thickened.
5. Wash and peel the ripe mangoes, remove the stones and weigh 500 g of edible flesh in total. Slice thinly with a clean knife.
6. Divide all the rice, sauce and mango equally among four portions. For a traditional presentation, place the rice and mango side by side. For a restaurant-style presentation, mound the rice and fan out the same mango slices. Add the sauce and serve shortly after preparation.
