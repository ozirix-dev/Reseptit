# Turkish sütlaç rice pudding

A chilled rice dessert scented with lemon and cinnamon.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and gently cook in 200 ml water for about 20 minutes, until tender. Mix the starch with the remaining cold water.
2. Add the milk and starch mixture to the rice; bring to a gentle bubble and stir along the base for about 10 minutes. Add sugar and zest and cook for another 2 minutes.
3. Divide into six shallow dishes and dust with cinnamon. Cool promptly, ideally within one hour, using a cold-water bath if needed.
4. Cover and refrigerate at 0–5 °C. Serve chilled within 24 hours.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Cornstarch is an editorial choice.
