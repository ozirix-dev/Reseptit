# Oat waffles

Oat flour gives these waffles a nutty flavour. Let the batter rest and cool the cooked waffles briefly on a wire rack to keep the outside crisp.

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter and let it cool until lukewarm. Mix the oat flour, wheat flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk the eggs, cream and honey in a second bowl. Add the melted butter and sparkling water. Stir the wet mixture into the flour mixture until smooth. Let it rest for about 8 minutes.
3. Heat a non-stick waffle iron according to the manufacturer's instructions. Add a portion of batter to suit the iron, leaving room for it to spread. Cook until the waffle releases easily, is golden on both sides and is fully cooked through.
4. Transfer the cooked waffles to a wire rack. Cook the remaining batter and divide the waffles into two servings.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, oats, milk and egg. Cook the batter all the way through. Do not give food containing honey to children under one year old.
