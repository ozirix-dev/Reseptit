# Cherry clafoutis

Cherries bake in a tender custard batter flavoured with browned butter.

## Preparation

1. Toss the pitted cherries with their sugar, cover and refrigerate for 2 hours.
2. Heat oven to 180 °C. Grease a shallow baking dish about 20–24 cm across with 10 g butter, coat with 30 g sugar and shake out excess.
3. Whisk eggs, batter sugar and vanilla. Brown 20 g butter in a small saucepan without burning. Combine flour, milk, cream, salt and slightly cooled butter with the egg mixture.
4. Add cherries and their juice and pour into the dish. Bake for 30–35 minutes or longer until the centre is set and reaches at least 71 °C. Rest for 10 minutes.

## Notes

- Contains milk, egg and wheat. Check the cherries for remaining stones.
