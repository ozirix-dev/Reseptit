# Apple oat crumble

## Description

Thin apple wedges bake under a crisp buttery oat topping.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Grease a small baking dish with a little of the butter.
2. Cut the apples into thin wedges. Spread them evenly in the dish and sprinkle with cinnamon.
3. Melt the remaining butter. Toss the sugar and oats in it and crumble over the apples.
4. Bake on the middle rack for about 20–25 minutes, until a knife slides easily into the apples and the topping is golden. Let stand briefly before serving.

- Contains milk and oats. Weigh the apples after trimming. The recipe covers the crumble alone.
