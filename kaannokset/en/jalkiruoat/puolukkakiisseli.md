# Lingonberry kissel

## Description

Tart lingonberries soften into a spoonable dessert thickened with potato starch.

## Instructions

1. Combine 7 dl water, lingonberries, sugar and salt. Once the whole mixture is boiling, boil it for at least 2 minutes.
2. Mix the starch with the remaining 1 dl cold water. Take the pan off the heat and pour in the slurry slowly while stirring.
3. Return to the heat. As soon as it bubbles and thickens, take it off the heat. Divide among six bowls and let cool.

## Notes

- Boil the entire batch before thickening, including any imported frozen berries. Serve lukewarm or cool quickly and refrigerate.
