# French lemon tart

French lemon tart.

## Preparation

1. Mix butter, icing sugar and two yolks. Add flour and water and bring together quickly; flatten and chill for 30 minutes.
2. Combine five eggs, filling sugar, lemon juice, zest and cream; refrigerate. Roll pastry to about 3 mm and line a 24 cm tin without stretching.
3. Trim, prick and chill for 30 minutes. Bake at 160 °C with parchment and baking weights for 10 minutes; remove weights and bake for 20 more minutes.
4. Brush with the remaining yolk and bake one minute. Lower oven to 140 °C.
5. Gently warm the filling while stirring, to no more than 40 °C. Pour into the shell and bake about 25 minutes or longer until just set and the centre reaches at least 71 °C.
6. Cool promptly, refrigerate and cut into eight slices.

## Notes

- Whipping cream is an editorial adaptation. The reference recipe uses higher-fat double cream.
- Contains wheat, milk and egg.
