# Low-carb rhubarb tart

## Description

Tart rhubarb goes well with an almond crust and a soft quark filling. Erythritol adds sweetness.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and grease a tart dish about 22 cm across.
2. Mix the almond flour, melted butter and one egg. Press the mixture into the base of the dish.
3. Chop the rhubarb into small pieces. Mix the quark, two eggs, erythritol, vanilla and psyllium.
4. Spread the rhubarb over the crust and pour the quark filling on top.
5. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until the filling is set. Let cool before serving.

## Notes

Rhubarb is tart. Adjust the sweetener to taste. The filling firms up as it cools.
