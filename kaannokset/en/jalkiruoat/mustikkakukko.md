# Finnish bilberry rye pie (mustikkakukko)

A rye pastry shell encloses a juicy bilberry filling.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Beat the softened butter with the crust sugar. Combine baking powder with rye flour and add to the butter.
2. Press about two thirds of the dough into the base and sides of an 18–20 cm dish. Shape the remaining dough into a lid on parchment and chill briefly.
3. Thaw the Finnish bilberries in the microwave. Mix with filling sugar and potato starch. Fill the dish and cover with the dough lid.
4. Bake for about 40 minutes. Let stand briefly, divide into eight portions and serve warm.

## Notes

- Contains milk and gluten-containing rye.
- If using imported frozen berries in Finland, heat throughout at 90 °C for 5 minutes or boil for 2 minutes first. Include the released juice in the filling.
