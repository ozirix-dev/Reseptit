# Low-carb strawberry layer cake

## Description

An almond-flour sponge filled with cream and fresh strawberries makes a summery low-carb dessert.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C. Line the base of a 20 cm springform tin with parchment.
2. Whisk the eggs and erythritol until pale and foamy. Fold in the almond flour and baking powder.
3. Pour the batter into the tin and bake for 22–26 minutes. Cool the cake completely.
4. Whip the cream to soft peaks, then mix in the quark and vanilla.
5. Split the cake horizontally into two layers. Spread some of the cream filling and sliced strawberries between the layers.
6. Set the top layer in place and decorate with the remaining filling and strawberries.

## Notes

Strawberries contain naturally occurring sugar, so this cake suits a less strict low-carb diet. Store the assembled cake in the fridge.
