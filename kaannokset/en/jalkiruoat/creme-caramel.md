# Crème caramel

A chilled egg custard is turned out onto a plate with amber caramel sauce.

## Preparation

1. Gently heat the caramel sugar and water until dissolved. Boil without stirring until golden brown. Carefully divide the hot caramel among six ovenproof ramekins of about 150 ml.
2. Heat oven to 160 °C and milk almost to boiling. Lightly whisk eggs, yolks and custard sugar. Gradually whisk in the hot milk, strain and add vanilla. Divide among the ramekins.
3. Place in a deep roasting pan and carefully add hot water halfway up the sides. Start with 15–20 minutes of baking; continue until each centre is set and reaches at least 71 °C.
4. Carefully lift the ramekins from the water bath, cool promptly and refrigerate for at least 4 hours. Loosen the edges with a knife and invert onto plates.

## Notes

- Contains milk and egg. Hot caramel and the water bath can cause burns. Do not touch caramel until cooled.
