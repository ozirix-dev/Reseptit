# Cardamom and ginger baked pears

Cardamom and ginger baked pears.

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Peel, halve and core pears.
2. Coat cut faces with butter and place on a lined rimmed tray. Distribute cardamom, cinnamon and honey.
3. Roast about 20 minutes, turning halfway, until fork-tender but intact; extend if needed. Drizzle with orange juice and rest 5 minutes.
4. Mix crème fraîche with both gingers and orange zest. Serve two halves per portion with a quarter of the cream.
5. Keep dairy chilled. Do not give honey to infants under one year.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Do not give honey to children under one year.
