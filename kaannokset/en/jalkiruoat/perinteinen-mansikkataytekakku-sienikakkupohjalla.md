# Traditional Finnish strawberry layer cake

## Description

A traditional strawberry cake with a light hot-water sponge, strawberry jam, fresh strawberries and vanilla whipped vanilla cream. The recipe makes one 24 cm cake, about 10 slices.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Line the base and sides of a round 24 cm cake tin with baking paper.
2. Beat the eggs and sugar for 7–10 minutes until thick and pale. Bring the water to a boil and pour it into the mixture in a thin stream while beating continuously.
3. Mix the flour and baking powder, sift into the mixture and gently fold until smooth. Pour into the tin and bake for about 30–35 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
4. Let the sponge cool for about 10 minutes in the tin, then turn it out and cool completely on a rack. Split it into two layers.
5. Wash and dry the strawberries. Slice about 200 g for the filling and reserve about 100 g for decoration.
6. Whip the cream and vanilla sugar until firm but spreadable. Divide roughly in half for filling and for covering the top and sides.
7. Lightly moisten the cut sides of the sponge with milk, using 0.5 dl in total. Spread the bottom layer with jam, sliced strawberries and the whipped vanilla cream reserved for filling.
8. Set the upper layer in place. Cover the top and sides with the remaining cream and decorate with the reserved strawberries.
9. Refrigerate the cake and serve chilled. Let the filling set in the fridge for at least two hours before slicing. Keep chilled until serving.
