# Avocado and banana ice cream with yogurt

## Description

Frozen banana and ripe avocado are pureed into a thick ice cream mass. Unflavored yogurt and lemon add tartness to the sweet banana. Ice cream can be made with a blender and in the freezer.

## Instructions

1. Spoon the avocado pulp into a blender. Add yogurt, honey and lemon juice. Add the frozen banana slices.
2. Puree until smooth. If the mass remains on the sides of the jug, stop the machine and scrape it back near the blade before continuing.
3. Spread the mass in a shallow freezer-proof container, close the lid and freeze for about 4 hours.
4. Let the ice cream soften at room temperature for 10-15 minutes or until you can roll it into a ball. Divide into three portions.

## Notes

- The preparation time is calculated from pre-frozen bananas. If you are starting with fresh bananas, slice them and freeze the pieces in a single layer beforehand until they are hard.
