# Lemon posset

Lemon juice sets sweetened cream into a soft chilled dessert.

## Preparation

1. Heat the cream and sugar in a saucepan with room for bubbling. Once boiling, stir and cook for about 3 minutes.
2. Remove from the heat. Stir a tablespoon of warm cream mixture into the lemon juice, then gradually add this back to the saucepan while stirring. Add the vanilla sugar.
3. Divide into four small dishes. Cool promptly, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours. Serve cold.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Do not replace the whipping cream with milk; the dessert may not set.
