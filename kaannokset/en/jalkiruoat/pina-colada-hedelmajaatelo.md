# Piña colada fruit ice cream

## Description

Pineapple and banana are blended with coconut milk and lime to make a thick, creamy fruit ice cream without an ice cream maker. The fruit provides natural sweetness, with no added sugar.

The preparation time is estimated using previously frozen fruit. If using fresh fruit, allow approximately two additional hours for freezing.

## Preparation

1. Peel and slice the bananas. Freeze the banana slices and pineapple chunks until completely frozen, usually for at least about two hours. Skip this step if using previously frozen fruit.
2. Place the frozen banana slices and pineapple chunks in a powerful blender or food processor. Add the coconut milk and lime juice.
3. Start with short pulses, then continue blending until the mixture is thick and smooth. Stop occasionally to scrape down the sides. Do not overload the appliance beyond the manufacturer's instructions.
4. Serve immediately as soft fruit ice cream, or transfer to a shallow freezer-safe container with a lid. Freeze for another 1–2 hours, until firm enough to scoop.
5. If the ice cream becomes too hard, let it soften at room temperature for approximately 5–15 minutes before serving. Divide into four servings.
