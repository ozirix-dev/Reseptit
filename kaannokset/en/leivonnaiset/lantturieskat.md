# Finnish swede flatbreads

Finely grated swede keeps these small tray-baked flatbreads moist. Four-grain flakes give them a rough-textured surface.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 225 °C and line a baking tray. Mix the flakes, flour for the dough, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. Add the grated swede, rapeseed oil and water. Mix just until no dry pockets of flour remain.
3. Spoon the dough onto the tray in 12 equal mounds. Dip your hand into the shaping flour and pat the mounds into thin rounds. Leave a little space between them.
4. Bake on the middle shelf for 15–20 minutes. The flatbreads are ready when the edges are lightly browned, the bases release from the paper and the centres are cooked through. Move to a wire rack.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat and may contain other gluten-containing grains depending on the flakes. Read the ingredient list on the packet. Let the flatbreads cool completely before packing them. Freeze any you will not use soon.
