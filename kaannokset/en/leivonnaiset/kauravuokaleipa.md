# Oatmeal loaf

Rolled oats soften the crumb of this tin loaf and show on its crust.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour, dough oats, softened butter, salt, sugar, yeast and milk. Knead for 5–7 minutes until soft and elastic.
2. Cover and rise in an oiled bowl for about an hour. Shape into a tight log and place in an oiled tin about 23 × 13 cm.
3. Cover and prove for 60–90 minutes, until the dough rises about 2.5 cm above the rim. Whisk the egg white with water, brush on lightly and scatter the topping oats.
4. Bake on the middle rack at 175 °C for 35–40 minutes. The centre should reach about 88 °C. Turn out onto a rack and cool completely before cutting into 16 slices.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, oats, milk and egg.
