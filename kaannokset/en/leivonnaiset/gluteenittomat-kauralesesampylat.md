# Gluten-free oat bran rolls

## Description

Oats, potato fibre and psyllium help keep these gluten-free rolls soft inside. The dough makes 16 small rolls and does not require gluten-developing kneading.

## Ingredients

Use the amounts in the ingredient list. Potato fibre and potato starch behave differently in the dough.

## Preparation

1. Measure the water at about 42 °C into a large bowl. Sprinkle over the psyllium, stir well and leave to thicken for 5–10 minutes.
2. Add the syrup, salt and gluten-free oat bran. Mix the dry yeast into some of the measured dark gluten-free flour blend and add it to the bowl.
3. Add the potato fibre and the rest of the dark flour blend. Mix vigorously for about 3–5 minutes until smooth. Add the oil last and mix briefly. The dough should stay soft enough to shape with a spoon. Avoid adding extra flour by guesswork.
4. Line a tray with baking parchment. Divide the dough into 16 portions and shape round rolls with a wet hand. Spread the measured gluten-free oat flakes evenly over the rolls. Keep ordinary wheat flour off the work surface.
5. Let rise in a draught-free place for about 15–20 minutes. Preheat the oven to 225 °C.
6. Bake on the middle rack for 15–20 minutes until browned and baked through. Cool on a rack before splitting.

## Notes

- Use Myllärin gluten-free oat bran (450 g, EAN 6415000164739) or another oat bran specifically labelled gluten-free. Check the flour blend, potato fibre, psyllium and yeast packages. Avoid cross-contact from flour dust and equipment. Contains gluten-free oats.
- Gluten-free rolls dry out easily and can be frozen once cool. Mixing well at the start follows the [Finnish Coeliac Society guidance](https://www.keliakialiitto.fi/ajankohtaista/uutiset/miksi-gluteenittomat-sampylat-eivat-kohoa/).
- These gram amounts are Recipavo's adaptation of the source ingredients and method. This version has not been test-baked. Check the binding effect of the Pofiber and psyllium and that the center of each roll is baked through.
