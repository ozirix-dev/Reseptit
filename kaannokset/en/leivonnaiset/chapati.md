# Chapati

These thin unleavened wheat flatbreads pick up brown spots in the pan.

## Preparation

1. Put the dough flours, salt and oil into a bowl. Add the water gradually until a soft dough forms. Knead on a floured surface for 5–10 minutes.
2. Divide into 10 balls and let rest covered for a few minutes. Roll each ball into a very thin round.
3. Heat a pan over medium heat and brush it lightly with oil. Cook one bread at a time for about 30 seconds on the first side, until brown spots appear underneath. Turn and cook the other side through.
4. Stack the flatbreads on a plate under a clean towel to keep them soft.
