# Potato and barley flatbreads

## Description

Leftover mash and barley flour make ten small, unleavened flatbreads.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 250 °C. Line a tray with baking paper rated for that temperature.
2. Stir the mash smooth. Mix in the egg and salt, then quickly stir in the barley flour.
3. Spoon ten mounds onto the tray. With damp fingers, flatten each to about 5 mm and prick with a fork.
4. Bake in the lower part of the oven for about 15 minutes, until brown spots appear and the breads lift from the paper. Cool on a rack.

## Notes

- Contains barley, milk and egg. Use freshly made mash or mash that was cooled quickly and refrigerated. The total time starts with prepared mash.
