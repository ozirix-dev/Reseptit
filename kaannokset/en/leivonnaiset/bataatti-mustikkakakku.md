# Flourless sweet potato and blueberry cake

## Description

Cooked sweet potato makes the cake juicy and gives it a warm orange hue. The frozen blueberries are mixed into the dough last, leaving the berries visible in the cut pieces.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C with constant heat. Line the bottom and sides of a 20 cm round cake tin with baking paper.
2. Puree the ripe, peeled and cooled sweet potato until smooth. Beat the eggs and maple syrup into it. Mix cinnamon and baking powder together, Sift them into the dough and mix until smooth.
3. Using a spatula, fold the frozen domestic blueberries into the dough. Pour the dough into the pan and smooth the surface.
4. Bake on the middle level of the oven for about 35–40 minutes. The cake is ready when the surface is golden brown, the center has congealed and its internal temperature is at least 71 °C. If necessary, continue baking for a few minutes.
5. Let the cake cool in the pan on a wire rack before cutting. Cut into eight pieces.

## Notes

- The indicated time is an estimate for previously cooked, cooled sweet potatoes. If you start with raw sweet potatoes, cut them in half and cook cut side down on baking paper at 200 °C until a fork easily sinks into the flesh. Allow to cool, scoop out the flesh and weigh 440 g.
- Contains egg. If you use foreign frozen berries instead of domestic blueberries, boil them first for 2 minutes and cool before adding to the dough.
