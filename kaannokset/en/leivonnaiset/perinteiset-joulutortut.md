# Traditional Finnish Christmas pastries

These crisp star-shaped pastries have a spoonful of bake-stable prune jam in the centre.

## Preparation

1. Thaw the pastry as directed on the package. Heat the oven to 225 °C and line a tray with parchment.
2. Cut the pastry into ten squares. Cut from the corners towards the centre without cutting through the middle. Fold every other tip into the centre and press them down.
3. Beat the egg and brush the pastry. Spoon about 10 g jam into the centre of each star.
4. Bake for 10–15 minutes until puffed and golden. Let the pastries cool a little before serving, as the filling will be hot.
