# Finnish archipelago bread

Buttermilk, rye and malt make a dark loaf with a glossy coffee-and-syrup crust.

## Preparation

1. Warm the buttermilk to body temperature and dissolve the yeast in it. Mix in the malt, dough syrup, brown sugar and salt.
2. Mix in the rye flour, wheat flour and bran. Cover and leave to rise for about 1½ hours, until doubled.
3. Butter three 1.5-litre loaf tins and dust with breadcrumbs. Divide the dough among them and bake on the lower rack at 175 °C for 80 minutes.
4. Mix the warm coffee with the syrup set aside for glazing. Brush the loaves, cover loosely with foil and bake another 40 minutes.
5. Let the loaves rest briefly in their tins. Turn out onto a rack and cool under a towel. Cut each loaf into 12 slices.

## Notes

- Contains rye, wheat and milk. Refrigerate the cooled bread.
