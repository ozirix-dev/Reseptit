# Light brioche-style burger buns – The Clever Carrot adaptation

## Description

Nine soft, lightly enriched brioche-style buns adapted in weighed quantities from Emilie Raffa's recipe.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies weighed amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Mix 270 g lukewarm water, 51 g whole milk, 7 g dry yeast and 35 g sugar. Leave a few minutes for the yeast to disperse.
2. Combine 405 g strong bread flour, 45 g regular wheat flour and 10 g salt. Rub in 39 g softened butter.
3. Add the yeast liquid and 62 g weighed beaten egg. Knead 8–10 minutes to a soft elastic dough, which may remain slightly sticky.
4. Cover and let rise for 1–3 hours until nearly doubled.
5. Divide into nine equal pieces and shape smooth buns. Place apart on parchment, using two trays if needed. Proof another 60–90 minutes until clearly puffy.
6. Preheat the oven to 205 °C and brush all 15 g beaten egg over the buns.
7. Optionally add a little hot water to a heatproof metal pan placed low in the oven for steam at the start of baking. Never pour water onto the oven floor or glass. Bake for 15–20 minutes and cool on a rack.

## Notes

- Adapted from Emilie Raffa's [Light Brioche Hamburger Buns at The Clever Carrot](https://www.theclevercarrot.com/2013/05/light-brioche-hamburger-buns/). Original yields eight buns; this weighed adaptation yields nine.
- Source flour cups were converted using about 120 g per cup, which varies by flour. The 275-minute total is an estimate. Nutrition counts all 15 g egg wash; no sesame, accepted GI/GK value or test bake.
