# Keto earplugs

Keto korvapuusti is a familiar Finnish food as a low-carb home version. The recipe has been rewritten to suit your own kitchen and serves four or the stated amount of servings.

## Description

This gluten-free pastry is made from almond flour and retains the familiar pastry taste in a low-carb form.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Gently melt the mozzarella and cream cheese together.
2. Mix in almond flour and egg. Roll the dough between baking papers into a disk.
3. Spread soft butter, erythritol and cinnamon on the surface. Roll up and cut into pieces.
4. Bake for about 15 minutes until the buns are golden brown.

## Notes

- Check for doneness and finally season according to your own taste.
