# Beetroot and chocolate cake with almond flour

## Description

The dough made from boiled beetroot puree is baked in a bread pan. Cocoa gives the cake a dark chocolate flavor and honey sweetens the dough.

## Instructions

1. Brush the beetroots clean and cook them whole for about 45-60 minutes, until a knife sinks into them easily. Drain and let cool to lukewarm. If the crust is tough, rub it off.
2. Preheat the oven to 175°C with constant heat. Line a 23 x 13 cm loaf tin with baking paper.
3. Weigh 300 g of ripe beetroot and mash until smooth. Mix the eggs and honey into the puree.
4. Mix almond flour, cocoa powder and baking powder in another bowl. Add the dry mixture to the beetroot mixture and mix until smooth.
5. Pour the batter into the pan. Bake on medium for about 55 minutes. Start checking at 50 minutes. The cake is ready when the center has settled and no more than moist crumbs stick to the test stick. If necessary, check that the center is at least 71 °C.
6. Let the cake cool in the pan for 15 minutes. Use baking paper to lift it onto a wire rack and cool before cutting.
