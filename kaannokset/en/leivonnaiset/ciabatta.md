# Ciabatta

An overnight preferment and wet dough give ciabatta its open crumb.

## Preparation

1. Stir together the preferment water, flour and yeast. Cover and leave at room temperature for 12–16 hours.
2. Mix the dough water into the preferment. Add the dough flour, yeast and salt. Mix for two minutes on low, then knead for 3–4 minutes on medium. The dough will be sticky.
3. Cover and rise for an hour. With a wet hand, fold the dough from the sides to the middle 8–12 times. Rise for another hour.
4. Turn onto a well-floured surface. Stretch gently to about 20 × 25 cm and cut lengthwise in two. Transfer to a floured surface, cover and prove for 1–2 hours.
5. Preheat a baking stone in the oven to 260 °C. Slide the loaves onto it and immediately reduce the heat to 220 °C. Bake for 22–25 minutes until golden brown.
6. Turn off the oven and leave the door ajar. Cool the loaves on the oven rack, then cut each into eight portions.

## Notes

- Contains wheat. Use a baking stone rated for the stated temperature.
