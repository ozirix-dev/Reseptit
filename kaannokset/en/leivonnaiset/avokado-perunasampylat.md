# Avocado and potato rolls

## Description

Small rolls mixed with potato, avocado and Greek yogurt. The surface will be smeared with yolk and sesame seeds. The portion makes six pieces.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C with constant heat. Prepare six silicone muffin tins or line the holes of the muffin tray with baking paper.
2. Mash the cooled potato and avocado with a fork as smooth as possible. In another bowl, whisk the eggs and yogurt lightly.
3. Mix the egg mixture with the potato-avocado puree. Sprinkle in the baking powder and salt and mix until smooth.
4. Divide the dough into six pans, about 70 g each, and smooth the surfaces with a spoon. Break the texture of the yolk and spread a thin layer on the surface of the dough. Sprinkle sesame seeds on top.
5. Bake on medium for about 25-35 minutes. Start checking for doneness at 25 minutes. The rolls are ready when the surface is golden brown, the center has set and the internal temperature is at least 71 °C.
6. Let it rest in the molds for 10 minutes. Remove to a wire rack and cool before slicing.
