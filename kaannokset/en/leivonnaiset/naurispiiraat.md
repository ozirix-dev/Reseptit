# Finnish turnip hand pies with potato and rye pastry

A slightly sweet turnip filling is sealed in rye pastry softened with mashed potato. The pies bake into golden-edged half moons.

## Preparation

1. Coarsely grate the peeled turnip. Heat a splash of water and cook the grated turnip, covered, for about 5 minutes. Drain well. Mix in the filling butter, salt and syrup while hot. Spread the filling on a plate to cool.
2. Mix the cold mash with the pastry flour, salt, baking powder and soft butter. Shape into a log, divide into 14 pieces and roll each to a round about 10 cm wide on a lightly floured surface.
3. Place about a tablespoon of filling on each round, leaving the edges clear. Fold into half moons and press the seams closed. Put the pies on a tray. Use baking parchment only if rated for at least 250 °C, or use a lightly greased metal tray.
4. Bake on the middle shelf of a conventional oven at 250 °C for 10–15 minutes until the pastry is cooked and coloured. Melt the brushing butter and brush it over the warm pies. Cover with a clean cloth for about 10 minutes before serving.

## Storage and safety

Contains rye and milk. Use mashed potato that was cooled promptly and kept refrigerated. Cool leftover pies quickly and keep refrigerated.
