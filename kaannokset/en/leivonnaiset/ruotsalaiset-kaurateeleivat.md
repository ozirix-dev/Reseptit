# Swedish oat teacakes

These soft, flat Swedish rolls use yeast and oats, with more oats scattered over the top.

## Preparation

1. Soak the dough oats in 300 ml milk for about 15 minutes. Warm the remaining milk to no more than 37 °C. Dissolve the yeast in it and add the syrup.
2. Add the soaked oats, egg, flour, butter and salt. Knead for five minutes with a mixer or ten minutes by hand. Cover and let rise for about 40 minutes.
3. Divide the dough into 16 pieces. Shape into balls and flatten into rounds on parchment. Prick with a fork and prove under a towel for 20 minutes.
4. Brush with water and scatter the topping oats over the rounds. Bake in batches on the middle rack at 250 °C for 8–10 minutes, until coloured. If checking with a thermometer, the centres should reach 94–96 °C.

## Notes

- Contains oats, wheat, milk and egg.
