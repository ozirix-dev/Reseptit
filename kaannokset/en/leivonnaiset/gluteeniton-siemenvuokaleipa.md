# Gluten-free seed loaf

## Description

A soft gluten-free tin loaf with oats, flaxseed and a sunflower seed topping.

## Preparation

1. Line a loaf tin of about 1.5 litres with baking paper. Pour the lukewarm water into a bowl and stir in the psyllium, flaxseeds and sunflower seeds, reserving a few sunflower seeds for the top.
2. Leave for about 10 minutes to thicken. Stir in the salt and gluten-free oats.
3. Mix the dried yeast into the light and dark gluten-free flour blends. Add the dry ingredients gradually, mixing for about 3–5 minutes with dough hooks or a stand mixer until even. The dough should be softer than wheat bread dough. Do not add flour by guesswork.
4. Transfer to the lined tin, scatter the reserved seeds over the top and smooth lightly with a damp spoon. Leave room to rise. Let it prove away from draughts for about 20 minutes. If the dough already reaches the rim, use a larger tin.
5. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Bake on the middle shelf for about 60 minutes. If the top darkens too much, cover it with foil for the remainder.
6. Let the loaf settle in the tin briefly, then turn it out onto a rack. Cool thoroughly before slicing.

## Notes

- Use only the named products in packages confirmed gluten-free. Check each label and keep the worktop, scale, bowl and tin clean to prevent gluten cross-contact. Contains gluten-free oats and seeds. Gluten-free does not automatically mean suitable for everyone with a cereal allergy.
- This editorial version uses its own specified gram amounts and has not been test-baked. The Finnish Coeliac Society source measures the flours by volume. The 210 g light blend, 180 g dark blend and 70 g oats are recipe amounts, not measured conversions of the source. Check the rise, centre and slicing texture when making it.
- Flour blends absorb different amounts of water. The longer initial mixing follows the [Finnish Coeliac Society's yeast dough guidance](https://www.keliakialiitto.fi/ajankohtaista/uutiset/miksi-gluteenittomat-sampylat-eivat-kohoa/).
- Estimated per slice (12 slices): 27.45 g available carbohydrate, 3.98 g protein and 3.08 g fat. These estimates use product data and assumed volume weights, including 60 g per 100 ml of flour blend. Glycaemic load has not been calculated.
