# Buckwheat galettes

Buckwheat flour gives these thin crêpes a nutty flavour suited to savoury fillings.

## Method

1. Mix the buckwheat flour, salt, eggs, milk and melted butter until smooth. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
2. Stir the water into the batter. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Brush it with a thin layer of oil.
3. Pour in just enough batter to coat the base thinly as you tilt the pan. Cook for 1–2 minutes, until the edge lifts easily, then turn and cook the other side for 1–2 minutes. Repeat with the rest of the batter.
4. Stack the galettes on a plate and cover with a cloth. Serve warm. Any filling is portioned separately.
