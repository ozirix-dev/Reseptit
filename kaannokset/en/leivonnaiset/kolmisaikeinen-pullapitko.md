# Three-strand cardamom pulla braid

This Finnish sweet loaf is flavoured with cardamom and braided from three strands of dough.

## Method

1. Warm the milk to lukewarm and dissolve the yeast in it. Add the sugar, salt and cardamom. Add the flour gradually, reserving a little for shaping. Knead in the butter and continue until the dough is elastic.
2. Cover the bowl and let the dough rise away from draughts until roughly doubled.
3. Divide the dough in three and roll into equal strands. Pinch the top ends together. Cross the right and left strands over the middle one in turn. Pinch the ends together and tuck beneath the braid.
4. Place the braid on baking parchment and let it rise, covered, until visibly puffy. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with pearl sugar.
5. Bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, until golden. Cool under a cloth and cut into 30 slices.
