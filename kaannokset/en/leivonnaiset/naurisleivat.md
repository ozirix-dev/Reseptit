# Finnish turnip flatbreads

Cooked turnip gives these thin barley, rye and wheat flatbreads a soft centre.

## Preparation

1. Peel and chop the turnips. Cover and boil them in the salted water until soft, about 35–45 minutes depending on the size of the pieces. Keep 200 ml of the cooking water and drain the rest.
2. Add the reserved water, the dough portion of the buttermilk, butter, dough salt and sugar. Blend until smooth and let cool to lukewarm. Dissolve the yeast in 50 ml of lukewarm water and stir it into the turnip mixture.
3. Knead in the flour. Cover and let rise for about 30 minutes. Divide the soft dough into eight pieces and pat them into rounds 1–1.5 cm thick on baking parchment. If desired, make holes in the centres and bake the cut-outs as well.
4. Cover the breads and let rise another 30 minutes. Prick the tops and brush lightly with buttermilk. Bake at 200 °C fan, no more than two trays at a time, for 20–25 minutes until cooked through and lightly browned.

## Notes

- Contains milk, barley, rye and wheat.
