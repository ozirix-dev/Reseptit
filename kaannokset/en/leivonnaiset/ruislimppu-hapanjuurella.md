# Rye sourdough loaves

An active rye starter raises two dark round loaves.

## Method

1. Mix the active starter, 200 ml of water and 110 g of rye flour. Cover loosely and leave in a warm spot overnight, until the preferment bubbles clearly and smells gently sour.
2. Stir in 600 ml of water and the salt. Add the dough flour in batches and knead by hand for about 10 minutes. Cover and let it roughly double, usually 3–6 hours.
3. Divide the dough in half on a floured surface and shape two round loaves. Place on baking parchment, cover and proof for 2–3 hours. Prick the tops with a fork.
4. Bake at 225°C for about 20 minutes, then lower to 180°C and bake for about 40 minutes more. A baked loaf sounds hollow when tapped underneath. Cool under a cloth. Cut each loaf into 16 slices.

## Notes

- Contains rye. Start with a ready-to-use sourdough starter.
