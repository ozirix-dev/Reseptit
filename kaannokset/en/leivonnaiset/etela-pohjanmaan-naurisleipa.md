# South Ostrobothnian turnip flatbread

Cooked turnip keeps these thin round flatbreads moist. The dough uses equal volumes of barley, rye and wheat flour.

## Preparation

1. Put the turnip pieces in a saucepan and cover with water. Boil until they are soft when pierced with a fork. Drain the cooking water into a separate container and mash the turnips. Let both cool until lukewarm.
2. Dissolve the yeast in a little lukewarm cooking water. Stir it into the mash with the salt. Mix the flours and add them gradually. Add more cooking water as needed to make a soft, shapeable dough.
3. Heat the oven to 275 °C. Divide the dough into six pieces. Pat each into a round 1–1.5 cm thick. Put the rounds on a tray rated for that oven temperature and prick the tops with a fork.
4. Bake one tray at a time until the tops have brown spots and the centres are cooked. Serve warm.

## Storage and safety

Contains barley, rye and wheat, which contain gluten. Use a tray rated for the oven temperature and handle it with oven gloves. Cool the breads on a rack before storing.
