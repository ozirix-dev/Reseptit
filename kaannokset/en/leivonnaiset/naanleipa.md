# Skillet naan

Yogurt dough bubbles and browns in patches on a hot skillet.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flours, water, yogurt, dough ghee, yeast, sugar and salt. Knead until smooth and elastic.
2. Cover and rise for 50–60 minutes until doubled. Divide into eight, shape into balls, cover and rest for 20 minutes.
3. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. On a lightly floured surface, roll or stretch the balls into ovals about 18 cm long.
4. Cook one at a time in the dry skillet. Cook for 30–40 seconds until bubbles form, then flip and cook for another 40–50 seconds until cooked and browned in spots.
5. Brush the hot breads with ghee and keep covered with a towel until serving.
