# Pão de queijo, Brazilian cheese buns

Tapioca starch gives these small cheese buns an elastic, chewy crumb.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 190 °C and line the baking trays with parchment. Put the tapioca starch in a heatproof bowl.
2. Bring the butter, water, milk and salt to a boil. Carefully pour the liquid onto the starch and mix right away. Beat until the mixture starts to stretch, then beat in the garlic and Parmesan.
3. Let the dough cool briefly. Add the lightly beaten eggs gradually and mix until smooth. The batter should hold a mound when spooned. If it spreads like liquid, add tapioca starch a little at a time.
4. Scoop mounds of about two tablespoons onto the trays, leaving space between them. Bake for about 20 minutes until the tops have browned flecks and the buns are cooked through. Rest briefly before serving.

## Notes

- Contains milk and eggs. For gluten-free buns, use tapioca starch labelled gluten-free and check the other products.
