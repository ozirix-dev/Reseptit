# Banana peanut butter muffins

Banana, cinnamon and peanut butter flavour these wholemeal muffins.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases. Mix the flour, cinnamon, bicarbonate of soda, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. Mash the banana. Whisk in the honey, yoghurt, oil, egg and almond drink. Stir in the peanut butter and vanilla.
3. Fold the dry ingredients into the batter just until no streaks of flour remain. Divide the batter evenly among the cases.
4. Bake for 5 minutes at 220°C. Reduce the temperature to 175°C without opening the oven and bake for about 15 minutes more, until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean. Cool for 5 minutes in the tin, then transfer to a rack.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, milk, egg, almonds and peanuts.
