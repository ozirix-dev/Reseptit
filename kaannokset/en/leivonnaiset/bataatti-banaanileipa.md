# Sweet potato and banana bread with chocolate and walnuts

## Description

Sweet potato puree and ripe banana form a moist dough. Peanut butter flavors the bread, and walnuts and chocolate are added to the dough and topping.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C with constant heat. Line a 20 x 10 cm loaf tin with baking paper.
2. Mash the sweet potato and bananas until smooth. Mix in the peanut butter and eggs. Add baking powder and mix evenly.
3. Fold most of the nuts and chocolate chips into the dough. Pour into a pan and sprinkle the rest on top.
4. Bake on medium for about 50-60 minutes. Start checking at 50 minutes. The center must coagulate and reach at least 71 °C. Damp crumbs and melted chocolate can be stuck to the test stick. If necessary, cover the surface lightly with foil at the end of baking.
5. Let rest in the pan for 15 minutes. Place on a wire rack with baking paper and cool before slicing.
