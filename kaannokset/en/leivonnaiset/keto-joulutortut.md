# Almond flour Christmas star pastries

These star-shaped pastries use a dough of almond flour, butter and cream cheese.

## Description

Plum jam fills the centres; its carbohydrate content depends on the product.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Mix almond flour, soft butter, cream cheese and egg into a dough.
2. Roll the dough between baking papers and cut into squares. Make incisions in the corners and turn the branches to the center.
3. Add a teaspoon of plum jam to the center of each tart.
4. Bake for 12–15 minutes. Allow to cool on the tray before transferring.

## Notes

- Check for doneness and finally season according to your own taste.
- The carbohydrate content depends on the products used and the portion size. Calculate it from the labels of your chosen products, including any accompaniments and toppings.
