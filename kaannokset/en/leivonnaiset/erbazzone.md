# Erbazzone, Italian chard and spinach pie

A thin pastry lid covers a filling of chard, spinach and Parmesan. Serve in rectangular pieces.

## Instructions

1. Knead the flour, 30 g of lard, 3 g of salt and the lukewarm water to form a smooth dough. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
2. Rinse and drain the spinach and chard. Remove the toughest chard ribs and roughly chop the leaves. Slice the spring onions and mince the garlic.
3. Brown the pancetta with the spring onions and garlic in a large pan. Add the greens in batches and cook for around 15 minutes, allowing the released water to evaporate. Season with the filling salt. Let it cool until lukewarm, then mix in the Parmesan.
4. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Use some of the reserved lard to grease a tin about 23 × 23 cm. Divide the dough and roll a thin base and an even thinner lid. Fit the base into the tin with a little overhang. Spread the filling evenly, lay the lid on top, pinch the edges together and prick the lid with a fork.
5. Brush the top with the remaining reserved lard. Bake for 30–34 minutes, until golden and the pastry is cooked. Rest for a few minutes, then cut into six pieces.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat and milk. Cook the pancetta according to the package directions. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
