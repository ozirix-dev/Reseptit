# Tray-baked avocado and potato rolls

## Description

Boiled potato and avocado form the dough for these rolls. Shape them directly on baking paper and sprinkle them with sesame seeds. The recipe makes six small rolls.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C with conventional top and bottom heat. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Mash the cooled potato and the avocado with a fork until smooth. Mix in the egg and yoghurt. Add the baking powder and salt.
3. Divide the dough into six mounds on the tray, about 70 g each. Round them with a damp spoon, leave space between them and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
4. Bake on the middle rack for about 25–35 minutes. Check for doneness at 25 minutes. The tops should be golden brown, the centres set, and the internal temperature at least 71 °C.
5. Let the rolls cool on the tray for about 10 minutes. Transfer them to a wire rack and let them cool before cutting them open.
