# Moomie's soft hamburger buns – nine-bun version

## Description

Soft, slightly sweet hamburger buns adapted to nine from Ellen Dorsey's (Moomie's) Beautiful Burger Buns.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies weighed amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Combine 473 g wheat flour, 56 g sugar, 8 g salt and 10 g dry yeast. Add 223 g lukewarm water, 55 g egg weighed without shell and 32 g softened butter.
2. Knead for about 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and let rise 60–120 minutes, until nearly doubled.
3. Divide into nine equally sized pieces, form balls and press slightly flat to approximately 8–9 cm across.
4. Place the buns well apart on parchment. Nine may require an extra-large tray or two trays. Cover and proof for another 45–60 minutes.
5. Preheat the oven to 190 °C using top and bottom heat. Melt 48 g butter, brush half (24 g) onto the buns and sprinkle all 9 g sesame seeds over them.
6. Bake for 15–18 minutes until golden and cooked through. Immediately brush with the remaining 24 g melted butter and cool on a wire rack.

## Notes

- Based on Ellen Dorsey's (Moomie's) [Beautiful Burger Buns at King Arthur Baking](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/beautiful-burger-buns-recipe) and [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QHvjvpVdBxw). Original yields eight buns; this version yields nine.
- Nutrition counts all 48 g butter and 9 g sesame seeds. The 165-minute total is an estimate; longer proofing can exceed it. No approved GI/GK value and this nine-bun version has not been test-baked.
