# Traditional Finnish Cinnamon Rolls

A traditional Finnish home cooking recipe.

## Preparation

1. Warm the milk until lukewarm (about 37°C). Dissolve fresh yeast in it. Mix in the egg, dough sugar, cardamom and salt.
2. Gradually add flour and knead until elastic. Work in 120 g soft butter towards the end. Cover and let rise for about 45 minutes until doubled.
3. Divide dough in two and roll each into a rectangle. Spread 100 g soft filling butter and sprinkle on the filling sugar and cinnamon evenly.
4. Roll both sheets into logs and cut about 30 slanted pieces in total. Place the wide side down, press the narrow tip in the middle and rise on trays for 30 minutes.
5. Brush with beaten egg and sprinkle pearl sugar. Bake at 225°C for about 8–12 minutes until golden; cool on a rack under a clean towel.
