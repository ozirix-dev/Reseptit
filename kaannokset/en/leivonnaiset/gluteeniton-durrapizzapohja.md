# Gluten-free sorghum pizza with tomatoes and mozzarella

## Description

Yogurt, gluten-free sorghum flour blend and oil make a base for one pizza about 30 × 40 cm or two smaller pizzas. The tomato and mozzarella topping is included because the base is baked with its toppings.

## Ingredients

The ingredient list includes the base, the oil for the parchment and all the toppings. The serving calculator scales them together.

## Preparation

1. Mix the plain yogurt, dry yeast and salt in a bowl until smooth. Let stand for a few minutes.
2. Combine the sorghum flour blend and baking powder separately. Stir into the yogurt mixture until all the flour is moistened.
3. Knead or stir the dough for about one minute and add the two tablespoons of olive oil measured for the dough.
4. Line a tray with baking parchment and spread the separately measured two tablespoons of oil on it. Pat out a 30 × 40 cm base with the edges slightly higher than the center.
5. Cover lightly and let rise in a warm place for about 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 225–250 °C.
6. Spread the measured gluten-free tomato sauce over the base. Add the torn mozzarella and halved cherry tomatoes.
7. Bake the topped pizza on the lower rack for about 15 minutes, until the edges and base are golden and the cheese has melted. Check that the center is cooked through before serving.
8. Add clean fresh basil after baking, cut into four portions and serve.

## Notes

- Bake the pizza with the toppings, following the method of the source recipe. Recipavo developed the measured tomato and mozzarella topping. Verify that each topping is gluten-free from the package and bake the center through.
- Check the packages of the sorghum blend, baking powder, sauce and all other toppings for gluten-free labelling and use clean equipment. The Dr. Oetker 160 g product is sold as gluten-free. Check the pack you actually use.
- The estimates per quarter pizza are 44.09 g available carbohydrate, 17.07 g protein and 32.83 g fat. They include all toppings and all oil on the parchment, some of which may stay on the paper. [Calculation notes](https://github.com/ozirix-dev/Reseptit/blob/main/docs/nutrition/gluteenittomat-fi-kaksikko-2026-11-11.md). GL is undetermined.
- The 500 g Virtasalmen Viljatuote blend (EAN 6430041081623) weighs about 65 g per dl according to its manufacturer. It contains 70% sorghum, potato and tapioca starch, and psyllium. Check the package and product choice before use.
- **Allergens:** milk in the yogurt and mozzarella, and celery in the Hungry Chef sauce. The image is an illustration. This version has not been test-baked.
