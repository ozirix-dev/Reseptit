# Indian besan chilla with tomato and onion

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Whisk all 100 g chickpea flour with 100 ml water into a smooth batter. Add only the listed optional salt and rest for 10 minutes.
2. Finely chop the 1 tomato, 1 onion and all 6 g cilantro. Stir all of them through the batter.
3. Heat a pan over medium heat. Divide the listed 1 tablespoon frying oil between batches. Spread the mixture into thin pancakes and cook both sides until firm and cooked through.
4. Divide all pancakes into two servings and serve warm. The tomato and cilantro are already in the batter; no unlisted tomato chutney is added.
