# Kongjuk, Korean soy bean and rice porridge

## Description

In this home-style kongjuk, soy beans are cooked until completely tender, then some are mashed into the rice porridge. Sesame is sprinkled over each serving.

## Ingredients

Use dried soy beans, dry white long-grain rice and white sesame seeds. Set aside separate cold water for soaking.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the beans. Cover generously with cold water and soak in the fridge for at least 8 hours. Discard the soaking water and rinse the beans.
2. Put the beans and 700 ml fresh water in a large saucepan. Bring to a boil and simmer with the lid ajar until the beans are fully cooked and tender. Allow about 1¼–3 hours for simmering and follow the packet cooking instructions. Add hot water as needed to keep the beans covered.
3. Rinse the rice and add it to the cooked beans and their cooking water. Simmer for 25–30 minutes or until the rice is very soft. Stir occasionally. Mash some of the beans with a spoon and stir the porridge until evenly mixed.
4. Add the listed 1 tsp salt. Taste and add more salt if desired. Divide between two bowls and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

## Notes

Contains soy and sesame. Total time includes soaking and varies with the beans. Cool leftovers quickly in a shallow container within one hour. Refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot throughout.
