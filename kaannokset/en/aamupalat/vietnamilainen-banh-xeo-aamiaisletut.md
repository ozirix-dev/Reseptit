# Vietnamese shrimp and bean-sprout pancakes (banh xeo)

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Whisk all 100 g rice flour with 200 ml full-fat coconut milk into a thin batter, using only the listed salt if desired. Turmeric is not in this measured recipe, so the pancakes will be pale.
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat and divide the listed 1 tablespoon oil between the cooking batches. Cook all 150 g shrimp thoroughly before adding them as a filling.
3. Pour in thin pancakes and fry until set with slightly crisp edges. Add all 150 g rinsed bean sprouts and the fully cooked shrimp, heating the sprouts thoroughly.
4. Fold the pancakes around the filling and divide into two servings. Do not add unlisted turmeric, extra frying oil or sauces.
