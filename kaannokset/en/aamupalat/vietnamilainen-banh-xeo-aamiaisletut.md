# Vietnamese bánh xèo with shrimp

## Description

Fold thin turmeric rice pancakes over cooked shrimp and mung bean sprouts.

## Ingredients

Use white rice flour rather than glutinous rice flour. Weigh the oil for the filling and pancakes separately.

## Preparation

1. Whisk the flour, coconut milk, water, turmeric and salt into a thin batter. Rest for 30 minutes.
2. Rinse and drain the sprouts well. Cook the shrimp and sprouts in the filling oil until the sprouts are hot all the way through and the shrimp are completely opaque. Transfer the filling and all pan juices to a clean bowl.
3. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat and lightly brush it with the pancake oil. Stir the batter before each pour. Spread a small ladleful in a very thin layer and cook without flipping until the batter is cooked through and the edges are dry and crisp.
4. Add the cooked filling to one side and fold the pancake over it. Repeat until all the batter and filling are used. Divide evenly into two servings and serve at once.

## Notes

Contains crustaceans. Do not serve raw sprouts or shrimp. The nutrition estimate excludes up to 1 g pancake oil per serving. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
