# Moroccan baghrir with honey

## Description

These thin Moroccan pancakes develop tiny holes on top as they cook on one side.

## Ingredients

Use fine semolina and ordinary starch-based baking powder. Add the honey at serving.

## Preparation

1. Blend the semolina, wheat flour, dry yeast, salt and lukewarm water until smooth. Add the baking powder and blend briefly.
2. Cover the bowl and let the batter rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
3. Heat a dry nonstick pan over medium heat. Pour a small ladleful of batter into the centre. Cook on one side only until holes open and the surface is dry all over, with no visible raw batter.
4. Transfer the pancakes to a plate. Divide the pancakes and honey evenly between two servings.

## Notes

Contains wheat and gluten. Do not taste raw batter. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
