# North African baghrir semolina pancakes with honey

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Mix all 100 g semolina, 100 g wheat flour, 5 g dry yeast and only the listed salt if needed. Whisk in all 150 ml warm water until as smooth as possible.
2. Rest in a warm place for at least 10 minutes. With this limited amount of water and short proof, the batter will be thicker than traditional baghrir; do not silently add extra liquid.
3. Heat the listed 1 tablespoon frying oil and divide the batter into small pancakes. Cook primarily on one side until holes appear and the tops are fully set without raw batter.
4. Share the pancakes between two portions and drizzle the listed 1 tablespoon honey over them in total. Butter or additional honey is not part of this measured recipe.
