# Korean vegetable rice porridge (yachaejuk)

## Description

This home-style recipe makes Korean rice porridge with vegetables. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Measure the rice into a saucepan and add the water. Bring to a boil while stirring along the bottom of the pan.
2. Simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally, to make a soft, thick porridge. Add liquid a little at a time if it gets too thick.
3. When the rice is nearly soft, add the finely chopped carrot and courgette. Simmer until the vegetables and rice are cooked through.
4. Stir in the sesame oil and season with salt.
