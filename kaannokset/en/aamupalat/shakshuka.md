# Shakshuka with smoked paprika

## Instructions

1. Soften 70 g onion and 120 g red pepper in 13.5 g olive oil. Add 200 g crushed tomatoes, 2 g cumin, 1 g Santa Maria smoked paprika and 0.3 g pure chilli flakes. Simmer for 10 min.
2. Make wells and cook 220 g shelled egg covered in the sauce. Add 8 g parsley, 0.5 g black pepper and salt. Divide all food and sauce into two servings.

## Notes

Weigh the edible ingredients listed above. All listed food, sauce and cooking fat belong to the servings; separate side dishes are excluded. Nutrition values are temporarily withheld pending source verification.
