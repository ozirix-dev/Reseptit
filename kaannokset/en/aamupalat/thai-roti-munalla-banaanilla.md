# Thai-style egg and banana coconut pancakes

## Description

Thai-inspired coconut-banana egg pancakes from a measured home recipe; these are not traditional stretched roti.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Whisk all 100 g wheat flour with 200 ml coconut milk into a thin batter. Beat in both eggs and only the listed optional salt.
2. Peel and slice the 125 g edible flesh of the one medium banana. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat, dividing the listed 1 tablespoon oil (13.5 g in the model) between two batches. The two eggs represent 110 g without shells and 200 ml coconut milk represents 184 g in the nutrition model.
3. Spread half the batter into a thin pancake and add half the banana slices. Cook gently on both sides until all egg and flour in the batter are cooked through. Repeat with the rest.
4. Fold each pancake into quarters and serve one per portion. The coconut-milk batter makes a roti-style pancake, not traditional stretched roti. Condensed milk or sweet sauce is not included.
