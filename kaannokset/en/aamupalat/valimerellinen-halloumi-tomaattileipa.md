# Halloumi and tomato toast with olives

## Description

Toasted sourdough topped with warm halloumi, tomato and olives.

## Preparation

1. Cut 150 g halloumi into even slices. Wash and slice 100 g tomatoes. Drain or slice 25 g green olives and remove pits if necessary. Use 100 g ready-baked sourdough bread in total.
2. Heat a pan over medium heat with the listed 1 tbsp neutral frying oil. Fry the halloumi on both sides until golden. Add no other fat.
3. Toast the bread in a dry pan or a toaster until crisp.
4. Divide the toast, tomato, olives and fried halloumi between two plates. Salt only if needed and serve the halloumi warm.
