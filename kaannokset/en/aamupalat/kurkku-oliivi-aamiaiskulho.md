# Greek-style cucumber and olive breakfast bowl

## Description

A savoury yogurt bowl with cucumber, tomato, olives and feta.

## Preparation

1. Cut the cucumber and tomatoes into bite-sized pieces. Halve the olives if needed.
2. Spread the yogurt in the bottom of a bowl and drizzle with half the olive oil.
3. Add the vegetables, olives and crumbled feta.
4. Season with oregano, pepper and the remaining olive oil.

## Notes

Feta varies in saltiness. Taste before adding salt.
