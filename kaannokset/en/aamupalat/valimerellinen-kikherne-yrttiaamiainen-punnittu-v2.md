# Lemony chickpea and herb breakfast with egg

## Description

Two fresh breakfast bowls with cooked chickpeas, cucumber, parsley, lemon and olive oil dressing, and hard-boiled eggs. This version specifies weighed amounts.

## Ingredients

Use the quantities in the ingredient list. For two servings, weigh 100 g of **cooked and drained** chickpeas, using neither dry weight nor the gross weight of a can.

## Preparation

1. Hard-boil two eggs. Cool, peel and slice them. The nutrition estimate uses about 50 g per egg without the shell. Actual weight varies.
2. Use chickpeas previously cooked through in unsalted water. Drain and weigh 100 g of the edible portion. If starting with dried chickpeas, follow the package instructions for safe soaking and cooking before starting the recipe, which takes 20 minutes.
3. Weigh and dice 150 g fresh cucumber. Chop 8 g parsley.
4. Mix 20 g lemon juice, 10 g olive oil and 1 g salt. Toss the dressing with the chickpeas, cucumber and parsley. Use all the oil and juice for the two servings.
5. Divide the salad between two bowls and top each with one sliced hard-boiled egg. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The nutrition figures are database estimates for two servings. Fineli 11089 represents cooked, drained chickpeas. Values for canned products vary by brand. Eggs are estimated at 2 × 50 g without shells. **Allergen: egg.**
- **GL: undetermined.** This version has no approved glycemic load calculation for the chickpea preparation used. A missing GL value does not indicate zero.
