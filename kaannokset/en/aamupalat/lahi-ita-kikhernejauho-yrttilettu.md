# Chickpea flour and herb pancakes

## Description

This home-style recipe makes chickpea flour pancakes with herbs. Adjust the seasoning and sides to taste.

## Preparation

1. Whisk the chickpea flour and water into a smooth batter. Stir in the finely chopped parsley, onion and cumin.
2. Leave the batter to rest for 10 minutes.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan. Fry two thin pancakes from the batter, cooking both sides through.
4. Season with salt and serve warm.
