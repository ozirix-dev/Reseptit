# Chinese tea eggs with black tea and star anise

## Description

A quick home-kitchen version of Chinese tea eggs. The eggs are cracked in their shells before they are steeped, then peeled and served.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Boil the 2 eggs in their shells in water for 8–9 minutes. Cool briefly until they can be handled; do not peel them yet.
2. Heat all 150 ml brewed black tea, 1 tablespoon soy sauce and 1 teaspoon star anise in a small saucepan. Add only the listed salt to taste if needed.
3. Tap the cooked eggs gently with a spoon to crack the shells all over without removing them. Put them into the tea mixture and turn them so the small amount of liquid touches the shells evenly.
4. Cover and simmer gently for about 10 minutes. Peel the eggs only after steeping and serve 1 egg per portion. The tea liquid is for seasoning, not an extra sauce.

## Notes

This 25-minute version gives a lighter tea flavour than traditional overnight steeping. The measured 150 ml tea and all seasonings remain the entire batch; no additional sauce is included.
