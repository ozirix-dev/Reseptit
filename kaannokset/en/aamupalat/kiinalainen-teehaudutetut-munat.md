# Chinese tea eggs

## Description

Hard-boiled eggs are gently simmered with cracked shells in a black tea and star anise infusion.

## Preparation

1. Boil 2 eggs in their shells in water for 8–9 minutes. Cool briefly until the shells can be handled, leaving the eggs unpeeled.
2. Heat 150 ml brewed black tea, 1 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tsp star anise to make the infusion. Add the listed salt only if needed.
3. Tap the shells of the hard-boiled eggs with a spoon to make small cracks. Leave the shells on. Put the eggs in the infusion in a small saucepan and turn them to coat the shells evenly even though there is little liquid.
4. Cover and simmer gently for about 10 minutes. Peel the eggs once they have absorbed the flavour and serve one egg per portion. The infusion need not be served as a separate sauce.
