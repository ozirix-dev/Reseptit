# Msemen – Moroccan layered flatbreads with honey

## Description

The dough is stretched thin and folded into squares with butter and semolina. The pan-fried breads are served warm with honey. Makes four flatbreads.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Mix the wheat flour, the semolina for the dough and the salt in a bowl. Add the lukewarm water and the oil measured for the dough. Knead for about 8 minutes until smooth and elastic.
2. Divide the dough into four pieces and shape into balls. Lightly oil the balls and the work surface with some of the remaining oil. Cover the balls and let them rest for 30 minutes.
3. Melt the butter and mix in the remaining oil. With your palm, press a dough ball into a nearly translucent sheet about 25 cm across. Brush lightly with the butter and oil mixture and sprinkle with some of the semolina reserved for folding.
4. Fold the top and bottom edges toward the middle so they overlap slightly. Brush the strip with a little of the fat mixture and sprinkle with semolina. Fold the sides in the same way to make a square. Repeat with the other balls. Cover the squares and let them rest for 15 minutes.
5. Press each square out to about 17–18 cm across. Fry over medium-high heat for about 2–3 minutes per side, until brown in spots and cooked through. Adjust the heat if the outside darkens before the layers are cooked.
6. Drizzle the honey over the warm breads. Serve two flatbreads per portion.

## Notes

This recipe contains wheat and milk. Do not give honey to children under one year of age. The nutrition estimate includes all the measured butter and oil.
