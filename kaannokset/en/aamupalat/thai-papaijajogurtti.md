# Papaya yogurt with fresh coconut

## Description

Papaya yogurt with fresh coconut.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Weigh 200 g of peeled raw papaya in pieces, not pureed, for this fresh-coconut version.
2. Mix 200 g of unsweetened yogurt with 15 g of lime juice. Add 15 g of freshly grated flesh from a mature coconut.
3. Divide all the yogurt, papaya and coconut into two servings. Do not substitute dried coconut or add unlisted sweetener.
