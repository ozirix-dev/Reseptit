# Indonesian breakfast fried rice with kecap manis

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g rice, tentatively treated as dry rice, in the package's recommended amount of water. Drain if needed and spread out briefly to cool. The raw-versus-cooked rice weight must still be verified.
2. Peel and chop the 1 shallot. Warm part of the listed 1 tablespoon oil in a pan and soften the shallot, then add all cooked rice.
3. Stir exactly the listed 150 g kecap manis into the rice and heat evenly. That amount is unusually large and must undergo recipe-source and nutrition review before publication; do not quietly reduce it.
4. Fry both eggs in the remainder of the same measured oil until cooked. Divide the rice between two portions and top each with one egg. Use only listed salt; garlic is not included.

## Notes

The 150 g sweet soy sauce is an unresolved ingredient anomaly, not a recommended customary serving.
