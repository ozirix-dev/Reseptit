# Gingerbread latte with honey

## Description

Coffee foam is whipped from instant coffee, honey and gingerbread spices. Freeze the foam in small portions and mix one portion with hot milk.

## Instructions

1. Combine instant coffee, honey and hot water in a narrow, tall bowl. Stir until the coffee dissolves.
2. Beat with an electric mixer for 2–5 minutes until the mixture becomes lighter and noticeably thickens. Add cinnamon, ginger and cloves. Vatkaa vielä hetki.
3. Spoon the foam into the four holes in the ice cube tray. Cover and freeze for at least 2 hours. The honey foam can remain a little soft, so you can remove it with a spoon if necessary.
4. Heat 2 dl of milk for one serving. Add one scoop of frozen coffee foam and stir until dissolved.
