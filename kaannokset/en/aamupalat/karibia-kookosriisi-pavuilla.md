# Caribbean coconut rice with kidney beans

## Description

Jasmine rice cooks in coconut milk and is served with kidney beans and thyme.

## Preparation

1. Cook 100 g dry white jasmine rice in 184 g coconut milk over low heat for the cooking time given on the rice packet.
2. Add 150 g cooked, drained whole kidney beans and 6 g fresh thyme. Heat through.
3. Divide all the rice, any remaining cooking liquid and the beans into two portions. Do not purée or count any carbohydrate reduction from cooling.
