# Orange olive oil cake

## Description

Orange gives this small yogurt cake a fruity flavour and olive oil keeps the crumb moist. Serve the cake cooled.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C with conventional top and bottom heat. Line a round cake tin about 16 cm across with baking parchment.
2. Peel the orange and remove the seeds and thick white pith. Weigh out 65 g of the peeled fruit and purée it.
3. Beat the shelled egg and weigh out 55 g. Whisk the egg and sugar together until smooth, then add the yogurt, olive oil and orange purée.
4. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt. Fold them into the wet ingredients just until combined. Pour into the lined tin.
5. Bake on the middle rack for about 35–45 minutes, until the centre is set and a skewer comes out without raw batter. Let cool before cutting into six slices.

## Notes

**Allergens are wheat, milk and egg.** Nutrition values are estimates. The orange and yogurt go into the batter.
