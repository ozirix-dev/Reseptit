# Ful medames with broad beans

## Description

A warm broad-bean dish seasoned with lemon, cumin and parsley.

## Preparation

1. Use 150 g cooked, drained broad beans. Rinse if needed and put them in a small saucepan. Dried beans cannot be cooked in the stated 25 minutes.
2. Warm the beans gently with just enough water to prevent sticking. Stir in 1 tsp cumin. Mash some of the hot beans with a fork and leave the rest whole.
3. Stir in all 1 tbsp neutral oil and 30 g lemon juice. Chop and add 2 tbsp parsley. Salt only if needed.
4. Divide into two portions and serve warm. The measured recipe contains no eggs, tomato, bread or extra olive oil.

## Notes

Use fully cooked beans. Dried beans would need separate preparation and a different starting weight. Accompaniments in the image are serving suggestions.
