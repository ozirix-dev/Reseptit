# Chinese tomato and egg stir-fry with rice

## Description

A home-kitchen version based on the measured ingredient quantities.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cook the listed dry rice according to the package instructions. Its dry weight is 100 g; the cooking water is not served as part of the meal.
2. Chop the tomato and finely slice the spring onion. Whisk the eggs in a bowl and season lightly with the listed salt.
3. Heat part of the listed frying oil in a pan. Briefly scramble the eggs until soft and set aside.
4. Add the remaining listed oil and cook the tomatoes until softened into a juicy sauce. Return the eggs to the pan, stir and heat through.
5. Serve the warm tomato-and-egg mixture over the cooked rice and finish with spring onion.
