# Malaysian peanut pancake (apam balik)

## Description

A folded pancake filled with peanuts, sweetcorn and sugar.

## Preparation

1. Whisk 100 g wheat flour, one egg and 100 ml water to a smooth, fairly thick batter. Add the listed salt and rest for 10 minutes.
2. Crush 20 g peanuts. Drain 80 g corn if needed and set aside 25 g sugar for the filling. The batter needs no raising agent.
3. Heat the listed 1 tbsp neutral oil in a pan. Spread the batter into one thick pancake or two smaller ones. Cook over medium-low heat until the surface is no longer runny and the underside has coloured.
4. Spread the corn, crushed peanuts and sugar over one half of the pancake. Fold the other half over the filling and continue cooking, covered, over low heat until the batter is cooked through. Divide into two servings.
