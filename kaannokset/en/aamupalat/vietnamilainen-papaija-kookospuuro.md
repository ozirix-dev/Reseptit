# Vietnamese papaya and coconut tapioca pudding

## Description

Soft tapioca pearls thicken this coconut pudding. Add ripe papaya and lime at serving.

## Ingredients

Use small dry pearls that can be boiled without presoaking according to the packet. Use all the weighed coconut milk.

## Preparation

1. Bring the water to a boil. Stir in the pearls and reduce the heat.
2. Simmer for 15–20 minutes or as directed on the packet. Stir and scrape the bottom until the pearls are translucent and fully soft. Do not drain the cooking water.
3. Stir the coconut milk and salt into the pudding and heat gently. Dice the papaya and toss with the lime juice.
4. Divide the pudding and fruit evenly between two bowls.

## Notes

Check the packages for allergens. Cool leftovers quickly and refrigerate. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
