# Vietnamese papaya and coconut tapioca porridge

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Place all 100 g dry tapioca pearls in a saucepan with the listed 700 ml water. Bring to a boil, stirring to prevent sticking.
2. Simmer according to the tapioca package instructions, stirring occasionally, until the pearls are fully translucent and tender. Keep the lid on if excessive evaporation occurs; do not add unmeasured water.
3. Stir in all 200 ml coconut milk, heat through and divide into two warm bowls. Add only the listed salt if needed.
4. Cut all 200 g edible papaya into pieces and divide over the bowls. Distribute the listed 15 g lime juice equally between them. No meat or other toppings are included.
