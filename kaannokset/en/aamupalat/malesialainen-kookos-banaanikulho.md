# Coconut banana bowl with gula melaka

## Description

Warm banana slices and tender tapioca pearls in coconut milk, drizzled with dark gula melaka palm sugar syrup. This bowl is adapted from the Malaysian dessert pengat pisang.

## Preparation

1. Cook the tapioca pearls in plenty of water according to the packet instructions. When they are soft and translucent, drain them in a sieve. Use separate water for cooking the pearls.
2. Dissolve the palm sugar in 35 g of water in a small saucepan over low heat. Stir until smooth and remove from the heat.
3. Warm the coconut milk and salt in a separate saucepan over low heat. Slice the banana, add it to the coconut milk and heat gently so the slices stay intact.
4. Divide the pearls, banana slices and coconut milk between two bowls. Drizzle with the syrup and serve warm.

## Notes

Use small pearls with cooking directions on the packet. Total time depends on the pearls. The coconut flakes pictured are a serving suggestion.
