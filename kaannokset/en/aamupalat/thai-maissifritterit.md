# Thai corn fritters with basil, pan-fried

## Description

Pan-fried corn fritters made from drained cooked corn, eggs, wheat flour and Thai basil, using one tablespoon oil for the whole batch.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Drain and pat dry all 150 g precooked corn kernels. Finely chop the listed 2 tablespoons fresh Thai basil; do not mash the corn.
2. Beat both eggs in a bowl, then mix in all 100 g wheat flour, the corn, Thai basil and only the listed salt to taste. Do not add water, extra flour or other ingredients.
3. Heat only the listed 1 tablespoon neutral frying oil for the entire batch in a skillet. Spoon small, slightly flattened fritters into the pan and cook over medium heat in batches; distribute the same measured oil between batches.
4. Turn and fry both sides until golden and thoroughly cooked through, leaving no raw egg or flour in the center. Divide all the fritters into two servings. Lime and sweet chili sauce are not included in this measured version.

## Notes

Use the entire listed egg, corn, flour, basil and oil amounts. The nutrient estimates do not include extra water, lime or chili sauce, and GI/GK approval has not been independently renewed.
