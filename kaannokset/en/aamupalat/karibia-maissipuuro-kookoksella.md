# Caribbean coconut cornmeal porridge

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version of this breakfast dish.

## Ingredients

All ingredient quantities are stored above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Mix 100 g of fine, non-nixtamalized cornmeal with 700 g of water. Bring to a simmer while stirring and cook until completely done.
2. Add 184 g coconut milk, 2.6 g ground cinnamon and 5 g vanilla extract. All the spices stay in the porridge; do not strain.
3. Divide the whole batch into two servings.
