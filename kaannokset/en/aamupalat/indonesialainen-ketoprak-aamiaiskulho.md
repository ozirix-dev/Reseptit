# Ketoprak-style tofu and rice noodle bowl

## Description

Inspired by Jakarta’s ketoprak, this bowl combines tofu, rice noodles, thoroughly cooked mung bean sprouts and peanut sauce.

## Ingredients

Use firm tofu and unsalted peanuts. The 30 ml of water listed is for the sauce.

## Preparation

1. Cook the dried rice noodles according to the packet, drain them and divide between two bowls.
2. Rinse the sprouts and boil them in a separate pan until thoroughly cooked and steaming hot throughout. Drain. Cut the tofu into pieces, heat it in a dry non-stick pan and add the tofu and sprouts to the noodles.
3. Toast the peanuts in a dry pan over low heat, stirring. Grind them as finely as possible and gradually stir in 30 ml of water. Add salt to taste if desired.
4. Spoon the peanut sauce over the bowls and serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains peanuts and soy. The illustration also shows vegetables and crunchy accompaniments as a serving suggestion.
