# Chinese-style rice porridge with whole black sesame seeds

## Description

A precisely weighed rice-based porridge with black sesame seeds for two servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredients are listed in the structured serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g dry white jasmine rice in 700 g water for about 40 minutes until it becomes a soft, hot porridge.
2. Add 200 g unsweetened soy drink, 20 g honey and 10 g whole black sesame seeds. Heat through and season with salt.
3. Divide all the porridge between two portions. Eat the seeds whole; do not grind them into paste. This is a rice-based version, not an estimate for a porridge containing a large amount of sesame paste.
