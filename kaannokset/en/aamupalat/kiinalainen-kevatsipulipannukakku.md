# Chinese scallion pancakes

## Description

Chinese scallion pancakes.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Mix wheat flour, water and a pinch of listed salt to make a pliable dough. Knead briefly, cover and rest for about 15 minutes.
2. Finely chop the spring onion. Divide dough in two and roll each piece into a thin sheet. Brush with all the listed sesame oil and sprinkle spring onion over the sheets.
3. Roll each sheet into a cylinder, coil the rolls and gently flatten them into pancakes again so the onion remains between the layers.
4. Heat the listed neutral frying oil over medium heat. Fry both pancakes on each side until golden, crisp and cooked through.
5. Cut into wedges and serve with the listed soy sauce as a dip; no other filling is needed.
