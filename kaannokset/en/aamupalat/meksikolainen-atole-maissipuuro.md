# Mexican atole-style corn porridge

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version of this breakfast dish.

## Ingredients

All ingredient quantities are stored above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Mix 100 g of fine, non-nixtamalized cornmeal with 700 g of water. Heat while stirring and simmer until the cornmeal is fully cooked and the porridge is hot.
2. Add 200 g of whole milk, 2.6 g ground cinnamon and 5 g of the specified vanilla extract. Consume all the spices; do not strain them out.
3. Divide the entire porridge into two servings. Do not add any other sweetener.
