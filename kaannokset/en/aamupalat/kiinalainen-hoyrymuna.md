# Chinese savoury steamed eggs

## Description

Egg and water steam to a silky custard, topped with soy and spring onion.

## Preparation

1. Lightly beat the eggs with lukewarm water and salt. Strain the mixture into four heatproof bowls and loosely cover them with foil.
2. Steam over gently simmering water for about 12–16 minutes, until the centres are set and reach at least 71 °C. Make sure the water does not boil dry.
3. Carefully lift the bowls out. Add the soy sauce, sesame oil and sliced spring onion.
