# Vietnamese egg bánh mì with fresh vegetables

## Description

A home-kitchen version based on the measured ingredient quantities.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cut the peeled carrot and cucumber into thin strips. Divide the baguette into two portions and split each piece lengthwise.
2. Heat the listed frying oil in a pan and fry the eggs until cooked through. Season if needed with the listed salt.
3. Warm the baguette pieces briefly in a dry pan or oven so their crusts become crisp while the insides remain soft.
4. Fill the baguettes with the fried eggs, carrot and cucumber. In this ingredient-limited version the carrot is fresh, not pickled; do not add unlisted vinegar or coriander.
5. Serve immediately while the bread is warm and the vegetables crisp. Do not knead or bake the ready-made baguette as a new dough.
