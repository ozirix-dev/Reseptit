# Warm flaxseed porridge with walnuts

## Description

This warm, spoonable seed porridge cooks in a saucepan without grains. Walnuts add texture.

## Preparation

1. Combine the ground flaxseed, coconut flour, almond milk, cream and a pinch of salt in a saucepan.
2. Heat over low heat, stirring constantly, for 4–6 minutes until the mixture thickens.
3. Season with cinnamon. Add a splash of water if needed to reach the desired consistency.
4. Serve with walnuts and raspberries.

## Notes

The porridge thickens as it stands. Serve immediately or loosen it with a little hot liquid.
