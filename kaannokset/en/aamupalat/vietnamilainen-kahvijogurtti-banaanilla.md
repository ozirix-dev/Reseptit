# Vietnamese-style coffee yogurt with banana

## Description

A lightly thick, pourable cold coffee-and-yogurt breakfast with sliced banana, for two servings.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Brew 200 ml coffee without added sugar or milk and cool it completely. Measure 100 g yogurt and 30 g condensed milk.
2. Combine the cooled coffee with all 30 g condensed milk. Gradually stir in all 100 g yogurt until smooth. The result is pourable, not a thick quark bowl.
3. Peel the one listed banana and slice its entire edible portion. Divide the coffee-yogurt mixture between two glasses or bowls.
4. Share the banana slices equally between the portions and serve cool. Add no extra sugar, milk or coffee accompaniments.

## Notes

The banana's edible gram weight and the particular condensed-milk product remain source-review items.
