# Jamaican-style scrambled eggs with tomato and thyme

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version of this breakfast dish.

## Ingredients

All ingredient quantities are stored above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Dice the tomato, finely chop the Scotch bonnet chilli and strip the thyme leaves. Handle the hot chilli carefully.
2. Heat the listed frying oil in a pan. Cook the tomato and chilli for a couple of minutes until the tomato softens. Add the thyme.
3. Lightly beat the eggs in a bowl and season with the listed salt. Pour into the pan and lower the heat.
4. Move the eggs gently with a spatula until softly scrambled with no liquid raw egg left. Serve immediately.
