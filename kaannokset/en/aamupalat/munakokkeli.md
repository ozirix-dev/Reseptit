# Creamy scrambled eggs – weighed version

## Description

Creamy scrambled eggs – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above is used by the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add the cream, salt and pepper, and stir gently with a fork until just combined.
2. Melt the butter in a pan over low heat and pour in the egg mixture.
3. Use a spatula to draw the setting egg from the edges towards the centre. Remove the pan from the heat while the scrambled eggs are still slightly glossy.
4. Finish with chopped chives and serve immediately. Use all listed ingredients; do not add extra fat.
