# North African maakouda potato patties

## Description

A home-kitchen version based on the measured ingredient quantities.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and boil until tender. Drain thoroughly and mash. Do not add the cooking water to the mixture.
2. Finely chop the parsley. Mix the mashed potatoes with eggs, parsley, cumin and a pinch of the listed salt.
3. Warm the listed frying oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat. Spoon the potato mixture into small flat patties; the mixture may be soft.
4. Let each patty firm up on the first side before turning. Cook the other side until golden and ensure the egg-containing centre is fully cooked.
5. Serve warm. No separate flour-and-water batter is needed.
