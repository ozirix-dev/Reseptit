# North African maakouda potato fritters

## Description

A home-style potato and egg fritter, divided into two servings without added flour.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Peel the listed 1 potato and boil it in water until tender. Drain thoroughly and mash; do not add the cooking water to the mixture.
2. Chop the listed 2 tablespoons parsley. Mix both eggs, parsley, 1 teaspoon cumin and only the listed optional salt into the mashed potato.
3. Heat the listed 1 tablespoon neutral frying oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat. Spoon the soft potato mixture into small flat fritters.
4. Let the fritters set on one side before turning. Fry the other side until golden and ensure the egg-containing centres are fully cooked.
5. Serve hot in two portions. This measured recipe includes no extra flour or water.
