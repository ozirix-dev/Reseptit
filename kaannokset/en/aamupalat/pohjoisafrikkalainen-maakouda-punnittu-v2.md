# Maakouda – Moroccan potato patties

## Description

Maakouda are Moroccan potato patties. In this home adaptation, the parsley- and cumin-seasoned patties are shallow-fried.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Dice the peeled potatoes and boil until tender, about 15–20 minutes. Drain and let excess moisture evaporate in the warm pot. Mash without adding liquid and let cool until just warm.
2. Beat the egg and weigh out 45 g. Mix it into the mash with chopped parsley, cumin and salt. Add 10 g flour. Divide into six patties about 1.5 cm thick and refrigerate for 10 minutes.
3. Dust both sides with the remaining 8 g flour. Heat a frying pan over medium heat. Fry in two batches, using 9 g rapeseed oil per batch.
4. Fry for 4–6 minutes per side until golden and the centre reaches at least 71 °C. Lower the heat if the outside browns before the centre cooks. Serve three patties per portion.

## Notes

**Allergens are egg and wheat.** The estimate counts all frying oil. Any oil left in the pan reduces the amount of fat in each portion.
