# Baked bell pepper omelets – weighed version

## Description

Baked bell pepper omelets – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above is used by the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Halve the peppers, remove the seeds and weigh 320 g of edible red pepper. Mix 220 g of shelled egg, 40 g of grated cheese, 3 g of chives and 0.5 g of black pepper.
2. Fill the peppers with all the egg mixture. Bake at 200 °C until the egg is cooked through and the peppers are tender.
3. Divide all the peppers and egg mixture into two servings. Do not add unlisted oil.
