# Nasi goreng – Indonesian fried rice with egg

## Description

Cold rice is fried with shallot and garlic and seasoned with homemade sweet soy sauce. Each serving is topped with a fried egg.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Combine the soy sauce, brown sugar and water in a small saucepan. Heat gently, stirring, until the sugar dissolves and the sauce starts to bubble. Set aside.
2. Finely chop the shallot and garlic. Heat about a third of the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Fry the eggs until the whites and yolks are firm. Transfer the eggs to a plate.
3. Add the remaining oil to the pan. Fry the shallot over medium heat for 2–3 minutes, adding the garlic for the last 30 seconds.
4. Increase the heat, add the cold rice and break up clumps with a spatula. Add the sweet soy sauce and stir-fry until the rice is steaming hot throughout.
5. Divide the rice between two plates, top each serving with a fried egg and serve immediately.

## Notes

The preparation time starts with cooked rice. If cooking rice for this dish, cool it quickly in a shallow container and refrigerate it within one hour. Use refrigerated rice within 24 hours of cooking and reheat it only once. This recipe contains egg and soy. Check whether your soy sauce contains wheat.
