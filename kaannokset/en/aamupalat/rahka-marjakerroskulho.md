# Layered berry quark cups

## Description

Smooth quark and yogurt are layered with strawberries and cultivated blueberries. Hazelnuts add crunch.

## Ingredients

Weigh the berries with their stems removed. Grate only the yellow surface of a washed lemon.

## Preparation

1. Mix the chilled quark, yogurt and lemon zest until smooth.
2. Rinse and drain the berries and slice the strawberries. Chop the hazelnuts.
3. Layer the quark mixture and berries in four glasses, dividing them evenly. Divide the chopped nuts over the tops just before serving.

## Notes

Contains milk and hazelnuts. Keep chilled. The nutrition estimate excludes up to 0.5 g lemon zest per serving. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
