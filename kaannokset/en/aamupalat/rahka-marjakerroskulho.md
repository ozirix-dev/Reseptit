# Layered quark and berry breakfast bowls

## Description

Four no-cook portions of quark, yogurt and layered fresh berries, finished with nuts.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Mix all 250 g quark, 100 g plain Turkish-style yogurt and 1 teaspoon lemon zest until smooth. Use only the listed optional sweetener if desired.
2. Slice all 100 g strawberries and chop all 20 g hazelnuts. Measure 50 g blueberries.
3. Layer the quark mixture, strawberries and blueberries evenly into four serving glasses.
4. Sprinkle all the chopped hazelnuts over the four portions just before serving (5 g per serving). Serve chilled.

## Notes

Switching berries changes the measured formulation and requires a separate nutrition calculation.
