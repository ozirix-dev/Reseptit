# Korean vegetable egg roll (gyeran-mari)

## Description

Korean vegetable egg roll (gyeran-mari).

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above is used by the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Peel and grate the carrot very finely. Finely chop the spring onion. Gently squeeze excess moisture from the carrot so the egg roll holds together.
2. Whisk the eggs with the carrot and spring onion, then season with the listed salt.
3. Warm the listed frying oil and sesame oil in a nonstick pan over low heat.
4. Pour in a thin layer of egg mixture. When almost set, roll it towards one edge; add another thin layer and roll it around the first. Continue until all the egg mixture is used.
5. Cook the roll completely through over low heat, let it cool briefly and cut into slices. No separate batter or filling is needed.
