# Thai coconut red curry with eggs and rice

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Rinse all 100 g dry long-grain white rice and cook it in the water specified on its package. Separately hard-boil both eggs in their shells, then peel them.
2. Heat the listed 1 tablespoon neutral frying oil. Briefly fry 20 g Mae Ploy red curry paste over medium heat and stir in all 200 ml coconut milk.
3. Chop the 1 tomato and add it to the sauce. Simmer until softened; put the peeled boiled eggs into the curry to warm through. Use only the listed salt if needed.
4. Divide the cooked rice and coconut curry between two portions, with one egg in each. Rice-cooking water is only for boiling the rice; no extra beans, meat or sauces are included.
