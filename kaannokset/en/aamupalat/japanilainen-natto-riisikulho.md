# Natto rice bowl with egg

## Description

Plain natto, warm jasmine rice and a boiled egg make a Japanese-style breakfast bowl. Natto is made from fermented soybeans.

## Preparation

1. Rinse 100 g of dry white jasmine rice and cook it according to the packet instructions.
2. Boil the eggs until both whites and yolks are firm throughout. Cool briefly in cold water, peel and halve them.
3. Finely chop the spring onion. Stir it into the plain whole-bean natto and add salt if you like.
4. Divide the hot rice between two bowls. Top each with natto and one halved egg. Serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains soy and egg. Use plain natto and set aside any sauce and seasoning sachets in the pack.
