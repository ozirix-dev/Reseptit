# Butternut squash oatmeal with brown sugar

## Description

A precisely weighed butternut squash oatmeal made with brown sugar, for two servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredients are shown above in the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cook 250 g peeled butternut squash, cut into pieces, in 500 g water until tender. Do not purée the squash.
2. Add 100 g rolled oats (0.5–0.6 mm thick) and cook for 10 minutes. Stir in 30 g brown sugar and 2.6 g ground cinnamon.
3. Divide all the hot porridge into two servings. All the cinnamon and cooking water remain in the dish.
