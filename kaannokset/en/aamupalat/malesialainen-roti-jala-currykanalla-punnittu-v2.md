# Malaysian roti jala with coconut chicken curry

## Description

Soft lace crepes are served with a mild coconut chicken curry.

## Ingredients

Use a roti jala mould or narrow-nozzle squeeze bottle and a nonstick pan. Weigh the eggs without shells and stir the coconut milk before weighing.

## Preparation

1. Whisk the eggs, batter coconut milk, water, oil and turmeric. Whisk in the flours and half the salt. Strain the batter and rest it for 30 minutes.
2. Thinly slice the onion. Simmer it in the curry coconut milk and curry powder for about 5 minutes. Add diced chicken and the remaining salt. Simmer gently, covered, until the chicken reaches at least 75°C throughout. Keep the curry warm. Wash your hands and anything that touched the raw chicken.
3. Heat the nonstick pan over medium-low heat. Stir the batter, squeeze thin crossing lines onto the pan, and cook until set with no wet batter visible. Move the cooked crepe to a clean surface and fold or roll it. Repeat. Do not add oil to the pan.
4. Divide all the crepes and the entire curry equally between two servings.

## Notes

Contains wheat and egg. Check the curry powder package for allergens. The nutrition estimate excludes up to 1 g of spices per serving. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
