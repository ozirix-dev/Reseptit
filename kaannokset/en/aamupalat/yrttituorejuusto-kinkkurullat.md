# Herbed cream-cheese ham rolls

## Description

Four portions of cold ham rolls with herbed cream cheese, pepper and fresh vegetables.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above supplies the weighed quantities for serving calculations.

## Instructions

1. Mix 100 g unflavoured 20%-fat cream cheese with 7 g chopped chives and 0.5 g ground black pepper.
2. Spread this mixture over all 150 g of the 3%-fat cooked ham slices and roll up. Cut the 100 g red bell pepper and 100 g cucumber into sticks.
3. Serve all ham rolls, vegetables and 40 g lettuce leaves divided into four portions. Do not add an unlisted sauce.
