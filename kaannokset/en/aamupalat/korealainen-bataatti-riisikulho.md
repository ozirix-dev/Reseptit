# Sweet potato rice bowl with gochujang

## Description

Roasted sweet potato, white rice and a fried egg are served with a sauce made from Korean gochujang chilli paste.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C. Cut the sweet potato into roughly 2 cm cubes. Toss with 7 g of oil and spread in a single layer on a baking tray. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until the cubes are fork-tender.
2. Rinse the rice and cook according to the packet instructions while the sweet potato roasts.
3. Mix the gochujang with 15 g of water. Heat the remaining 3 g of oil in a non-stick frying pan. Cook the eggs over low heat with the lid on until the whites and yolks are completely firm.
4. Divide the rice and sweet potato between two bowls. Add one egg to each bowl, spoon over the sauce and serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains egg. Gochujang may contain wheat and soy. Check the packet for allergens.
