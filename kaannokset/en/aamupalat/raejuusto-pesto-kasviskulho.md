# Cottage cheese, pesto and vegetable bowl

## Description

Cottage cheese, pesto and crunchy vegetables make a filling cold breakfast bowl.

## Preparation

1. Halve the tomatoes and dice the cucumber.
2. Lightly stir the pesto into the cottage cheese so the mixture stays streaky.
3. Add the rocket and vegetables to the bowl.
4. Finish with pine nuts, olive oil and black pepper.

## Notes

The saltiness and carbohydrate content of pesto vary by brand.
