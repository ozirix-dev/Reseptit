# Korean seaweed rice balls

## Description

Jumeokbap are hand-shaped Korean rice balls. This version seasons the rice with toasted gim, sesame oil and carrot.

## Ingredients

Measure the rice dry and weigh the carrot after peeling. Use plain toasted gim (nori) with a known iodine content and follow the serving limit on the package.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the dry white long-grain rice and cook it according to the package directions. Let it rest with the lid on, then transfer it to a bowl while still warm.
2. Finely grate the carrot. Sauté it in part of the sesame oil for about 3 minutes until soft.
3. Crumble the gim into small flakes. Fold the carrot, gim and remaining sesame oil into the warm rice. Add a pinch of salt if desired.
4. Divide into six portions. Once the rice is cool enough to handle, press each warm portion firmly into a ball with damp hands. Serve immediately, three balls per serving.

## Notes

Contains sesame. Cool any leftovers immediately, refrigerate and use within 24 hours. If reheating the rice, do so only once and heat until steaming hot throughout.
