# Lablabi – chickpea soup with pita bread and egg

## Description

This weighed variation of Tunisian lablabi makes two servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredients are listed above in the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Bring 700 g of prepared, diluted chicken stock to a boil and add 100 g cooked, drained chickpeas and 2 g ground cumin.
2. Steam 110 g of egg, weighed without its shell, in a small container without adding fat. Cook the egg thoroughly and use the full amount.
3. Tear 100 g of ready-made white pita bread into serving bowls. Divide all the hot soup and cooked egg equally between two portions. Do not add any other oil or harissa.
