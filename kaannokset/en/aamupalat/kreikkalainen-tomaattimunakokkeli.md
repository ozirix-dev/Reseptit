# Greek tomato and feta scrambled eggs

## Description

In Greek strapatsada, eggs are scrambled in a thick tomato sauce. Salty feta is crumbled through the eggs at the end.

## Preparation

1. Chop the onion. Heat the oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 4–5 minutes until soft. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1–2 minutes.
2. Add the chopped tomatoes with their juice and the black pepper. Simmer uncovered for about 8–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
3. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them lightly with a fork. Reduce the heat to medium-low, pour the eggs into the pan and stir along the bottom with a spatula until the eggs are set throughout and no runny egg remains.
4. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the crumbled feta. Taste, add salt if needed and serve immediately.
