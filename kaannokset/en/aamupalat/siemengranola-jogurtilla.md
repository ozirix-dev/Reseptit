# Toasted seed granola with yogurt

## Description

A crisp oven-toasted seed mix makes a grain-free, nutty topping for breakfast yogurt.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 160 °C. Melt the butter and stir in the cinnamon.
2. Combine the seeds and flaked almonds in a bowl. Pour in the cinnamon butter and mix.
3. Spread over baking parchment and toast for 18–22 minutes. Stir once and watch closely towards the end.
4. Cool completely so the seeds crisp up. Serve with yogurt and raspberries.

## Notes

Store the completely cooled granola in an airtight jar. Serve the berries and yogurt separately.
