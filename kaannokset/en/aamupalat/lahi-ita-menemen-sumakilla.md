# Menemen with sumac

## Description

Softly set eggs with tomato, green sweet pepper and sumac.

## Preparation

1. Dice the tomato and green sweet pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the pepper until soft. Add tomato and cook until it begins to break down.
3. Add the eggs to the pan and stir into the tomatoes. Cook gently until just set.
4. Season with sumac and salt and serve immediately.
