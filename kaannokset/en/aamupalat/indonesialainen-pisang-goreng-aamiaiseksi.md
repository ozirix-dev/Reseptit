# Indonesian pisang goreng banana fritters

## Description

A home-kitchen version based on the measured ingredient quantities.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Mix the rice flour, dried coconut, brown sugar and a pinch of listed salt. Gradually add the listed water and stir into a smooth batter that will coat the banana.
2. Peel and slice the banana lengthwise or diagonally into evenly sized pieces. Coat the pieces in the batter.
3. Heat the listed frying oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat. Fry the coated banana pieces on both sides until golden. Work in batches if necessary, using only the total oil quantity in the list.
4. Check that the batter is fully cooked at the thickest points. Drain briefly on kitchen paper and serve warm.
