# Vietnamese bánh bao with mushrooms and egg

## Description

In these Vietnamese steamed buns, hard-boiled egg and soy-seasoned button mushrooms are wrapped in a soft yeast dough. The recipe makes four small buns.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the measured lukewarm water and the dough oil. Knead for about 8 minutes. Cover and leave to rise for 60–75 minutes, until almost doubled.
2. Boil the egg until the white and yolk are firm. Cool, peel and divide the measured 50 g into four pieces. Finely chop the mushrooms and fry them in the filling oil for 8–10 minutes, until cooked through and the liquid has evaporated. Stir in the soy sauce and leave the filling to cool.
3. Divide the dough into four pieces. Roll into rounds about 10 cm across, keeping the centres slightly thicker than the edges. Divide the mushrooms and egg between the rounds. Bring the edges up over the filling, pleat and pinch to seal. Set each bun on a piece of baking paper, cover and let rise for 15–20 minutes.
4. Bring the steamer water to a boil. Space the buns on the rack above the water. Cover and steam for 15–18 minutes, until the dough is cooked through at the seam and the filling is steaming hot throughout. Turn off the heat, wait 3 minutes and lift the lid carefully. Serve two buns per person.

## Notes

Contains wheat, soy and egg. Check the soy sauce label. The steamer water is separate from the dough and the nutrition calculation.
