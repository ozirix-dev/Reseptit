# Malaysian kaya toast

## Description

Toasted sourdough with homemade coconut and egg spread, butter and a fried egg to share.

## Preparation

1. Put 100 ml coconut milk and 20 g sugar in a heatproof bowl. Whisk in one of the two listed eggs. Place the bowl over a pan of simmering water without letting its base touch the water.
2. Stir the kaya constantly over the water bath until smooth, thick and cooked through. Check with a food thermometer that it reaches 71 °C throughout. Stir gently to keep the mixture from curdling.
3. Toast both sides of the full 100 g ready-made sourdough. Spread all 10 g butter over the toast and divide the kaya evenly over it.
4. Fry the second egg in the listed 1 tbsp neutral oil until both the white and yolk are firm. Divide the toast and warm egg between two servings. Use the listed salt if needed.

## Notes

- One egg goes into the kaya and the other is shared alongside. This version has no pandan. The 25-minute total is an estimate from the source and has not been verified in a test kitchen.
- [FDA guidance on cooking eggs safely](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-egg-safety).
