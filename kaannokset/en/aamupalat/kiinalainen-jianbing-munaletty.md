# Chinese jianbing-style egg pancake

## Description

A thin wheat pancake with egg, spring onion, sesame and soybean paste.

## Preparation

1. Gradually whisk about 180 ml cold water into 100 g wheat flour and a pinch of the listed salt until the batter is smooth and thin enough to pour. Cover and rest for about 10 minutes. The water adds no energy.
2. Finely chop the listed 2 tbsp spring onion and measure 8 g sesame seeds. Heat a wide non-stick pan over medium heat. Use the listed 1 tbsp neutral oil in total for both pancakes. Pour in half the batter and quickly spread it into a thin circle about 20–25 cm across.
3. Crack one egg over the pancake while the surface is still wet and spread it evenly. Sprinkle with half the spring onion and sesame. When the edges start to dry and the underside releases, flip with a wide spatula. Cook until the egg is fully set and the batter has no raw centre.
4. Spread half of the total 1 tbsp soybean paste over the cooked pancake and fold it. Repeat with the remaining batter, egg, spring onion, sesame, soybean paste and oil. Serve the two pancakes without extra sauces or crispy fillings.
