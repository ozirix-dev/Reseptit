# Shakshuka with homemade fresh harissa – weighed version

## Description

A carefully weighed shakshuka with homemade harissa for two servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredients are listed in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Weigh all listed edible ingredients in grams. Do not add any unlisted fat or side dishes.
2. Make exactly 5 g of fresh harissa from 2.5 g chilli, 0.8 g garlic, 1 g olive oil, 0.3 g ground cumin, 0.2 g ground coriander and 0.2 g lemon juice. Use all of the paste.
3. Soften the red pepper and tomato in 13.5 g olive oil. Add the fresh harissa and simmer until tender.
4. Add 110 g egg weighed without its shell, cover and cook thoroughly. Divide all vegetables, sauce and eggs between two servings.
5. Cook all ingredients containing raw animal products thoroughly. Consume all ingredients and pan juices. The raw ingredients' nutrient composition is used as a proxy for the finished dish; unmeasured cooking losses are not deducted.
