# Mexican breakfast tacos with black beans

## Description

Warm corn tortillas with fried eggs, avocado and cooked black beans.

## Preparation

1. Use 150 g black beans already cooked until edible. If starting with dried beans, soak and cook them separately before starting this recipe. Allow 25 minutes for the recipe itself. Heat the cooked beans in a pan or small saucepan.
2. Halve the avocado, remove the stone and slice the flesh. Warm 100 g ready-made corn tortillas in a dry pan until pliable.
3. Heat a total of 1 tbsp neutral oil in a separate pan. Fry two eggs until the whites are firmly set. Continue cooking until the yolks are firm. Use no other cooking oil.
4. Divide all the warm tortillas, hot beans, eggs and avocado between two servings. Use the listed salt if needed. Do not add lime or sauces outside the ingredient list.
