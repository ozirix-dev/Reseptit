# North African harcha semolina flatbreads with cream cheese

## Description

North African harcha semolina flatbreads with cream cheese.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Rub the listed butter into the semolina until the mixture is crumbly throughout. Add the listed milk and a pinch of salt.
2. Mix into a dough and allow the semolina to swell for about 10 minutes. It may remain crumbly but should hold together when pressed.
3. Divide the dough into two thick, flat discs. Shape gently by hand or between sheets of baking paper; do not roll into thin flatbreads.
4. Warm the listed frying oil in a pan over low-to-medium heat. Cook the discs slowly on both sides until golden outside and cooked through in the centre.
5. Serve warm with the listed plain cream cheese. The cheese is an accompaniment, not a filling inside the dough.
