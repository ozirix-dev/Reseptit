# Chinese shrimp wonton soup

## Description

Shrimp and ginger wontons with bok choy in vegetable or chicken stock.

## Preparation

1. Thaw frozen shrimp in the refrigerator and drain. Peel and devein if needed. Finely chop all 150 g shrimp and mix with 1 tsp ginger and a pinch of the listed salt. Wash the bok choy and cut into strips.
2. Divide all 150 g wonton dough into small wrappers according to the manufacturer's instructions. Divide the filling evenly among them. Moisten the edges with a little water and press them together firmly to seal. Use all the filling.
3. Bring all 700 ml stock to a boil. Reduce the heat slightly and gently add the wontons, in batches if needed. Simmer until the wrappers are cooked and the shrimp in the centre are pearly white, firm and opaque throughout. Cut open one of the thickest wontons to check. Keep cooking if the shrimp are raw or translucent.
4. Add the bok choy strips and heat thoroughly. Taste the broth and add only the listed salt if needed. Divide the cooked wontons, bok choy and broth between two bowls. Use no oil, spring onions or other unlisted fillings.

## Notes

- The small amount of water used to seal the wrappers is a handling aid. Use wonton dough intended for food preparation and cook the shrimp through. FDA Safe Food Handling identifies pearly-white, opaque flesh as a sign of cooked shrimp.
- The serving image is illustrative. Follow the ingredient list and preparation above.
