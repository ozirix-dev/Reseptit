# Baked avocado and eggs

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Halve the avocados and remove the stones; gently scoop out enough space for the egg while retaining all 200 g weighed edible avocado flesh.
2. Set the avocado halves securely in a small ovenproof dish so they cannot tip over.
3. Use all 200 g shelled eggs: divide among the avocado cavities. If some egg will not fit, pour the remainder into a small ovenproof ramekin and cook it alongside; discard none of the measured egg.
4. Season with the listed 0.5 g ground black pepper and salt if desired. Bake about 12–16 minutes until the whites are fully set, including the extra ramekin if used.
5. Drizzle all 5 g lemon juice over the servings and finish with all 7 g finely chopped chives. Divide the cooked eggs and avocado between two portions.
