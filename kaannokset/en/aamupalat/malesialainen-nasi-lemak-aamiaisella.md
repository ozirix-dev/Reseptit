# Simplified Malaysian nasi lemak breakfast

## Description

Simplified Malaysian nasi lemak breakfast.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Measure 100 g of dry white basmati or jasmine rice. Cook with 184 g of coconut milk and 100 g of water for the time stated on the rice package; serve hot.
2. Steam 110 g of shelled egg in a small dish without any additional fat and use all of it.
3. Divide all the coconut rice, egg and 100 g cucumber between two servings. This simplified version does not include sambal, anchovies, nuts or other accompaniments.
