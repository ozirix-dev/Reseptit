# Greek yogurt with figs, honey and walnuts

## Description

Two small cold yogurt bowls with fresh fig, walnut and measured honey.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Divide all 100 g plain 7%-fat Greek yogurt between two small cold serving bowls (50 g each).
2. Weigh 50 g edible fresh fig, cut it into pieces and divide evenly between the bowls.
3. Chop all 30 g walnuts and divide between the bowls (15 g per portion).
4. Drizzle the one listed tablespoon of honey over the two bowls in total. Serve chilled without warm fillings or additional sauces.
