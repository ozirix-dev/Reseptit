# Spinach chilla with Finnish farmer cheese

## Description

Thin Indian-style chickpea pancakes are filled with crumbled farmer cheese. Makes about four pancakes for two people.

## Ingredients

Weigh the rinsed, dried spinach after removing any tough stems.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour, cumin and salt. Gradually whisk in the water. Whisk all the oil into the batter and let it rest for 10 minutes.
2. Finely chop the spinach and stir it into the batter. Crumble the farmer cheese into a separate bowl.
3. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat. Stir the batter before cooking each pancake. Spread a quarter of the batter thinly in the pan and cook without extra oil for 2–3 minutes, until the top sets and the edge lifts.
4. Flip and cook for about 2 minutes more, until cooked through and there is no wet raw batter in the centre. Repeat with the remaining batter.
5. Divide the farmer cheese among the hot pancakes, fold and serve.

## Notes

Contains milk and chickpea. Do not taste raw batter. Nutrition values are estimates. Glycemic load is undetermined. The image is an illustration.
