# Spinach and paneer chickpea pancakes

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Whisk 100 g chickpea flour with 1 teaspoon cumin, the listed salt if desired, and all 140 ml water into a smooth batter. Let rest for 10 minutes.
2. Rinse and dry 50 g spinach, chop it and stir it into the batter. Crumble all 100 g paneer to use as a filling.
3. Heat the listed 1 tablespoon oil over medium heat. Spread the batter into thin pancakes and cook on both sides until the chickpea flour is fully cooked.
4. Divide all the paneer over the pancakes, fold them and warm through in the pan.
5. Divide the pancakes into two servings and serve warm. The weighed version includes no cilantro chutney or lemon wedges.
