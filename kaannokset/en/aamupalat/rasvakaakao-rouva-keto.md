# Keto hot cocoa with dark chocolate

An indulgent hot cocoa made with unsweetened cocoa, butter, coconut oil, cream and a little 90% dark chocolate. Makes one generous mug.

## Preparation

1. Heat the water until steaming, but not boiling.
2. Finely chop the dark chocolate. Stir the cocoa powder into a splash of hot water to form a smooth paste.
3. Add the chocolate, butter, coconut oil and cream. Stir until the chocolate melts and the mixture is smooth.
4. Add the remaining water and carefully use an immersion blender in a tall, open container. Never blend a hot drink in a sealed blender. Serve warm.

## Notes

This is a rich, high-fat drink, not a low-calorie cocoa.
