# Rich hot cocoa with dark chocolate

## Description

Dark chocolate and a little whipping cream make this cocoa rich and smooth. One serving fills a large mug.

## Preparation

1. Finely chop the chocolate. In a saucepan, mix the cocoa powder with a little of the measured water to make a smooth paste.
2. Whisk in the rest of the water and the cream. Heat gently, stirring, until the drink is steaming.
3. Take the saucepan off the heat. Add the chocolate, butter and coconut oil. Whisk until the chocolate and fats have melted and the drink is smooth.
4. Pour into a large mug and serve immediately. Stir with a spoon if fat rises to the surface.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Check the chocolate and other packaging for possible traces of allergens.
- The chocolate contains sugar. Using a different chocolate changes the flavour and nutritional content.
