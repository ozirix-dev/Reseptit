# Caribbean johnny cakes – pan-fried breakfast breads

## Description

Small Caribbean-style pan-fried breakfast breads made with wheat flour, milk and butter.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above supplies the amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Mix the wheat flour, baking powder and a pinch of the listed salt in a bowl. Rub in the listed butter.
2. Add the listed milk gradually and mix into a soft dough that can be shaped by hand. Do not knead for long; if sticky, let it rest briefly before shaping.
3. Divide into equal pieces and shape into flat discs about a finger thick. Do not add any filling in this version.
4. Heat the listed frying oil in a pan over medium heat. Pan-fry the discs on both sides until golden and cooked through in the middle. Serve warm.

## Notes

Use only the listed butter, milk and frying oil. No separate filling is included in this measured version.
