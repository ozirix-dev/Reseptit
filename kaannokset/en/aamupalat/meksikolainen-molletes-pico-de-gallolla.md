# Molletes with black beans and pico de gallo

## Description

These Mexican open-faced rolls are toasted, spread with mashed black beans and topped with melted cheese. Spoon fresh tomato and chilli salsa over the hot rolls.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 225 °C. Split the rolls lengthwise and toast the halves cut-side up on baking paper for 4 minutes.
2. Dice the tomato. Finely chop the onion, chilli and coriander. Mix them with the lime juice and half the salt.
3. Heat the oil in a small frying pan over medium heat. Add the drained beans, water and remaining salt. Heat for 3 minutes, then mash the beans to a coarse paste.
4. Spread the beans on the roll halves. Grate the cheese over them and bake for 5–7 minutes until it melts and starts to colour.
5. Spoon the pico de gallo over the hot rolls and divide the halves between two servings.

## Notes

Use two small bolillo rolls and rinse and drain the canned black beans before weighing. Contains wheat and milk. The avocado in the photo is a serving suggestion.
