# Callaloo-style omelette — spinach version

## Description

Callaloo-style omelette made with spinach. A precisely weighed recipe for two servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are shown in the structured recipe data used by the portion calculator.

## Instructions

1. Soften 50 g yellow onion and 12 g spring onions in 13.5 g oil. Add 150 g fresh spinach and 6 g fresh thyme.
2. Add 110 g egg without shells and cook covered until set. Season with salt.
3. Divide the entire omelette, including pan juices, into two servings. This is the spinach version, not a calculation for another plant sold as callaloo.
