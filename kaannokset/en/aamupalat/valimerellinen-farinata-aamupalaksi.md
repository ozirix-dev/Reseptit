# Chickpea farinata with tomatoes and olives

## Description

This home-kitchen adaptation makes a chickpea pancake topped with tomatoes and olives. Adjust the seasoning and accompaniments to your taste.

## Ingredients

The ingredients are available in the structured recipe data used by the portion calculator.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C with fan). Grease a shallow, ovenproof dish about 20–22 cm across with the recipe's cooking oil.
2. Whisk the chickpea flour with 150 ml water until smooth. Leave the batter to rest for 20 minutes.
3. Finely chop the rosemary and mix it into the batter. Slice the tomato and olives. Season the batter with salt to taste.
4. Pour the batter into the dish and arrange the tomato and olive slices on top. Bake on the middle shelf for about 20–25 minutes, until the top is lightly browned and the centre has set.
5. Let rest for about 5 minutes before cutting the farinata into two portions.
