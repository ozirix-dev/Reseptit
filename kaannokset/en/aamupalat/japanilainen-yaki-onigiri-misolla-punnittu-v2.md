# Pan-grilled rice balls with miso (yaki onigiri)

## Description

Triangles of warm Japanese rice are pan-grilled until crisp and brushed with light miso. This recipe makes four small rice balls, or two servings.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice with cold water until the water runs almost clear. Drain in a sieve. Put the rice and the cooking water in a small pot with a lid and soak for 20 minutes.
2. Bring to a boil with the lid on, then reduce the heat and simmer gently for about 12 minutes, until the water is absorbed. Take the pot off the heat and let the rice steam, covered, for 10 minutes. Fluff gently.
3. Divide the warm rice into four equal portions. Lightly wet your hands and press each portion into a firm triangle about 2 cm thick.
4. Heat the sesame oil in a nonstick frying pan over medium heat. Fry the triangles for about 3–4 minutes on each broad side until golden and crisp. Turn each triangle once it releases easily from the pan.
5. Mix the miso paste with the water measured for thinning. Brush a thin layer onto the tops of the triangles. Turn them miso-side down for 20–30 seconds, then lift them out immediately so the miso does not burn. Serve immediately.

## Notes

One serving is two rice balls. This recipe contains soy and sesame. Check the miso label for allergens. The nutrition estimate uses reference data for white rice and miso paste, so values vary by product. If you have leftovers, cool them quickly in a shallow container, refrigerate within one hour and use within 24 hours.
