# Miso soup with tofu and bok choy

## Description

Soft tofu and bok choy simmer in a mild vegetable stock. Light miso is stirred in at the end. Makes two bowls.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the bok choy leaves and stalks. Slice the pale stalks and cut the leaves into bite-size pieces. Drain the tofu and cut it into cubes about 1.5 cm across.
2. Bring the vegetable stock to a boil. Add the bok choy stalks and simmer for about 3 minutes.
3. Add the leaves and tofu. Simmer gently for about 2 minutes, until the leaves are tender and the tofu is hot. Take the pot off the heat.
4. Ladle some hot stock into a bowl and stir in the miso until smooth. Pour it back into the pot and stir gently. Divide between two bowls and serve hot.

## Notes

This miso soup uses vegetable stock. Choose a mild stock because miso adds salt. This recipe contains soy. Check the miso and vegetable stock labels for other allergens. The nutrition estimate uses average food composition data.
