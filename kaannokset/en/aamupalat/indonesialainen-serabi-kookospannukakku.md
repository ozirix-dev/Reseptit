# Indonesian serabi coconut pancakes

## Description

Thick rice-flour pancakes with coconut milk, dried coconut and soft brown sugar.

## Preparation

1. Combine 100 g rice flour, 20 g coconut flakes and a pinch of the listed salt. Gradually stir in 150 ml coconut milk to make a smooth, fairly thick batter.
2. Rest the batter for about 10 minutes so the rice flour can hydrate. Use no other flour or liquid.
3. Heat the listed 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Spoon in small, fairly thick pancakes and cook over low to medium heat until the undersides colour and the tops start to set. Turn carefully and cook the other sides until the pancakes are cooked through.
4. Scatter all 30 g soft brown sugar over the cooked pancakes and let it melt slightly in the pan's residual heat. Divide between two servings.
