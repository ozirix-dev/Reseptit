# Vietnamese steamed rice rolls with pork and mushrooms

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Whisk all 100 g rice flour and 250 ml water into a thin batter. Finely chop 100 g fresh button mushrooms; measure 150 g ground pork and 1 tablespoon fish sauce.
2. Cook the pork and mushrooms in a dry nonstick pan or small saucepan until the ground pork is completely cooked through. Season with all 1 tablespoon fish sauce and only the listed salt if needed.
3. Pour very thin layers of rice batter onto a heatproof nonstick steaming surface. Steam in batches under a lid until each sheet is set with no raw batter remaining.
4. Carefully lift the steamed rice sheets, divide all the hot pork-and-mushroom filling between them and roll up. Divide between two portions; no extra oil, herbs or sauce is included.
