# Vietnamese bánh cuốn with pork and mushrooms

## Description

Thin rice sheets cooked in a lidded pan are filled with ground pork and button mushrooms.

## Ingredients

Weigh the fish sauce in grams. Use ordinary white rice flour rather than glutinous rice flour. Set out a clean silicone mat for rolling.

## Preparation

1. Whisk the rice flour, tapioca starch, water and salt into a smooth batter. Stir half the oil into the batter and rest for 30 minutes.
2. Finely chop the mushrooms. Cook the pork and mushrooms in the remaining oil, breaking up the pork and cooking it to at least 70°C throughout. Stir in the fish sauce and keep the filling warm.
3. Heat a small nonstick pan with a lid over medium heat. Stir the batter and add a small ladleful. Tilt the pan immediately to make a thin layer. Cover and cook for 30–60 seconds or until translucent, set and fully cooked. Do not flip.
4. Turn the sheet onto the clean silicone mat. Place cooked filling along one edge and roll up. Repeat, stirring the batter before each sheet. Divide all the rolls evenly between two servings and serve at once.

## Notes

Contains fish (anchovy). Keep raw meat separate from cooked rolls. The nutrition estimate excludes up to 0.5 g fish sauce per serving. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
