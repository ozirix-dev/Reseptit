# Skillet manakish with thyme and sesame

## Description

Quick yogurt flatbreads with a thyme and sesame topping.

## Preparation

1. Mix 150 g wheat flour, 100 g yogurt and 40 ml water with a pinch of the listed salt. Knead just until smooth and easy to shape. Cover and rest for about 5 minutes.
2. Mix 1 tbsp olive oil, 2 g dried thyme and 3 g sesame seeds in a cup. This is the za’atar-style topping.
3. Divide the dough in two and press it into thin, even rounds. Spread all the topping over them. Heat a heavy pan over medium heat and use only 1 tbsp neutral oil in total for frying.
4. Cook the flatbreads one at a time, covering the pan if needed. Cook until both sides have coloured and no raw, floury dough remains inside. Serve warm as two portions.

## Notes

This yogurt dough uses no yeast. The pictured accompaniments are serving suggestions.
