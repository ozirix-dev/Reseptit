# Middle Eastern rosewater yogurt with pistachios

## Description

Cold rosewater yogurt topped with measured pistachios and pomegranate.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Measure 200 g plain unsweetened yogurt (3.9% fat), 20 g raw unsalted edible pistachio kernels, 5 ml unsweetened food-grade rose water (e.g. Al Wadi Rose Water, never rose syrup), and 60 g edible pomegranate seeds.
2. Stir all 5 ml rosewater into the yogurt. Do not heat it or add any unlisted sauces.
3. Divide the rosewater yogurt between two bowls and divide all 60 g pomegranate seeds over them.
4. Chop all 20 g pistachios and sprinkle over the bowls (10 g per serving). Add only the listed salt if desired and serve cold.
