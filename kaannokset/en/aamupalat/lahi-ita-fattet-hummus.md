# Fattet hummus with yogurt and chickpeas

## Description

Toasted pita, warm chickpeas and cool tahini yogurt in layers.

## Preparation

1. Tear 100 g pita into bite-size pieces and toast them in a dry pan until crisp.
2. Heat 100 g cooked, drained chickpeas in a small saucepan until hot throughout.
3. Stir 1 tbsp tahini into 100 g plain yogurt and add salt to taste.
4. Divide the toasted pita between two bowls, add the hot chickpeas and spoon over the tahini yogurt just before serving.
