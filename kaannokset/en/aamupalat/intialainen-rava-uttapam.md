# Rava uttapam with tomato and onion

## Description

These small South Indian semolina pancakes are topped with finely chopped tomato and onion. The semolina rests in a yogurt-and-water batter while you chop the vegetables.

## Ingredients

Makes four small pancakes, two servings. Use fine wheat semolina and plain yogurt with about 4% fat.

## Preparation

1. Mix 100 g semolina with 100 g yogurt. Stir in 70 ml of the listed water and a pinch of salt if desired. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes.
2. Finely chop the tomato and peeled onion. Aim for 125 g of edible tomato and 55 g of peeled onion. Stir the remaining water into the batter a little at a time, until it falls from a spoon in a thick ribbon.
3. Heat a large frying pan over medium heat. Use 1 tbsp oil in total for all four pancakes. Spoon in two pancakes about 10–11 cm wide and gently press half the vegetables into their wet tops.
4. Cook for about 3 minutes, until the edges set and the underside browns. Flip and cook the vegetable side for 2–3 minutes, until the centres are cooked through. Repeat with the remaining batter and vegetables. Serve immediately, two pancakes per serving.

## Notes

Contains wheat and milk. The nutrition estimate uses 125 g of tomato and 55 g of onion and counts all the cooking oil. Illustrative image.
