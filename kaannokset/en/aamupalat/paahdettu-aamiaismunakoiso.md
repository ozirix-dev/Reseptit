# Roasted aubergine with tahini yogurt

## Description

Tender roasted aubergine with cool tahini yogurt makes a savoury breakfast or brunch.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 220 °C. Halve the aubergine lengthwise and score the cut faces in a crosshatch pattern.
2. Brush with oil and season with salt and pepper. Roast cut-side up for 25–30 minutes.
3. Mix the yogurt, tahini and lemon juice. Thin with a splash of water if needed.
4. Serve the aubergine with the tahini yogurt, parsley and sesame seeds.

## Notes

You can roast the aubergine the night before and reheat it in the morning.
