# Turkish menemen with feta

## Description

This Turkish breakfast dish combines scrambled eggs, tomato and green pepper. This version is finished with crumbled feta.

## Ingredients

Use one medium tomato and one medium green bell pepper.

## Preparation

1. Wash and chop the medium tomato and green pepper. Beat the two eggs lightly with a fork.
2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the pepper until soft, add the tomato and cook until the excess liquid evaporates.
3. Lower the heat. Add the eggs and fold them through the vegetables until set throughout, with no liquid egg remaining.
4. Divide between two plates. Crumble the 150 g feta over the servings and add salt if needed.

## Notes

Contains eggs and milk. The nutrition estimate uses a medium tomato and pepper. Values vary with vegetable size and the feta used.
