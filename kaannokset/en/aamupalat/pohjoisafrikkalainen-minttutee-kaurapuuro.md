# Mint oat porridge with milk and honey – leaves eaten

## Description

This version includes fresh mint leaves that remain in the porridge and are eaten; it is not a strained mint infusion.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above supplies the amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g of fine 0.5–0.6 mm rolled oats in 500 g of water for 10 minutes.
2. Add the milk, finely chopped fresh mint leaves and honey. Heat through and season with the listed salt.
3. The mint leaves stay in the porridge and are eaten. Do not strain an infusion in this version. Divide the whole mixture into two servings and serve warm.

## Notes

The modeled glycemic load covers mint leaves eaten with the porridge, not strained mint tea. A strained-infusion alternative has not been assigned a glycemic-load estimate; do not reuse this value for it.
