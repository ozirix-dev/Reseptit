# Breakfast couscous bowl with dates and almonds

## Description

This home-style breakfast bowl combines couscous, dates and nuts. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Measure the couscous into a bowl. Bring 100 ml of water to a boil, pour it over the couscous and cover the bowl for five minutes.
2. Fluff the couscous with a fork. Chop the pitted dates and coarsely chop the almonds.
3. Divide the couscous between two bowls. Add the yogurt, dates and almonds and serve.
