# Aloo paratha with yogurt

## Description

Aloo paratha is an Indian skillet flatbread filled with seasoned mashed potatoes. This version seasons the filling with fresh cilantro and cumin. The recipe makes two flatbreads to serve with plain yogurt.

## Ingredients

Use finely milled whole-wheat flour for the dough. Weigh the potatoes after peeling and before cooking.

## Preparation

1. Dice 150 g of peeled potatoes. Boil in water for about 12–18 minutes, until the pieces break apart easily with a fork. Drain well and mash until smooth.
2. Make the dough while the potatoes cook. Mix 100 g flour, 60 ml warm water and half the salt. Knead for about 5 minutes, until smooth. Cover and let rest for 20 minutes.
3. Finely chop 6 g cilantro. Mix the cilantro, 2 g cumin and the remaining salt into the mashed potatoes. Let the filling cool until just warm, then divide it into two balls.
4. Divide the dough into two equal pieces. Flatten each into a disc and place a ball of filling in the centre. Pull the dough around the filling and pinch the seam closed. Gently roll each filled ball into a roughly 16 cm round between two sheets of baking parchment.
5. Heat a skillet over medium heat. Use 6 g rapeseed oil for each flatbread, half on the first side and half on the second. Cook for about 3–5 minutes per side, until brown spots appear and the dough is cooked through at the seam.
6. Serve one warm flatbread and 30 g plain yogurt per portion.

## Notes
