# Japanese hojicha yogurt with persimmon

## Description

Chilled yogurt with finely milled roasted green tea, persimmon and sesame.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Measure 200 g yogurt, 150 g edible persimmon flesh, 2 g dry hojicha tea and 5 g sesame seeds.
2. Grind all 2 g hojicha tea into an extremely fine edible powder. For this measured version, do not brew and discard the leaves or add extra water.
3. Divide the yogurt between two bowls and stir the powdered hojicha through it. Slice the persimmon and divide all 150 g between the bowls.
4. Sprinkle all 5 g sesame seeds over the two servings (2.5 g each), add only the listed salt if needed and serve cold. No frying is required.

## Notes

The source's dry hojicha is eaten in powdered form; verify the tea's suitability and nutrient matching before publication.
