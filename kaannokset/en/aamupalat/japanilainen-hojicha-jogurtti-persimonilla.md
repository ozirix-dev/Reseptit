# Hojicha yogurt with persimmon

## Description

Hojicha, a powdered Japanese roasted green tea, flavours the yogurt. Persimmon adds sweetness.

## Ingredients

Use food-grade powder made only of roasted green tea. Weigh the persimmon after peeling.

## Preparation

1. Wash, peel and slice the persimmon. Remove the stem and any seeds.
2. Mix the hojicha into a spoonful of yogurt first, then into the rest. Divide between two bowls.
3. Divide the persimmon and sesame evenly between the bowls. Serve cold.

## Notes

Contains milk and sesame. The nutrition estimate excludes up to 1 g tea powder per serving. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
