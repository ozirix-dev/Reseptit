# Oven-Baked Barley Porridge with Milk

Cracked barley grains soften slowly in the oven for this Finnish porridge.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Grease a 2.5-litre dish with some of the butter.
2. Mix the barley, milk and salt in the dish. Dot the surface with the remaining butter.
3. Bake on a low shelf for about 2 hours. Stir a couple of times during the first hour. Cover towards the end if the top darkens before the grains are tender.
4. Let the porridge rest for 10 minutes before serving.
