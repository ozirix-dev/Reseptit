# Chilaquiles with green zucchini salsa and scrambled eggs

## Description

Crisp corn tortilla pieces are tossed in a green salsa made with zucchini and green pepper, then topped with scrambled eggs and crumbled feta.

## Ingredients

Remove the stem and seeds from the bell pepper before weighing. Peel and deseed the lime before weighing.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the tortillas into triangles and bake in a single layer without oil for 10–15 minutes. Turn them halfway through and bake until crisp.
2. Dice the zucchini, bell pepper and onion. Finely chop the jalapeño. Put them in a saucepan with the water and half the salt. Cover and cook on medium heat for about 10 minutes until the vegetables soften.
3. Take the pan off the heat. Add the lime flesh and all the oil. Blend in the saucepan with an immersion blender, then reheat the salsa.
4. Beat the eggs with the remaining salt. Cook in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat, stirring with a spatula, until set with no liquid egg visible.
5. Toss the crisp tortilla pieces in the hot salsa and divide into two portions. Top with the eggs and crumbled feta. Serve immediately.

## Notes
