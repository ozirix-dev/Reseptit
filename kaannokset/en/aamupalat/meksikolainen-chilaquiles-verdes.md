# Mexican green chilaquiles with eggs and queso fresco

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Cut all 100 g corn tortillas into triangles. Crumble the listed 150 g queso fresco and measure 150 g prepared salsa verde.
2. Heat the listed 1 tablespoon neutral oil. Fry the tortilla triangles in batches until crisp using part of the oil; reserve the remainder for the eggs.
3. Warm all 150 g salsa verde in the pan and briefly toss the tortillas through it without soaking them. Fry both eggs separately in the remaining measured oil until the whites are set.
4. Divide the salsa-coated tortillas between two portions, add one egg to each and crumble all 150 g queso fresco over the top. Use only listed salt; do not add beans or extra sauces.
