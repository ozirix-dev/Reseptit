# Indian semolina upma

## Description

Toasted semolina cooked with peas, carrot and fresh curry leaves.

## Preparation

1. Heat a dry, heavy pan over medium heat. Add 100 g semolina and stir constantly until fragrant, without browning. Transfer it to a bowl.
2. Peel and finely dice the carrot. Rinse 150 g peas and 5 g fresh curry leaves. Heat all 300 ml water in a saucepan and add the carrot, peas and curry leaves. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
3. Gradually stir the toasted semolina into the simmering vegetable mixture, stirring constantly to prevent lumps. Reduce the heat and simmer until the semolina is soft and has absorbed the water. Use only the listed salt if needed.
4. Divide the hot upma into two portions. Serve as it is, without meat, coconut chutney, yoghurt, added fat or other unlisted sides.
