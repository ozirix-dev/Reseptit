# Cardamom-date oat porridge with tahini

## Description

Oat porridge with dates, ground cardamom and unsweetened tahini. This weighed version makes two servings.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above supplies the amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Simmer 100 g of fine 0.5–0.6 mm rolled oats in 300 g of whole milk over low heat for 10 minutes, stirring regularly.
2. Stir in all the measured pitted dried dates, ground cardamom and unsweetened sesame tahini.
3. Divide all the hot porridge between two servings. Do not add other sweeteners or strain out the spices.
