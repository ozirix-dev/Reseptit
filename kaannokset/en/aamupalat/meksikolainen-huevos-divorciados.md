# Mexican huevos divorciados

## Description

Fried eggs with black beans and separate red tomato and green salsa sauces.

## Preparation

1. Drain and rinse canned cooked black beans. Weigh 150 g drained beans for both servings. Allow 25 minutes for this recipe. Soaking and cooking dried beans takes additional time.
2. Wash and finely chop the tomato. Heat it in a small saucepan without added oil until it softens into a red sauce. Use some of the listed salt if desired. In a separate pan heat all 150 g ready-made Mexican green salsa. Keep the sauces separate.
3. Heat the listed 1 tbsp neutral oil in a frying pan. Cook both eggs until the whites are firmly set and the yolks are firm. Heat the drained beans through separately without added oil.
4. Put one egg and 75 g hot drained beans on each plate. Spoon the red sauce on one side of each egg and the green salsa on the other, dividing each sauce equally. Serve immediately. Tortillas and other sides are outside this recipe's portion calculation.
