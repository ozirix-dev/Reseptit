# Baked eggs with spinach – weighed version

## Description

Baked eggs with spinach – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above is used by the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Grease two small ovenproof dishes with the listed butter.
2. Divide the spinach between the dishes and spoon the cream over it. Crack two eggs into each dish, using the listed total of 220 g shelled egg.
3. Season, scatter over the grated cheese and bake for 10–14 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks reach your preferred firmness.
4. Let cool for a couple of minutes and serve in the dishes. Use only the listed fat and seasonings.
