# Vietnamese hu tieu rice-noodle soup with pork and sprouts

## Description

A simplified weighed hu tieu soup with pork loin, rice noodles, sprouts and coriander, served with all its cooking liquid.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above supplies the weighed quantities for serving calculations.

## Instructions

1. Bring all 700 g water to a boil. Add 150 g lean pork loin cut into thin strips and cook until the pork is fully cooked throughout.
2. Add the 100 g dry rice noodles and cook according to the noodle package. Add all 150 g bean sprouts and heat them through thoroughly.
3. Stir in all 6 g chopped coriander and the listed salt to taste. Divide the entire soup, including all its broth, evenly between two portions. Do not add oil, separate stock or other unlisted toppings.

## Notes

The two servings include all 700 g cooking water. The rice noodles are dry before cooking; no extra oil or broth is counted. The 20-minute total is a source estimate, not measured.
