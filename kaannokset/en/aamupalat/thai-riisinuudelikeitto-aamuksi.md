# Thai breakfast rice vermicelli soup – weighed version

## Description

A precisely weighed Thai-style chicken and rice vermicelli soup for two servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredients appear in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g dry rice vermicelli according to the package instructions, then drain. The dry noodles' nutrient composition is used as a proxy for the cooked product; leaching losses have not been measured or deducted.
2. Cook 150 g raw skinless chicken breast in 700 g prepared chicken stock until thoroughly cooked. Add the noodles and 6 g fresh coriander.
3. Stir in 50 g peeled ordinary lime flesh, including all its juice. Divide all the soup and broth into two portions. The chicken's internal temperature must reach at least 75 °C.
