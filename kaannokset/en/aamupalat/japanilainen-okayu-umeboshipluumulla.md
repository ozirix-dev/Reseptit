# Okayu rice porridge with umeboshi

## Description

Japanese okayu is soft rice porridge simmered in water. Salted umeboshi, spring onion and sesame go on each serving.

## Ingredients

Use dry white rice. Weigh the umeboshi after removing the pits.

## Preparation

1. Rinse and drain the rice. Put it in a saucepan and add 700 ml water.
2. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer gently for about 45 minutes, until the rice grains are very soft. Stir along the bottom occasionally.
3. Finely slice the spring onion and chop the pitted umeboshi. Divide the porridge between two bowls and share the umeboshi, spring onion and sesame seeds evenly between them.

## Notes

Contains sesame. Cool leftovers quickly in a shallow container within one hour, refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot throughout. The image shows a serving suggestion.
