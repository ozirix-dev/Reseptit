# Tamago kake gohan with nori

## Description

Tamago kake gohan is a Japanese warm rice bowl with egg and soy sauce stirred through the rice. This version is topped with crumbled nori.

## Ingredients

Weigh out 100 g of dry rice. Use medium eggs. If you stir them into the rice raw, use pasteurised eggs labelled as suitable for eating raw.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet instructions. Divide the hot rice between two bowls.
2. Use one egg per bowl. For the traditional serving method, stir the pasteurised egg into the hot rice straight away.
3. If using regular eggs or serving someone at higher risk from foodborne illness, cook the eggs in a non-stick pan, stirring until set throughout. Stir the cooked eggs into the rice.
4. Divide the soy sauce between the bowls, crumble the nori on top and add salt if needed. Serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains egg and soy. Check the soy sauce label for wheat. The sesame, spring onions and side dishes in the photo are serving suggestions.
