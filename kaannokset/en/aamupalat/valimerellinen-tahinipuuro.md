# Oat porridge with tahini and orange

## Description

Oat porridge with tahini and orange.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g of fine 0.5–0.6 mm rolled oats in 700 g of water for 10 minutes.
2. Stir all the weighed unsweetened sesame tahini and honey into the porridge.
3. Divide the entire porridge between two bowls. Top with 130 g of peeled raw navel orange pieces. Do not strain the juice; no figs or nuts are included in this version.
