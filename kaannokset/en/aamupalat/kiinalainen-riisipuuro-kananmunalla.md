# Rice porridge with a soft-cooked egg

## Description

This home-style rice porridge is topped with a soft-cooked egg. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and simmer it gently in water until it becomes a soft porridge. Stir often.
2. Add the grated ginger and keep simmering until the porridge is thick.
3. Cook the eggs separately so they remain soft and serve them on top of the porridge.
4. Season with salt and finish with spring onion.
