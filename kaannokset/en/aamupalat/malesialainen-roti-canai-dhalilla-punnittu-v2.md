# Roti canai with coconut lentil dhal

## Description

Pan-fried roti canai gets its layers from stretching the dough thin and winding it into a coil. Serve the two flatbreads with soft red lentil dhal made with coconut milk.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour, 65 g water, sugar, 1 g salt for the roti and 5 g of the roti oil. Knead for about 8 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
2. Divide the dough into two balls. Brush with 4 g of the roti oil. Cover and rest at room temperature for at least 4 hours.
3. Rinse the lentils. Finely chop the onion and garlic. Soften the onion in a saucepan in the 5 g oil for the dhal for about 4 minutes. Add the garlic and curry powder and stir for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the lentils, 200 g water for the dhal and coconut milk. Bring to a boil and simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, stirring along the base occasionally, until the lentils are soft. Add the crushed tomatoes and 1 g salt for the dhal. Simmer for another 5 minutes and keep warm.
5. Spread 4 g of the roti oil over the work surface. Flatten the dough balls one at a time and stretch the edges by hand into a thin sheet. Roll the sheet into a strip, coil it and press into a 15–17 cm flatbread.
6. Heat a frying pan over medium heat and use the last 2 g of roti oil. Fry one flatbread at a time for about 2–3 minutes on each side, until golden and cooked through. Serve one roti and half the dhal per person.

## Notes

Allow 4 hours and 30 minutes for the dough to rest. This home version uses mild curry powder. Contains wheat. Check the curry powder label for allergens. The nutrition estimate counts all measured oil.
