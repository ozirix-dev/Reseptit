# Bindaetteok, Korean mung bean pancakes

## Description

Korean bindaetteok are pan-fried pancakes made with soaked, ground mung beans. This version has mung bean sprouts, spring onion and napa cabbage kimchi mixed into the batter.

## Ingredients

Measure the beans dry. You will also need water for soaking and thinning the batter.

## Preparation

1. Rinse 150 g of dry whole mung beans. Cover with plenty of water and soak in the fridge for 8 hours. Discard the soaking water and drain the beans.
2. Boil the mung bean sprouts until cooked through and drain well. Drain and finely chop the napa cabbage kimchi. Finely chop the spring onion.
3. Grind the soaked beans into a thick, coarse paste in a food processor. Start with 60 ml of fresh water. If the blade cannot move freely, add more water a tablespoon at a time. Mix the cooked sprouts, kimchi and spring onion into the paste. Add salt if needed.
4. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Divide the oil between batches. Spoon small portions of batter into the pan and press them thin. Fry on both sides until golden, hot and cooked through in the centre. Cut the first pancake in half to check.
5. Divide between two servings and serve hot.

## Notes

The total time includes 8 hours of soaking. Check the kimchi label for fish, crustaceans, soy and wheat. The dipping sauce and sides in the photo are serving suggestions.
