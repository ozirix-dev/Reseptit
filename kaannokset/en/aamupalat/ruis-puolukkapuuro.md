# Rye and lingonberry porridge

## Description

Slow-cooked rye porridge gets its tart flavour from crushed lingonberries added at the end.

## Instructions

1. Bring the water to a boil and dissolve the sugar and salt in it.
2. Sprinkle in the rye flour a little at a time, whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
3. Simmer gently for 50–60 minutes, stirring regularly right down to the bottom. Crush the berries with a fork meanwhile.
4. Stir the berries into the cooked porridge. Once the whole mixture is bubbling again, cook for at least 2 minutes more. Divide among four bowls.

## Notes

- Contains rye. To serve cold, cool the porridge quickly in small containers and refrigerate.
