# Halloumi omelette

## Description

Salty halloumi and fresh tomatoes make this omelette a filling breakfast without bread.

## Preparation

1. Dice the halloumi and halve the tomatoes. Brown the halloumi in a dry frying pan and transfer it to a plate.
2. Lightly beat the eggs and season with pepper. Melt the butter in the frying pan.
3. Pour the eggs into the pan. When the underside starts to set, add the halloumi and tomatoes to one half.
4. Fold the omelette over and cook briefly. Sprinkle with parsley.

## Notes

Halloumi is salty, so taste the omelette before adding any salt.
