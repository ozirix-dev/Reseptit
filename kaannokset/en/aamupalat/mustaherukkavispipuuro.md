# Whipped blackcurrant semolina porridge

## Description

Blackcurrants lend tartness to dark wheat semolina. Once cool, whip the porridge with an electric mixer until light and airy.

## Preparation

1. Purée the blackcurrants. Put 1 dl of the water and the purée in a small saucepan and boil for at least 2 minutes.
2. Heat the remaining 9 dl of water in another saucepan. Whisk in the semolina in a thin stream and simmer gently for about 10 minutes. Stir occasionally.
3. Stir the blackcurrant purée, sugar and vanillin sugar into the cooked porridge. Transfer to a wide bowl and cool in a cold water bath until cool throughout.
4. Beat with an electric mixer for a few minutes until the porridge turns paler and fluffy. Divide into portions.

## Notes

- Contains wheat. Boil the purée for at least two minutes so that imported frozen berries also receive the treatment recommended by the Finnish Food Authority.
