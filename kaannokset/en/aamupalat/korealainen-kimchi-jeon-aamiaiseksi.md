# Korean kimchi pancakes (kimchi-jeon)

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Chop all 150 g kimchi and measure 100 g wheat flour, 2 eggs and 2 tablespoons chopped spring onions. Keep any liquid from the measured kimchi.
2. Whisk both eggs and mix in the kimchi with its liquid, all the flour and spring onions to make a thick batter. Use only the listed optional salt, with no extra sauce.
3. Heat all 1 tablespoon neutral oil over medium heat. Make two relatively thin pancakes and fry until crisp and fully cooked on both sides, with no raw egg or flour in the centres.
4. Serve one pancake per portion. If the kimchi is too dry to make a spreadable batter, the source needs a separate texture decision; no unmeasured water is included here.
