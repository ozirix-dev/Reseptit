# Kimchi-jeon with fresh napa cabbage kimchi

## Description

Napa cabbage is seasoned with garlic and Korean chili in the style of geotjeori. The fresh kimchi goes straight into the batter. Makes about four small pancakes for two people.

## Ingredients

Rinse and dry the napa cabbage and spring onion before weighing.

## Preparation

1. Finely shred the napa cabbage and slice the spring onion. Mince the garlic. Massage the garlic, gochugaru, rice vinegar and salt into the cabbage. Let stand for 10 minutes.
2. Whisk the egg, cold water and all the rapeseed oil into the flour. Fold in the spring onion, seasoned cabbage and all the liquid released by the cabbage. Cook immediately.
3. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat. Spread the batter into small, thin pancakes and cook without extra oil for about 3 minutes, until the edges set and the underside browns.
4. Flip and cook for about 3 minutes, until the other side browns and the centre is cooked through with no wet raw batter. Cook the rest of the batter the same way and divide into two portions.

## Notes

Contains wheat (gluten) and egg. Do not taste raw batter. Nutrition values are estimates. Glycemic load is undetermined. The image is an illustration.
