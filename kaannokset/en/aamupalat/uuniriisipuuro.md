# Oven-baked rice porridge

## Description

Rice porridge cooks in the oven without constant stirring. Whole milk gives it a creamy texture and a thin baked skin forms on top.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Grease a roughly 2-litre baking dish with the butter.
2. Put the rice, milk and salt in the dish. Stir and put it on the middle oven rack.
3. Bake for about 90 minutes. Stir twice early in the baking time. The porridge is ready when the rice is tender and the mixture has thickened. Cover with foil if the top is getting too dark.
4. Let the porridge rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Cool any leftovers quickly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
