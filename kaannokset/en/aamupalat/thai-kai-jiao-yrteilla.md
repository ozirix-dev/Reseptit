# Thai herb omelette – weighed version

Weigh all the listed edible ingredients in grams, with the egg weighed without its shell. Use only the specified oil and seasonings.

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs with the fish sauce. Finely chop the spring onions and Thai basil and mix into the eggs. The nutrient calculation uses ordinary fresh basil as the composition proxy for Thai basil.
2. Heat the rapeseed oil in a frying pan until hot and pour in the egg mixture.
3. Fry until the edges are crisp and the omelette is fully cooked; turn carefully if needed.
4. Serve immediately.

Divide and consume all the listed ingredients and any cooking juices across the stated number of portions. Nutrient values for raw ingredients are used as a proxy for the cooked dish; unmeasured cooking losses are not deducted.
