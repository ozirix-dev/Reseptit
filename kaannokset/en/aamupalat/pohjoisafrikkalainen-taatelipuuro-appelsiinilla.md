# North African date and orange porridge

## Description

This home-style North African porridge is made with dates and orange. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Measure the semolina into a saucepan and add the water. Bring to a boil while stirring along the bottom of the pan.
2. Simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally, until soft and thick. If it gets too thick, add liquid a little at a time.
3. Finely chop the dates and cut the peeled orange into pieces.
4. Stir the dates, orange and cinnamon into the cooked porridge. Season with salt.
