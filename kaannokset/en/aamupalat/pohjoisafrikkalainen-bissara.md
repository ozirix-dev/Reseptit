# Moroccan bissara fava bean soup

## Description

Bissara is a Moroccan puréed soup flavoured with fava beans, garlic and cumin. Olive oil is drizzled over the individual servings.

## Ingredients

Use dried, peeled split fava beans. Set aside extra cold water for soaking.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the beans. Cover generously with cold water and soak in the fridge for at least 8 hours. Discard the soaking water and rinse the beans.
2. Put the beans, 700 ml fresh water and the minced garlic in a saucepan. Bring to the boil. Simmer with the lid ajar for 45–60 minutes or longer, until the beans are very tender all the way through and break apart when pressed with a spoon. Follow the packet instructions for soaking and cooking, too.
3. Blend the beans in their cooking liquid and stir in the ground cumin. Add salt to taste if needed.
4. Divide the soup between two bowls and drizzle with the measured olive oil.

## Notes

The total time includes about 8 hours of soaking. Avoid fava beans if you have G6PD deficiency. The illustration shows a serving suggestion with bread and herbs.
