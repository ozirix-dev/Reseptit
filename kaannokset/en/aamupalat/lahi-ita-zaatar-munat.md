# Za’atar eggs with toasted pita

## Description

A homemade za’atar of thyme, sesame and sumac seasons these pan-cooked eggs. Serve them with pita and yogurt.

## Ingredients

Weigh the eggs without their shells. Use plain, unsweetened yogurt.

## Preparation

1. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan until light golden. Mix with the thyme, sumac and salt.
2. Toast the pita in a dry pan. Cook the eggs in a dry nonstick pan over medium heat. Cover if needed and cook until both whites and yolks are firm.
3. Divide the eggs, pita and yogurt evenly between two servings. Sprinkle the spice mix over the eggs and divide the oil over the yogurt.

## Notes

Contains egg, milk, wheat (gluten) and sesame. Nutrition values are estimates and exclude up to 0.5 g of sumac per serving. Glycemic load: undetermined.
