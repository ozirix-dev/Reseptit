# Za'atar eggs with warm pita and yogurt

## Description

Eggs pan-fried with za'atar and served with pita and yogurt, two servings.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Measure 100 g ready-made pita and warm it in a dry pan or oven without added fat. Divide between two portions.
2. Heat the total listed 1 tablespoon neutral frying oil in a pan. Fry both eggs to your preferred doneness, ensuring the whites are set.
3. Sprinkle the listed 1 teaspoon za'atar over both eggs evenly. Use only the listed salt, if needed, because za'atar blends may already contain salt.
4. Divide 100 g yogurt into two small portions. Serve one egg with warm pita and 50 g yogurt per portion; no additional butter or sauce is included.
