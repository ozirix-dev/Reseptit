# Spinach and feta shakshuka

## Description

In this simple shakshuka, eggs cook in a pan of softened tomatoes and spinach. Crumbled feta goes on just before serving.

## Ingredients

Choose a medium tomato. You will need a small frying pan with a lid.

## Preparation

1. Chop one medium tomato and rinse the 100 g of spinach. Crack the two eggs into separate cups and crumble the 150 g of feta.
2. Heat one tablespoon of oil in a small frying pan with a lid. Soften the tomato over medium heat. Add the spinach and cook, stirring, until the leaves have wilted and the excess liquid has evaporated.
3. Make two wells in the vegetables and slide one egg into each. Cover and cook over low heat for about 8–12 minutes, until both the whites and the yolks are firm.
4. Scatter the feta over the pan and divide into two servings, with one egg in each. Add salt if needed and serve hot.

## Notes

Contains eggs and milk. The nutrition estimate uses a medium tomato and feta with 25% fat. The illustration shows a serving suggestion with bread, herbs and seeds.
