# Tempeh and egg rice bowl with sambal

## Description

Crisp pan-fried soy tempeh, cooked eggs and sambal over warm jasmine rice.

## Ingredients

Measure the rice dry. Use sambal oelek and check its label for allergens. The picture shows serving suggestions.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet instructions. Use the amount of cooking water specified on the packet.
2. Slice the tempeh thinly. Heat the rapeseed oil in a nonstick pan and fry the tempeh until golden on both sides and hot throughout, following the packet instructions.
3. Transfer the tempeh to a plate. Fry the eggs in the same pan until both the whites and yolks are firm.
4. Divide the rice, tempeh and eggs between two bowls. Divide the 10 g sambal evenly between the bowls and serve warm.

## Notes

Contains soy and eggs. Cool leftover rice quickly in a shallow container within one hour, refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot throughout.
