# Chickpea shakshuka with eggs and parsley

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version of this breakfast dish.

## Ingredients

All ingredient quantities are stored above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Gently sauté 125 g tomato in 13.5 g olive oil and add 100 g cooked, drained whole chickpeas.
2. Add 110 g of shelled egg and cook under a lid until the eggs are done. Finish with 8 g parsley and the listed salt.
3. Divide the entire pan between two servings. Do not substitute dry beans or chickpea flour.
