# Egg tacos – huevos rancheros

## Instructions

1. Soften 27.5 g red onion in half of the 14 g butter. Add 150 g crushed tomatoes, 2 g Santa Maria smoked paprika and 1 g cumin. Simmer for 8 min.
2. Beat 220 g shelled egg and cook two thin omelettes through in the remaining butter.
3. Fill with all the tomato mixture, 120 g peeled stoned avocado and 40 g cheddar. Finish with 10 g coriander, 0.5 g black pepper and salt. Serve two portions without tortillas.
