# Dashimaki tamago with rice

## Description

This Japanese rolled omelette is seasoned with dashi and soy sauce and served with warm rice.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet instructions. Make the omelette while the rice cooks.
2. Mix the eggs, prepared dashi and soy sauce with a fork until even. Heat a small non-stick pan over medium-low heat and brush it with a thin layer of oil.
3. Pour in about a third of the egg mixture. Once the bottom sets, roll the layer to the edge of the pan with a spatula. Lightly oil the bare pan and pour in another thin layer. Lift the edge of the roll to let the egg run underneath. Once the new layer sets, roll it around the first one. Repeat with the remaining egg mixture.
4. Cook the roll over low heat. Cut at the thickest point and check that the centre is fully set. Slice, divide between two servings and serve with warm rice.

## Notes

Contains egg and soy. Dashi may contain fish and soy sauce may contain wheat. Check the labels. The other side dishes pictured are serving suggestions.
