# Huevos a la mexicana

## Description

Huevos a la mexicana. A precisely weighed recipe for two servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredients are listed in the structured recipe data used by the portion calculator.

## Instructions

1. Heat 13.5 g oil in a frying pan. Soften 55 g onion and 15 g fresh jalapeño. Add 125 g tomato.
2. Add 110 g egg without shells and cook, stirring. Season with salt.
3. Divide the entire panful, including all cooking juices, between two servings. This version does not include tortillas or other side dishes.
