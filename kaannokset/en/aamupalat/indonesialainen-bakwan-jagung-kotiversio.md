# Bakwan jagung, Indonesian corn fritters

## Description

These pan-fried fritters are made with sweetcorn and spring onion. The recipe makes about six small fritters for two people.

## Ingredients

Weigh the corn off the cob and the spring onion after trimming.

## Preparation

1. Finely slice the spring onion. Mix the flour, water and salt into a thick batter. Fold in the corn and spring onion.
2. Heat half the oil in a nonstick frying pan over medium heat. Spoon in three separate mounds and flatten them into thin fritters.
3. Fry for about 3–4 minutes on each side. Turn when the edges are set. Lower the heat if the outside browns before the centre cooks. Check the thickest part before serving. There should be no wet, raw batter in the centre.
4. Transfer to a rack to drain. Fry the remaining batter in the rest of the oil and divide all the fritters between two people.
