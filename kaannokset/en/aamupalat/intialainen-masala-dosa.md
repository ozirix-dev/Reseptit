# Masala dosa with potato filling and ready-made batter

## Description

Crisp dosa pancakes are filled with potatoes, onion, mustard seeds and turmeric. Ready-made rice and urad batter makes preparation quicker.

## Ingredients

Choose refrigerated rice and urad batter intended for dosa. Store and prepare it according to the packet instructions. Set aside water for boiling the potatoes.

## Preparation

1. Dice the potatoes and boil them in water until completely tender. Drain and roughly mash them.
2. Finely chop the onion. Heat 8 g rapeseed oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the mustard seeds and cook until they start popping. Add the onion and cook until soft. Stir in the turmeric, potatoes and 1 g salt. Heat the filling all the way through.
3. Stir the dosa batter and thin it only as directed on the packet. Spread a thin layer of batter in a hot nonstick pan. Use the remaining 4 g oil to cook the dosas in batches until no raw batter remains and the edges are crisp.
4. Spread the hot potato filling over the dosas, fold them and divide into two portions.

## Notes

Contains mustard. Check the ready-made batter label for allergens. The image shows a serving suggestion.
