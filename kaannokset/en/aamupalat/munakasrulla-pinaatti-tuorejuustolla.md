# Omelette roll with spinach and cream cheese

## Description

A thin oven-baked omelette is rolled around a spinach and cream cheese filling and sliced.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Line a rimmed baking tray with parchment and lightly grease the paper.
2. Whisk the eggs and cream. Season, stir in the grated cheese and pour into the tray in an even layer.
3. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the surface is set. Turn out onto a fresh sheet of parchment and peel off the baking paper.
4. Finely chop the spinach and mix it with the cream cheese. Spread over the cooled omelette.
5. Use the parchment to roll the omelette tightly. Let it settle for 10 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

The omelette rolls best while still lukewarm and before it dries out.
