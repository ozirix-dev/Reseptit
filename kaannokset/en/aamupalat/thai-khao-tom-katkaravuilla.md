# Thai khao tom rice soup with shrimp

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version of this breakfast dish.

## Ingredients

All ingredient quantities are stored above for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g of dry white jasmine rice in 700 g of ready-to-use chicken stock according to the rice package's cooking time. Keep the grains in the soup; do not cook for 40 minutes into a thick porridge.
2. Add all the garlic, ginger and 150 g of peeled shrimp. Cook until the shrimp are cooked all the way through.
3. Divide all the hot rice soup, including the broth, into two servings.
