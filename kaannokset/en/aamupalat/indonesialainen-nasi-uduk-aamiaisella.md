# Indonesian nasi uduk with egg and cucumber

## Description

A home-kitchen version based on the measured ingredient quantities.

## Ingredients

The structured list above supplies amounts for the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g of dry white jasmine rice with 200 g full-fat coconut milk in a covered saucepan over low heat, following the rice package's cooking time. If the rice starts to dry out before the grains are cooked, add only a small amount of hot water at a time as needed. Do not add oil or other nutrient-containing ingredients.
2. Steam 110 g of egg weighed without the shell in a small dish with no added fat. Use all of it.
3. Serve all the hot coconut rice, egg and 100 g cucumber as two portions. Do not add unlisted sauces or accompaniments.
