# Japanese soba breakfast soup

## Description

Soba noodles with spinach and poached eggs in dashi and stock.

## Preparation

1. Wash the bunch of spinach and discard damaged leaves. Bring 200 ml dashi and the separately listed 700 ml vegetable or chicken stock to a boil together. Do not replace either stock with water or oil.
2. Cook 100 g soba in a separate pan of water, following the package timing, until cooked and still holding their shape. Drain and divide between two bowls. The noodle cooking water is discarded.
3. Lower the heat so the broth gently simmers. Crack each egg into a cup and slide it carefully into the broth. Cook until the whites are completely set. Continue cooking as needed until the yolks are fully cooked. Lift out with a slotted spoon.
4. Add the washed spinach to the hot broth and cook until tender. Divide all the spinach and the full 900 ml of measured broth between the bowls. Top each with one cooked egg and add only the listed salt if needed. Use no oil, tofu, seaweed or other unlisted ingredients.

## Notes

- Cook the soba separately and serve with both stocks. A bunch of spinach has no specified edible weight; the updated preparation does not approve nutrition or glycaemic-load values.
- Check the soba and broth labels for allergens. Soba may contain wheat and dashi may contain fish.
