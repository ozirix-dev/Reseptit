# Steamed bao buns with egg

## Description

Soft folded bao buns are steamed and filled with hard-boiled egg, cucumber and hoisin. The recipe makes two filled buns.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Add the water and rapeseed oil. Knead for about 8 minutes until smooth. Cover and let rise in a warm place for about 60–90 minutes, until almost doubled.
2. Divide the dough in half. Roll each piece into a roughly 12 cm oval. Fold in half around a small piece of baking parchment. Set the buns on separate pieces of parchment, cover and let rise for 20–30 minutes, until visibly puffy.
3. Bring the water in the steamer to a boil. Space the buns out on the steamer rack above the water. Cover and steam for about 12–14 minutes, until the dough is cooked through at the fold. Turn off the heat and let stand, covered, for 3 minutes. Open the lid carefully to avoid the steam.
4. While the dough rises, hard-boil the eggs until the whites and yolks are firm. Cool, peel and weigh 100 g of egg. Wash and slice 100 g cucumber. Remove the parchment and divide the egg, cucumber and 15 g hoisin evenly between the two buns. Serve immediately.

## Notes

**Allergens are wheat and egg.** Hoisin may contain soy, wheat and sesame. Check the ingredients of your product. Nutrition values are estimates based on the AFCD generic hoisin entry and may vary by brand. The effect of fermentation on the dough’s carbohydrates has not been measured.
