# Spinach and cottage cheese egg bake

## Description

The baked omelet supplemented with spinach and cottage cheese is cut into six pieces. Mozzarella becomes juicy from frying and browns on the surface.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C with constant heat. Line an approximately 18 × 18 cm baking dish with baking paper.
2. Rinse the spinach, dry it carefully and chop it. Drain the mozzarella and cut it into small cubes. Beat the eggs to break the texture. Mix in cottage cheese, spinach, smoked paprika, salt and 40 g of mozzarella.
3. Pour the mixture into the pan and sprinkle the remaining 20 g of mozzarella on top. Bake on medium for about 25-35 minutes. Check doneness for the first time at 25 minutes. Baking is ready when the surface has acquired color, the center has congealed and the cooking gauge shows at least 71 °C in the center.
4. Let rest for 5-10 minutes and cut into six pieces.
