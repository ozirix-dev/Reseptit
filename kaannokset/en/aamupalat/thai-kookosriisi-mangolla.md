# Coconut jasmine rice with mango and sesame – weighed version

## Description

A precisely weighed version of coconut rice with mango and sesame, making two servings.

## Ingredients

The measured ingredients are displayed in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Cook 100 g dry white jasmine rice in 184 g coconut milk and 100 g water, following the rice package's cooking time. Serve the grains hot. The estimate does not apply to sticky rice or cooked-and-chilled rice.
2. Add 6 g whole hulled sesame seeds. Peel the mango, weigh 150 g of edible flesh and serve in raw pieces, not puréed.
3. Divide all the hot coconut rice and mango into two portions. Do not add any other sweetener.
