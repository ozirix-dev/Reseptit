# Warm chocolate chia porridge

## Description

Dark chocolate melts into soft chia porridge. The saucepan makes two small adult portions.

## Preparation

1. Mix the water and cream in a small saucepan. Sprinkle in the chia seeds while whisking.
2. Let the seeds swell for 20 minutes. Stir again after a couple of minutes to break up any clumps. Meanwhile, finely chop the chocolate.
3. Heat gently, stirring, until the porridge is steaming and drops slowly from a spoon. If still runny, take the saucepan off the heat and let stand for another 5 minutes.
4. Take the saucepan off the heat and stir in the chocolate, butter and salt. If the porridge is too thick, stir in 10–20 g warm water. Divide equally between two bowls and serve warm.

## Notes

- Each portion contains 12 g chia seeds. The Finnish Food Authority recommends no more than about 15 g of oilseeds a day for adults, varying the seeds eaten. Count oilseeds from other foods eaten that day as well. [Finnish Food Authority guidance](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/elintarvikkeet/elintarvikeala/valvonta/tutkimukset-ja-projektit/vierasaineisiin-liittyvia-hankkeita/oljysiementen-kartoitus--ja-valvontahanke/)
- Contains milk. Check the seeds and chocolate packaging for possible traces of allergens.
