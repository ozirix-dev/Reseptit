# Scrambled eggs with pico de gallo on corn tortillas

## Description

Scrambled eggs with pico de gallo on corn tortillas.

## Ingredients

The structured ingredient list above is used by the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Dice the tomato finely and chop the coriander. Mix them into a fresh pico de gallo-style topping. This version contains no unlisted onion or other ingredients.
2. Warm the corn tortillas in a dry pan and keep them warm.
3. Lightly beat the eggs in a bowl and season with the listed salt.
4. Warm the listed frying oil over low heat. Add the eggs and gently move them with a spatula until the scramble is soft but fully cooked.
5. Divide the eggs and tomato-coriander topping over the warm tortillas and serve immediately.
