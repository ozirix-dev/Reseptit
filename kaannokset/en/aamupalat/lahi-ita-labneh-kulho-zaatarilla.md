# Labneh bowl with za’atar and olives

## Description

Cool, tangy labneh is served with cucumber, olives and za’atar.

## Preparation

1. Measure 100 g ready-made labneh and check that it is ready to eat. Serve this bowl cold and do not cook or heat it.
2. Slice or dice the cucumber. Drain 150 g ready-to-eat olives and remove any pits before serving.
3. Divide the labneh, cucumber and olives evenly between two cold bowls. Sprinkle with 1 tsp za’atar in total and add the listed salt only if needed.
4. Serve immediately. Do not add olive oil, beans, tofu, eggs, a warm base or a separate dressing, which are outside this weighed recipe.
