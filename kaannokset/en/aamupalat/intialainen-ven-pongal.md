# Ven pongal with whole mung beans

## Description

In this home-style South Indian ven pongal, rice and whole mung beans cook down into a soft porridge. Cashews toasted in ghee are stirred in at the end.

## Ingredients

Use dried whole green mung beans, dry white long-grain rice, raw unsalted cashews and ground black pepper.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the mung beans and rice separately. Put them in a saucepan and add 700 ml water.
2. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer gently for 40–50 minutes or until both the beans and rice are completely tender. Stir along the bottom occasionally. If the porridge starts drying out before the beans are cooked, add a little hot water. Do not drain the cooking liquid.
3. Heat 1 tbsp ghee in a small pan. Toast the cashews in the ghee until golden, then add the black pepper. Stir the cashews, pepper and ghee into the porridge. Add salt if desired and divide into two portions.

## Notes

Contains cashews and milk (ghee). Cool leftovers quickly in a shallow container within one hour. Refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot throughout.
