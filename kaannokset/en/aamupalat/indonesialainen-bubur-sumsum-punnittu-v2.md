# Bubur sumsum – coconut pudding with pandan

## Description

This smooth Indonesian rice flour pudding is cooked with coconut milk and scented with pandan. Spoon warm coconut palm sugar syrup over the pudding.

## Ingredients

Weigh the water for the pudding and syrup separately. Use unsweetened coconut milk and sugar made solely from coconut blossom sap.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the pandan leaf and tie it into a knot. Whisk the rice flour with the cold coconut milk and pudding water in a saucepan until smooth. Add the salt and pandan.
2. Heat over medium heat, stirring continuously and scraping the bottom, until the mixture bubbles. Reduce the heat and simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, stirring throughout, until smooth and thick.
3. Combine the coconut palm sugar and syrup water in another saucepan. Simmer gently for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sugar dissolves and the syrup thickens slightly.
4. Lift the pandan leaf out of the pudding. Divide the pudding and syrup equally among four bowls and serve warm.

## Notes

Cook the rice flour mixture before tasting. Cool leftovers quickly and refrigerate. Glycemic load: undetermined. The image is an illustration.
