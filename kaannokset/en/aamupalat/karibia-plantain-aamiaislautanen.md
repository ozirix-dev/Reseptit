# Caribbean plantain and fried-egg breakfast plates

## Description

Pan-fried plantain served with eggs, sliced avocado and lime for two portions.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Peel the 1 plantain and cut it diagonally into slices about 5 mm thick. Halve the 1 avocado, remove the stone and slice the flesh. Cut the 1 lime into wedges.
2. Heat part of the listed 1 tablespoon neutral frying oil in a pan and fry the plantain slices on both sides until golden and tender. Keep them warm.
3. Use the remaining portion of the same listed oil to fry both eggs to your preferred doneness. Season only with the listed salt if needed.
4. Divide the plantain, both eggs and avocado between two plates. Squeeze the lime over just before serving. Do not make any additional batter or patties.

## Notes

The Finnish source specifies whole plantain, avocado and lime by count, without their edible weights or four macronutrient values. These must be verified before publication.
