# Salmon and sesame onigiri

## Description

Pan-cooked salmon fills triangular rice balls wrapped in nori and sprinkled with sesame.

## Preparation

1. Rinse 100 g dry white rice and cook it in the amount of water specified on the packet. Let the cooked rice cool slightly so that it can be handled.
2. Cook 150 g raw salmon in a dry non-stick pan, without added fat, until cooked through. Remove any bones, flake the salmon and season with the listed salt if needed.
3. Divide the cooked rice into two equal portions. Wet your hands with water, place half of the salmon in the centre of each portion and press the rice firmly into a triangle around the filling.
4. Cut 3 g dried nori into strips and wrap them around the rice triangles. Sprinkle with 5 g sesame seeds in total, 2.5 g per portion. Serve without unlisted fillings or sauces.
