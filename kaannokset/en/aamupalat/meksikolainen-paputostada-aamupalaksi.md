# Mexican black bean, egg and avocado tostadas

## Description

A measured home-kitchen version following the Finnish source; this text is an unpublished translation draft.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are listed in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Measure 150 g already cooked, drained black beans. Dry beans must be safely cooked ahead of time; the 25-minute recipe assumes cooked beans. Slice the edible flesh from the 1 listed avocado.
2. Toast all 100 g corn tortillas in a dry pan until crisp. Warm the beans in another dry pan, lightly mash and season only with listed salt.
3. Heat all 1 tablespoon neutral frying oil and cook both eggs until the whites are set.
4. Divide the tortilla bases and all 150 g beans into two servings, topping each with one egg and half the edible avocado. No unlisted sauces are included.
