# Breakfast tostadas with kidney beans and scrambled eggs

## Description

Crisp corn tortillas are topped with warm mashed beans, scrambled eggs and sliced avocado.

## Ingredients

Weigh the avocado flesh without the skin or stone. Drain the cooked canned beans before weighing.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Bake the tortillas on a tray in a single layer without oil for about 10–15 minutes. Turn them halfway through and bake until crisp.
2. Heat the drained beans in a saucepan until steaming. Roughly mash with a fork, then stir in all the oil and half the salt.
3. Beat the eggs with the remaining salt. Cook in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat, stirring with a spatula, until set with no liquid egg visible.
4. Spread the beans on the crisp tortillas and add the sliced avocado and scrambled eggs. Divide into two portions and serve immediately.
