# Butternut squash and coconut rice porridge

## Description

Butternut squash gives this soft rice porridge its sweetness. Coconut milk makes it creamy, and black sesame seeds go on top.

## Ingredients

Use dried long-grain white rice and full-fat canned coconut milk. Weigh the 250 g squash after peeling and removing the seeds.

## Preparation

1. Rinse and drain the rice. Cut the butternut squash into roughly 2 cm cubes.
2. Put the rice and 700 ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, lower the heat and simmer with the lid ajar for 20 minutes. Stir the bottom occasionally.
3. Add the squash and simmer for another 20–25 minutes, until the rice breaks down and the squash is soft. Crush some of the squash with a spoon to thicken the porridge.
4. Stir in 200 ml coconut milk. Heat for about 3 minutes, stirring. Add salt to taste if desired, divide between two bowls and sprinkle with the black sesame seeds.

## Notes

Contains sesame. Cool any leftover porridge in a shallow container within one hour, refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once, until steaming hot throughout. The illustration also shows serving accompaniments.
