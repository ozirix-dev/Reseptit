# Soft millet porridge with dates

## Description

This home-kitchen version makes a soft millet porridge with dates. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the millet. Put the millet, finely chopped fresh ginger and water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil while stirring.
2. Simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the porridge is soft and thick. Add liquid a little at a time if it gets too thick.
3. Season with salt. Serve warm with chopped dates and sesame seeds.
