# Korean mandu soup with egg

## Description

Manduguk is a Korean dumpling soup. This home version makes twelve small pork-and-cabbage dumplings. Egg ribbons and spring onion finish the broth.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Set aside about a teaspoon of the flour for rolling. Mix the rest with 1 g salt and 55 g water. Knead for about 5 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest for 25 minutes.
2. Finely chop the patted-dry napa cabbage and the spring onion. Mix the pork, cabbage, half the spring onion, soy sauce and sesame oil. Keep the filling chilled until shaping.
3. Divide the dough into 12 pieces and roll them into rounds about 8–9 cm across, using the reserved flour. Divide the filling among the rounds, about 14 g per round. Fold into half-moons, press out the air and pinch the edges tightly closed.
4. Bring the stock to a boil. Lower the dumplings into it and simmer for about 7–9 minutes. Check that the centre of the filling in the largest dumpling reaches at least 71 °C. Continue cooking and check again if needed.
5. Beat the egg and drizzle it in a thin stream into the gently simmering broth. Simmer for about 1 minute until the egg ribbons are fully set. Divide the dumplings and broth between two bowls, sprinkle with the remaining spring onion and serve hot.

## Notes

Contains wheat, egg, soy and sesame. Check the chicken stock package for allergens. Wash hands and utensils that touched raw pork before handling cooked food. One serving is six dumplings with broth.
