# Cold ricotta and peach bowls with pistachios

## Description

A cold breakfast bowl made with weighed ricotta, fresh peaches, lemon juice and pistachios, divided into two servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Divide all 100 g plain ricotta equally between two serving bowls. Do not heat the ricotta.
2. Add all 150 g fresh pitted peach slices, 75 g to each bowl.
3. Divide the listed 1 tablespoon undiluted lemon juice equally between the two portions. Add only the listed salt to taste, if needed.
4. Sprinkle all 30 g raw unsalted pistachio kernels over the bowls, 15 g per serving, and serve cold. Do not add oil, sauces or unlisted ingredients.

## Notes

The measured two-serving recipe includes no olive oil or separate toppings. The nutrient and glycemic-load figures are ingredient-based estimates, not personal blood-glucose measurements.
