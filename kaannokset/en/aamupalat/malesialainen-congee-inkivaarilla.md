# Malaysian congee with soy sauce and ginger

## Description

This home-style Malaysian congee is flavoured with soy sauce and ginger. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice, put it in a saucepan with the water and bring to a boil.
2. Simmer over low heat, stirring, until the rice is soft and the porridge has thickened.
3. Add the grated ginger and soy sauce. Stir and heat for one more minute.
4. Season with salt, divide the porridge among bowls and sprinkle spring onion on top.
