# Thai pork jok with ginger

## Description

This home-style version makes Thai pork jok with ginger. Adjust the seasoning and toppings to taste.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and simmer it gently in water until it becomes a soft porridge. Stir occasionally.
2. Add the minced pork and grated ginger. Cook, stirring, until the pork is fully cooked through.
3. Cook the eggs separately and serve them on top of the porridge.
4. Season with salt and serve immediately.
