# Korean egg and rice bowls (gyeranbap)

## Description

Two warm bowls of rice topped with a fried egg, soy sauce and dried seaweed.

## Ingredients

All measured ingredient quantities are in the structured list above.

## Instructions

1. Rinse all 100 g dry white rice and cook it in the amount of water specified on the package until tender. Divide the cooked warm rice between two bowls.
2. Warm a nonstick pan over low to medium heat. Fry both eggs without adding oil or butter, until the whites are fully set.
3. Place one fried egg on each bowl of rice. Divide the listed 1 tablespoon soy sauce evenly between the bowls.
4. Tear all 5 g dried nori into small pieces and sprinkle over the two servings (2.5 g each). Add only the listed salt if needed; sesame oil is not part of this measured recipe.
