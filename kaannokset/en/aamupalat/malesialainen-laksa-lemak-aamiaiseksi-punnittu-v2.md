# Laksa lemak with tofu and rice noodles

## Description

In this vegetarian adaptation of Malaysian curry laksa, rice noodles and tofu are served in a rich coconut broth. Shallots, ginger, chilli and spices make a fresh curry paste, and lime brightens the soup.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Finely chop the peeled shallots, garlic and ginger, and finely chop the chilli. Grind or mince them with the ground coriander and turmeric to a smooth paste. If needed, loosen it with a spoonful of the measured stock.
2. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Fry the paste, stirring, for about 4 minutes, until the raw onion smell gives way to the aroma of the spices. Add the remaining stock and the coconut milk. Bring to a boil and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
3. In a separate pan, cook the rice noodles according to the packet and drain them. Cut the tofu into bite-sized pieces and rinse the sprouts under running water.
4. Add the tofu and all the sprouts to the broth. Bring it back to a boil and cook until the sprouts are thoroughly cooked and steaming hot throughout. Check that the tofu is hot, too.
5. Crush the lime flesh with a fork and stir it into the hot broth. Divide the drained noodles between two bowls and ladle over the broth, tofu and cooked sprouts. Serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains soy. Check the stock and noodle labels for allergens. Cook all the sprouts in the broth.
