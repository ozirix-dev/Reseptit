# Tofu and kimchi rice bowl

## Description

Pan-fried tofu, tangy napa cabbage kimchi and warm rice topped with toasted sesame seeds.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet instructions.
2. Pat the drained tofu dry and cut into roughly 2 cm cubes.
3. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan over low heat until lightly golden and tip them onto a plate. Heat the oil in the same pan. Fry the tofu cubes over medium heat, turning them until golden on all sides.
4. Divide the rice, tofu and kimchi between two bowls. Sprinkle with the toasted sesame seeds and serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains soy and sesame. Kimchi may contain fish or shellfish. For a vegetarian bowl, choose vegan kimchi and check the label for allergens.
