# Tortilla española – parboiled potato version

## Description

A measured Spanish potato omelette using potatoes precooked in water, for two portions.

## Ingredients

Ingredients are listed in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. For this precooked-potato variation, boil 60 g of weighed, peeled raw potato in 500 g water until tender; drain and discard the cooking water. The macro estimates use the raw ingredients' nutrient composition without deducting any leaching loss.
2. Soften 55 g onion in all 13.5 g olive oil plus 13.5 g rapeseed oil. Add the cooked potato and 110 g egg weighed without its shell.
3. Cook until the egg is just set. Divide all the food and cooking oil between two portions. Do not transfer the GL estimate from the original variant in which potatoes cook for a long time in oil.
