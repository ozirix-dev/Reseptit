# Lontong sayur with cabbage and tofu in coconut broth

## Description

This Indonesian-style lontong sayur serves firm pressed rice cubes in a spiced coconut broth. This vegetable version uses tofu, white cabbage and carrot. Press the cooked rice into a shallow dish.

## Ingredients

Weigh the ingredients as listed before starting.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and drain well. Put it in a small saucepan with 250 g water. Bring to a boil, cover and cook over low heat for 15–20 minutes, until the rice is soft and the water has been absorbed. Rest for 5 minutes with the lid on.
2. Press the hot rice firmly into a clean shallow dish in a layer about 2 cm thick. Start cooling immediately. Set the dish in cold water without letting water touch the rice, then refrigerate as soon as heavy steaming stops. Chill to refrigerator temperature within one hour of cooking.
3. Finely chop the shallot, garlic and chilli. Slice the cabbage, cut the carrot into thin matchsticks and dice the tofu. Soften the shallot in canola oil for 3 minutes. Add garlic, chilli and turmeric and stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add the stock, coconut milk and carrot. Simmer for 5 minutes. Add the cabbage and tofu and simmer for 5–7 minutes until the vegetables are soft and the tofu is hot.
5. Dice the chilled rice cake and add it to the broth. Heat until the cubes are steaming hot throughout. Stir gently, divide between two bowls and serve immediately.

## Notes

Contains soy. Check the vegetable stock label for allergens. If making the rice cake ahead, refrigerate it and use it within 24 hours. Reheat the rice only once.
