# Herbed whipped feta with vegetable sticks

## Description

Feta, Greek yogurt, lemon and dill make a smooth dip for cucumber and red bell pepper.

## Ingredients

Weigh the feta after draining and the vegetables after trimming.

## Preparation

1. Drain the feta and weigh out 120 g. Weigh out 100 g of yogurt and 10 g of lemon juice as well.
2. Crumble the feta into a bowl. Blend it with the yogurt and lemon juice using an immersion blender until smooth.
3. Finely chop 5 g of dill and stir it into the whipped feta.
4. Rinse the cucumber and bell pepper. Remove the stem and seeds from the pepper. Weigh out 200 g of cucumber and 150 g of pepper, then cut them into sticks.
5. Divide the whipped feta and vegetables into four portions. Serve cool. If making the portions ahead, cover and refrigerate them.

## Notes

**Contains milk.** The nutrition estimates use drained goat’s milk feta with 25% fat and plain Greek yogurt with 7% fat. Nutrition values may vary by product.
