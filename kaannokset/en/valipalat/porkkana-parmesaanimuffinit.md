# Savoury carrot and Parmesan muffins

Grated carrot and Parmesan keep these savoury snack muffins moist.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.
2. Combine the flour, baking powder, grated carrot and Parmesan. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, buttermilk and oil.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry one and fold just until no dry flour remains. Divide the batter among the cases, filling each about two-thirds full.
4. Bake for about 25 minutes, until risen and the tops spring back when pressed lightly. Cool on a wire rack.
