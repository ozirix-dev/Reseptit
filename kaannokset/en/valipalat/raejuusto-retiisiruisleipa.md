# Rye bread with cottage cheese and radishes

Thin slices of radish add crunch to this open rye sandwich.

## Preparation

1. Wash the radishes and chives. Slice the radishes thinly and chop the chives.
2. Spoon the cottage cheese over the rye bread. Add the radishes, chives and pepper and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Contains rye and milk. Check the bread label for other grains.
