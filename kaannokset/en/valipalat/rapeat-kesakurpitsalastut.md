# Crispy Parmesan Zucchini Chips

## Description

Thin zucchini slices are baked under a layer of Parmesan. The chips are at their best when the cheese has just browned and set.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 205 °C using conventional top and bottom heat. Line one or two baking trays with baking parchment.
2. Rinse the zucchini and trim off the ends. Weigh out 350 g of zucchini and cut it into slices approximately 3 mm thick. If using a mandoline slicer, use its hand guard.
3. Arrange the slices in a single layer on the tray without overlapping. Use two trays if necessary. Season with salt and black pepper. Finely grate the Parmesan and distribute all of it over the slices.
4. Bake one tray at a time for approximately 20–25 minutes, until the cheese is golden brown and the edges are crisp. Check the colour towards the end of baking and remove any chips that brown faster than the others.
5. Let the chips cool briefly on the tray so that the cheese can set. Transfer the chips and any loose crispy cheese pieces from the tray to a serving dish. Divide into three portions and serve immediately.

## Notes

**Allergen: milk.** Do not use zucchini that tastes unusually bitter. The chips soften during storage.
