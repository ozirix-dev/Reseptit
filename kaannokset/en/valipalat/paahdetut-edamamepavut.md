# Roasted edamame

Smoked paprika and Parmesan coat the oven-roasted soybeans.

## Preparation

1. Cook the frozen beans as directed on the package. Drain and pat dry. Heat the oven to 190 °C.
2. Toss the beans with oil, Parmesan, paprika, salt and pepper. Spread them in a single layer on parchment.
3. Roast on the middle rack for 12–15 minutes, until lightly browned. Divide into four portions and serve warm.

## Notes

- Contains soy and milk. This version precooks the beans according to the package instructions.
