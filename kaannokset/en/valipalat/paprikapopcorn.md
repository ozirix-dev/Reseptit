# Smoked paprika popcorn

Smoked paprika seasons freshly popped stovetop popcorn.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oil with a few kernels in a heavy pot with a lid. When they pop, add the remaining kernels, cover and take the pot off the heat for 30 seconds.
2. Return the pot to the heat and shake it while holding the lid in place. Take it off the heat when pops are a few seconds apart.
3. Tip the popcorn into a bowl. Toss with the paprika and salt, then divide into six portions.
