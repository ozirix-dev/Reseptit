# Goat cheese stuffed mini peppers

Sweet peppers are roasted until tender and filled with lemon-scented goat cheese.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Halve the peppers lengthwise and remove the seeds. Toss the halves with the oil, salt and pepper.
2. Roast the halves on a baking sheet for about 15 minutes, until slightly soft and coloured. Let them cool to room temperature.
3. Mix the cheeses into a smooth paste and season with the parsley and lemon zest. Fill the peppers just before serving. Allow four halves per serving.

## Notes

- Contains milk.
- Preparation reference https://mycasualpantry.com/roasted-mini-sweet-peppers-goat-cheese/
- Use fresh goat cheese made with pasteurised milk, without a mould-ripened or washed rind, for the chilled filling. Use it well before its use-by date. Pregnant people, older adults and people with weakened immunity should avoid unheated raw-milk cheeses and unheated soft mould-ripened or washed-rind cheeses.
