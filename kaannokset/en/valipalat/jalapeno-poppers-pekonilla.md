# Bacon and Cheese Stuffed Jalapeños

## Description

Cream cheese and cheddar mellow the heat of the jalapeños, while a thin strip of bacon crisps up in the oven. Serve the stuffed jalapeños warm as a snack or finger food.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C using conventional top and bottom heat. Line a rimmed baking tray with baking parchment. If you have an oven-safe wire rack that fits the tray, place it over the parchment so that fat released from the bacon can drip onto the tray.
2. Rinse the jalapeños. Wear protective gloves when halving the chillies lengthwise and removing the stems, seeds and pale inner membranes. Avoid touching your face and weigh out 150 g of prepared halves.
3. Mix the cream cheese and grated cheddar. Divide the filling among the halves according to their size and smooth it with a spoon so that most of the filling remains within the edges of the chillies.
4. On a separate cutting board, cut the bacon into as many strips as you have jalapeño halves. Wrap one thin strip around the middle of each half and place it seam-side down. Allow the ends of the bacon to overlap only slightly. Leave space between the halves and keep the filling facing upwards. Wash your hands, cutting board and utensils after handling raw bacon.
5. Bake on the middle oven rack for approximately 20–25 minutes. Follow the cooking instructions on the bacon packaging and cook the bacon to at least 70 °C throughout. Use clean tongs to check the underside and the seam as well. If possible, measure the temperature at the thickest part of the bacon using a thin-probe food thermometer, ensuring that the probe is in the bacon. If the strip is too thin for a reliable temperature measurement, follow the oven-cooking instructions on the packaging. Continue baking for a few minutes at a time if necessary. Crispness or colour alone does not establish that the bacon is fully cooked. If there is any uncertainty about whether it is fully cooked, do not serve.
6. Allow to cool for approximately 5 minutes and check before serving that the filling is cool enough to eat without burning your mouth. Divide the entire cooked batch into six equal portions, taking the size of the chillies into account. The number of halves will vary.

## Notes

**Allergen: milk.** Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate them.

Allow approximately 15–20 minutes for preparation while the oven heats up. The total time of approximately 45 minutes is an estimate. The image shows an example serving of two halves.
