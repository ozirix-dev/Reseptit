# Beetroot and feta spread

Cooked beetroot gives this creamy feta spread its vivid pink colour.

## Preparation

1. Chop the beetroot and crumble the feta. Place them in a food processor with the oil, seeds, lemon juice and pepper.
2. Blend into a spread, scraping down the sides as needed. Divide into six portions. Serve or cover and refrigerate.

## Notes

- Contains milk. A serving refers to the spread alone.
