# Apple cinnamon oat bars

Apple chunks dot the surface of these cinnamon-scented oat bars.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Line a metal pan about 23 × 33 cm with parchment.
2. Grind 89 g of the oats finely. Mix the ground and whole oats with the cinnamon, baking powder and salt.
3. Whisk the apple sauce, eggs and syrup. Stir in the oat mixture. Spread in the pan and gently press the apple, cut into roughly 1 cm cubes, into the top.
4. Bake on the middle rack for about 20 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean. Cool completely and cut into 16 bars.

## Notes

- Contains oats and egg.
