# Crispy cheddar cheese crisps

## Description

Grated cheddar melts into thin, crispy cheese crisps in the oven. Smoked paprika adds a light smoky flavour.

## Ingredients

Weigh out 140 g of grated cheddar and 1 g of smoked paprika.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C using conventional top and bottom heat. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. Divide the grated cheese into 12 equal, shallow mounds. Leave space between them because the cheese will spread as it melts. Sprinkle the smoked paprika evenly over the mounds.
3. Bake on the middle rack for about 6–9 minutes, until the cheese bubbles and the edges turn golden brown. Watch carefully towards the end, as the thin edges brown quickly.
4. Leave to cool on the baking tray for at least 5 minutes, until the crisps are firm and crunchy. Carefully lift them from the parchment. Serve three crisps per portion.
