# Mango lassi

Ripe mango gives this yogurt drink a creamy texture. Lime and cardamom add brightness and fragrance.

## Preparation

1. Peel the mango and cut the flesh away from the stone. Weigh the flesh and cut it into chunks.
2. Blend the mango, yogurt, honey and cardamom until smooth. Add the lime juice and blend briefly again.
3. Divide between two glasses and serve cold immediately.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Do not give honey to children under one year old.
