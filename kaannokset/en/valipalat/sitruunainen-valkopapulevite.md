# Lemon white bean spread

Cannellini beans make a smooth spread with the flavours of lemon and olive oil.

## Preparation

1. Rinse and drain the beans thoroughly. Peel the garlic. Wash the lemon and grate only the yellow part of the peel.
2. Blend the beans, oil, lemon juice and zest, garlic, salt and pepper in a food processor. Add the water in two batches and scrape down the sides as needed.
3. Divide the spread into four portions. Serve with bread or vegetable sticks, or cover and refrigerate immediately.

## Notes

- One serving is a quarter of the spread. Bread and vegetables served alongside are separate.
