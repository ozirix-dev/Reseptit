# Apple wedges with peanut butter

Crisp apple and smooth peanut butter make a snack that takes just a few minutes to assemble.

## Preparation

1. Wash and core the apple, then weigh the edible portion. Cut the apple into wedges.
2. Toss the wedges with the lemon juice. Spoon the peanut butter into a small bowl for dipping and serve immediately.

- Contains peanuts.
