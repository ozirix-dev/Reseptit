# Date and peanut butter bites

Peanut butter, chopped almonds and a thin chocolate topping cover a soft date base.

## Preparation

1. Halve the dates and press them together into a sheet on parchment.
2. Warm the peanut butter gently to loosen it. Spread it over the dates and scatter the almonds on top.
3. Melt the chocolate gently and spread it in a thin layer. Scatter over the salt. Freeze for about 30 minutes, until firm.
4. Cut the sheet into 24 pieces. Keep covered in the refrigerator.

## Notes

- Contains peanuts and almonds. Check the chocolate label for milk.
