# Almond chocolate balls with erythritol

## Description

Cocoa gives these almond balls a rich flavour. The coconut flakes are mixed into the mixture, and the balls firm up in the refrigerator.

## Instructions

1. Grind the erythritol to a fine powder, for example using a spice grinder. Mix it with the almond flour, cocoa powder, desiccated coconut and salt.
2. Melt the butter and coconut oil in a small saucepan over low heat. Remove the saucepan from the heat and stir the almond butter into the melted fats.
3. Pour the fat mixture into the dry ingredients and mix until no dry patches remain. Refrigerate for about 10 minutes to make the mixture easier to shape.
4. Divide the mixture into 12 equal portions. Press each portion firmly together before rolling it into a ball. Refrigerate the balls, covered, for at least 30 minutes.

## Notes

**Allergens: almonds and milk.** Use butter made from pasteurised cream. Store the balls covered in the refrigerator. Nutritional values are calculated per ball. Total carbohydrates include fibre and erythritol. **GL: not determined.**
