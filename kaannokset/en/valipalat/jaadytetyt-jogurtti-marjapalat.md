# Frozen yogurt and berry bark

A thin sheet of frozen vanilla yogurt holds fresh berries and granola.

## Preparation

1. Wash and drain the fresh berries and chop the strawberries. Line a small freezer-safe tray with parchment.
2. Spread the yogurt about 6 mm thick. Scatter the strawberries, blueberries and granola over it, pressing them in lightly.
3. Freeze for about three hours until solid. Break or cut into 12 pieces and serve frozen. Store leftovers in a sealed container in the freezer.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Check the granola label for grains and other allergens. Use fresh berries for this version.
