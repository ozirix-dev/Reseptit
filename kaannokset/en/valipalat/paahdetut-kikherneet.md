# Roasted chickpeas

Cumin and cayenne season these oven-roasted chickpeas. Let them cool on the tray before serving.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Rinse the chickpeas in a sieve, drain well and pat them dry with a clean kitchen towel.
2. Toss the chickpeas with the oil, cumin and cayenne. Spread them in a loose single layer on a baking tray lined with parchment.
3. Roast on the middle rack for about 30 minutes. Shake the tray halfway through. Take the chickpeas out when their surfaces have browned.
4. Sprinkle salt over the warm chickpeas. Let them cool on the tray and divide into four portions.
