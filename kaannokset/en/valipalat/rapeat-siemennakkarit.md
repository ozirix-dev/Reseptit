# Crispy Seed Crackers

## Description

These thin seed crackers are baked as a single sheet and broken into pieces once cooled. Toasted crushed flaxseeds, pumpkin seeds and sesame seeds give the crackers a roasted flavour.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C using conventional top and bottom heat. Line a large baking tray with baking parchment.
2. Combine the seeds, crushed flaxseeds, psyllium and salt in a bowl. Bring the water to a boil, pour it over the seed mixture and stir in the olive oil. Let stand for 10 minutes.
3. Use a spatula to spread the gel-like dough into a thin sheet of even thickness. Bake for approximately 60 minutes. The sheet is ready when the centre is dry and the surface is golden brown.
4. Let the sheet cool completely on a wire rack. Break it into 24 pieces of approximately equal size. Store the completely cooled crackers in a dry container.

## Notes

**Allergen: sesame.** One piece is an adult serving and contains approximately 7.2 g of oilseeds, based on calculation. Include oilseeds consumed in other foods during the day when calculating the total intake. The Finnish Food Authority recommends a maximum of approximately 15 g per day for adults.
