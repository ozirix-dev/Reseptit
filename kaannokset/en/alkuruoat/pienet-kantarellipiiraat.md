# Mini Finnish chanterelle tartlets

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Rub the flours and cold butter together into crumbs. Mix in the cold water quickly and chill the dough for about 15 minutes.
2. Clean and chop positively identified chanterelles and the onion. Cook mushrooms until liquid evaporates, then add butter and onion and fry until soft.
3. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Press the pastry into eight small greased tart tins and pre-bake for about 8 minutes.
4. Mix crème fraîche, eggs and grated cheese. Stir in cooled mushrooms and salt, then divide among the pastry shells.
5. Bake for about 18–22 minutes until the filling is set and lightly golden.

## Safety notes

Use positively identified edible chanterelles. Cook the egg filling until set and refrigerate leftovers.
