# Thai mixed vegetable stir-fry

Mixed vegetables are quickly wok-fried with garlic and oyster sauce.

## Preparation

1. Wash and cut vegetables; peel thick gai lan stems. Separate firm and quick-cooking vegetables.
2. Heat oil, soften garlic. Stir-fry carrot, cauliflower and onion for 2–3 min, then add mushrooms, cucumber, cabbage and gai lan; cook until mushrooms are done and vegetables tender-crisp.
3. Stir in sauces and sugar, adding water as needed. Heat through and finish with spring onion.

## Notes

- Contains molluscs and soy, possibly wheat. Ordinary oyster sauce is not vegetarian.
