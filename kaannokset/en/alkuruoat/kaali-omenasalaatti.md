# Cabbage and apple slaw

## Description

Thinly sliced cabbage, carrot and apple make a crisp side dish, lightly coated with Caesar dressing.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the vegetables and apple before preparing them. Cut out the tough cabbage core and slice the leaves thinly.
2. Coarsely grate the apple and carrot. Combine them with the cabbage and toss with the dressing.
3. Serve immediately or keep covered in the fridge until serving.

## Notes

- Ingredients and allergens vary between dressings. Check the label, especially for fish, milk, egg and mustard.
