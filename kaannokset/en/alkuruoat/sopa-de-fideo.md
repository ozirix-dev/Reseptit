# Mexican tomato noodle soup (sopa de fideo)

Thin noodles toasted in oil simmer in a tomato broth. Makes four starter portions.

## Preparation

1. Halve the tomatoes and roast them cut-side up at 225°C for 15–20 minutes until soft. Blend the tomatoes with the chopped onion and peeled garlic. Strain the purée into a bowl.
2. If using long spaghetti, break it into pieces about 3 cm long. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir the dry pasta in the oil for 3–4 minutes until pale golden.
3. Carefully add the tomato purée. Stir for one minute and pour in the stock. Bring to a boil. Cover and simmer for about 8 minutes until the pasta is tender.
4. Divide among four bowls and serve immediately.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat if made with standard wheat pasta. Check the stock label for allergens. Cool leftovers quickly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
