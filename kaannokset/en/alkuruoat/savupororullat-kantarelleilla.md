# Smoked reindeer rolls with chanterelles

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Clean and chop positively identified chanterelles. Finely chop the onion.
2. Cook the mushrooms until their moisture evaporates. Add the butter and onion; continue frying until the onion softens. Cool completely.
3. Combine the cooled mushroom mixture with the cream cheese, chives and black pepper.
4. Spread a small amount on ready-to-eat cooked smoked reindeer slices, roll up and refrigerate until serving.

## Safety notes

Use fully cooked ready-to-eat smoked reindeer. Identify and cook all mushrooms properly; keep the finished rolls cold.
