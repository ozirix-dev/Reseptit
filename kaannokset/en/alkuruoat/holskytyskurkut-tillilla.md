# Quick Finnish Dill Cucumbers

Thin cucumber slices take on the flavour of vinegar and dill quickly.

## Method

1. Slice the cucumber very thinly and chop the dill.
2. Put the slices in a lidded container, sprinkle with salt and shake until the cucumber releases a little liquid.
3. Stir in the vinegar, sugar and dill. Refrigerate for 20 minutes and stir again before serving.

## Notes

- Keep refrigerated and serve as a side dish the same day.
