# Traditional Finnish Egg Sauce for Fish

A mild milk-based sauce with chopped hard-boiled eggs and fresh dill.

## Preparation

1. Use fully hard-boiled, peeled eggs. Chop the eggs and dill. Allow extra time if you need to boil and cool the eggs.
2. Melt butter in a saucepan. Stir in flour and cook for one minute without browning.
3. Add the milk little by little while whisking. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 5 minutes until thick.
4. Stir in chopped eggs and dill, season with salt and white pepper, and heat through. Serve immediately with cooked fish and potatoes.
