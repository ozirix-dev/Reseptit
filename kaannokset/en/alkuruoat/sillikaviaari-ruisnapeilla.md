# Finnish herring spread on rye crisps

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Hard-boil the eggs for about 9–10 minutes and cool them promptly.
2. Drain the ready-to-eat herring and finely chop it. Chop the red onion, eggs and dill.
3. Combine the chopped ingredients with smetana, crème fraîche and lemon juice. Taste for salt.
4. Refrigerate the spread and spoon it onto the rye crisps just before serving, so they remain crisp.

## Safety notes

Use only ready-to-eat herring and keep chilled. Contains fish, eggs, milk and rye. Despite its traditional Finnish name, the spread contains no fish roe.
