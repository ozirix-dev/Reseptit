# Swedish shrimp toast (Toast Skagen)

## Description

Chilled shrimp salad is served over crisp buttered toast.

## Preparation

1. Drain the cooked, peeled shrimp well and chop any large pieces. Mix with the mayonnaise, lemon juice and chopped dill. Keep the topping refrigerated.
2. Divide the bread into four slices. Fry them in butter over medium heat until golden on both sides.
3. Let the toast cool briefly, then add the shrimp topping just before serving.
