# Date-sweetened ketchup

## Description

Tomato puree and soft dates are pureed into a thick ketchup. Apple cider vinegar gives acidity and garlic sharpness.

## Instructions

1. Cover the dates with hot water and soak for about 15 minutes. Drain and discard the soaking water.
2. Place the dates, tomato paste, apple cider vinegar, peeled garlic and salt in a tall mixing bowl. Purée with a hand blender until smooth.
3. If necessary, add clean cold water a tablespoon at a time until the sauce is suitably thick. Transfer to a clean container with a lid and keep in the fridge.

## Notes

- Make a small batch and use within three days. The sauce is stored in the cold.
- The reel has a small jar of tomato paste, three soaked dates, a splash of vinegar, garlic and salt. Gram amounts and soaking time are an adjustment. [Reel by Peter Jackson](https://www.facebook.com/reel/4522751821280480)
