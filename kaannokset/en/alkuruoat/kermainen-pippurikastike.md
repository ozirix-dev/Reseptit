# Creamy Peppercorn Sauce for Steak

A rich peppercorn sauce for steaks, pork fillet and potato sides.

## Preparation

1. Finely chop the shallot and crush the peppercorns coarsely.
2. Melt butter in a saucepan. Soften the shallot for 3 minutes and stir in pepper for 30 seconds without burning it.
3. Pour in the meat stock and cream. Simmer gently for 8–10 minutes, stirring now and then, until slightly thickened.
4. Stir in Dijon mustard and taste. Add salt only if needed, depending on the stock. Serve hot.
