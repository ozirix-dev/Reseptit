# Finnish glassmaker's pickled herring

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Use commercially prepared, ready-to-eat seasoned herring fillets, not untreated raw fish. Drain and cut into pieces.
2. Peel and thinly slice the carrot and red onion, then slice the leek.
3. Bring the water, sugar, 10% vinegar and spices to a boil. Allow the marinade to cool completely.
4. Layer the herring and vegetables in a clean container with a lid. Cover with the cooled marinade.
5. Refrigerate for approximately two days for the flavours to develop. Serve chilled. This only re-marinates ready-to-eat herring; it is not a safe method for preserving raw fish.

## Safety notes

Use ready-to-eat herring only. Maintain refrigeration and follow the fish's original use-by date. This marinade does not make untreated raw fish safe.
