# Thyme-roasted root vegetables

## Description

Carrots, parsnips and potatoes roast in a single layer with thyme and black pepper.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 225 °C. Cut the vegetables into batons about 1–1.5 cm thick.
2. Mix the oil, thyme, salt and pepper in a bowl. Toss the batons in the mixture and spread them in a single layer on baking paper.
3. Roast on the middle rack for about 25 minutes. Turn halfway through and continue until the carrots and potatoes are tender in the middle and the edges are browned.

## Notes

- Weigh the vegetables after peeling. Use sound potatoes that have not turned green.
