# Cold-Smoked Salmon Crêpe Cake with Cucumber and Fish Roe

This savoury celebration cake is assembled from thin cooked crêpes, a pale filling flavoured with cucumber and dill, and slices of smoked salmon between the layers. A generous topping of red fish roe completes the cake. The quantities have been adapted for eight servings; the original video does not specify them.

## Preparation

1. Combine both types of flour and the salt in a bowl. Whisk together the eggs, 300 ml of milk and melted butter. Gradually pour the liquid mixture into the flour and mix until smooth. Let the batter rest for approximately 30 minutes.
2. Lightly grease a non-stick frying pan approximately 18 cm in diameter with oil. Cook 9–11 crêpes from the batter, making them as thin as possible. Pour approximately 45–55 ml of batter into the pan at a time and tilt the pan to spread it. Cook until the surface has set and the edges release, then turn the crêpe over and quickly cook the other side. Allow all the crêpes to cool completely.
3. Rinse the cucumber, grate it coarsely and squeeze out excess liquid using a clean sieve or kitchen towel. Finely chop the dill. Mix the cream cheese and crème fraîche into a smooth, thick mixture. Fold in the grated cucumber and dill. Do not thin the filling with water, so that the layers hold together. Refrigerate the filling.
4. Place one cooled crêpe on a serving plate or inside a removable cake ring. Spread a thin layer of cucumber and dill filling over it and arrange slices of smoked salmon on top of the filling. Place another crêpe on top and repeat until the crêpes, filling and salmon have been used. Reserve a little filling for the top crêpe to hold the fish roe in place.
5. Cover the cake and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow it to firm up. Remove the cake ring if used. Spread a thin layer of filling over the top and spoon on the fish roe just before serving. Cut the cake into portions using a clean, sharp knife. Serve cold and refrigerate leftovers without delay.
