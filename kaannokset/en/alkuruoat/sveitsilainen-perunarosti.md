# Swiss potato rösti

Cooked potatoes are coarsely grated and pan-fried as one crisp potato cake.

## Preparation

1. Coarsely grate the chilled cooked potatoes and mix in the salt. Leave the skins on if they are clean.
2. Heat the clarified butter in a wide non-stick pan. Add the potatoes and fry over low to medium heat for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Press into one cake and fry without moving it for about 10 minutes.
3. Put a sturdy wide plate or lid over the pan. Hold it firmly and turn the rösti out onto it. Slide it back with the unbrowned side down. Fry over low heat for 12–15 minutes, until it has browned on the second side and is hot throughout. Season with pepper.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Use potatoes that were cooled promptly after cooking and kept in the refrigerator.
