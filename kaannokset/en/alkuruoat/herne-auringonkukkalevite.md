# Green pea and sunflower seed spread

Green peas make a soft spread for bread. Toasted sunflower seeds add a nutty flavor.

## Instructions

1. Cook the peas according to the package directions. Drain them, cool them quickly under cold running water, then drain thoroughly again.
2. Toast the seeds in a dry pan over medium heat, shaking the pan until they begin to color.
3. Blend the peas and seeds in a narrow bowl. Add the lemon juice and oil, blend briefly again, then season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Refrigerate the finished spread. It contains sunflower seeds, which may affect people with a seed allergy.
