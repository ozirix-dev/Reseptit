# Jiaozi dumplings with pork and cabbage filling

Homemade Chinese dumplings are filled with pork, cabbage and ginger. They can be boiled or fried until crispy.

## Description

Homemade Chinese dumplings are filled with pork, cabbage and ginger. They can be boiled or fried until crispy.

## Preparation

1. Squeeze excess liquid from the chopped cabbage. Mix the cabbage, minced meat, spring onion, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger and salt.
2. Put a teaspoon of filling in the center of the dough sheet. Moisten the edge with water, fold into a half moon and press the seam tightly. Do the same for the rest.
3. Fry the dumplings in a pan in oil for 2–3 minutes until the bottoms are browned.
4. Carefully add water and immediately cover the pan. Steam for about 6 minutes until the filling is cooked and the water has evaporated.
5. Mix soy sauce and rice wine vinegar as a dipping sauce. Serve with dumplings.

## Notes

- Keep unused dough sheets covered so they don't dry out.
