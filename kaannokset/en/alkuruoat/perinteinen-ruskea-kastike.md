# Traditional Finnish Brown Gravy

Toasted flour, butter and beef stock make a rich gravy for meatballs and mashed potatoes.

## Preparation

1. Warm the beef stock in a separate saucepan.
2. Melt the butter in a pan and add the flour. Stir over medium heat for 6–8 minutes until nut brown, without burning.
3. Gradually whisk in the warm stock. Simmer for 5 minutes until the gravy is smooth and thickened.
4. Add soy sauce and black pepper. Check the salt only at the end, as stock salt levels vary.
