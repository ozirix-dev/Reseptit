# Sicilian caponata

Caponata is a sweet and sour Sicilian eggplant stew, where celery, olives and capers add saltiness and texture.

## Description

Caponata is a sweet and sour Sicilian eggplant stew, where celery, olives and capers add saltiness and texture.

## Preparation

1. Fry the eggplant cubes in oil in batches until soft and golden brown. Set aside.
2. Sauté the onion and celery in a pan for about 8 minutes.
3. Add crushed tomatoes, olives and capers. Simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Mix in vinegar, sugar, salt and eggplant. Simmer for another 5 minutes.
5. Allow to cool to lukewarm and finish with basil. Serve with bread.

## Notes

- Caponata is flavored when it is left to cool in the fridge overnight. Bring to room temperature before serving.
