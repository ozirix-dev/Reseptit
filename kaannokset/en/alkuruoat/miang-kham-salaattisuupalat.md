# Miang kham-style lettuce bites

Romaine leaves hold toasted coconut, peanuts, dried shrimp and fresh aromatics.

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 175 °C. Enclose kapi in a small foil packet and roast about 10 min. Dry-toast sauce galangal, shallot, ginger and lemongrass until aromatic and slightly dry.
2. Pound roasted aromatics and kapi. Heat with fish sauce and palm sugar, stirring until sugar dissolves and sauce thickens slightly. Stir in chopped sauce peanuts and coconut; cool.
3. Wash and dry lettuce, chilli and lime. Peel and deseed lime, dice flesh; chop shallot and ginger. Distribute fillings and sauce over 20 leaves just before serving, or serve separately for assembly. Five leaves per portion; use chilli cautiously.

## Notes

- Contains peanuts, crustaceans and fish. Romaine is a source-supported adaptation: the original is edible Piper sarmentosum (bai cha plu), not betel or an ornamental plant. Check dried shrimp are ready to eat.
