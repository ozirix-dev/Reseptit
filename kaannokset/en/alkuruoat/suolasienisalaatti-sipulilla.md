# Finnish salted mushroom salad with red onion

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Use only edible salted milk-cap mushrooms positively identified and pre-treated according to their species. If too salty, soak briefly in cold water and drain well. Do not use false morels.
2. Squeeze excess liquid from the mushrooms; finely chop the mushrooms, red onion and dill.
3. Mix the smetana, crème fraîche and lemon juice. Fold in the mushrooms, onion, dill and black pepper.
4. Chill for about 20 minutes. Check the saltiness before serving.

## Safety notes

Species-specific pre-treatment is essential; salting or soaking alone cannot make poisonous mushrooms safe.
