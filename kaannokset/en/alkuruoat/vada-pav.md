# Vada pav – spicy potato fritter in a bun

Mumbai's popular street food dish combines a spicy, crispy deep-fried potato patty with a soft bun and chutneys.

## Description

Mumbai's popular street food dish combines a spicy, crispy deep-fried potato patty with a soft bun and chutneys.

## Preparation

1. Boil the potatoes until cooked, peel and mash.
2. Heat the oil, add the mustard seeds. When they pop, add garlic and turmeric. Mix with mashed potatoes and coriander.
3. Shape the potato mixture into 4 round patties.
4. Mix chickpea flour, water, chili and salt into a thick dough.
5. Dip the steaks in the batter and deep-fry in 170-degree oil until golden brown, about 4 minutes.
6. Split the rolls and spread chutney inside. Put the potato patty in between and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Serve with green mint chutney and sweet tamarind chutney if desired.
