# Thai fish cakes

Fish cakes seasoned with red curry paste and lime leaves are fried in small batches.

## Preparation

1. Thaw fish in the refrigerator, pat dry and check for bones. Pound or briefly pulse into a paste. Mix curry paste, starch, fish sauce, egg and sugar into a sticky mixture.
2. Remove the central veins of washed lime leaves, slice them finely and chop beans very small. Fold into paste. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour.
3. Shape roughly 12 small flat cakes. Heat oil to 170 °C in a high-sided pot of at least 2 l, no more than a quarter full. Fry in batches until browned, springy and at least 71 °C in the centre. Drain and divide into four portions.

## Notes

- Contains fish and egg. Check curry paste for crustaceans, soy and wheat. Yield and oil are editorial allowances; oil uptake has not been measured. Do not taste raw paste.
