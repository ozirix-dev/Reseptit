# Paneer tikka – grilled cheese and vegetable skewers

Pieces of paneer cheese and vegetables marinated in yogurt, lemon and Indian spices are quickly fried on the grill or under the grill resistance of the oven.

## Description

Pieces of paneer cheese and vegetables marinated in yogurt, lemon and Indian spices are quickly fried on the grill or under the grill resistance of the oven.

## Preparation

1. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, spices, salt and oil to make a marinade.
2. Turn the paneer and peppers in the marinade. Allow to season in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
3. Thread the cheese and peppers onto the skewers alternately.
4. Grill on medium heat or bake under the grill resistance of a 230-degree oven for 10–14 minutes. Turn once.
5. Serve hot with lemon wedges and mint chutney.

## Notes

- If you use wooden skewers, soak them in water for 20 minutes before grilling.
