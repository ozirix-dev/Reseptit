# Greek fava (yellow split pea purée)

Dried yellow split peas make a thick purée finished with lemon and olive oil. Makes four starter portions.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the peas in a sieve. Peel and halve the onion. Put the peas, onion, bay leaf and water in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil, skim any foam and reduce the heat. Simmer for about 45 minutes, stirring occasionally. The peas are ready when they collapse under a spoon and most of the water has been absorbed. Add a splash of hot water if needed to stop them catching.
3. Take the saucepan off the heat and remove the onion and bay leaf. Purée with an immersion blender, keeping the blade submerged. Stir in 20 ml of the olive oil, the lemon juice and salt.
4. Spread the purée on a plate and spoon over the remaining oil. Serve warm.

## Storage and safety

Use yellow split peas and follow any soaking instructions on the packet. Cool leftovers quickly in a shallow container and refrigerate.
