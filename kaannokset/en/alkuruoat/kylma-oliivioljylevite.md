# Chilled olive oil spread

## Description

Freeze olive oil in a shallow container to make a spread. Serve it cold on bread.

## Preparation

1. Pour the oil into a dry, shallow, freezer-safe container. Close the lid and freeze overnight until the oil has set.
2. Let the oil soften briefly. Spread it on the bread as soon as it is soft enough to spread.
3. Return any leftover spread to cold storage in its lidded container. If it turns liquid, put it back in the freezer to set again.

## Notes

- One serving is about 10 g of spread. The total recipe time is an estimate because freezing time depends on the oil and the thickness of the layer.
