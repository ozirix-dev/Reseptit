# Tomato and ginger drink

Fresh ginger, onion and lemon give this chilled tomato drink a sharp kick. Serve it in small glasses as a starter.

## Instructions

1. Rinse the ginger before peeling it. Peel and weigh the onion, garlic and ginger, then chop them finely.
2. Put the passata, water and lemon juice in a blender. Add the chopped ingredients, turmeric, chilli, pepper and salt. Blend for about 30 seconds, until the onion is finely broken down.
3. Drizzle in the olive oil in a thin stream and blend briefly. Pour into two glasses and serve immediately. If serving later, cover and refrigerate the drink, then stir before serving.

## Notes

- Use a clean blender and cold liquids. Refrigerate the drink if serving later.
