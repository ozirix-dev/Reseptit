# Open-faced roast beef sandwich with horseradish sauce

## Description

Cold roast beef slices, horseradish sour cream sauce and arugula are assembled into open rye breads. Pickles, capers and onion bring acidity and crunch.

## Instructions

1. Cut [mature, chilled roast beef](https://ozirix-dev.github.io/Reseptit/naudan-paahtopaisti-uunissa/) into thin slices and weigh 240 g. Place four slices of rye bread on plates. You can toast the bases lightly. Let them cool before filling.
2. Mix sour cream, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard and lemon juice. First grate 12 g of horseradish. Taste and add more grated if desired up to a maximum of 20 grams. Season with black pepper.
3. Rinse and dry the arugula. Thinly slice the pickled cucumber and red onion. Drain the pickles and capers.
4. Spread the sauce on the bottoms and add arugula. Fold 60 g of roast beef onto each piece of bread. Finish with pickles, onions and capers. Offer now.

## Notes

- Contains rye (gluten), milk, egg and mustard. Check the packaging labels of bread and ready-made products.
- The total time refers to the assembly of ready-made soles and mature, cold meat. Store the roast and sauce in the refrigerator until ready to assemble.
- Try the same fillings [with an oat bran roll](https://ozirix-dev.github.io/Reseptit/paahtopaistileivat-kauralesesampyloilla/).
