# Traditional Finnish Rosolli Beetroot Salad

A traditional Finnish Christmas salad of diced root vegetables and apple, with pink cream dressing served separately.

## Preparation

1. Use precooked, cooled root vegetables. If starting raw, boil until tender, cool quickly in shallow containers and peel. Weigh the edible cooked vegetables; drain beetroot and remove the apple core.
2. Dice the vegetables, pickled cucumber, apple and onion into small, even pieces.
3. Mix the salad gently and season with salt and white pepper. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
4. Whip the cold cream to soft peaks. Stir in vinegar and sugar; if desired, mash one small cooked beetroot cube from the salad into the dressing. Serve separately and keep both salad and cream dressing refrigerated below +6°C until serving.

## Notes

- Check packaging for allergens; no gluten-free claim has been reviewed.
- Preparation comparison reference (not reproduced): https://www.martat.fi/reseptit/rosolli/

- The 50-minute estimate assumes the root vegetables are already cooked and cooled. Cooking and cooling raw beetroot quickly adds time. The cream dressing contains milk; use cold cream and clean utensils.
