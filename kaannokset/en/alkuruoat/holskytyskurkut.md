# Finnish shaken dill cucumbers

## Description

Thin cucumber slices are dressed with vinegar, sugar and fresh dill.

## Preparation

1. Wash the cucumber and slice it very thinly. Put the slices in a container with a lid and sprinkle with the salt.
2. Close the container and shake until the cucumber releases a little juice. Add the vinegar, sugar and chopped dill. Shake again until the sugar dissolves.
3. Chill for about 10 minutes. Serve cold the same day.
