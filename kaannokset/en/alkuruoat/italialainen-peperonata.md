# Italian stewed peppers (peperonata)

## Description

Sweet peppers slowly soften with onion and tomatoes.

## Preparation

1. Deseed and slice the peppers. Slice the onion and chop the tomatoes.
2. Soften the onion in the oil in a lidded frying pan for about 8 minutes. Add the peppers and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Add the tomatoes, salt and pepper. Cover and simmer gently for about 25 minutes. Uncover and cook for another 10–15 minutes, until the peppers are soft and the sauce is slightly thick.
