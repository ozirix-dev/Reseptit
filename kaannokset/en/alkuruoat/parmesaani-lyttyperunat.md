# Parmesan smashed potatoes

## Description

Boiled potatoes are pressed flat and roasted with olive oil, garlic and parmesan. Leave a little space between the potatoes on the tray so that the edges crisp up.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C with constant heat. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper suitable for this temperature.
2. Dry the surface of the potatoes and spread them on a tray. Flatten each potato with the bottom of a glass to a thickness of about 1-1.5 cm.
3. Brush the potatoes with olive oil. Sprinkle salt, garlic powder and grated Parmesan on top.
4. Roast on medium for about 30-35 minutes, until the edges are golden brown and crispy. Start checking at 25 minutes.

## Notes

- Contains milk.
- The total time is calculated from pre-cooked and refrigerated potatoes. If you cook them in advance, drain them, divide into a shallow dish and immediately transfer to the refrigerator to cool.
