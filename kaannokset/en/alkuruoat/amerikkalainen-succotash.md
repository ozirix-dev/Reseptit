# American corn and lima bean succotash

## Description

Corn and lima beans cook in a skillet with sweet red pepper.

## Preparation

1. Chop the onion and dice the pepper. Soften them in the oil for about 5 minutes.
2. Add the frozen corn, lima beans and water. Bring to a boil and cook until the beans and corn are tender, following package directions.
3. Let excess water evaporate uncovered. Season with salt and pepper and serve warm.
