# Cucumber som tam – weighed version

## Description

Cucumber som tam – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Weigh all listed edible ingredients. Do not add unlisted oil or side dishes.
2. Remove the cucumber seed core, then weigh 500 g edible cucumber. Weigh 100 g already cooked and drained green beans; do not use raw beans. Cut the tomato.
3. Crush the garlic and chilli. Coarsely chop half the whole roasted peanuts; do not grind them into a paste. Add lime juice, fish sauce and 4 g Foodin erythritol.
4. Combine all vegetables and dressing, then add the remaining peanuts. Consume all dressing and divide into four portions.
