# Roasted beet carpaccio with goat cheese

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200 °C. Wash beets, wrap in foil or place in a covered oven dish and roast for 45–60 minutes, depending on size, until tender.
2. Cool until safe to handle, peel and slice very thinly.
3. Mix olive oil, apple cider vinegar, honey and salt. Arrange the slices on a plate and drizzle with dressing.
4. Crumble soft goat cheese over the beetroot, add walnuts and thyme. Serve cool or after a short rest at room temperature.

## Safety notes

Check goat cheese pasteurisation and storage especially for risk groups. Contains milk and walnuts.
