# Dill-cured Finnish whitefish

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Choose whitefish suitable for raw consumption and follow the package instructions. If parasite-control freezing has not been carried out and no applicable exemption exists, follow the Finnish Food Authority's guidance before curing: at or below −20 °C for at least 24 hours, −35 °C for at least 15 hours, or at −18 °C for at least 96 hours, counted after the required temperature is reached. If raw-use suitability or treatment cannot be established, do not serve uncooked.
2. Combine the coarse sea salt, sugar and white pepper. Distribute over the fillet and cover with dill.
3. Cover in a food-safe container and cure at 0–3 °C for about 24 hours, turning once. Curing does not replace parasite-control freezing.
4. Remove excess salt and herbs, then slice thinly just before serving.

## Safety notes

Use whitefish suitable for raw consumption, observe Finnish Food Authority freezing requirements and follow the product's package and storage instructions. No manufacturer letter is automatically required. A frozen label or cooking-only instruction is not proof of suitability for raw use. Salting and freezing do not eliminate listeria; people at increased listeria risk should avoid uncooked cured fish.
