# Dill-cured Finnish whitefish

Draft English adaptation for editorial review, not a tested Recipavo recipe.

## Instructions

1. Before starting, verify that the boneless whitefish fillet is suitable for raw consumption and that legally required parasite-control freezing has been performed. If uncertain, do not serve uncooked.
2. Combine the coarse sea salt, sugar and white pepper. Distribute over the fillet and cover with dill.
3. Cover in a food-safe container and cure at 0–3 °C for about 24 hours, turning once. Curing does not replace parasite-control freezing.
4. Remove excess salt and herbs, then slice thinly just before serving.

## Safety notes

HOLD pending verification of fish provenance, parasite-control treatment and cold-chain handling. Salting does not kill parasites. Raw cured fish is unsuitable for people at increased listeria risk.
