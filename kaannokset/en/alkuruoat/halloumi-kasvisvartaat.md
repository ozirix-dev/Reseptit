# Halloumi and vegetable skewers

Lemon zest and mint season halloumi, courgette, pepper and tomatoes. The skewers cook under the oven grill.

## Preparation

1. Wash the vegetables and lemon. Cut the halloumi and pepper into bite-sized pieces about 2 cm wide. Halve the courgette lengthwise and slice roughly 5 mm thick. Leave the tomatoes whole.
2. Finely chop the mint leaves. Mix the mint, lemon zest, olive oil and black pepper in a bowl, then toss with the cheese and vegetables.
3. Preheat the oven grill to high. Thread alternating pieces of cheese and vegetables onto six metal skewers and arrange them on a baking tray.
4. Grill for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway through, until the vegetables soften and the cheese is golden. Lift the hot skewers off the tray with tongs.

## Notes

- Contains milk. For a vegetarian version, use halloumi made with vegetarian rennet.
