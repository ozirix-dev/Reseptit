# Traditional Finnish White Milk Sauce

A simple butter-and-milk white sauce for fish, potatoes and vegetables.

## Preparation

1. Measure out the milk. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Stir flour into the melted butter until smooth and cook for one minute without browning.
3. Gradually whisk in milk to prevent lumps. Bring to a gentle boil and simmer for 4–5 minutes.
4. Season with salt, white pepper and a little nutmeg. Serve warm with cooked fish or vegetables.
