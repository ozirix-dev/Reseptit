# Stracciatella alla romana, Roman egg drop soup

Beaten egg and cheese set in thin ribbons when poured into hot stock. Lemon zest and nutmeg season the broth.

## Preparation

1. Heat the stock in a saucepan until it simmers gently. Wash and dry the lemon and grate only the yellow part of the peel.
2. Beat the eggs and mix in the cheese, nutmeg, zest and pepper. Pour in a thin stream into the hot stock, stirring gently.
3. Simmer until the egg ribbons are firm throughout. Cook longer if needed and check for at least 72 °C with a food thermometer. Divide among four bowls and serve hot.

## Storage and safety

Contains egg and milk. Check the stock label for allergens such as celery. Wash utensils that touched the raw mixture. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
