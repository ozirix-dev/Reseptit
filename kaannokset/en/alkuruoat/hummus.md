# Creamy chickpea hummus

Smooth hummus is made from chickpeas, tahini, lemon and garlic. Serve as a bread or vegetable dip.

## Description

Smooth hummus is made from chickpeas, tahini, lemon and garlic. Serve as a bread or vegetable dip.

## Preparation

1. Place the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic and salt in a food processor.
2. Puree and add cold water a spoonful at a time until the hummus is smooth and fluffy.
3. Taste and adjust lemon or salt.
4. Spread in a serving dish and pour olive oil on top. Sprinkle paprika on top.

## Notes

- If you want a particularly smooth hummus, peel the chickpeas before pureeing.
