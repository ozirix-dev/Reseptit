# Japanese sesame spinach (goma-ae)

## Description

Quickly blanched spinach is tossed with a toasted sesame dressing.

## Preparation

1. Wash the spinach. Boil it in plenty of water for about one minute. Cool immediately in cold water, drain and squeeze out the excess water.
2. Toast the sesame seeds lightly in a dry pan and crush them with a mortar and pestle. Mix in the soy sauce and sugar.
3. Cut the spinach into lengths of about 4 cm. Loosen the pieces and toss them in the dressing.
