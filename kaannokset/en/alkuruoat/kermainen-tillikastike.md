# Creamy Finnish Dill Sauce for Fish

A cool, fresh dill sauce for baked fish, fried fish and new potatoes.

## Preparation

1. Rinse and dry the dill, then finely chop the leaves.
2. Mix the cultured sour cream, dill, lemon juice, sugar, salt and pepper until even.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes before serving cold with cooked fish.
