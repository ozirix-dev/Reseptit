# Basque Gilda skewers

## Description

Salty anchovy, green olive and pickled chilli make this Basque pintxo.

## Preparation

1. Drain the pitted olives, chillies and anchovies. Stem the chillies and cut them into eight pieces. Divide the anchovies into eight pieces.
2. Thread an olive, a piece of chilli and a folded anchovy onto each of eight cocktail picks. Finish each with a second olive.
3. Drizzle with the oil. Serve immediately or keep refrigerated until serving. Allow two picks per serving.
