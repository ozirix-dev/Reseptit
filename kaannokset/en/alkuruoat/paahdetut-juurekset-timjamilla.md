# Thyme-Roasted Root Vegetables

Carrots, swede and parsnips roast on one tray. Cut the swede a little smaller so everything becomes tender at the same time.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 225 °C. Cut the carrots and parsnips into roughly 2 cm pieces and the swede a little smaller.
2. Toss the vegetables with the oil, thyme and salt. Spread them on a lined baking tray in a single layer with space between the pieces.
3. Roast for 15 minutes, turn the pieces with a spatula and roast for about 15 minutes more. Remove the tray when a fork goes through the swede easily.
