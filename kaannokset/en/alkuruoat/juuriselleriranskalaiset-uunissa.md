# Oven-baked celeriac fries

Thick celeriac sticks are briefly boiled before roasting so they brown outside and soften inside.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 230 °C. Trim the ends of the celeriac and peel it with a sharp knife on a stable board. Cut it into thick sticks.
2. Bring water to a boil in a large saucepan and boil the sticks for 1–2 minutes. Drain well and toss with the oil, curry powder and salt.
3. Spread the sticks out on a tray or two so there is space between them. Roast for about 30–35 minutes, until browned outside and tender inside. Serve warm.

## Notes

- Contains celery. Check the curry blend for allergens such as mustard.
