# Open-faced roast beef on oat bran rolls

## Description

Cold roast beef slices, horseradish sour cream sauce and arugula are assembled into open rolls. Pickles, capers and onion bring acidity and crunch.

## Instructions

1. Cut [mature, chilled roast beef](https://ozirix-dev.github.io/Reseptit/naudan-paahtopaisti-uunissa/) into thin slices and weigh 240 g. Cut the cooled oat bran rolls in half and place the halves cut side up. You can toast the bases lightly. Let them cool before filling.
2. Mix sour cream, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard and lemon juice. First grate 12 g of horseradish. Taste and add more grated if desired up to a maximum of 20 grams. Season with black pepper.
3. Rinse and dry the arugula. Thinly slice the pickled cucumber and red onion. Drain the pickles and capers.
4. Spread the sauce on the bottoms and add arugula. Fold 60 g of roast beef onto each piece of bread. Finish with pickles, onions and capers. Offer now.

## Notes

- Contains oats, almonds, milk, egg and mustard. If you use sesame seeds in the rolls, they are also an allergen.
- The total time refers to the assembly of ready-made soles and mature, cold meat. Store the roast and sauce in the fridge until ready to assemble.
- They are the basis [oat bran rolls](https://ozirix-dev.github.io/Reseptit/keto-kauralesesampylat/). The same fillings are also suitable [for rye bread](https://ozirix-dev.github.io/Reseptit/paahtopaistileipa-piparjuurikastikkeella/).
- Roast beef and horseradish medley are based on a traditional combination. [Valio's comparison guide](https://www.valio.fi/reseptit/paahtopaisti-ja-piparjuurismetana/) · [Roast beef from Uotila as a cold cut](https://www.uotilat.fi/reseptit/paahtopaisti-leikkeleeksi)
