# Peruvian broad bean and corn salad (solterito)

## Description

Broad beans, corn and fresh cheese are tossed in a tangy dressing.

## Preparation

1. Cook the broad beans and corn according to their packages. Drain, cool quickly and remove any thick broad-bean skins if needed.
2. Dice the tomatoes and cheese. Chop the red onion and parsley. Drain and slice the olives.
3. Combine everything in a bowl. Add vinegar, oil and salt. Serve cold or refrigerate until serving.
