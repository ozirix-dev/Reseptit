# Turkish red lentil soup (mercimek çorbası)

## Description

Carrot, red lentils and cumin make a warming soup brightened with lemon.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the lentils and rice. Chop the onion and dice the peeled carrot and potato. Soften the vegetables in the oil for about 3 minutes.
2. Add the lentils, rice and water. Bring to a boil and simmer for about 35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender.
3. Lower the heat and add the butter, cumin, salt and lemon juice. Blend with a stick blender, keeping the blade submerged.
