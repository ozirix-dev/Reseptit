# Creamy parsnip soup

Potato and onion balance the sweetness of the parsnips. Blend until smooth and finish with cream.

## Instructions

1. Dice the parsnips and potatoes. Finely chop the onion and garlic.
2. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan. Add the vegetables and salt. Cook for about 20 minutes, until the potatoes and parsnips break apart easily under a fork.
3. Take the saucepan off the heat. Blend with an immersion blender, keeping the blade submerged. Stir in the cream and heat the soup again until steaming.
4. Season with pepper and divide among four bowls.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Take care to avoid splashing the hot soup as you blend. Use potatoes without green areas.
