# Mizeria, Polish cucumber salad with sour cream

Thin cucumber slices are coated in a sour cream dressing with dill and lemon.

## Preparation

1. Wash and peel the cucumber. Slice it thinly and mix with the salt. Let the slices drain in a colander for about 10 minutes.
2. Rinse and chop the dill. Mix the sour cream, dill, lemon juice and pepper in a bowl.
3. Drain the cucumber well and fold the slices into the dressing. Serve cool or refrigerate covered until serving.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk. Use sour cream made from pasteurised milk. Wash the produce and utensils. Keep the salad refrigerated.
