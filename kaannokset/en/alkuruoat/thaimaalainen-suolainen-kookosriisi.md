# Thai savoury coconut rice

## Description

Jasmine rice is cooked in coconut milk for a savoury side dish.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice several times in cold water and drain well.
2. Combine the rice, coconut milk, water and salt in a lidded saucepan. Bring gently to a simmer, stir once, cover and cook on the lowest heat for about 15 minutes until the liquid has been absorbed.
3. Remove from the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork and serve hot.
