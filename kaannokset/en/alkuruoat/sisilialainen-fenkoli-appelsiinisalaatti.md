# Sicilian fennel and orange salad

Paper-thin fennel, membrane-free orange segments and black olives are dressed with juice saved from the oranges.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the fennel and oranges. Trim the dry bases and tough stalks from the fennel. Save the tender fronds. Halve the bulbs and slice them thinly.
2. Cut away the orange peel and white pith with a knife. Working over a bowl to catch the juice, cut the segments away from the membranes. Squeeze any remaining juice from the membranes into the bowl.
3. Halve the olives. Mix the fennel, orange segments and olives in a serving dish. Whisk the reserved juice with the oil and salt and toss through the salad. Finish with the fennel fronds and divide into six servings.

## Storage and safety

Check the olive label for ingredients and allergens. Keep the prepared salad cold and serve promptly so the fennel stays crisp.
