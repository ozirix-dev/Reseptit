# Beef, pork and root vegetable stew with buckwheat

Apple and dark beer flavour the braising liquid. A few mashed vegetables thicken the stew, and buckwheat is cooked separately.

## Preparation

1. Halve the shallots. Cut the apple and root vegetables into roughly 2 cm pieces. Slice the mushrooms. Divide the apple and root vegetables into two equal batches.
2. Heat 10 ml of the oil in a lidded casserole. Brown the meat in two batches and transfer it to a plate. Soften the shallots in the casserole for a couple of minutes. Return the meat, add the water and beer, then scrape up the browned bits.
3. Add half the apple and root vegetables, along with the salt, pepper and thyme. Bring to the boil, reduce the heat and cover. Simmer gently for about 2 hours. Mash a few tender pieces of root vegetable with a fork and stir them back into the stew.
4. Fry the sliced mushrooms and remaining apple in the last 5 ml of oil for a few minutes. Add them and the remaining root vegetables to the stew. Simmer for 20–30 minutes more, until the vegetables are tender and the meat yields easily to a fork. Check that the largest pieces of pork have reached at least 70 °C in the centre.
5. Rinse the buckwheat. In a separate saucepan, cook it in 600 ml water for the time given on the packet. Drain any excess water. Divide the buckwheat and stew between six plates.

## Storage and safety

Contains celery and gluten from standard dark beer. Handle raw meat separately from cooked food and wash the equipment after use. Cool leftovers promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
