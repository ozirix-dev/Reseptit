# Meatballs in cream sauce and cauliflower mash

Succulent Finnish meatballs are accompanied by peppery cream sauce and buttery cauliflower mash.

## Description

In this VHH version, the breadcrumbs have been replaced with a small amount of almond flour and the mashed potatoes with cauliflower. Season the salt sparingly, as the meat broth also affects the saltiness of the sauce.

## Preparation

1. Mix the almond flour and the meatball dough cream. Let it swell for 5 minutes.
2. Chop the onion. Add minced meat, egg, onion, salt, black pepper and allspice to the bowl. Mix just enough to combine the ingredients.
3. Roll the dough into small meatballs. Fry them in butter in a pan in several batches until brown and cooked all over. Transfer to a plate.
4. Pour the meat broth and whipping cream into the pan. Bring to a boil and cook slowly for 5-8 minutes until the sauce thickens. Return the meatballs to the pan to warm up.
5. Chop the cauliflower and cook in salted water until soft, about 10–12 minutes. Drain well and let the steam evaporate for a while.
6. Puree the cauliflower with butter until smooth. Season and serve with meatballs.

## Notes

- You should drain the cauliflower properly before pureeing, so that the mash does not become watery.
- If you wish, offer a small amount of lingonberries or a fresh salad.
