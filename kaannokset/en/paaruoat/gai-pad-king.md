# Gai pad king, Thai ginger chicken

Matchsticks of ginger give this quick stir-fry its bite. The mushrooms stay pleasantly firm. Oyster sauce and fermented soybeans flavour the sauce.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the dried mushrooms under running water. Put them in a clean bowl, cover with hot water and soak for about 15 minutes or until softened. Follow any more specific soaking and cooking directions on the packet. Drain, trim the tough bases and use the mushrooms straight away.
2. Cut the ginger and red pepper into thin strips. Slice the onion into thin wedges, finely chop the garlic and chop the spring onions. Use a separate board to cut the chicken into strips, then toss with 10 ml of the soy sauce.
3. Mix the sauce water, oyster sauce, tao jiew, the remaining 10 ml soy sauce, sugar, toasted sesame oil and white pepper in a bowl.
4. Heat the rapeseed oil in a wok or large frying pan. Stir the garlic in the oil just until fragrant. Turn up the heat, add the chicken and stir-fry until the outsides of the strips turn pale.
5. Pour in the sauce, then add the ginger, mushrooms, onion and red pepper. Stir-fry until the vegetables are still firm and the thickest chicken pieces reach at least 75 °C in the centre. Mix in the spring onions and divide into three servings. Serve with separately cooked rice if desired.

## Storage and safety

Contains soy, sesame and molluscs from the oyster sauce. Check the sauce labels for wheat and other allergens. Keep raw chicken away from other ingredients and wash the utensils used to handle it. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
