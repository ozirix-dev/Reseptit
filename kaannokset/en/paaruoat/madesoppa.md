# Finnish burbot soup

Whole burbot flavours the broth before the boneless pieces are warmed in a creamy potato soup.

## Preparation

1. Bring the water, salt and bay leaves to a boil. Add the gutted fish whole or in large pieces. Skim any foam and add the allspice.
2. Simmer the fish for about 15 minutes, or longer if needed, until it is cooked through and the flesh comes away from the bones. Lift it out and strain the broth.
3. Halve and rinse the leek. Chop the leek and peeled onion, slice the carrots and dice the peeled potatoes. Soften the onion and leek in butter, then add the carrots, potatoes and strained broth. Simmer for about 15 minutes or until the vegetables are tender.
4. Lift the flesh from the bones and check each piece carefully. Add the fish, cream and chopped dill to the soup and heat through.

## Notes

- Contains fish and milk. See the Finnish Food Authority for advice on eating fish where relevant.
