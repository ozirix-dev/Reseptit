# Finnish milk and cabbage soup

Thin strips of white cabbage cook until soft in a mild milk broth, finished with a little butter.

## Preparation

1. Slice the cabbage into thin strips. Bring a separate pan of water to a boil, blanch the cabbage for about a minute and drain it in a colander. Discard the blanching water.
2. Bring 500 ml water for the broth to a boil. Add the drained cabbage and simmer gently for about 15 minutes until tender.
3. Set aside 50 ml of the milk. Whisk the flour into this cold milk until smooth. Add the remaining milk to the cabbage and heat gently, stirring. Stream the flour mixture into the simmering broth and cook over low heat for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in the butter and salt. Divide into four bowls and serve immediately.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk and wheat. Cool leftovers quickly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
