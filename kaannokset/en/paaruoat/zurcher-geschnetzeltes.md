# Zürcher Geschnetzeltes, Swiss veal strips with mushrooms

Swiss pan-fried veal and mushrooms are finished in a creamy white-wine sauce. Serve with rösti if you like.

## Preparation

1. Pat the meat dry. Heat a wide pan and use 20 ml of the clarified butter to brown the veal in batches, turning each batch for 2–3 minutes. Dust the meat with the flour towards the end, stir and transfer to a clean plate. Season with half the salt.
2. Reduce the heat and add the remaining 10 ml of clarified butter. Cook the onion for 2 minutes, then add the mushrooms and fry for about 5 minutes, until most of their liquid has evaporated.
3. Pour in the wine and reduce until nearly evaporated. Whisk the cream, cold meat stock and cornstarch together. Pour into the pan, bring to a boil and simmer for about 3 minutes, stirring.
4. Return the veal and its juices to the sauce. Cook until the thickest strips reach at least 71 °C in the centre. Add half the parsley, the remaining salt and the pepper. Serve in four portions topped with the remaining parsley.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk and wheat. Check the stock and wine labels for allergens, particularly celery and sulphites. Keep utensils used for raw meat separate from cooked food. Cool leftovers rapidly in small containers and refrigerate.
