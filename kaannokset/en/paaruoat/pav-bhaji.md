# Pav bhaji – Mumbai spiced vegetable mash

A spiced mash of potatoes, cauliflower and peas is served with soft wheat rolls.

## Description

A spiced mash of potatoes, cauliflower and peas is served with soft wheat rolls.

## Preparation

1. Boil potatoes, cauliflower, peas and carrot until tender. Drain and reserve the cooking water.

2. Sauté onion in oil. Add garlic and the measured cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric and chili; fry briefly.

3. Add tomatoes and salt. Simmer for 10 minutes.

4. Add vegetables and mash roughly in the pan. Thin with a little reserved cooking water if needed.

5. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Mix in half the butter and coriander.

6. Toast the soft rolls with the remaining butter and serve with the vegetable mash.

## Notes

- The spice quantities are listed individually above.
