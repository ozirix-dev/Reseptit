# Finnish beef in dill sauce

Slow-cooked beef shoulder in a light, dill-flecked sauce made with its cooking broth.

## Preparation

1. Put the beef in a pot with enough cold water to cover it. Bring gently to a boil and skim the foam.
2. Add the roughly chopped carrot and onion, dill stems, white pepper and salt according to the amount of water used. Cover and simmer gently for about 2 hours, until the beef is cooked through and fork-tender.
3. Lift out the beef and cut it into bite-sized pieces. Strain the broth and measure 500 ml into a saucepan. Whisk the flour into the cold water, mix it into the hot broth and simmer the sauce for a few minutes.
4. Season with sugar and vinegar. Return the beef to the sauce and heat through. Finish with the chopped dill leaves.
