# Pan-fried tofu in orange and ginger sauce

Cornstarch-coated tofu is browned in a pan, then tossed with vegetables in an orange and ginger sauce.

## Preparation

1. Cut the tofu into pieces about 2.5 cm across. Pat off excess moisture with kitchen paper. Wash the orange. Mix the ginger, orange zest and juice, soy sauce, agave, vinegar, crushed garlic and chilli flakes in a bowl.
2. Slice the courgette, pepper and spring onions, separating the pale and green parts of the onions. Rinse the snow peas and drain the water chestnuts.
3. Toss the tofu with the cornstarch and salt. Heat the oil in a large frying pan. Cook the tofu for 2–3 minutes on each side until browned, then transfer it to a plate.
4. Cook the pale spring onion pieces, courgette, pepper and snow peas in the pan for 4–6 minutes. Add the water chestnuts, tofu and sauce. Stir over the heat for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened. Top with the spring onion greens.

## Notes

- Contains soy. Check the soy sauce label for wheat and the vinegar label for sulphites.
