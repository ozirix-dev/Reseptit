# Low-carb pad see ew with chicken and konjac noodles

The salty-sweet Thai wok gets its flavor from soy sauces, egg and vegetables fried in a hot pan. konjac noodles replace the traditional wide rice noodles.

## Description

Pad see ew is best fried in a hot pan in small batches. Dry the konjac noodles well so that no excess liquid accumulates in the wok.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the noodles thoroughly and drain. Dry fry them in a hot pan for 3–4 minutes. Move aside.
2. Mix the soy sauces, oyster sauce and erythritol in a small bowl.
3. Heat half the oil in a wok. Fry the chicken strips until cooked and set aside.
4. Add the rest of the oil and garlic. Crack the eggs into the pan and mix quickly.
5. Add the vegetables and fry until crisp. Add the noodles, chicken and sauce. Turn over high heat for a couple of minutes.
6. Season with white pepper and serve immediately.

## Notes

- This is a low-carb variation of the pad see ew dish; konjac noodles do not match the texture of rice noodles.
- Check the carbohydrates of oyster and soy sauces on the package.
