# Oven-roasted beef

## Description

Brown the whole roast beef in a pan and cook it in a moderate oven using a cooking gauge. Cut in half and serve warm or cool the roast for loaves.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 125°C with constant heat. Dry the surface of an intact whole piece of meat with a paper towel and rub salt and black pepper on the surface.
2. Heat the rapeseed oil in a pan and brown the roast all over, including the ends. At the end, add butter, crushed garlic cloves and thyme. Grease the surface of the roast for a while with pan grease.
3. Transfer the roast to the baking dish. Insert the tip of the thermometer into the center of the thickest part. Bake on the middle level of the oven until the thermometer reads at least 63 °C. You can set aside 1½–2½ hours for a roast of about one kilo. The shape and starting temperature affect the time.
4. Let rest loosely covered for about 15-20 minutes before cutting. Remove the thyme sprigs and cut the meat crosswise into thin slices.
5. If you serve the roast cold, after it has rested, transfer it to a clean shallow container and cool it immediately in the refrigerator. You can use clean utensils to divide a thick roast into smaller pieces to speed up cooling. Only cut the thin slices after the meat has cooled.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Use whole, whole cut roast beef. Wash hands, utensils and cutting board that have touched raw meat before handling cooked meat. [The Food Agency's meat handling instructions](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/elintarvikkeet/ohjeita-kuluttajille/kasittely-ja-sailyttaminen/hygienia-kotikeittiossa/liha/)
- The gauge target of 63 °C for a roast beef and a rest of at least three minutes are based on FoodSafety.gov guidelines. If meat is recommended for the diner, prepare a portion cooked all over. [Cooking temperatures](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures)
- Oven temperature, browning and slicing are based on the corresponding roasting instructions. [Heart sign](https://ammattilaiset.sydanmerkki.fi/resepti/paahtopaisti) · [Champion](https://www.valio.fi/reseptit/paahtopaisti-ja-piparjuurismetana/)
