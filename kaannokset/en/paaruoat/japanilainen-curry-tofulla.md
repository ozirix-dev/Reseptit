# Japanese tofu curry with cauliflower rice

## Description

A warm, spiced Japanese curry broth brings together tofu and vegetables. Serve with steamed cauliflower rice.

## Preparation

1. Pat the tofu dry and cut it into cubes. Brown it in oil in a frying pan, then set it aside.
2. Sauté the sliced onion. Add the diced carrot and courgette and the vegetable stock. Simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Stir in the curry roux and cook according to the package directions until the broth thickens. Add the tofu and heat through.
4. Grate the cauliflower to resemble rice and steam or pan-fry for 5 minutes.
5. Serve the tofu curry with the cauliflower rice.

## Notes

Japanese curry roux products often contain wheat and sugar. Check the ingredients on the package. You can make your own roux with suitable spices and a thickener.
