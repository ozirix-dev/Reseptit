# Shoyu chicken soup with konjac noodles

## Description

A weighed chicken soup with shoyu seasoning and konjac noodles. Makes four servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredient amounts are shown in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Bring the weighed, ready-to-use chicken stock to a boil. Cook the chicken in it until thoroughly cooked through.
2. Rinse and drain the konjac noodles, then weigh **200 g of the ready-to-use drained product**. Add the noodles, mushrooms, spring onions and ginger to the soup.
3. Add all the soy sauce, vinegar and sesame oil. Divide and eat all the broth and ingredients in four servings.
