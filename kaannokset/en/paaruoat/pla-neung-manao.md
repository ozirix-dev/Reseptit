# Thai steamed fish with lime and garlic

A whole steamed white fish is dressed with a sharp, aromatic lime sauce.

## Preparation

1. Check the fish is scaled and gutted; pat dry and score the sides. Split lemongrass and place in the cavity. Set fish on a heatproof plate in a covered steamer above boiling water.
2. Steam for about 15–20 min or longer until the thickest part reaches at least 63 °C. Open the lid away from your face.
3. Wash herbs and chillies; crush garlic, chillies and coriander roots. Mix in palm sugar, fish sauce and lime juice. Transfer fish, remove lemongrass, spoon over sauce and scatter coriander and celery. Divide into three, watching for bones.

## Notes

- Contains fish and celery. Whole weight is not edible fillet weight. Rice is not included.
