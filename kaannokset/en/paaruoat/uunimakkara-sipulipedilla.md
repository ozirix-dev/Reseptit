# Baked sausage with onion and mustard topping

## Description

Traditional oven-baked sausage is topped with a creamy onion and mustard mixture and melted cheese.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Cut lengthwise slits in the sausage and place it in a greased baking dish.
2. Finely chop the onion and sauté it in butter until soft. Mix the crème fraîche, mustard, paprika and pepper into the onion.
3. Spread the mixture over the sausage and sprinkle with grated cheddar.
4. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the sausage is hot and the cheese has browned.
5. Let cool briefly before slicing.

## Notes

Sausage composition varies. Check the nutrition label if you track carbohydrates.
