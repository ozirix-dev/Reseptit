# Thai grilled pork salad

Grilled pork is tossed with lime, herbs and toasted rice.

## Preparation

1. Cut pork into roughly 2 cm steaks. Mix marinade oyster sauce, fish sauce and lime; marinate covered in the fridge for 2 h.
2. Discard excess marinade. Grill over medium heat for about 5–8 min per side until the thickest point reaches 70 °C. Rest 10 min on a clean plate.
3. Wash and chop herbs. Mix shallot, dressing fish sauce, lime, pepper, sugar and chilli. Slice pork across the grain and toss with dressing. Add herbs and toasted rice powder; divide into four.

## Notes

- Contains fish and molluscs, possibly soy and wheat. Steak thickness is a home-kitchen adaptation. Extra sticky rice is not included.
