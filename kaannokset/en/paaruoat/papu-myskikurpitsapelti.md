# Butternut squash and white bean tray bake

## Description

Butternut squash, Brussels sprouts and white beans roast on one tray. A cool lemon and soy yoghurt dressing goes alongside.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the prepared squash into slices just under 1 cm thick. Trim the tough bases from the sprouts and halve them.
2. Toss the vegetables with oil, salt and pepper on a baking tray. Spread in one layer and roast for 25 minutes.
3. Rinse and drain the beans. Add them and the pecans to the tray. Roast for another 5–8 minutes, until the beans are hot, the nuts are toasted and the squash is tender.
4. Mix the soy yoghurt with the lemon juice and zest from a washed lemon. Serve the dressing with the roasted vegetables.

## Notes

- Use cooked canned beans. Contains soy and pecans.
