# Kebab omelette roll

## Description

A soft baked omelette is rolled around pan-fried kebab, crisp lettuce, tomato, cucumber and red onion. Görans kebab and garlic sauces flavour the filling and top the roll alongside mild pepperoncini and flat-leaf parsley.

## Ingredients

See the serving calculator for the ingredients. Use the first four ingredients for the omelette base. The kebab, oil, vegetables and half of each sauce go into the filling. Use the remaining sauces, pepperoncini and parsley to finish the roll.

## Preparation

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them until the whites and yolks are combined. Add the milk, flour and salt. Whisk until smooth and let the batter rest for 20 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 225 °C with conventional top and bottom heat.
2. Line a rimmed baking tray with baking paper. Stir the batter once more, reaching down to the bottom of the bowl, then pour it evenly into the tray. Bake on the middle rack for about 15 minutes, until the omelette is set throughout and lightly coloured. Take care not to bake it until dry.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan while the omelette bakes. Fry the cooked kebab slices until hot, about 4 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
4. Rinse and dry the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber and parsley. Peel and thinly slice the red onion. Dice the tomatoes and cucumber and shred the lettuce. Drain the pepperoncini and chop the parsley.
5. Let the omelette settle briefly until it is easy to handle. Loosen the edges from the paper. Spread the kebab over the base and drizzle with half of the kebab sauce. Add the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber and red onion, followed by half of the garlic sauce.
6. Use the baking paper to roll the omelette up tightly from a short side, then transfer it to a serving dish seam side down. Finish with the remaining sauces, pepperoncini and parsley. Divide into five servings and serve.

## Notes

- Bake the base on a full-size rimmed baking tray. A smaller oven dish makes a thicker omelette that takes longer to bake and is harder to roll.
- Weigh out 100 g of each sauce. Drain the pepperoncini before weighing them. The weights given for individual vegetables are approximate.
- Contains egg, milk and wheat, plus mustard from the sauces. The pickled pepperoncini may also contain sulphites. Check the package labels.
