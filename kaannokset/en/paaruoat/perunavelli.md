# Finnish potato and milk soup

Floury potatoes thicken this mild milk soup. Mash the potatoes until smooth for a soft, spoonable texture.

## Preparation

1. Cut the potatoes into even pieces. Put them in a saucepan and add just enough clean water to cover. Simmer for about 15–20 minutes, until a fork easily pierces the thickest piece.
2. Drain the potatoes and mash them in the pan. Stir in the butter and salt.
3. Heat the milk in a separate saucepan to just below boiling. Gradually stir it into the potatoes until the soup is smooth. Serve hot.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk. Use sound potatoes and discard any that have turned green or sprouted. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
