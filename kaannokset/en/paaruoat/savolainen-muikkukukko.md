# Savonian vendace pie (muikkukukko)

## Description

Small vendace and pork belly cook slowly inside a thick rye crust. Slice the pie while warm so each portion gets some crust and filling.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 250 °C (conventional heat). Keep the gutted vendace and pork belly chilled until you make the filling. Drain the fish and pat dry with paper towels.
2. Mix the flours and dough salt in a bowl. Add the water gradually and knead in 50 g of melted butter. Use just enough water to make a firm, workable dough. Set aside a small piece for patching. Press the rest into an oval about 30 cm long on a baking tray, about 1.5 cm thick in the centre and thinner at the edges.
3. Pile the vendace tightly in the middle. Scatter 10 g of salt and most of the 20 g of butter in small pieces between the layers. Cover the fish with the pork belly slices and sprinkle the remaining 5 g of filling salt on top.
4. Bring the edges of the dough up over the filling to enclose it. Dampen your fingers and smooth the seams to seal. Reinforce thin spots with the reserved dough. Wash your hands and any utensils that touched raw fish or pork.
5. Bake at 250 °C for 15–20 minutes, until the crust has set. Remove the tray briefly and lower the oven to 150 °C. Patch any cracks with reserved dough and brush the crust with the remaining butter.
6. Bake gently at 150 °C for about 4 hours. Cover with foil if needed to protect the crust. Use a clean food thermometer to check the centre and thickest parts of the filling, including the pork belly layer. Continue cooking until the filling reaches at least 70 °C throughout.
7. Let the pie settle briefly and serve warm. Divide it into ten roughly equal portions. Cool leftovers quickly in small pieces in shallow containers and refrigerate.
