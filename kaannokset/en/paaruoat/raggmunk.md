# Raggmunk, Swedish potato pancakes

Raw grated potatoes give these thin pancakes crisp edges.

## Preparation

1. Whisk the flour, milk and sugar until smooth. Beat in the eggs, salt and white pepper.
2. Peel and finely grate the potatoes. Stir them into the batter straight away to keep them from darkening.
3. Heat a little butter in a frying pan. Spoon in the batter and spread it into pancakes about 10 cm wide. Fry both sides until golden and the batter has set and the potato softened. Serve with lingonberry jam if you like.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, milk and eggs.
