# Jamaican jerk chicken

In jerk chicken, allspice, thyme, chilli, spring onions and lime form an aromatic marinade. The chicken is roasted until it develops a dark spiced crust and served with rice and beans.

## Instructions

1. Purée the spring onions, garlic, chilli, lime juice, soy sauce, allspice, thyme, ginger, brown sugar and oil into a marinade.
2. Rub the marinade over the chicken thigh meat and refrigerate for **at least 1 hour**.
3. Heat the oven to **200 °C**. Put the chicken into a baking dish and roast for **45–55 minutes**, until the thickest part reaches an internal temperature of at least **75 °C** and the surface is browned. You may finish browning it under the broiler. Never use marinade that has touched raw chicken to finish the cooked dish.
4. Rinse the rice. Put the rice, drained beans, coconut milk and water into a saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat according to the rice packet instructions, until the rice is cooked and the liquid has been absorbed.
5. Serve the rice and beans with the jerk chicken.

## Notes

- Habanero is hot. Remove its seeds or use only part of the chilli for a milder taste. The approximately 130-minute total includes at least 1 hour of refrigerated marinating, preparation and 45–55 minutes of roasting; cook the rice during roasting. Cook the chicken thoroughly.
