# Poulet chasseur, French hunter’s chicken

Bone-in chicken legs braise in a sauce of mushrooms, tomato and thyme. The sauce is reduced before serving.

## Preparation

1. Chop the onion and garlic and slice the mushrooms. Heat the oil and about half the butter in a lidded casserole. Brown the chicken legs for about 5 minutes on each side, then put them on a separate plate until they return to the pan.
2. Melt the remaining butter in the casserole and soften the onion over medium heat for about 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic for about a minute, then cook the mushrooms for a couple of minutes. Add the wine and tomato purée and boil for about 5 minutes.
3. Add the thyme, stock and chicken. Cover and simmer gently for about an hour until tender and at least 75 °C in the thickest part, with the thermometer clear of the bone.
4. Move the cooked legs to a clean plate and keep warm. Boil the sauce uncovered for about 10 minutes until it thickens. Remove the thyme, return the chicken and serve hot.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk. Check the stock for allergens and the wine label for sulfites. Keep raw chicken away from cooked food. Wash hands and utensils. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers and refrigerate.
