# Butternut squash and sage risotto

Half the roasted squash is mashed into the rice. The remaining cubes and crisp sage leaves go on top.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C. Peel and deseed the squash, then cut it into bite-sized pieces. Pick the sage leaves and chop half of them. Toss the squash with 1 tbsp oil and the chopped sage. Roast on a tray for about 30 minutes until tender and browned.
2. Bring the stock to the boil and keep it hot. Melt half the butter in a wide saucepan. Cook the finely chopped onion for 8–10 minutes without browning, then stir in the rice until the edges of the grains look translucent.
3. Add the hot stock a ladleful at a time, stirring frequently. Wait until each addition is almost absorbed before adding more. Cook for 25–30 minutes until the rice is al dente and the risotto flows slowly off the spoon. Some stock may be left over.
4. Fry the whole sage leaves in the remaining oil until crisp. Mash half the squash and fold it into the risotto with the cheese and remaining butter. Serve immediately with the squash cubes and crisp sage.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Check the stock label for allergens. Cool leftovers in shallow portions, ideally within an hour. Refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Reheat only once and until steaming hot throughout.
