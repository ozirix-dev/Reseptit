# Thai green chicken curry with cauliflower rice

Chicken curry with the scent of coconut milk, lime leaves and Thai basil. Cauliflower rice keeps the portion low in carbohydrates.

## Description

The hotness and sugar content of curry pastes vary. Start with a moderate amount and check the carbs on the package.

## Preparation

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Cut the courgette and aubergine into wedges.
2. Heat the oil in a pot. Fry the curry paste on low heat for a minute.
3. Add about a deciliter of coconut milk and mix. Add the chicken and cook, turning.
4. Add the remaining coconut milk, fish sauce, erythritol and kaffir lime leaves. Simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Add the vegetables and simmer for another 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked and the vegetables are tender.
6. Stir in the basil. Heat the cauliflower rice in a pan and serve with the curry.

## Notes

- Nutritional values are indicative and depend on the brand of curry paste and coconut milk.
