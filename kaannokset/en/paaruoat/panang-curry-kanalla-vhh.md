# Panang chicken curry with cauliflower rice

Panang is a rich coconut curry with flavors of peanut and kaffir lime. Serve the chicken with cauliflower rice.

## Description

The hotness of the curry paste varies. Add the paste gradually and check the carbohydrate content of the product.

## Preparation

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and halve the beans.
2. Fry the curry paste in a pot on low heat for a minute.
3. Add the coconut milk gradually and mix. Add the chicken and simmer for about 12 minutes.
4. Add the beans and continue cooking for about 6 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked.
5. Season with fish sauce and erythritol. Add peanuts and kaffir lime leaves.
6. Stir in the basil just before serving. Serve with heated cauliflower rice.

## Notes

- In the low-carb version, the rice is replaced by cauliflower rice. The nutritional values ​​are indicative and depend on the curry paste.
