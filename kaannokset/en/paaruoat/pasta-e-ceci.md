# Pasta e ceci, Roman pasta with chickpeas

Chickpeas and small pasta cook in one pot. Crushing some of the chickpeas thickens the broth.

## Preparation

1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Finely chop the garlic. Heat the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat and briefly stir in the garlic and rosemary until fragrant.
2. Add the chickpeas and about three quarters of the stock. Bring to a boil and crush some of the chickpeas against the side of the pan.
3. Add the pasta. Cook, stirring, for the time on the packet until the pasta is firm to the bite. Add the remaining stock gradually as the pasta absorbs liquid. Keep the pasta covered with stock as it cooks and use a splash of hot water to adjust the consistency if needed.
4. Remove the rosemary sprig. Cover the pan and let the pasta rest for a few minutes before serving.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat. Check the stock and pasta labels for other allergens, such as celery or egg. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
