# Creamy chicken and mushroom skillet

## Description

Browned chicken and mushrooms simmer briefly in a creamy mustard sauce.

## Preparation

1. Slice the mushrooms and finely chop the shallot. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
2. Brown the chicken in butter in a hot frying pan in two batches. Transfer it to a plate.
3. Fry the mushrooms in the pan until golden. Add the shallot and cook until soft.
4. Pour in the chicken stock, cream and mustard. Bring to a boil, return the chicken to the pan and simmer for 8–10 minutes until cooked through.
5. Season with thyme, salt and pepper.

## Notes

Serve on its own or with cauliflower mash.
