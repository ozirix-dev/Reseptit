# Chettinad Chicken with Roasted Spices

Tamil Nadu's Chettinad chicken is known for its roasted whole spices, black pepper and rich tomato sauce.

## Description

Tamil Nadu's Chettinad chicken is known for its roasted whole spices, black pepper and rich tomato sauce.

## Preparation

1. Toast the black peppercorns, coriander seeds, fennel seeds and cinnamon in a dry pan until fragrant. Cool and grind.
2. Heat the oil in a pot. Saute the onion. Add chopped garlic and ginger.
3. Add the chicken and lightly brown on all sides.
4. Mix in the spice mixture and curry leaves. Add tomatoes and salt.
5. Pour in the water, cover and simmer for 25-30 minutes until the chicken is cooked and the sauce is thick.
6. Serve with basmati rice or bread.

## Notes

- Adjust the amount of black pepper if you want a milder sauce.
