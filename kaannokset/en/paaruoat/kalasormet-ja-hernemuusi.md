# Fish fingers with mashed peas

Homemade fish fingers come with mashed peas flavoured with mint and lemon.

## Preparation

1. Cut fish into 12 pieces about 3 cm thick. Put flour, beaten egg and crumbs in separate dishes. Coat in that order and chill covered 15 min. Discard leftover coating that touched raw fish or egg.
2. Heat oil and fry, turning, about 8 min, in batches if needed. Continue until the centre reaches 63 °C and coating is cooked.
3. Boil peas according to packet until hot and tender. Drain, mash with butter, lemon zest and mint. Serve three fish fingers with peas and lemon wedges per portion.

## Notes

- Contains fish, wheat, egg and milk.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
