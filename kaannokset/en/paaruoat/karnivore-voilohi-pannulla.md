# Pan-fried salmon in butter

## Description

Salmon is fried skin side down until crisp, then finished in butter.

## Instructions

1. Pat the skin dry and divide the fillet into two pieces of equal thickness. Salt both sides.
2. Melt 15 g butter in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the salmon skin side down and press lightly for the first 20–30 seconds so the skin stays against the pan.
3. Fry the skin side for about 5–7 minutes until crisp. Adjust the heat so the butter does not burn.
4. Turn carefully, add the remaining 20 g butter and cook for about 2–5 minutes more, depending on thickness. Check for at least 63 °C in the thickest part.
5. Serve at once and spoon the melted butter from the pan over the fish.

## Notes

- For very thick fish, continue at lower heat and check with a thermometer. Butter contains small amounts of naturally occurring carbohydrate.
- The per-serving estimates use 400 g edible, boneless Kalaneuvos Atlantic salmon with skin and 35 g butter. Serve all butter and rendered fish fat to match the estimate.
