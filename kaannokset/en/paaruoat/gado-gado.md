# Gado-gado – Indonesian salad with peanut sauce

Potato, tempeh, egg and vegetables meet a warm peanut dressing.

## Instructions

1. Crush garlic. Combine peanut butter, curry paste, kecap manis, sambal, salt, garlic, lime juice, coconut milk and measured water in a pan. Heat and simmer, stirring, for about 5 minutes.
2. Cut potatoes and boil until tender, then drain. Boil sprouts separately until thoroughly cooked. Blanch spinach and drain. Wash and slice cucumber.
3. Slice tempeh and fry in oil until golden on both sides and hot throughout, following at least the package cooking directions.
4. Peel and halve eggs. Divide potatoes, vegetables, tempeh and eggs between two plates. Spoon over sauce and scatter peanuts.

## Notes

- Contains peanuts, soy and egg; curry paste may contain crustaceans or fish and soy sauce may contain wheat. No vegetarian claim without checking products. Red Thai curry paste is the source's shortcut; spinach, sprout and cucumber weights are editorial specifications. Optional prawn crackers omitted.
