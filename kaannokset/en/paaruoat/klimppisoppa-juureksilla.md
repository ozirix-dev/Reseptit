# Ostrobothnian root vegetable and dumpling soup

Small wheat dumplings with raisins cook in a root vegetable soup.

## Preparation

1. Dice the peeled root vegetables and onion into pieces about 2 cm across. Heat the vegetable stock in a saucepan and add the vegetables and rosemary. Simmer for about 20 minutes until the root vegetables are almost tender.
2. Beat the milk, eggs, flour and salt to a smooth batter. Stir in the raisins and let the batter rest briefly.
3. Dip a spoon into the stock and use it to drop small spoonfuls of batter into the gently boiling soup. Cook the dumplings for 5–10 minutes. Split the largest dumpling and cook longer if the centre is still raw. Serve hot.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat, egg, milk and celery. Check the stock label for allergens. Do not taste the raw batter. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
