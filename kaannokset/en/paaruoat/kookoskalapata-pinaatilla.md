# Coconut fish curry with spinach

Cod and spinach simmered in coconut milk are served with rice and mango chutney.

## Preparation

1. Thaw fish in the fridge, drain and cut into 3 cm chunks. Cook rice as directed.
2. Soften chopped onion in oil 6–8 min. Add grated ginger, chopped garlic, chilli and turmeric; heat 2 min. Add coconut milk, water, stock cube and spinach. Simmer 10–12 min until spinach is hot.
3. Add fish and lime zest, return to simmer and cover. Cook 4–5 min or longer until the thickest fish piece reaches 63 °C. Add coriander; divide rice, curry and chutney among four bowls and serve with lime wedges.

## Notes

- Contains fish. Check stock cube and chutney for allergens (e.g. celery, mustard).
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool rice or risotto in shallow containers as quickly as possible, ideally within an hour. Refrigerate at 0–5 °C for no more than 24 hours; reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
