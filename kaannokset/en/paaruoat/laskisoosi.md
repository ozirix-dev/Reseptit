# Finnish pork belly gravy

This traditional brown gravy gets its flavour from browned pork belly and allspice.

## Preparation

1. Cut the pork belly into small pieces and finely chop the onion. Brown the pork in its own fat. Lift out the pieces and leave a little fat in the pan for the flour.
2. Soften the onion in the pan. Stir the flour into the fat and cook until it darkens slightly. Gradually add the water, stirring continuously.
3. Return the meat to the pan, add the allspice and salt, and simmer until the gravy thickens and the pork reaches at least 70 °C throughout. Serve with boiled potatoes, if you like.
