# Gaeng liang, Thai shrimp and vegetable soup

White pepper, fingerroot and dried shrimp flavour the clear broth. Kabocha, mushrooms and lemon basil give the soup its distinctive taste.

## Preparation

1. Cut the zucchini and chayote into batons. Tear the oyster mushrooms into bite-size pieces. Rinse the basil and pick the leaves.
2. Grind the dried shrimp finely. Crush the white pepper in a mortar, add the fingerroot and shallots and pound to a paste. Mix in the ground shrimp and shrimp paste.
3. Bring the chicken stock to a boil. Stir in the paste and simmer for 2–3 minutes. Add the fish sauce and kabocha and simmer for about 2 minutes. Add the zucchini, chayote and mushrooms. Simmer for another 3–4 minutes, or until the kabocha is fork-tender.
4. Add the raw shrimp to the simmering broth. Cook until the flesh is opaque and pearly or white all the way through. Take the pot off the heat, stir in the basil leaves and divide among 4 bowls.

## Storage and safety

Contains crustaceans and fish. Check the stock label for other allergens, such as celery. Thaw frozen shrimp in the refrigerator and keep utensils used on raw shrimp away from cooked food. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
