# Crispy pan-fried chicken thighs

## Description

Start the thighs skin side down in a cold pan to render the fat slowly. Finish covered and check the internal temperature.

## Instructions

1. Pat the thighs dry with kitchen paper and salt all sides.
2. Set the thighs skin side down in a cold, heavy frying pan. Gradually bring it to medium heat so the skin starts to render.
3. Cook skin side down for about 12–15 minutes. Move the pieces if needed to keep the skin from sticking and burning.
4. Turn, add butter and cook covered for about 12–18 minutes, depending on thickness. Turn again if needed for even cooking.
5. Without touching bone, check that the thickest part reaches at least 75 °C. Divide the thighs into four portions. Serve all edible butter, rendered fat and juices alongside to keep the skin crisp.

## Notes

- Crisp skin alone does not show that chicken is cooked. Use a meat thermometer.
