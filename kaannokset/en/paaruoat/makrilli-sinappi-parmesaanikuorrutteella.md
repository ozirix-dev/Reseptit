# Mustard and Parmesan crusted mackerel

A mustard, Parmesan and breadcrumb topping turns crisp over mackerel.

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 220 °C. Melt butter and mix with crumbs, Parmesan and herbs.
2. Check for bones and dry fillets. Oil a tray, lay skin-side up. Spread mixed mustards and press on crumbs.
3. Bake initially 10–15 min; continue until thickest part reaches 63 °C. Cover loosely with foil if browning too fast. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Contains fish, wheat, milk and mustard. Cooking does not remedy a broken mackerel cold chain.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
