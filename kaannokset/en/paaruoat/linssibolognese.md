# Red lentil bolognese

Red lentils simmer with tomatoes, vegetables and herbs until tender. Spoon the thick sauce over spaghetti.

## Preparation

1. Finely chop the onions, carrots, celery and garlic. Heat the oil in a large saucepan and cook the vegetables gently for 15–20 minutes until soft.
2. Rinse the lentils and add them with the chopped tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, thyme, bay leaves and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 40–50 minutes until the lentils are tender. Stir occasionally and add water if it becomes dry before the lentils are cooked.
3. Cook the spaghetti as directed on the pack. Remove the bay leaves from the sauce, drain the pasta and divide it into portions. Spoon the lentil sauce over the pasta.

## Notes

- Contains celery and wheat. Check the stock label for allergens.
