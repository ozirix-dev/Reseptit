# Traditional Finnish Sweetened Potato Casserole

A Finnish Christmas casserole in which warm mashed potatoes develop natural sweetness before baking.

## Preparation

1. Boil the peeled potatoes in unsalted water until tender. Reserve 180 ml of the cooking water and mash the potatoes while hot.
2. Let the mash cool and measure it with a clean food thermometer: the mash should be 55–70°C. Stir in the reserved water and half the flour. Do not add flour while the mash is above 75°C.
3. Cover the saucepan and place it in an oven set to about 60°C for 3–4 hours. The oven setting is not the measured temperature of the mash: check with a food thermometer that the mash stays around 55–70°C throughout sweetening and never rises above 75°C. Add the remaining flour after 30 minutes and stir occasionally.
4. Warm the milk and stir it into the sweetened mash. Add butter and salt, and syrup if desired. Do not taste the flour-containing mixture before baking. It should resemble a thick porridge.
5. Divide among greased baking dishes, filling them no more than halfway because the mixture rises. Bake at 150°C for about 2 hours until golden and set.

## Notes

- Contains **wheat and milk** (wheat flour, whole milk and butter). Check product labels. Do not describe the recipe as gluten-free.
