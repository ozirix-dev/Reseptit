# Homemade beef döner kebab

Four breads weigh 240 g total. The 200 g cabbage salad consists of 180 g cabbage,10 g wine vinegar and 10 g olive oil; this is an oil-and-vinegar salad. Include all cooking juices and fat in the servings.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Mix beef, yoghurt, grated onion, garlic, dried spices and salt.
2. Spread thinly on baking paper and roll evenly through another sheet. Remove the top sheet.
3. Bake 12–15 minutes until the minced beef is cooked throughout, then slice into shavings.
4. Brown the shavings in batches in a hot pan. Retain all cooking juices and fat for serving.
5. Toss 180 g finely shredded cabbage with 10 g wine vinegar and 10 g olive oil.
6. Warm four ambient-temperature pittas in a toaster for 1–2 minutes per package instructions. Divide all meat, juices and salad among the four warm breads and serve immediately.
