# Traditional Finnish Karelian Beef and Pork Stew

A traditional Finnish oven dish.

## Preparation

1. Preheat oven to 175°C. Dry the meat and brown beef and pork in batches in hot rapeseed oil. Transfer to an ovenproof casserole.
2. Add the onion wedges, carrots and swede. Sprinkle in salt, whole allspice and bay leaves.
3. Pour 700 ml water into the frying pan, bring to a boil to loosen the browned bits, then add to the casserole. The liquid should almost cover the meat.
4. Cover and braise at 175°C for about 3 hours. Check once during cooking that there is enough liquid and add hot water if necessary.
5. Once the meat is completely cooked and fork-tender, remove the bay leaves and adjust seasoning. Serve with boiled potatoes if desired.
