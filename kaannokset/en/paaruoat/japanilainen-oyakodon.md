# Japanese chicken and egg rice bowl (oyakodon)

## Description

Chicken, onion and egg cook in a sweet-savoury dashi broth and are served over rice.

## Preparation

1. Slice the onion and cut the chicken into bite-size pieces. Combine the dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sugar in a frying pan and bring to a boil.
2. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes. Add the chicken in one layer and simmer until the largest pieces reach at least 74 °C in the centre.
3. Lightly beat the eggs, pour them into the pan and cover. Cook gently until the egg is set throughout. Divide the hot cooked rice among four bowls and spoon over the chicken, egg and broth.
