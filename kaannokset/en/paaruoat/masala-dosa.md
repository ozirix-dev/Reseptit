# Masala dosa and spicy potato filling

A crispy lettu fried from fermented rice-lentil dough is filled with spicy potato. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar sauce.

## Description

A crispy lettu fried from fermented rice-lentil dough is filled with spicy potato. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar sauce.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and lentils separately. Soak them with fenugreek for 4 hours. Drain.
2. Puree rice and lentils separately with water into a smooth, slightly thick dough. Mix together.
3. Cover the dough and ferment in a warm place for 8–12 hours. Mix in the salt.
4. Boil the potatoes until cooked and dice them. Saute mustard seeds, onion, ginger, turmeric and curry leaves in oil. Add the potatoes and coriander.
5. Heat a non-stick frying pan. Spread a scoop of dough into a thin circle.
6. Fry on medium heat until the surface dries and the underside browns. Add a little oil to the edges.
7. Spoon the potato filling in the middle, fold the dosa and serve immediately.

## Notes

- The dough needs a warm place to ferment; in cool weather it may take longer.
