# Masala dosa and spicy potato filling

A crisp, thin dosa made from fermented rice and urad dal batter is filled with spiced potatoes. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Description

A crisp, thin dosa made from fermented rice and urad dal batter is filled with spiced potatoes. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the rice and lentils separately. Soak them with fenugreek for 4 hours. Drain.
2. Grind the rice and urad dal separately, adding water a little at a time, just enough to keep the mixture moving. Grind the urad dal until smooth and fluffy and the rice until finely grainy. Combine into a thick but pourable batter.
3. Cover the batter and ferment in a warm place for 8–12 hours. Mix in the salt.
4. Boil the potatoes until cooked and dice them. Saute mustard seeds, onion, ginger, turmeric and curry leaves in oil. Add the potatoes and coriander.
5. Stir the fermented batter and add water a little at a time if needed, until it can be spread thinly with a ladle. Heat a non-stick frying pan and spread a ladleful into a thin circle.
6. Fry on medium heat until the surface dries and the underside browns. Add a little oil to the edges.
7. Spoon the potato filling in the middle, fold the dosa and serve immediately.

## Notes

- The batter needs a warm place to ferment; in cool weather it may take longer.
