# Kung pao chicken with peanuts

A quick wok known from Sichuan, where tender chicken, roasted peanuts and sweet and salty chili sauce meet.

## Description

A quick wok known from Sichuan, where tender chicken, roasted peanuts and sweet and salty chili sauce meet.

## Preparation

1. Mix the chicken with a tablespoon of soy sauce and cornstarch. Leave to season for 10 minutes.
2. For the sauce, mix soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar and water.
3. Heat the wok until hot. Add oil and fry the chicken in batches. Put on a plate.
4. Fry the chilies, garlic and ginger in the wok for about 30 seconds. Return the chicken to the wok and add the sauce.
5. Stir until the sauce is glossy and sticks to the chicken. Add peanuts and spring onion. Serve with rice.

## Notes

- Dried chilies flavor the oil, and you don't need to eat them. Use gloves when handling if necessary.
