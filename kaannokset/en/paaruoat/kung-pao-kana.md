# Kung pao chicken with peanuts

Stir-fried chicken, peanuts and chilies are served with cooked white rice.

## Description

Stir-fried chicken, peanuts and chilies are served with cooked white rice.

## Preparation

1. Mix the chicken with one tablespoon of soy sauce and cornstarch. Leave to season for 10 minutes.

2. Mix soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar and water for the sauce.

3. Heat a wok, add oil and fry the chicken in batches until browned. Set aside.

4. Fry chilies, garlic and ginger briefly. Return chicken, add sauce and stir until glossy.

5. Stir in peanuts and spring onion. Serve with the cooked rice.

## Notes

- Dried chilies flavor the oil; remove them if you prefer not to eat them.
