# Butter-basted lamb chops

## Description

Brown lamb chops in a pan and finish with butter. No herb mixes or vegetable sides are used.

## Instructions

1. Pat the chops dry and salt both sides.
2. Heat a heavy frying pan over medium heat and add 10 g butter.
3. Fry for about 3–4 minutes per side and brown the fat edge as well. The time depends on thickness.
4. Lower the heat, add the remaining 15 g butter and spoon it over the chops for about a minute. Measure in the thickest part without touching bone. Keep cooking as needed until the internal temperature is at least 63 °C.
5. Rest on a plate for at least 3 minutes. Divide the chops and all edible butter, pan fat and meat juices from the pan and plate between two portions.

## Notes

- Use bone-in rib chops with the fat edge. The AFCD estimate is 70.8% edible raw meat and fat, about 425 g from 600 g bone-in. The actual proportion varies.
- About 586 kcal per portion if all edible meat, fat, butter and juices are consumed. This is an estimate from raw ingredients.
