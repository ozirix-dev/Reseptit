# Massaman chicken curry with cauliflower rice

The mildly spicy massaman curry gets richness from coconut milk and peanuts. Turnips and cauliflower provide a lower-carb addition than potatoes.

## Description

Massamani combines warm spices and a slightly sweet coconut sauce. Serve with cauliflower rice.

## Preparation

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces. Peel and dice the turnip. Cut the onion into wedges.
2. Fry the curry paste in a pot on low heat for a minute.
3. Add coconut milk gradually. Add chicken, turnip and onion.
4. Bring to a boil and simmer under the lid for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Add cauliflower and peanuts. Simmer for another 10 minutes until the chicken and vegetables are cooked.
6. Season with fish sauce, lime juice and erythritol. Heat the cauliflower rice and serve with the curry.

## Notes

- The potato has been replaced with turnip and cauliflower. Curry paste may contain sugar; check the packaging.
- The nutritional values ​​are indicative.
