# Carnivore smashburger

## Description

Two portions with two thin seared beef patties each, without bun, cheese, sauce or vegetables. Weigh the meat raw.

## Instructions

1. Divide 500 g cold raw ground beef into four 125 g portions and roll loosely. Measure 10 g tallow and 4 g salt.
2. Heat a heavy pan over medium-high heat. Melt half the tallow and reserve the other half for the next batch. Do not add other fat.
3. Put two beef balls in the pan and immediately press each to about 1 cm with a broad spatula. Season with some of the salt. Sear the first side for about 2–3 minutes.
4. Turn the patties and cook the other side. Check that the centre of the thickest part reaches at least 75 °C. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining tallow and salt.
5. Serve two patties per plate immediately, with no sides. Spoon the edible pan fat and juices over them to match the nutrition estimate.
