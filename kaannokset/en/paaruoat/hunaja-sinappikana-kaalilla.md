# Honey mustard chicken traybake with roasted cabbage

## Description

Mustard and a little honey season the chicken. Cabbage wedges roast on the same tray until tender with crisp edges.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 210 °C.
2. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and the cabbage into wedges. Arrange them on a baking tray.
3. Mix the mustard, honey, oil, garlic powder and paprika. Coat the chicken and cabbage and season with salt and pepper.
4. Roast for 35–40 minutes, turning the cabbage once, until the chicken is cooked through and the cabbage is tender.

## Notes

Honey adds carbohydrates to the serving. You can leave it out.
