# Marry Me chicken spaghetti

## Description

Creamy chicken spaghetti with sun-dried tomatoes and Parmesan. Four servings; regular wheat pasta, not keto.

## Preparation

1. Cut 600 g chicken into even pieces. Chop 70 g onion, 12 g garlic and 80 g drained sun-dried tomatoes.
2. Brown the chicken in batches using 15 g olive oil. Cook onion until soft and add garlic last.
3. Add tomatoes, 200 g cream, 150 g water, 3 g thyme, 4 g salt and 1 g pepper. Simmer 8–12 minutes until the centre of the thickest chicken piece exceeds 75 °C. Stir in 50 g Parmesan.
4. Boil 240 g dry De Cecco Spaghetti n°12 for about 10 minutes until al dente; drain without adding unmeasured oil.
5. Combine spaghetti and sauce, divide into four equal portions and weigh the finished batch during the test cook.

Contains wheat gluten and milk. The EAN-matched spaghetti has conflicting online carbohydrate declarations (69 and 72 g/100 g); verify the physical package. GI 50 is a candidate only and GK is HOLD.
