# Peppers stuffed with quinoa and black beans

Bell pepper halves soften in the oven around a tomato-salsa filling of quinoa, black beans and sweetcorn.

## Preparation

1. Rinse and drain the quinoa. Bring the stock and quinoa to a boil. Cover and simmer for about 20 minutes until the stock has been absorbed and the quinoa is tender.
2. Heat the oven to 190 °C. Halve the peppers lengthwise and remove the seeds. Brush them with rapeseed oil and put them in a baking dish. Rinse and drain the black beans.
3. Mix the hot quinoa with the salsa, cumin, chilli seasoning, garlic powder, beans and sweetcorn. Divide the filling among the peppers and cover the dish with foil.
4. Bake for 30 minutes. Remove the foil, raise the oven to 205 °C and bake for another 15–20 minutes until the peppers are soft and the filling is hot throughout.

## Notes

- Check the stock, salsa and chilli seasoning labels for allergens.
