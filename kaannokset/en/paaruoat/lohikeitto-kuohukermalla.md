# Creamy salmon soup – water-based version without stock

## Description

Creamy salmon soup – water-based version without stock.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. For this version without stock, soften 55 g onion, 50 g leek and 70 g carrot in 15 g butter. Add 800 g water and 360 g raw peeled potato. Boil until the potato is cooked.
2. Add 400 g raw salmon fillet, 200 g cream and 0.5 g ground black pepper. Cook the fish thoroughly. Do not add fish stock concentrate to this named version.
3. Add 10 g dill and divide the whole soup, including all liquid, into four portions. Consume the ground pepper; there are no whole peppercorns to remove.
