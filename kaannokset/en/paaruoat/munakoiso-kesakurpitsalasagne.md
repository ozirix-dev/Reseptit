# Eggplant and zucchini lasagne

## Description

Roasted vegetables, tomato sauce and two cheeses are layered between lasagne sheets. A home adaptation of a video by Hans Välimäki.

## Ingredients

Weigh the no-boil durum wheat sheets dry and use plain crushed tomatoes.

## Preparation

1. Heat a fan oven to 200°C. Cut the eggplant and zucchini into slices about half a centimetre thick and slice the onion. Toss with 50 g oil and the salt. Spread on two trays and roast for 20–25 minutes until pliable and lightly coloured. Swap the trays halfway through and reserve the juices.
2. Set aside 150 g crushed tomatoes for serving and stir in the wine vinegar. Heat the remaining 650 g tomatoes with the water, crushed garlic, thyme and tray juices. Simmer gently for 5–10 minutes.
3. Set the oven to 180°C conventional heat. Oil a roughly 3-litre dish with 5 g oil. Cover the base with sauce. Layer the sheets, vegetables, sauce, 175 g mozzarella, 60 g Parmesan and 5 g basil. Cover the edges and tops of the sheets with sauce. Finish with reserved sauce and 75 g mozzarella.
4. Bake for about 45 minutes, covering with foil for the first 25–30 minutes. Cook until the centre bubbles and a knife passes easily through all pasta layers. Rest for 15 minutes.
5. Heat the serving sauce. Divide the lasagne and sauce among four plates. Finish with a total of 20 g Parmesan, 5 g basil, 5 g olive oil and the black pepper.

## Notes

Allergens: wheat (gluten) and milk. Parmesan may contain animal rennet. Cool leftovers quickly and refrigerate. The user-supplied image illustrates the dish. This recipe was not test-cooked. The original video was marked as a paid collaboration. Glycemic load: undetermined.
