# Roasted eggplant and zucchini lasagne

## Description

Four-serving home-kitchen adaptation of a vegetable lasagne. Roasted vegetables, dry lasagne sheets, tomato sauce and two cheeses are baked together. The exact method and timing are an adaptation, not a verbatim original recipe.

## Ingredients

The same measured ingredient quantities appear in the structured metadata above.

## Instructions

1. Heat a fan oven to 200 °C. Dry the cleaned vegetables. Slice the 400 g eggplant and 400 g zucchini about 5 mm thick, and slice the peeled 100 g onion. Toss with 50 g of the measured olive oil and up to 3 g salt. Do not salt and drain the vegetables separately in advance.
2. Spread the vegetables over two trays in a single layer and roast for about 20–25 minutes, swapping tray positions halfway through; watch the thin onion slices. Without a fan, use 225 °C and allow about 25–35 minutes. Do not roast the 12 g garlic; reserve it for the sauce. Reduce the oven to 180 °C conventional heat for the lasagne.
3. While the vegetables roast, reserve 150 g of the tomato sauce for serving and mix it with all 10 g wine vinegar. Gently simmer the other 650 g sauce with the 12 g crushed garlic and 3 g thyme leaves for 5–10 minutes without reducing it heavily. Add about 100 ml of the listed water; adjust the total to 50–150 ml if the sauce or lasagne-sheet instructions require it. Drain and measure 250 g mozzarella, and grate the 80 g hard cheese.
4. Oil a roughly 3-litre baking dish (about 20 × 25 cm, at least 6 cm deep) with 5 g of the measured oil. Reserve about a quarter of the dish sauce for the top and spread some sauce across the base. Make 3–4 layers using the measured 200 g dry lasagne sheets; fit them without thick overlaps. Layer roasted vegetables, sauce, 175 g mozzarella, 60 g hard cheese and 5 g basil, keeping sauce on both sides and edges of the sheets. Cover the last pasta layer with the reserved dish sauce and 75 g mozzarella. Leave at least 1 cm headspace.
5. Bake on the middle rack at 180 °C conventional heat for about 45 minutes. Cover loosely with foil for the first 25–30 minutes, then uncover. Follow your pasta package's instructions, including any required pre-cooking. Test the centre and edges through every pasta layer with a thin knife: there should be no hard resistance. Extend cooking in 5–10 minute intervals if needed, covering a browning top.
6. Rest the lasagne for 15 minutes. Warm the reserved 150 g serving sauce mixed with vinegar and divide it among four plates with the lasagne. Finish the entire dish with the remaining 20 g hard cheese, 5 g basil, 0.5 g black pepper and 5 g olive oil.

## Notes

An estimated 110 minutes for 4 portions; actual time may be 100–130 minutes. No validated GI/GK or fully verified nutrition values are provided. Wheat and dairy are allergens. Vegetarian suitability depends on the rennet used in both cheeses. This home adaptation has not been physically test-cooked.
