# Palak chana with spinach and chickpeas

Blended spinach and fresh coriander are stirred into tomatoes and chickpeas near the end of cooking.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the spinach and coriander. Peel and slice the ginger and garlic, and split the chilli. Heat 1½ tbsp oil in a pan and cook the ginger, garlic and chilli for one minute. Add the onion and cook until soft and golden.
2. Stir the spinach in the pan for 2–3 minutes until wilted, then transfer the mixture to a bowl. Let it cool slightly and blend it with the coriander using a hand blender.
3. Heat the remaining oil in the pan. Add the chopped tomatoes and salt and cook until soft. Rinse the chickpeas and add them with the garam masala and water. Bring to the boil, cover and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Stir the blended spinach into the chickpeas. Cook over low heat for 1–2 minutes until the sauce bubbles, then serve.

## Notes

- Check the chickpea and spice blend labels for allergens.
