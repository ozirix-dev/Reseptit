# Finnish sailor’s beef and potato casserole

Beef, onions and potatoes braise together in a dark beer broth.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Thinly slice the beef across the grain. Peel and slice the onions, and wash and thinly slice the potatoes.
2. Season the beef with salt and brown it in the oil in batches. Set it aside and brown the onions in the same pan. Add the water and concentrated stock and stir to lift the browned bits.
3. Layer the potatoes, onions and beef in an ovenproof casserole, finishing with potatoes. Slip the allspice and bay leaves between the layers. Add the beer and just enough pan broth to barely cover the layers.
4. Bake for about 2 hours, covering the casserole halfway through. Check that the potatoes are tender and the beef is cooked through and fork-tender.

## Notes

- Check the beer and stock labels for allergens. Ordinary beer may contain barley.
