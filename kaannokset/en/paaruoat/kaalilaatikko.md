# Low-Carb Cabbage Casserole without Rice

A weighed low-carb cabbage casserole without rice or syrup.

## Description

Use the exact ingredient compositions and edible weights listed. Keep all liquid, fat and spices in the dish. The salt quantity is unchanged from the original recipe.

## Preparation

1. Cook 500 g ground beef and 55 g onion in 14 g butter. Add 900 g finely sliced white cabbage.
2. Mix 200 g cream, 110 g egg, 100 g meat broth, 1 g black pepper and 0.6 g ground marjoram. Combine everything in a baking dish.
3. Bake at 175 degrees until the egg mixture and meat are cooked and the cabbage is tender. Divide the whole dish, including its liquid, into six portions.
