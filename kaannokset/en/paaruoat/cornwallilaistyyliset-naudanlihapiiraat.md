# Cornwall-style beef pasties

## Description

Beef, potato, swede and onion cook together inside these baked pasties. Chill the pastry before rolling and crimp the curved seam firmly at the side to hold in the filling.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour and pastry salt in a bowl. Rub the cold lard and butter into the flour until crumbly. Add the cold water and knead until the dough is elastic. Cover and rest in the fridge for 3 hours.
2. Dice the beef, potatoes and swede into small pieces. Thinly slice the onion. Keep the beef refrigerated until filling the pasties. Wash your hands, board and utensils after contact with raw beef.
3. Heat a fan oven to 165 °C. Roll the pastry out and cut six rounds about 20 cm across. Distribute the vegetables and beef evenly over one half of each round. Season between layers with salt and pepper. Keep the edges clear.
4. Fold the pastry over the filling to make half-moons. Press the edges closed and crimp the seam along the side. Put the pasties on baking parchment and brush the tops with beaten egg.
5. Bake for about 50–55 minutes. Use a clean food thermometer to check the centre of the filling has reached at least 75 °C. Continue baking if needed. Serve one pasty per portion.
6. Cool leftovers quickly and refrigerate within two hours. Use within two days.

## Notes

Allergens are wheat (gluten), milk and egg. The estimated total time includes the pastry rest. Nutrition values and glycaemic load are undetermined. The image is illustrative.
