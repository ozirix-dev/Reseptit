# Larb moo – Thai spicy pork salad

The warm Isan-style minced meat salad gets its flavor from lime, fish sauce, chili and fresh herbs. Serve with cabbage and cucumber.

## Description

Roasted rice powder brings traditional roastiness and texture. A small amount is optional.

## Preparation

1. Heat water in a pan. Add the minced meat and cook over low heat, crumbling until the meat is completely cooked.
2. Remove the pan from the stove. Mix in the fish sauce, lime juice and chili.
3. If desired, add rice powder. Stir in the shallot, mint and coriander.
4. Taste and adjust saltiness, acidity and hotness.
5. Serve warm with cabbage wedges and cucumber slices.

## Notes

- Rice powder can be left out if you want to minimize carbohydrates; a small amount belongs to the traditional taste of the larbi.
- Nutritional values ​​are indicative and calculated with rice powder.
