# Chicken enchilada cauliflower bake with homemade salsa

## Description

A precisely weighed chicken and cauliflower bake with homemade salsa. Makes four servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredient amounts are listed in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. For the homemade salsa, mix 200 g finely chopped tomato, 35 g onion, 10 g lime juice and 5 g fresh green chilli. This 250 g mixture replaces prepared commercial salsa.
2. Combine 500 g chicken breast weighed **after cooking**, 500 g cauliflower, all the homemade salsa, 150 g Cheddar, 100 g crème fraîche, all the weighed cumin, smoked paprika and coriander, and 50 g peeled regular lime including all flesh and juice.
3. Bake in an ovenproof dish at 200 °C until the cauliflower is tender and the dish is hot throughout. Divide the entire bake, including all its juices, into four servings. No tortillas or additional sauce are used.
