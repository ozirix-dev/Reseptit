# Flammekueche, Alsatian bacon and onion tart

An unleavened thin crust holds a creamy topping, soft onion strips and cooked bacon. The dough makes two thin tarts.

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour for the dough with the salt. Gradually add the oil for the dough and the lukewarm water. Knead for a few minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and rest for about an hour.
2. Slice the onions and bacon on separate boards. Soften the onions in butter over low heat for about 5 minutes without browning. Set them aside and cook the bacon separately until cooked through.
3. Mix the fromage blanc, crème fraîche, nutmeg and pepper. Heat the oven to 250 °C. Divide the dough in two and roll each piece out on a floured surface to about 1–2 mm thick. Transfer to lightly floured metal trays.
4. Spread a thin layer of the cream mixture over each base. Divide the onions and cooked bacon between them and drizzle with oil. Bake one tray at a time for about 8–12 minutes until the edges brown and the bases are crisp. Cut each tart into two servings.

## Storage and safety

Contains wheat and milk. Keep raw bacon separate from cooked ingredients and wash the utensils. Pork must reach at least 70 °C throughout for this recipe. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
