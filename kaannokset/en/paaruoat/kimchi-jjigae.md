# Korean kimchi jjigae

Kimchi jjigae is a warming, tangy Korean stew in which fermented kimchi, pork and tofu simmer together in broth. Serve with rice.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a pot and brown the pork.
2. Add the kimchi and onion. Fry for a few minutes to deepen the kimchi flavour.
3. Stir in the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce and stock or water.
4. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer for about 25 minutes.
5. Add the tofu and simmer for another 5–7 minutes. Finish with spring onions.

## Notes

- Use mature, tangy kimchi. If the kimchi and stock are already salty, taste before adding any extra salt.
