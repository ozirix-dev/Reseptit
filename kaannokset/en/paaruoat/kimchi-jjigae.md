# Korean kimchi-jjigae stew

Kimchi-jjigae is a warming, tangy Korean stew in which mature fermented kimchi, pork and tofu cook in the same broth. Serve with rice.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan and brown the pork.
2. Add the kimchi and onion. Fry for a few minutes to deepen the kimchi flavour.
3. Stir in gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce and the broth.
4. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer for **about 25 minutes**.
5. Add the tofu and simmer for **another 5–7 minutes**. Finish with the spring onions.

## Notes

- Use mature, pleasantly sour kimchi. If the kimchi and broth are already salty, taste before adding extra salt.
