# Pea soup with smoked pork hock

## Description

This slow-simmered pea soup gets a smoky broth from pork hock and its traditional seasoning from marjoram.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the peas and soak them overnight in plenty of cold water. Discard the soaking water.
2. Put the peas, water, smoked pork hock, onion cut into wedges and bay leaves in a pot. Bring to a boil and skim off the foam.
3. Cover and simmer gently for 1.5–2 hours until the peas are soft and the meat comes away from the bone. Add water if needed.
4. Lift out the hock, remove the meat, cut it into pieces and return it to the soup. Season with marjoram and pepper. Taste before adding salt.
5. Serve with mustard.

## Notes

Pea soup is high in carbohydrates and is not a keto dish. The total time includes about 12 hours of overnight soaking as well as simmering.
