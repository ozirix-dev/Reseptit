# Beef and avocado taco bowl

## Description

Warm spiced beef is served in a fresh bowl with lettuce, tomatoes, avocado and lime yogurt.

## Preparation

1. Brown the minced beef in a frying pan. Add cumin, smoked paprika, salt and chilli if desired.
2. Mix the yogurt with the lime juice. Dice the tomatoes and avocado and tear the lettuce.
3. Divide the lettuce among the bowls. Add the warm beef, tomatoes, avocado and grated cheddar.
4. Serve with the lime yogurt.

## Notes

You can add a small amount of beans or salsa, which will change the carbohydrate content.
