# sausage sauce and cauliflower mash

Quick sausage sauce cooks in a pan until creamy, and instead of mashed potatoes, cauliflower mash finished with butter is served.

## Description

The familiar everyday food is suitable for a low-carbohydrate diet when the sauce is thickened by boiling and cauliflower is chosen as an addition. Check the amount of carbohydrates in the sausage on the product packaging.

## Preparation

1. Chop the walnuts and chop the onion. Brown the sausage in a pan and add the onion until soft.
2. Pour the meat broth and whipped cream into the pan. Mix in the mustard and black pepper. Simmer gently for 8-10 minutes until the sauce thickens a bit. Taste before adding salt.
3. Chop the cauliflower and cook in salted water until soft. Drain carefully and allow excess steam to evaporate.
4. Puree the cauliflower with butter. Season with salt and serve with sausage sauce.

## Notes

- Choose as low-carb snacks as possible and check the ingredient list.
- If you want the sauce to be thicker, let it boil a little longer. Do not use wheat flour.
