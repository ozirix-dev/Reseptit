# Lindström beef patties with butter-fried cabbage

## Description

Juicy beef patties with beetroot and mustard, served with tender pan-fried cabbage.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are provided by the structured serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Grate the beetroot and finely chop the onion. Mix the egg, beetroot pickling liquid and mustard into the ground beef. Season.
2. Shape the mixture into eight flat patties. Fry them in 1 tbsp of the listed butter over medium heat for approximately 4 minutes on each side, until cooked through.
3. Shred the cabbage. Fry it in the remaining 1 tbsp of butter in a separate pan for 10–12 minutes until soft and lightly browned. Use the full listed 2 tbsp of butter, without adding any extra frying fat.
4. Season the cabbage with salt and pepper, then serve with the patties.

## Notes

Beetroot contains carbohydrates. Use less beetroot if you are aiming for a very low-carbohydrate serving.
