# Greek okra and tomato stew

Whole okra pods simmer in a tomato and onion sauce.

## Preparation

1. Trim the okra caps without cutting into the pods. Rinse and drain. Toss with vinegar and coarse salt, cover and refrigerate for about an hour. Drain off the liquid and pat the pods dry.
2. Heat half the oil in a wide pot. Brown the okra for 3–4 minutes, moving the pods gently. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3–4 minutes. Add crushed garlic, then the tomato paste shortly after. Cook the paste for one minute.
3. Grate the tomatoes and add them with pepper. Simmer for about 25 minutes until tender and intact, shaking the pot gently from time to time.
4. Add the remaining oil and chopped parsley. Simmer for a few more minutes until the sauce thickens.

## Notes

- Check the vinegar label for sulphites.
