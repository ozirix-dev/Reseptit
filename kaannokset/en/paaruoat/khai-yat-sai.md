# Thai stuffed omelette (khai yat sai)

Thin omelettes are folded around a sweet-savoury pork and tomato filling. Makes two omelettes.

## Preparation

1. Chop the garlic and half an onion. Dice the tomato. Mix the fish sauce, sugar, white pepper and ketchup in a bowl.
2. Heat 30 ml oil in a frying pan and soften the onion and garlic. Add the pork and break it up with a spatula. Add the sauce and tomato. Keep frying until the pork is fully cooked to at least 70°C and there is no loose liquid in the pan. Transfer the filling to a clean dish.
3. Beat the eggs with the water. Heat 15 ml oil in a non-stick pan about 22 cm across. Pour in half the egg mixture and tilt the pan to spread it thinly. Cook over medium heat until the surface is set with no visible raw liquid egg.
4. Spoon half the filling onto the centre and fold the edges over it. Transfer to a plate. Fry and fill the second omelette with the remaining oil, egg mixture and filling. Serve immediately.

## Storage and safety

Contains egg and fish. Check the sauces for product-specific allergens. Wash your hands and utensils that touched raw pork before handling cooked food.
