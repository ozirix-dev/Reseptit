# Oven-roasted pork belly with crackling

## Description

Roast skin-on pork belly slowly until tender, then raise the heat to crisp the skin.

## Instructions

1. Pat the skin very dry. With a sharp knife, score it in a close crosshatch without cutting deep into the meat.
2. Rub salt over the skin and meat. Heat the oven to 150 °C.
3. Place the pork in a roasting dish, skin up. Cook for about 2.5–3 hours until tender and at least 70 °C in the thickest part.
4. Raise the oven to 230 °C and roast for about 10–25 minutes more until the skin bubbles and crisps. Watch continuously to prevent burning.
5. Rest for 10 minutes and cut into four equal portions. Divide the edible fat and juices from the roasting dish and resting plate equally among four small bowls. Serve them separately so the skin stays crisp.

## Notes

- The hot finish is needed because the skin may not crisp at the lower temperature.
- The estimate uses boneless skin-on HK Viljaporsaan belly (EAN 2391119000004). Check the fat content on the packaging if substituting.
- About 420 kcal per portion if the skin and all edible fat and juices are eaten. This is a raw-ingredient estimate. Leaving fat behind lowers fat and energy intake.
