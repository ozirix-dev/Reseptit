# Moroccan couscous with chicken and vegetables

This thigh-fillet version uses freshly boiled whole chickpeas. Boil them separately and fold them in hot at the end; do not cook them again in the stew for 15 minutes. Serve all listed stock in the portions. The named San Remo GI comparator uses a separate Fineli composition proxy; the result is a LOW-confidence estimate.

## Instructions

1. Boil whole dried chickpeas separately in salted water according to the packet until fully tender, using part of the measured salt. Drain and weigh 400 g cooked chickpeas. Time them to finish with the stew and use hot. Cooking time varies; the estimated total of 150 minutes excludes any packet-required soaking.
2. Cut 600 g skinless boneless thigh fillets into pieces. Brown in the olive oil in a pot. Add the chopped onion and soften.
3. Add cumin, coriander, turmeric, remaining salt and 1000 g prepared stock. Add the carrots and simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Add the courgette and cook for another 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked throughout and the vegetables are tender. Do not add the chickpeas yet.
5. Pour 375 ml boiling water over 250 g dry couscous. Stir, cover and stand for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork. Add no butter or extra oil.
6. Remove the stew from the heat and fold in all 400 g freshly boiled hot chickpeas. Divide the entire couscous, chicken, vegetables and all remaining broth into four deep bowls. Serve immediately; do not chill or reboil the chickpeas.

## Notes

Weights refer to edible portions. Drain chickpea cooking water before weighing; use cooked-food composition. No nutrient loss is credited to broth evaporation. Separate harissa or other sides are outside the estimate.
