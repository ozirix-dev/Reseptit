# Gombapaprikás, Hungarian mushroom paprikash

The mushrooms cook in their own juices. Sweet paprika flavours the stew and soured cream thickens the sauce.

## Preparation

1. Clean and slice the mushrooms. Chop the onion finely and dice the tomato and pepper. Soften the onion in the oil over low heat.
2. Take the pan off the heat briefly and stir in the paprika. Add the mushrooms, tomato, green pepper, salt and black pepper. Return to the heat, cover and simmer for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms are cooked.
3. Boil uncovered for about 5 minutes to reduce excess liquid. Mix the flour into the soured cream, loosen with a spoonful of cooking liquid and stir into the pan. Simmer gently, stirring, for a few more minutes until thickened.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk and wheat. Use cultivated mushrooms from a shop and cook them thoroughly. Cool leftovers quickly, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
