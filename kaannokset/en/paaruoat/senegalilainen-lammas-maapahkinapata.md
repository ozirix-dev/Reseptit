# Senegalese lamb and peanut stew (mafé)

## Description

Lamb and carrots simmer in a rich tomato and peanut sauce.

## Preparation

1. Cut the lamb into large pieces and brown in batches in the oil. Transfer to a plate. Soften the chopped onion, garlic and diced pepper in the same pot for about 6 minutes.
2. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for a minute. Return the lamb, add the water and salt, bring to a boil and simmer gently, covered, for about 80 minutes.
3. Add the carrots cut into thick pieces. Loosen the peanut butter with a ladleful of broth in a bowl and stir it into the stew. Simmer uncovered for 25–35 minutes until the lamb and carrots are tender, stirring along the bottom occasionally.
