# Pan-fried beef liver and bacon

## Description

Brown sliced beef liver in the bacon fat and butter. Use a thermometer to check doneness.

## Instructions

1. Remove any membranes from the liver. Weigh 400 g, cut into slices about 1 cm thick and pat them dry.
2. Fry the bacon over medium heat until crisp. Transfer it to a plate, leaving the fat in the pan.
3. Add butter and fry the liver for about 2–3 minutes per side, depending on thickness.
4. Lower the heat and continue as needed until the internal temperature reaches at least 72 °C. Measure the thickest slice.
5. Return the bacon to the pan and heat it. Divide the liver, bacon and all edible butter, rendered fat and juices into three portions. Divide the 1 g salt evenly too.

## Notes

- Avoid liver dishes throughout pregnancy because of their high vitamin A content. See the Finnish Food Authority's guidance.
- Use bacon containing only pork and salt. The estimate uses Canadian Old Country Dry Salted Bacon as a fatty reference. Check your own bacon label because fat content can differ substantially.
- About 566 kcal per portion with the reference bacon and all edible fat and juices. Liver contributes about 6.7 g carbohydrate per portion. GL is undetermined.
