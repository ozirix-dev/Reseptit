# Southern Thai sour fish curry with bamboo shoots (gaeng som)

A bright, broth-like curry of turmeric, chilli and lime, without coconut milk.

## Instructions

1. Cut the fish crosswise into four pieces. Rinse and drain the canned bamboo shoots; use only a fully cooked product.
2. Stem the chillies. Peel the garlic and shallots, chop the turmeric and blend with the chillies and 300 ml of the measured water. Wear gloves and avoid chilli vapour.
3. Put the paste into a pot of at least 4 litres. Add the remaining water, kapi and bamboo shoots. Bring to a boil and gently add the fish. Simmer for 5–8 minutes or longer until the thickest piece reaches at least 63 °C.
4. Remove from the heat and season with the measured lime juice and salt to taste. Divide the fish and broth into four portions; watch for bones.

## Notes

- Contains fish and crustaceans. Very hot. Fish weight is whole-fish weight; rice is not included. The source allows bamboo shoots instead of green papaya. The 500 g drained quantity and four portions are editorial adaptations; the green-papaya version remains on a separate sourcing hold.
