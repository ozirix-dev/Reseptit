# Mushroom and barley cabbage rolls

Button mushrooms, cooked barley and Emmental fill tender cabbage leaves. A little dark syrup in the baking liquid gives a gentle sweetness.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the cabbage, discard any damaged outer leaves and cut out the core. Put the whole cabbage in a large pot of hot water. Simmer gently and lift off the softened leaves one at a time with tongs. Set aside 15 whole leaves and shave down the thickest ribs.
2. Chop the mushrooms, onion and garlic. Stir-fry the mushrooms until most of the liquid they release has evaporated. Add the oil, root vegetables, onion and garlic. Cook, stirring, for 5–10 minutes until the vegetables are hot and tender.
3. Stir the barley, oregano, salt and pepper into the pan. Take the filling off the heat and let it cool a little. Stir in the grated cheese.
4. Heat the oven to 175 °C. Scrape all the filling from the pan and divide it between the 15 leaves. Fold the sides of each leaf over the filling and roll it up. Put the rolls seam-side down in a baking dish.
5. Mix the syrup with 400 ml water and pour around the rolls. Bake for about 90 minutes. Baste a few times and cover roughly halfway through once the rolls have browned. Check that the cabbage is tender and the centre of the filling reaches at least 74 °C. One serving is one roll.

## Storage and safety

Contains barley, milk and celery. Use cooked barley immediately, or cool it promptly and refrigerate it for later use. Cool leftovers quickly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
