# Georgian chicken and tomato stew (chakhokhbili)

## Description

Chicken simmers with onion and tomato. Fresh herbs are added just before serving.

## Preparation

1. Slice the onion and chop the chicken. Brown the chicken in batches in the oil and transfer to a plate.
2. Soften the onion in the pan for about 8 minutes. Add chopped garlic, the canned tomatoes, salt and pepper. Return the chicken to the sauce.
3. Cover and simmer for about 20–25 minutes until the largest pieces reach at least 74 °C in the centre. Stir in chopped coriander leaves and basil just before serving.
