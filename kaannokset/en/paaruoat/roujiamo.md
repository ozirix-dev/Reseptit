# Roujiamo – Xi’an braised pork sandwiches

Renowned for Xi'an street food, roujiamo fills a crispy pancake with long-braised, spicy pork.

## Description

Renowned for Xi'an street food, roujiamo fills a crispy pancake with long-braised, spicy pork.

## Preparation

1. Put pork, water, soy sauces, cinnamon, star anise, cumin and salt in a pot. Boil and simmer under the lid for about 2 hours, until the meat falls apart easily.
2. Mix the flour, warm water and yeast. Knead into a smooth dough and let rise covered for about 45 minutes.
3. Divide the dough into four parts. Roll into round loaves, about a centimeter thick.
4. Fry the breads in a dry pan on medium heat for about 4-5 minutes on each side, until they are cooked and have brown spots.
5. Chop the stewed meat and mix in a little cooking stock and spring onion. Split the breads and fill with meat.

## Notes

- This home version uses pan-fried wheat bread. Traditional breads may have regional differences.
