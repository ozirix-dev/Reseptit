# Traditional Finnish Baltic Herring and Potato Bake

A traditional Finnish casserole of thinly sliced potatoes, boneless Baltic herring, onion and bacon, baked in an egg-and-milk mixture.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Grease an oven dish of about 3 litres with butter. Check the Baltic herring fillets for any remaining bones and remove them.
2. Slice the peeled potatoes about 2–3 mm thick. Chop the onion, cut the herring into pieces, slice the bacon and chop the dill.
3. Layer half of the potatoes in the dish, followed by the herring, onion, half the bacon and dill. Add the allspice and white pepper between the layers. Cover with the remaining potatoes and bacon.
4. Whisk the eggs and milk until evenly mixed and stir in the salt. Pour the egg-and-milk mixture evenly over the layers.
5. Bake at 175°C for about 80–90 minutes until the potatoes are tender throughout, the fish is cooked and the egg-and-milk mixture has fully set. Cover loosely with foil if the surface browns too quickly. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- **Fish intake advice:** The Finnish Food Authority (updated 2 January 2025) advises that preschool children and women of childbearing age, including those pregnant or breastfeeding, eat large Baltic herring (over 19 cm before gutting) no more than once every two months. Check the full official guidance. Contains fish, milk, eggs and pork bacon.
- Serve the fully cooked bake hot. Cool leftovers promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate below +6°C. Check the herring catch area and, if necessary, the original ungutted fish size with the supplier; the special over-19-cm guidance applies to the groups named by the authority.
