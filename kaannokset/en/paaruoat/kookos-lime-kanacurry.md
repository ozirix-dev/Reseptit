# Coconut and lime chicken curry

## Description

This coconut milk chicken curry balances tangy lime, spicy curry paste and fragrant fresh basil.

## Preparation

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and slice the bell pepper into strips. Fry the curry paste in the coconut oil for one minute.
2. Add half the coconut milk and stir until smooth. Add the chicken and cook for 5 minutes.
3. Pour in the rest of the coconut milk. Add the beans and bell pepper and simmer for 10–12 minutes until cooked through.
4. Season with fish sauce and lime juice. Add torn Thai basil just before serving.

## Notes

Curry pastes vary in heat and saltiness. Add the paste gradually and taste as you go.
