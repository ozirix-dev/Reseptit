# Kua gling, Southern Thai dry pork curry

A turmeric and chile paste coats the pork as liquid cooks off. Lemongrass and makrut lime leaves give this hot curry a fresh fragrance.

## Preparation

1. Grind the dried chiles and peppercorns in a spice mill. Slice the lemongrass very thinly. Pull the stalks from the lime leaves and slice the leaves into fine strips.
2. Pound half the lemongrass, the galangal, turmeric and salt in a mortar. Add the ground chile and pepper. Pound in the shallots and garlic, then work in the shrimp paste.
3. Heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan over medium heat. Stir-fry the paste for a couple of minutes. Add a splash of the measured water if the paste starts to stick.
4. Add the pork and break it up with a spatula. Add the sugar and the rest of the measured water. Stir-fry until the pork is cooked throughout, the centre of a mound of pork measures at least 71 °C and any liquid pooled in the pan has evaporated.
5. Stir in the fish sauce, lime leaves and remaining lemongrass. Heat briefly and divide into four servings. Serve with separately cooked jasmine rice if desired.

## Storage and safety

Contains crustaceans and fish. Keep raw ground pork separate from other ingredients and wash the utensils used to handle it. Wash your hands after handling the chiles and ventilate the kitchen while frying. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
