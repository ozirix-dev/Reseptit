# Icelandic fish and potato stew (plokkfiskur)

## Description

Flakes of cod and diced potato are folded into a creamy onion and milk sauce.

## Preparation

1. Dice the peeled potatoes. Boil them in water for about 12–15 minutes until tender, then drain.
2. Poach the boneless cod separately in barely simmering water until the centre reaches at least 63 °C. Drain and break it into flakes with a fork.
3. Chop the onion and soften it in the butter. Stir in the flour and cook for one minute. Gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer the sauce for about 5 minutes, stirring.
4. Add the fish, potato, salt and pepper. Heat through and stir gently so some pieces of fish remain intact.
