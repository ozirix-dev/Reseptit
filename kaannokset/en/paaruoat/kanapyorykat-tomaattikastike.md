# Chicken meatballs in tomato and cream sauce

## Description

Chicken meatballs in a tomato and cream sauce. This precisely weighed recipe makes four servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredient quantities are stored in the structured serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Mix 500 g ground chicken with 55 g egg without shell, 30 g Parmesan and 1 g dried oregano. Shape into meatballs.
2. Gently sauté 55 g onion in 13.5 g oil. Add 400 g crushed tomatoes, 100 g liquid whipping cream and 0.5 g ground black pepper.
3. Cook the meatballs thoroughly in the sauce until fully cooked throughout. Divide all the meatballs and sauce equally into four servings.
