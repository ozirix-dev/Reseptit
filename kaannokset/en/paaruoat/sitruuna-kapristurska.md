# Cod with lemon and capers

Pan-cooked cod is served with a buttery lemon and caper sauce.

## Preparation

1. Pat fish dry and check for bones. Dust lightly with flour. Heat oil and 10 g butter in a pan.
2. Fry over medium heat initially 2–3 min per side; continue until the thickest part reaches 63 °C. Transfer to a clean plate.
3. Melt 20 g butter, cook capers 1–2 min. Add stock and reduce about 3 min. Stir in lemon juice, parsley and 10 g butter. Serve over fish.

## Notes

- Contains fish, wheat and milk; stock may contain celery.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
