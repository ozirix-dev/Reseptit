# Juicy Low-Carb Meatloaf

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Weigh all edible ingredients in grams. Grate the courgette and retain it all without squeezing.
2. Mix all listed ingredients, including all the weighed ground pepper and paprika, into an even mixture.
3. Shape the loaf in a baking-paper-lined tin without extra grease.
4. Bake for about 50 minutes until cooked through, rest for 10 minutes and divide into 6 portions. Consume the whole mixture and pan juices.
