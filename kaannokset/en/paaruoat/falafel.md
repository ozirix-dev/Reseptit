# Middle Eastern falafel with tahini sauce

Falafel are made from dried chickpeas soaked overnight, fresh herbs and spices. Do not cook the chickpeas before shaping the mixture; this helps the balls hold together as they bake.

## Instructions

1. Rinse the chickpeas, then soak them in plenty of cold water in the refrigerator for 12 hours. Drain and dry them thoroughly.
2. Pulse the chickpeas in a food processor with the onion, garlic, parsley, fresh coriander, cumin, ground coriander, baking powder, salt and lemon juice until you have a coarse mixture that can be shaped.
3. Cover the mixture and leave it in the refrigerator for 30 minutes. Shape into approximately 20 small balls.
4. Mix the tahini and the lemon juice allocated to the sauce. Gradually add the cold water until the sauce is smooth and pourable.
5. Heat the oven to 220 °C. Brush the falafel all over with the measured oil and bake on parchment paper for 20–25 minutes, until cooked through and browned. Turn them once during baking.
6. Serve with the tahini sauce, salad and pita bread.

## Notes

- Use dried chickpeas that have been soaked. Canned chickpeas are too moist for this dough and may cause the falafel to fall apart.
