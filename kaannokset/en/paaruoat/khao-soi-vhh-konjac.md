# Low-carb Khao Soi with chicken and konjac noodles

Khao soi is a northern Thai curry noodle soup. This low-carb variation is made with konjac noodles instead of traditional noodles. Rich coconut broth, chicken and pickled mustard cabbage retain the familiar flavor combination.

## Description

Red curry paste, curry powder and turmeric form the readily available Khao soi spice base.

## Preparation

1. Fry the curry paste, curry powder and turmeric in a pot on low heat for a minute.
2. Add coconut milk and mix. Add chicken and chicken stock. Bring to a boil.
3. Simmer under the lid for about 30 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
4. Season with fish sauce, lime juice and erythritol.
5. Rinse and drain the noodles. Dry fry in a pan for a few minutes and divide into bowls.
6. Place the chicken on the noodles and pour the hot broth over it. Finish with shallots and mustard cabbage.

## Notes

- konjac noodles replace traditional noodles. The sugar content of curry paste and pickled cabbage varies.
- The nutritional values ​​are indicative.
