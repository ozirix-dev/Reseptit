# Pan-seared scallops

Scallops are seared golden and served with lemon.

## Preparation

1. Thaw frozen scallops in the refrigerator. Remove any tough side muscle, pat dry and season.
2. Heat oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Sear in a single layer for about 2 minutes on the first side, in batches if needed.
3. Turn, add butter and spoon the melting butter over the scallops. Continue until the centre of the largest scallop reaches 63 °C and flesh is firm, pale and opaque throughout. Do not judge doneness from the crust alone.
4. Serve three scallops per person with lemon wedges.

## Notes

- Contains molluscs and milk.
- Keep raw seafood chilled, thaw in the refrigerator and use clean utensils for cooked food. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours at 0–5 °C; use by the next day and reheat once to 74 °C.
