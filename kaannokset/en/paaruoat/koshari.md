# Egyptian koshari with rice, lentils and pasta

Koshari gathers rice, lentils and pasta in the same bowl. Spicy tomato sauce, garlic vinegar and crispy onion complete the dish.

## Description

Koshari gathers rice, lentils and pasta in the same bowl. Spicy tomato sauce, garlic vinegar and crispy onion complete the dish.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the lentils and boil them in water until they are cooked but tender, about 20–25 minutes. Drain.
2. Cook the rice until cooked. Cook the macaroni in a separate pot according to the instructions on the package and drain.
3. Saute one clove of garlic in a small pot in a splash of oil. Add crushed tomatoes, cumin, salt and lemon juice. Simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Mix the rest of the garlic with the vinegar. Fry onion rings wrapped in cornstarch in oil until crispy. Drain with paper towels.
5. Put rice, lentils and macaroni in bowls. Spoon tomato sauce and garlic vinegar on top. Finish with crispy onions.

## Notes

- Offer the sauces separately so that everyone can adjust the acidity and hotness of the dish.
