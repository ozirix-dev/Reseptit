# Koshari – uncoated onion version

## Description

Koshari – uncoated onion version.

## Ingredients

## Instructions

1. For this uncoated onion version, cook 200 g whole dry brown or green lentils until fully tender. Cook 200 g dry white jasmine rice as hot separate grains and boil 150 g dry durum wheat macaroni according to the pack, then drain.
2. Brown 110 g onion in 40.5 g olive oil without a corn-starch coating. Make the sauce with all 400 g crushed tomatoes, 7 g garlic, 30 g red wine vinegar, 2 g cumin and 15 g lemon juice.
3. Combine all hot components into four portions. Consume all onion oil and sauce. The dry-ingredient composition proxy gives no deduction for unmeasured leaching.
