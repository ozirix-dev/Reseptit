# Beef meatza with bacon and mozzarella

## Description

A baked beef base topped with crisp bacon and melted mozzarella, for three servings.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C. Fry the bacon until crisp without extra fat. Transfer it to a plate and save the fat and juices in a heatproof container.
2. Gently mix the salt into the beef. Press it into a round 22–24 cm base about 1 cm thick on a rimmed tray lined with baking paper.
3. Bake on the middle rack for about 12–18 minutes. Insert a thermometer sideways into the thickest part and continue until the centre reaches at least 75 °C.
4. Carefully pour the tray fat and juices into the same container as the bacon drippings. Drain the mozzarella, weigh 90 g and pat dry. Spread chopped bacon and torn mozzarella over the cooked base.
5. Bake about 3–5 minutes more until the cheese melts. Rest briefly and cut into three portions. Divide all saved edible fat and juices, including from the final bake, among three bowls and serve alongside.

## Notes

- This version contains dairy. The chosen sugar-free bacon contains additives, and both bacon and mozzarella labels list 1 g carbohydrate per 100 g.
- About 572 kcal per portion if all saved edible fat and juices are eaten. The estimate uses raw ingredients. Leaving fat behind reduces fat and energy intake.
