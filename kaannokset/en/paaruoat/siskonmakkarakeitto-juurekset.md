# Finnish fresh-sausage soup with root vegetables

## Description

A soup of siskonmakkara, swede, carrot and celeriac.

## Preparation

1. Peel the swede, carrot and celeriac. Cut into small even cubes. Chop the onion.
2. Bring the stock, onion and bay leaf to the boil. Add the root vegetables and cook for 12–15 minutes.
3. Squeeze small pieces of fresh sausage directly into the boiling soup.
4. Cook for about another 8 minutes, until the sausage is fully cooked and the vegetables are tender.
5. Season with pepper, remove the bay leaf and add parsley.
