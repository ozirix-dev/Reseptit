# Brazilian moqueca fish stew

This Brazilian-style fish stew combines coconut milk, tomatoes, peppers and lime.

## Preparation

1. Cut fish into 2.5 cm pieces. Mix fish lime juice and salt, marinate covered in fridge 20 min.
2. Heat frying oil in pan. Sear fish, continue cooking until thickest part reaches 63 °C. Transfer to clean plate.
3. Soften chopped garlic and onion in stew oil 1–2 min; add sliced pepper for 2 min. Add sugar, cumin, paprika, optional cayenne, stew salt, coconut milk, tomatoes and fish stock. Simmer 15–20 min.
4. Return fish and heat about 2 min or until hot throughout. Finish with remaining lime juice and coriander; divide in four.

## Notes

- Contains fish. Check stock allergens. Rice or other sides not included.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
