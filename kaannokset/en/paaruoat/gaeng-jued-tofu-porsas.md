# Thai clear pork, tofu and cabbage soup

A mild clear soup with ground pork, firm tofu and napa cabbage.

## Preparation

1. Soften onions and garlic in oil. Add water and stock and bring to a boil.
2. Mix pork, pepper and soy. Drop small portions into the broth and cook until the meat reaches at least 71 °C throughout.
3. Cut tofu and simmer in the soup about 5 min. Add washed napa cabbage and cook until tender. Season with fish sauce and coriander; divide into six.

## Notes

- Contains fish and soy; check stock and soy sauce for wheat/celery. Napa cabbage, pork gram weight and oil are editorial adaptations; not vegetarian.
