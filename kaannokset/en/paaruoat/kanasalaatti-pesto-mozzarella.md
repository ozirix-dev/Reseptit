# Pesto chicken salad with mozzarella

## Description

Warm pesto chicken complements rocket, mozzarella and avocado in this substantial salad.

## Preparation

1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Pan-fry in the oil until cooked through and let it rest briefly.
2. Mix the pesto and lemon juice to make the dressing. Thin with a splash of water if needed.
3. Arrange the rocket, halved tomatoes, sliced avocado and mozzarella on a serving platter.
4. Slice the chicken, add it to the salad and drizzle over the dressing.

## Notes

Serve straight away to keep the salad fresh.
