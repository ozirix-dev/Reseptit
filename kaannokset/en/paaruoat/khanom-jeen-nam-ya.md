# Nam ya kati – Thai coconut fish curry with rice noodles

Flaked cooked fish thickens a spicy coconut sauce, served here with dried rice vermicelli instead of fresh khanom jeen.

## Instructions

1. Cut the gutted fish and poach in a separate pot of water until the thickest part reaches at least 63 °C. Drain and cool only until safe to handle. Remove skin and all bones; flake thoroughly.
2. Chop the tender pale lemongrass, turmeric and peeled garlic. Grind with chillies, pepper and a pinch of the measured salt, then add kapi. Protect hands and avoid chilli dust.
3. Mix coconut milk and measured water in a pot of at least 4 litres. Add coconut cream, paste, fish and washed lime leaves. Bring to a boil while stirring and simmer for 5 minutes. Adjust with the remaining salt if needed; remove the lime leaves.
4. Cook the noodles according to the package. Wash cucumber and basil; slice cucumber. Divide noodles among four plates, spoon over hot curry and serve with vegetables.

## Notes

- Contains fish and crustaceans. Fish weight is whole-fish weight, not edible yield. The 300 g dried vermicelli, dilution and vegetable quantities are editorial choices. Separate fish cooking water is not used in the sauce. Fresh khanom jeen and fried chilli garnish are omitted.
