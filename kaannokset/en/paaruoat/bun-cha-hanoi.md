# Hanoi bún chả with grilled pork and rice noodles

Pork patties and thin slices of pork belly are grilled and served with rice vermicelli, herbs and a sweet-and-sour fish-sauce dip. This home version uses readily available ingredients.

## Instructions

1. Mix the ground pork with **half of the fish sauce for the meat marinade**, half the brown sugar, half the garlic and shallots, and the black pepper. Shape into small, flat patties.
2. Coat the sliced pork belly with the **remaining meat-marinade ingredients**. Refrigerate both meats for **at least 30 minutes**.
3. Combine the separately listed dipping-broth fish sauce, rice vinegar, sugar and warm water. Add the carrot slices.
4. Cook the rice vermicelli according to the packet instructions and rinse with cold water.
5. Grill or pan-fry the patties and pork belly in batches until cooked through and lightly browned.
6. Serve the pork in the dipping broth or alongside it. Serve the noodles and fresh coriander separately.

## Notes

- Use a separate chopping board for raw pork and cooked ingredients. Cook the pork completely through.
