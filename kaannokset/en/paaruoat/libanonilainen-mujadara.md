# Lebanese lentils and rice with browned onions (mujadara)

Brown lentils and short-grain rice cook together in one pot, then get a topping of deeply browned, sweet onions.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the lentils and rice separately. Thinly slice one onion into half-moons and dice the other. Heat the oil in a heavy saucepan. Fry the sliced onion over medium heat, stirring often, until deep golden brown. Lift the slices onto a plate and leave the oil in the pan.
2. Soften the diced onion in the remaining oil. Add the lentils, stir briefly and add 360 ml water. Cook for about 10 minutes until most of the water has been absorbed.
3. Add the rice, cumin, salt, pepper and 480 ml water. Boil over medium heat until little water is visible. Cover and simmer on low for about 10 minutes. Check that both the rice and lentils are tender. Add a splash of hot water and cook a little longer if needed.
4. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork, divide into four portions and top with the browned onion.

## Storage and safety

Serve cooked rice immediately or divide leftovers into shallow containers, cool quickly and refrigerate. Reheat portions until steaming hot throughout.
