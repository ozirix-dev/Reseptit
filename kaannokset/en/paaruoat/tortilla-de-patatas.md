# Tortilla de patatas – pre-boiled potato version

## Description

Tortilla de patatas – pre-boiled potato version.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Boil 600 g raw peeled potato in 1000 g water until cooked, then drain. No unmeasured leaching loss is deducted from the raw-potato macros.
2. Soften 55 g onion in 67.5 g olive oil. Add the cooked potato, 330 g egg without shell and 0.5 g ground black pepper.
3. Cook just until the egg is fully set; do not cook the potatoes for a long time in oil. Divide all food and oil into four portions. This is the pre-boiled potato version.
