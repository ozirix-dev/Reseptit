# Spanish potato and onion omelette

A Spanish tortilla is slowly cooked from potatoes, onions and eggs. It tastes warm or at room temperature.

## Description

A Spanish tortilla is slowly cooked from potatoes, onions and eggs. It tastes warm or at room temperature.

## Preparation

1. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a frying pan. Add the potatoes and onion, cover and cook on low heat for about 20 minutes, turning occasionally.
2. Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt and pepper. Drain the soft potatoes and onion from excess oil and mix with the eggs.
3. Heat the rest of the oil in a pan. Pour the mixture into the pan and cook over low heat until the edges set.
4. Put the plate on the pan and turn the tortilla onto the plate. Slide it back into the pan and cook the other side for 3-5 minutes.
5. Let the tortilla rest for a few minutes before cutting.

## Notes

- If you want a fully cooked center, use low heat and extend the cooking time.
