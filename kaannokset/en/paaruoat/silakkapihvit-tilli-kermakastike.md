# Baltic herring patties with dill cream sauce

## Description

Crisp pan-fried Baltic herring with a quickly prepared creamy dill sauce.

## Ingredients

The serving calculator above contains all ingredient quantities.

## Instructions

1. Season the Baltic herring fillets with salt and white pepper. Fold the fillets in pairs, skin sides facing out.
2. Lightly coat the fillets with rye flour. Fry in butter over medium heat for **3–4 minutes on each side**.
3. Transfer the fish to a warm serving dish.
4. Pour the cream into the frying pan and bring to a boil. Add lemon juice and dill; season with pepper.
5. Serve the cream sauce with the herring and squeeze lemon over the dish.

## Notes

Rye flour makes the surface crisp, but this serving is **not gluten-free and not low-carbohydrate**.
