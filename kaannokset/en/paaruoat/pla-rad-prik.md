# Thai fried fish fillets with chilli-tamarind sauce

Crisp white fish fillets are topped with a spicy sweet-sour sauce.

## Preparation

1. Pat thawed fish thoroughly dry and check for bones. Coat with starch and shake off excess.
2. Heat frying oil over medium heat in a high-sided pan, no more than a quarter full. Fry fillets in batches, turn carefully and cook until crisp and the thickest part reaches 63 °C. Drain on a rack.
3. Chop garlic and chilli. Fry in the sauce oil in a clean saucepan, add palm sugar, tamarind, fish sauce and 5 ml water. Heat to dissolve sugar. Mix sauce starch with 15 ml cold water, add and bring to a boil while stirring. Divide fish and spoon sauce over.
