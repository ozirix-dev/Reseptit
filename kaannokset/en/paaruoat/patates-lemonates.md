# Patates lemonates, Greek lemon potatoes

Potato wedges bake in a lemon and oregano liquid. As it reduces, the edges brown and the centres stay tender.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the potatoes lengthwise into thick, evenly sized wedges and spread in one layer in a metal roasting tin.
2. Chop the garlic. Whisk the oil, water, lemon juice, garlic, semolina, salt, pepper and 10 ml oregano in a bowl. Pour over the potatoes and turn the wedges to coat them.
3. Roast on the middle shelf for about 40 minutes. Turn the wedges with a spatula and sprinkle with the remaining 5 ml oregano.
4. Roast another 30–40 minutes, until the wedges are soft inside and golden at the edges. If the tin dries out before the potatoes are tender, carefully pour in a little hot water at the edge. Divide into six servings.

## Storage and safety

Semolina contains wheat. Discard green or damaged potatoes. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
