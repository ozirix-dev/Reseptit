# Finnish-style liver bake with cauliflower mash

## Description

Inspired by Finnish liver casserole: browned liver, onion and marjoram provide flavour, while cauliflower replaces rice to make a soft-textured bake.

## Ingredients

The ingredient quantities are available in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Steam the cauliflower until tender and mash it coarsely.
2. Finely chop the onion and sauté it in butter. Cut the liver into small pieces and brown it in a frying pan, cooking it through.
3. Combine cauliflower, liver, onion, liquid whipping cream and egg. Season with marjoram, salt and allspice.
4. Transfer to a buttered ovenproof dish and bake for 35–40 minutes, until the centre has set.
5. Leave to rest for 10 minutes and serve with the lingonberries.

## Notes

This is inspired by a traditional Finnish liver casserole but contains neither the customary rice nor raisins.
