# Parmesan chicken and broccoli skillet

## Description

Chicken and broccoli simmer in a creamy Parmesan sauce in a single pan.

## Preparation

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and divide the broccoli into small florets.
2. Brown the chicken in butter in a frying pan. Add the garlic and fry for 30 seconds.
3. Pour in the chicken stock and cream. Add the broccoli and simmer, covered, for 8–10 minutes.
4. Stir in the Parmesan and heat until the cheese melts and the chicken is cooked through.
5. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

If the sauce gets too thick, add a splash of water or chicken stock.
