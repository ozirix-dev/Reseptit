# Thai stir-fried long aubergine with basil

Tender aubergine and ground pork are seasoned with fermented soybeans, oyster sauce and fresh Thai basil.

## Preparation

1. Wash and dry the aubergine and slice it. Heat about a third of the oil in a pan over medium heat. Fry the slices in small batches until they begin to soften, then set them aside.
2. Add the remaining oil. Briefly fry the garlic and chilli, add the pork and break it up with a spatula. Cook the pork through to at least 70 °C. Add the bean paste, oyster sauce, sugar and water and stir.
3. Return the aubergine and simmer until the slices are cooked and tender. Stir in the basil at the end, heat briefly and serve hot.

## Storage and safety

Contains soy and molluscs. The sauces may contain wheat. Keep raw ground pork separate from the other ingredients. Keep the oil below smoking. If it starts to smoke, turn off the heat and leave the pan where it is to cool.
