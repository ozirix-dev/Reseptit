# VHH yellow chicken curry with cauliflower

Gaeng garee or yellow curry is a mildly spicy coconut curry. In this VHH version, cauliflower is used instead of potato, which absorbs the aromatic sauce.

## Description

Cauliflower cooks soft in curry but keeps its shape. If you like a firmer texture, add cauliflower only towards the end of cooking.

## Preparation

1. Heat coconut oil in a pot. Add yellow curry paste and turmeric. Stir fry for a minute.
2. Add the chicken and stir in the paste for a few minutes.
3. Pour in the coconut milk. Add the onion and simmer on low heat for 15 minutes.
4. Add the cauliflower and continue cooking for 8-10 minutes until the chicken and cauliflower are cooked through.
5. Season with fish sauce and, if desired, erythritol. Serve with cilantro on its own or as an addition to cauliflower rice.

## Notes

- Cauliflower replaces the potato that is often used in yellow curries. Check the carbs in curry paste and coconut milk.
