# Finnish Pan-Fried Vendace in Rye Crumbs

A traditional Finnish home cooking recipe.

## Preparation

1. Keep the fresh fish chilled. Check that the vendace are gutted, rinse briefly if needed and pat thoroughly dry.
2. Combine rye flour, breadcrumbs, salt and white pepper. Coat the vendace immediately before frying.
3. Heat some butter and oil in a pan over medium heat. Fry fish in batches for about 2–3 minutes per side until fully cooked.
4. Add the remaining butter and oil as needed between batches. Serve the hot vendace immediately, for example with boiled potatoes.
