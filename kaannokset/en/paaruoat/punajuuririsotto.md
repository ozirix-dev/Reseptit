# Beetroot risotto

## Description

Beetroot gives this risotto its colour and mellow flavour. Purée half the beetroot and dice the rest.

## Preparation

1. Wash the beetroot and boil it unpeeled in separate cooking water for 40–45 minutes or until a knife slides into the centre. Drain, cool until it can be handled and peel. Purée half and dice the rest.
2. Finely chop the onion and garlic and strip the thyme leaves. Heat 9 dl water and stir in the stock concentrate. Keep the stock hot.
3. Soften the onion and garlic in olive oil. Add the rice and stir for a couple of minutes. Add 2 dl water and the lemon juice and wait until almost absorbed. Stir in the beetroot purée and thyme.
4. Add the hot stock a ladle at a time. Stir frequently, adding the next ladle when the previous one is almost absorbed. After about 25 minutes the rice should be cooked with a little bite.
5. Add the diced beetroot and heat through. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the Parmesan and season with salt and pepper. Serve straight away.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Check the stock concentrate for allergens such as celery. Cool any leftovers quickly in a small container and refrigerate.
