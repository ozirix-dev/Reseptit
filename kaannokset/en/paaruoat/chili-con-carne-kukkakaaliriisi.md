# Chili con carne with cauliflower rice – weighed recipe

## Description

An ingredient-weighed chili con carne with cauliflower rice. Makes four servings.

## Ingredients

The quantities above are also available in the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Brown 500 g ground beef and 55 g onion in all 27 g olive oil. Add 120 g bell pepper, 400 g crushed tomatoes, 2 g ground cumin, 1 g smoked paprika and 0.5 g ground chilli.
2. Add 200 g whole, cooked and drained kidney beans. Simmer until fully cooked. Finely chop 500 g cauliflower and steam it separately until tender.
3. Divide all the chili sauce and cauliflower evenly between four servings. Keep the kidney beans whole; do not drain off the fat or sauce.
