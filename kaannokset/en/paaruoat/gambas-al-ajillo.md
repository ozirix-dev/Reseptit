# Gambas al ajillo

Spanish-style prawns cook in olive oil flavoured with garlic and chilli.

## Preparation

1. Pat thawed prawns dry and toss with two grated garlic cloves and salt.
2. Warm oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Slice remaining four cloves and cook 3–4 min until pale golden. Add chilli.
3. Cook prawns in one layer, in batches if needed, turning for about 4 min or longer until every prawn is pearly white and opaque throughout. Remove from heat, add lemon juice and parsley. Divide prawns and serving oil into four.

## Notes

- Contains crustaceans. Do not store garlic oil at room temperature. Bread not included.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
