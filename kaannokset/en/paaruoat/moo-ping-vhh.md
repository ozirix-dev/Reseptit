# Moo ping – pan-cooked with all marinade

## Description

## Instructions

1. Weigh all listed edible ingredients. Do not add unlisted oil or side dishes.
2. Mix coconut milk, fish sauce, soy sauce, garlic, coriander stems, black pepper and erythritol. Marinate the pork for 30 min. This version explicitly uses black pepper.
3. Pan-cook in the rapeseed oil. Add all marinade and cook both meat and marinade that touched raw meat thoroughly. Do not drain any marinade or estimate grill losses.
4. Serve with 200 g cucumber and 60 g peeled lime flesh and juice. Consume every listed edible part and divide into four portions.
5. Cook every part that contains raw animal ingredients thoroughly. Consume all ingredients and pan juices. Raw ingredient composition is a nutrient-retention proxy; unmeasured losses are not deducted.
