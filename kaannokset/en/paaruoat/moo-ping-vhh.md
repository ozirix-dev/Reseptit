# low-carb-moo ping – Thai pork skewers

Moo ping is grilled pork skewers seasoned in coconut milk and garlic. In this version, the sweetness of the marinade comes from erythritol instead of palm sugar.

## Description

Thin slices of meat cook quickly. Wooden sticks should be soaked in water before grilling.

## Preparation

1. Soak the skewers in water for at least 20 minutes.
2. Mix coconut milk, fish sauce, soy sauce, garlic, coriander, white pepper and erythritol.
3. Add the pork slices to the marinade and leave to marinate for at least 30 minutes.
4. Thread the meat onto skewers. Grill on medium heat or fry on a hot grill until the meat is cooked and caramelized on the surface.
5. Serve with cucumber and lime.

## Notes

- Replacing the traditional palm sugar with erythritol makes this a low-carb variation and affects the caramelization.
- Check the labels on the sauces. The nutritional values ​​are indicative.
