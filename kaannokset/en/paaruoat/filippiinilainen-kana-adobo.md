# Filipino chicken adobo

Chicken thighs braise in a tangy soy, vinegar, garlic and bay leaf sauce.

## Instructions

1. Crush peeled garlic. Mix vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, peppercorns and bay leaves in a pot. Add chicken and refrigerate covered for 1 hour. Do not rinse raw chicken; wash hands and utensils.
2. Bring chicken and marinade to a boil, reduce heat and simmer covered for about 30 minutes. Turn the pieces carefully from time to time.
3. Uncover and simmer another 20 minutes or longer until the sauce reduces and the thickest part of every piece reaches at least 75 °C without touching bone. If sauce reduces before the chicken is done, cover and lower the heat.
4. Remove bay leaves and divide chicken and sauce into six servings. Watch for bones.

## Notes

- Contains soy; check soy sauce for wheat. The source's 4–5 lb quantity is specified as 2 kg and its 6–8 servings as six. Rice is excluded; chicken weight includes bones.
