# Oven-braised beef short ribs

## Description

Bone-in beef short ribs braise slowly in a covered pot until the meat comes away from the bone easily.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 150 °C. Pat the ribs dry and rub the salt over all sides.
2. Pack the ribs bone side down in a lidded ovenproof pot. Pour 400 g water into the bottom. Use a pot that leaves part of the meat above the waterline.
3. Cover tightly and braise on the middle rack for about 4–4.5 hours.
4. The ribs are ready when a fork slides in easily and the meat comes away from the bone without pulling hard. If needed, continue in 20–30 minute intervals.
5. Lift out the ribs and rest for a few minutes. Divide the ribs, all edible braising liquid and the rendered fat into four portions.

## Notes

- Use fatty bone-in short ribs. The USDA Choice reference assumes 27% bone by raw weight, or about 876 g edible material from 1,200 g. Bone and fat proportions vary.
- The estimate is about 850 kcal per portion if all edible meat, juices and rendered fat are eaten. Removing fat reduces the estimate.
