# Indonesian sate ayam skewers with peanut sauce

The chicken skewers are marinated in a sweet-and-savoury soy-and-sugar mixture inspired by kecap manis, then grilled. Serve with a creamy peanut sauce.

## Instructions

1. Mix the soy sauce for the marinade, brown sugar, lime juice, garlic, ground coriander and turmeric. Toss in the chicken pieces and marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
2. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water during this time. Thread the chicken onto the skewers.
3. Combine the peanut butter, coconut milk, remaining soy sauce and remaining brown sugar in a saucepan. Heat gently until smooth. Add a little water if the sauce is too thick.
4. Grill the skewers or fry them in a hot pan, turning until the chicken is fully cooked throughout.
5. Serve the chicken skewers with the peanut sauce and lime wedges.

## Notes

- The peanut sauce can become quite thick; adjust with water. Check for peanut allergies before serving.
