# Low-carb khao man gai – Thai chicken and cauliflower rice

This low-carb variation retains Khao man gai's gently cooked chicken and ginger dipping sauce. Warm cauliflower rice is served instead of the traditional seasoned rice.

## Description

The cooking broth makes a light, clear broth for serving. The ginger sauce is salty, sour and aromatic.

## Preparation

1. Heat the water and chicken broth to a boil. Add the chicken and lower the heat so that the broth is just bubbling.
2. Cook under the lid for 15–20 minutes until the internal temperature of the broiler is at least 75 °C. Remove the meat from the broth and let it rest.
3. Mix ginger, soybean paste, fish sauce, lime juice and garlic into a sauce. If necessary, thin with a spoonful of stock.
4. Heat the cauliflower rice in a pan until soft and season lightly.
5. Slice the chicken and serve with rice, cucumber, sauce and coriander. If you wish, serve the broth separately.

## Notes

- In the low-carb variant, the traditional seasoned rice is replaced by cauliflower rice. The compositions of the sauces vary.
- The nutritional values ​​are indicative.
