# Chickpea and sweet potato curry

Sweet potato and chickpeas simmer in a tomato and coconut sauce. Fresh spinach goes in at the end.

## Preparation

1. Chop the onion and garlic. Dice the sweet potato into roughly 2 cm cubes. Rinse and drain the chickpeas. Soften the onion in the oil in a large pot for about 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder, coriander, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, salt and black pepper. Stir over the heat for one minute.
3. Add the sweet potato, chickpeas, tomatoes, tomato paste and coconut milk. Bring to the boil and simmer for about 20 minutes until the sweet potato is soft. Stir occasionally and add a splash of water if the sauce thickens before the potato is cooked.
4. Rinse the spinach and stir it into the sauce. Heat until the spinach wilts and serve hot.

## Notes

- Check the curry powder label for allergens such as mustard. Use cooked canned chickpeas.
