# Korean beef bibimbap

## Description

Top the rice with separately prepared vegetables, beef, egg and spicy gochujang sauce. Mix everything in the bowl just before eating.

## Preparation

1. Cook the rice according to the package directions. Mix the gochujang with one tablespoon of sesame oil to make the sauce.
2. Season the beef with a tablespoon of soy sauce and half the garlic. Fry in a hot pan until cooked through, then set aside.
3. Fry the mushrooms in a little oil. Season lightly with soy sauce.
4. Sauté the carrot separately for 2–3 minutes. Quickly blanch the spinach and bean sprouts in boiling water, drain and dress with a little sesame oil.
5. Fry the eggs, leaving the yolks runny if desired.
6. Divide the rice among bowls. Arrange the vegetables and beef on top in separate groups. Put an egg in the centre of each bowl and serve with the gochujang sauce and sesame seeds.

## Notes

Gochujang varies in heat and saltiness by brand. Serve the sauce separately so everyone can adjust the amount.
