# Cottage Cheese Oven Omelette

The soft baked omelette gets richness from smooth pureed cottage cheese.

## Description

A small change to the traditional baked omelette: the cottage cheese is pureed and mixed into the creamy egg mixture. When fried in a water bath, the omelet becomes evenly coagulated and soft.

## Preparation

1. Heat the convection oven to 125 degrees or a regular oven to 150 degrees. Grease a 22 cm baking dish.
2. Puree the cottage cheese until smooth. Lightly break the texture of the eggs in a bowl without whisking the mixture until frothy. Mix in the cooking cream, pureed cottage cheese, salt and a pinch of pepper.
3. Pour the mixture into the pan. Place the pan on a deep baking pan and pour hot water around the pan.
4. Cook in the lower part of the oven for 45–55 minutes. Stir the omelet gently at around 15 and 30 minutes. Take the pan out of the oven when the mixture has congealed but is still soft.
5. If you want, chop chives on top and serve warm.

## Notes

- The water bath helps the omelet to coagulate softly and evenly.
