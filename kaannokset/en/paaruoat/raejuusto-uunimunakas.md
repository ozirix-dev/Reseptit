# Soft cottage cheese omelette bake – weighed version

## Description

Soft cottage cheese omelette bake – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Grease the dish with all 5 g butter, which is included in the calculation. Mix 550 g egg without shell, 250 g cooking cream with 15% fat and 200 g cottage cheese with 2% fat.
2. Add 0.5 g black pepper and 5 g chives. Bake at 200 °C until the egg is fully set throughout.
3. Divide the entire dish into four portions. There are no unquantified optional additions of butter, pepper or herbs.
