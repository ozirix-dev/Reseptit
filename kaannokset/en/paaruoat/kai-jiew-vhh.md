# Kai jiew – Thai omelette with crispy edges

Kai jiew is a fluffy, crispy-edged Thai omelette seasoned with fish sauce. It is prepared quickly and is suitable as a light meal or part of a larger dining table.

## Description

In hot oil, the omelet rises and gets crispy edges. Use a small, deep pan and fry the omelette in two batches if necessary.

## Preparation

1. Break the eggs into a bowl. Add the fish sauce and water and beat until the mixture foams a little. Stir the chopped spring onion into the egg mixture, if using.
2. Heat the oil in a small deep pan until very hot.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan. Fry on medium heat until the edges are crispy and the center is set.
4. Carefully turn over and fry the other side until golden brown.
5. Serve immediately with cucumber and coriander.

## Notes


- This basic version is low in carbohydrates. Fish sauces vary in saltiness. Cook a small test portion fully if you want to check the seasoning before frying the whole omelette.
