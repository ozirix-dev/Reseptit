# Pad kra pao – Thai basil minced meat with cauliflower rice

A hot and quickly prepared everyday meal: ground pork, garlic, chili and Thai basil, served with cauliflower rice and a crispy egg.

## Description

Holy basil, fish sauce and chili bring a recognizable flavor to the dish. Cauliflower rice replaces regular rice.

## Preparation

1. Chop the garlic and chili. Heat up a wok or a large frying pan.
2. Add oil and brown the minced meat until crumbly.
3. Add garlic and chili. Fry for about 30 seconds.
4. Mix in fish sauce, soy sauce and erythritol. Add the basil and toss until the leaves soften.
5. Heat the cauliflower rice in a pan until cooked but crisp.
6. Fry the eggs separately with hot oil until the edges are crispy. Serve over minced meat and cauliflower rice.

## Notes

- In the low-carb variant, the rice is replaced by cauliflower rice. Check the product descriptions of the sauces.
- The nutritional values ​​are indicative and depend on the fat content of the minced meat and the sauces.
