# Greek shrimp saganaki

Tomatoes and feta surround prawns in this Greek-style bake.

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 200 °C fan. Thaw frozen prawns in the refrigerator and drain. Chop pepper, onion and garlic; grate tomato flesh.
2. Soften onion, pepper, garlic, chilli and star anise in two tablespoons oil for 5–8 minutes. Add purée, water, tomatoes and sugar; bring to a boil, season and remove star anise.
3. Spread hot sauce in a baking dish. Add half the feta and chopped basil, arrange prawns in a single layer and top with remaining feta, basil, oregano and oil.
4. Bake initially 20 minutes. Extend until the thickest part of the largest prawns reaches 63 °C and all prawns are pearly white and opaque throughout. Divide into four.

## Notes

- Contains crustaceans and milk. Water replaces ouzo in this adaptation.
- Keep raw seafood chilled, thaw in the refrigerator and use clean utensils for cooked food. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours at 0–5 °C; use by the next day and reheat once to 74 °C.
