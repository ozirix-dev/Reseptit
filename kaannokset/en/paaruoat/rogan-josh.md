# Rogan josh with ground spices – variant

## Description

Rogan josh with ground spices – variant.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Dice 800 g lamb shoulder and chop 55 g onion, 6 g garlic and 15 g ginger. Brown the lamb and onion in 27 g olive oil.
2. Add garlic, ginger, 4 g cumin, 2 g coriander, 4 g paprika, 1 g chilli, 0.5 g cinnamon and 0.5 g cardamom. Gradually stir in 200 g plain 2.5% yoghurt over low heat, then add 400 g water.
3. Simmer covered for about 1.5 hours until the lamb is tender. All spices are ground and consumed. Season with salt and divide all meat, fat and sauce into four portions; rice is excluded.
