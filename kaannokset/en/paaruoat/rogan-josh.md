# Kashmiri Rogan Josh

## Instructions

1. Dice 800 g lamb shoulder, 55 g onion, 6 g garlic and 15 g ginger. Brown the lamb and onion in all 27 g olive oil.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, 4 g ground cumin, 2 g ground coriander seed, 4 g paprika, 1 g chilli powder, one whole cinnamon stick weighing 2 g and four whole cardamom pods weighing 1.6 g together. Lower the heat to prevent the yogurt from curdling. Gradually add 200 g plain 2.5%-fat yogurt and 400 g water.
3. Simmer covered for 1.5 hours until tender. Remove the cinnamon stick and cardamom pods before serving. Salt and divide all meat, sauce and fat into four portions. No rice or bread.
