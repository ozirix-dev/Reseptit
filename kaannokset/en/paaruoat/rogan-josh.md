# Kashmiri Rogan Josh

Kashmiri Rogan Josh is long-braised lamb in an aromatic sauce softened with yogurt.

## Description

Kashmiri Rogan Josh is long-braised lamb in an aromatic sauce softened with yogurt.

## Preparation

1. Brown the lamb pieces in oil in batches and set aside.
2. Saute the onion in the same pan. Add garlic, ginger, cinnamon and cardamom.
3. Mix cumin, coriander, paprika powder and chili with yogurt. Add the mixture to the pot over low heat while stirring.
4. Return the meat to the pot. Add salt and water. Bring to a boil.
5. Cover with a lid and simmer on a very low heat for about 1.5 hours until the lamb is tender. Stir occasionally.
6. Remove the cinnamon stick and cardamoms. Serve with rice or bread.

## Notes


- Gradually stir the yoghurt into the warm stew to keep it from curdling.
