# Thai cashew chicken

Crisp chicken and cashews meet peppers in a sweet-savoury wok sauce.

## Preparation

1. Mix both soy sauces, vinegar, sugar and 2.5 ml starch. Cut the chicken, onion and peppers; chop garlic.
2. Heat the frying oil to 165 °C in a high-sided pot of at least 2 l; fill no more than a quarter. Salt and coat chicken; shake off excess starch.
3. Fry dry-surfaced pieces in small batches for about 3–4 min and drain. Never add water to hot oil. Move the frying oil off the heat; do not pour into a drain.
4. Heat 15 ml oil in a clean pan. Fry onion and garlic, add nuts and chilli, then peppers. Add chicken and sauce; stir until thick and the thickest pieces reach at least 75 °C. Fold in spring onion.

## Notes

- Contains cashews and soy; check soy sauce for wheat. Frying oil allowance is not the consumed amount.
