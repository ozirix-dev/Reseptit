# Spanish vegetable stew with fried eggs (pisto manchego)

## Description

Peppers and zucchini simmer in a thick tomato sauce, and each serving gets a fried egg.

## Preparation

1. Dice the onion, peppers and zucchini. Heat 20 g of the oil in a deep frying pan. Cook the onion and peppers over medium heat for about 8 minutes.
2. Add the zucchini and cook for 5 minutes. Add the tomatoes and salt. Simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the sauce is thick.
3. Fry four eggs in the remaining oil until the whites and yolks are set. Divide the stew among four plates and top each with an egg.
