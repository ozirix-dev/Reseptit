# Cottage cheese pizza with an oat flour crust

## Description

Cottage cheese, eggs and oat flour make a soft pizza base that is prebaked before adding tomato and cheese. This recipe makes one pizza, serving two.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C (conventional top and bottom heat). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper rated for that temperature.
2. Blend 200 g cottage cheese until smooth. Add 110 g shelled egg and mix.
3. Stir in 25 g oat flour, 5 g garlic, 40 g onion, 3 g basil and 1 g salt. The mixture will be softer than ordinary pizza dough.
4. Spread the mixture on the parchment into an even circle about 25 cm across. Bake for 15–20 minutes until set and lightly coloured.
5. Remove the crust from the oven. Spread 60 g tomato passata over it, then add 80 g grated mozzarella and 50 g fully cooked turkey pepperoni.
6. Bake for about 10 minutes more, until the cheese melts, the toppings are hot and the crust holds together. Rest briefly and divide into two servings.
