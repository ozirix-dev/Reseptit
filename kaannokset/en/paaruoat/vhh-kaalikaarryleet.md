# VHH cabbage rolls

VHH cabbage rolls are a familiar Finnish food in a low-carb home version. The recipe has been rewritten to suit your own kitchen and serves four or the stated amount of servings.

## Description

The familiar taste in food is built from simple ingredients. Vegetables and creamy ingredients bring juiciness, and the portion size can be adjusted with the portion counter in the recipe.

## Preparation

1. Boil the cabbage until the leaves come off easily. Remove the leaves and save the cooking water.
2. Mix minced meat, cauliflower, chopped onion, egg, cream and spices.
3. Dose the filling on the leaves and roll into tight rolls.
4. Place in a greased pan, pour melted butter on top and a splash of cabbage boiling water.
5. Bake at 200 °C for about 45–55 minutes. Baste with the cooking broth once.

## Notes

- Check for doneness and finally season according to your own taste.
