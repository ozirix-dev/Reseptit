# Thai crab fried rice

Cooked jasmine rice, egg and real crab meat come together in a hot wok.

## Preparation

1. Use freshly cooked hot rice or rice cooled quickly (ideally within an hour) and refrigerated at 0–5 °C for no more than 24 h. Check crab for shell. Mix sauces, sugar, pepper and lime juice.
2. Chop onion and garlic and fry in 15 ml oil. Push aside, add remaining oil and beaten eggs. Scramble until fully set.
3. Add rice and stir-fry until steaming throughout. Fold in crab and sauce and heat the whole dish until steaming hot. Add sliced spring onion, divide into four and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Contains crustaceans, egg, fish and soy; check sauces for wheat and crab for sulphites. Do not use surimi. Reheat precooked rice only once; do not keep reheated rice as new leftovers.
