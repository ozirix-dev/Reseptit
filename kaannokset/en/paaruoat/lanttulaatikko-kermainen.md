# Creamy swede casserole – unsweetened version

## Description

An unsweetened version of creamy Finnish swede casserole, weighed for four servings.

## Ingredients

The ingredient quantities are provided in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Weigh the listed edible ingredients in grams. Do not add any unlisted fat or accompaniments.
2. This unsweetened version omits the optional syrup of the original recipe. Weigh 800 g peeled swede and cook it covered in 100 g water until tender; retain all the liquid in the mash. If necessary, add only enough water to replace what has evaporated. Do not drain the swede.
3. Mash the swede. Use part of the total 50 g butter to grease the baking dish and coat the surface, and add the remainder to the mixture. Add the cream, egg, nutmeg and black pepper. The total butter allowance is 50 g; do not add any extra fat.
4. Bake at 175 °C for approximately 60 minutes until cooked through. Let stand for 10 minutes, then divide the entire casserole into four servings.
5. Cook all components containing raw animal-derived ingredients fully. Consume all the weighed ingredients and any sauce or pan liquid. The raw ingredient nutrient composition is used as the basis for estimating the cooked dish; no unmeasured cooking loss is deducted.
