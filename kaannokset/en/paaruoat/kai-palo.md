# Thai five-spice braised pork and eggs

Boiled eggs, pork belly and fried tofu simmer in a sweet-savoury five-spice broth.

## Preparation

1. Crush washed coriander roots, garlic, pepper and salt. Cut pork into roughly 3 cm cubes and tofu into bite-sized pieces.
2. Fry spice paste in oil, add pork and brown. Add five-spice, soy sauces, sugar, star anise, tofu and water. Bring to a boil and simmer gently 30 min.
3. Add hard-boiled peeled eggs and simmer about another hour until pork is fork-tender. Remove star anise, ensure eggs and tofu are hot and finish with coriander. Divide into four, one egg each.

## Notes

- Contains egg and soy; check soy sauces and tofu product for wheat. Rice is not included. Use ready-fried tofu, not dry tofu at the same weight.
