# Broccoli and blue cheese frittata – weighed version

## Description

Broccoli and blue cheese frittata – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Soften 27.5 g onion in 14 g butter and add 200 g broccoli. Cook the broccoli until tender.
2. Mix 330 g egg without shell, 150 g cream, 100 g blue cheese and 0.5 g ground black pepper. Pour into a baking dish with the vegetables.
3. Bake at 200 °C until the egg is fully set. Divide all food and fat into four portions.
