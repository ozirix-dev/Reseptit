# Ground beef bowls with fried eggs

## Description

Cook the beef in its own fat and top each of two bowls with a fully cooked fried egg.

## Instructions

1. Heat a frying pan over medium heat. Add the beef without extra fat and break it into bite-sized pieces with a spatula.
2. Cook while stirring for about 7–10 minutes. Check that the beef is cooked throughout and holds at least 70 °C for 2 minutes, or reaches 75 °C.
3. Season with salt. Divide the beef and its rendered fat between two bowls.
4. Fry the eggs in the same pan until the whites and yolks are firm. Place one egg on each portion.
5. Serve immediately while hot.

## Notes

- The public recipe uses cooked eggs. The Finnish Food Authority notes that Finnish eggs may also be eaten raw, but cooking reduces microbial risk. Consider the origin and diners' risk factors separately.
