# Finnish spinach soup

Frozen spinach makes this milk-based soup a quick weeknight meal. Add the milk gradually to keep the flour thickener smooth.

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a heavy-bottomed saucepan. Whisk in the flour and heat briefly over low heat until the mixture foams slightly.
2. Whisk in about half the milk a little at a time. Pour in the rest, stirring along the bottom of the pan until the soup comes to a boil.
3. Add the frozen spinach. Once the soup is boiling again and the spinach has thawed completely, simmer for 3 minutes, stirring.
4. Season with salt, sugar and black pepper. Divide the soup among four bowls.

## Notes

- Contains milk and wheat. Heat the frozen spinach all the way through before serving.
