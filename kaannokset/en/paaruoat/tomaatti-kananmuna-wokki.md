# Chinese tomato and egg wok with hot rice

## Description

Chinese tomato and egg wok with hot rice.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Use 450 g hot cooked white jasmine or basmati rice, weighed after cooking. The named comparison scenarios remain separate.
2. Cook all 220 g egg in 27 g oil. Add 500 g tomato, 4 g sugar and 15 g soy sauce. Simmer until the eggs are cooked.
3. Add 15 g spring onion and season with salt. Divide the entire pan, sauce and rice into three portions.
