# low-carb red chicken curry with cauliflower rice

Red curry paste, coconut milk and Thai basil form a rich sauce for chicken and bamboo shoots. Cauliflower rice keeps the portion low in carbohydrates.

## Description

The hotness and sugar content of curry pastes vary. Add the paste sparingly at first and taste the sauce before serving.

## Preparation

1. Heat coconut oil in a pot. Add the curry paste and stir-fry for a minute.
2. Add a few tablespoons of coconut milk and mix into a paste.
3. Add the chicken and fry until the pieces are completely covered with the paste.
4. Add the rest of the coconut milk. Simmer on low heat for about 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked.
5. Add bamboo shoots and paprika. Simmer for another 5 minutes. Season with fish sauce and, if desired, erythritol.
6. Heat the cauliflower rice in a pan. Finish the curry with Thai basil and serve with lime.

## Notes

- Cauliflower rice replaces regular rice. Check the packaging labels for curry paste and coconut milk.
