# Finnish pike patties

Lemon lifts these tender pike patties, which are spooned straight into the frying pan.

## Preparation

1. Check the fillet for bones. Cut the fish into pieces and put it in a tall mixing jug. Add the zest, lemon juice, eggs, crème fraîche, salt and pepper.
2. Blend with a stick blender until smooth. Heat the butter in a frying pan and spoon in small, shallow patties.
3. Fry for about 3 minutes per side, or longer if needed, until the centres have set and the thickest part reaches at least 71 °C.

## Notes

- Contains fish, egg and milk. See the Finnish Food Authority’s advice on eating pike where relevant.
