# Ayam percik – Malaysian coconut-glazed chicken

Turmeric-seasoned chicken is steamed and finished in the oven with a spiced coconut sauce.

## Instructions

1. Grate ginger and mix with turmeric and marinade salt. Rub over chicken and refrigerate covered for 30 minutes. Do not rinse raw chicken; wash hands and utensils.
2. Grind peeled shallots, garlic, belacan and pale parts of the paste lemongrass. Briefly heat chilli paste in a nonstick pan, add the ground mixture and cook gently, stirring, for about 15 minutes.
3. Add coconut milk, bruised sauce lemongrass, sauce salt, sugar and tamarind. Simmer while stirring for 15 minutes. Reserve some sauce in a clean container for serving before the chicken touches it.
4. Steam chicken for 15 minutes, transfer immediately to the hot sauce for 5 minutes and then to a lined tray. Roast at 200 °C for about 30 minutes or longer, until the thickest part of every piece reaches at least 75 °C without touching bone. Do not store partially cooked chicken.
5. Remove lemongrass from reserved clean sauce, reheat and divide among four servings with chicken. Discard excess sauce that contacted raw or partially cooked chicken.

## Notes

- Contains crustaceans. Rice is not included. The 1 kg weight of four thighs, 15 g ginger, 500 ml coconut milk and 30-minute refrigerated marinade are editorial specifications; reserving clean serving sauce is a safety adaptation.
