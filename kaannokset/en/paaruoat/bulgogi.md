# Korean bulgogi

Thinly sliced beef is marinated with soy sauce, sesame, garlic and pear. Sear the beef quickly in a hot pan and serve with rice.

## Instructions

1. Mix the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, grated pear and black pepper.
2. Toss the beef slices in the marinade, add the onion and marinate in the refrigerator for 20–30 minutes.
3. Rinse the rice and cook according to the package instructions.
4. Heat a large frying pan until very hot. Sear the beef quickly in batches so it browns instead of stewing in its juices.
5. Add the spring onions and sesame seeds. Serve immediately with the rice.

## Notes

- For easier thin slicing, place the beef in the freezer for about 20 minutes beforehand.
