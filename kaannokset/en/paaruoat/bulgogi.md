# Korean bulgogi beef

Thinly sliced beef is marinated with soy sauce, sesame, garlic and pear. Sear it quickly in a hot pan and serve with rice.

## Instructions

1. Mix the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, grated pear and black pepper.
2. Coat the beef slices in the marinade, add the onion and refrigerate for **20–30 minutes**.
3. Rinse the rice and cook according to the packet instructions.
4. Heat a large frying pan until very hot. Fry the beef quickly in batches so that it browns instead of stewing in its own juices.
5. Add the spring onions and sesame seeds. Serve immediately with the rice.

## Notes

- The beef is easier to slice thinly if it has been in the freezer for about 20 minutes beforehand.
