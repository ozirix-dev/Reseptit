# Tuna pasta with olives and spinach

Tuna, black olives and spinach dress wholewheat pasta.

## Preparation

1. Cook pasta as directed; reserve about 150 ml cooking water.
2. Soften sliced onion in oil 5–6 min. Add drained tuna, sliced olives, lemon zest and juice, and washed spinach.
3. Cover and heat about 3 min, stir and continue until hot throughout. Add pasta, cooking water as needed, dill and parsley. Divide into four.

## Notes

- Contains fish and wheat.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
