# Casagai, bean polenta from Reggio Emilia

Borlotti beans and a tomato sauce are stirred into creamy polenta. The total time includes soaking the beans.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the beans and soak them in plenty of water in the refrigerator for about 12 hours. Discard the soaking water and rinse the beans.
2. Cover the beans with plenty of fresh water in a saucepan. Bring to a rolling boil and boil briskly for at least 30 minutes. Continue cooking as necessary until soft. Follow any longer cooking time stated on the package. Reserve a little of the liquid from the cooked beans.
3. Chop the onion. Fry it and the lardo in butter until the onion is soft. Add the tomatoes and their juice, break the tomatoes up with a spatula and simmer for about 15 minutes. Stir in the cooked beans and a ladleful of their cooking water. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer for about 30 minutes. Season with salt.
4. Bring 2 litres of water to a boil in a second pot. Slowly whisk in the cornmeal. Cook according to the cornmeal package directions, stirring regularly. Add the bean sauce about 10 minutes before the polenta is done. Stir and finish cooking.
5. Let the polenta stand briefly off the heat. Divide it between six bowls and serve warm.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk. Soak the dried beans in the refrigerator, change the water and cook them as directed above. Cool any leftovers promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
