# Finnish braised beef stew

Browned beef and vegetables simmer until tender, then the broth is thickened into a light cream sauce.

## Preparation

1. Cut the beef into cubes about 2 cm across. Peel the carrots and onions, chop the carrots into chunks and finely chop the onions.
2. Brown the meat in butter in three batches and transfer it to a casserole. After each batch, add 200 ml of water to the frying pan, scrape up the browned bits and pour the liquid into the casserole. Soften the onions in the pan and add them with the carrots.
3. Bring to a boil. Add salt, allspice and bay leaves, lower the heat and cover. Simmer for about 2 hours, until the beef is cooked through and tender.
4. Whisk the flour into 100 ml of cold water. Pour it slowly into the simmering stew while stirring. Stir in the cream and simmer for 5 minutes.
