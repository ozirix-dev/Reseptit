# Bacon Cauliflower Gratin

Bacon Cauliflower Gratin is a familiar Finnish dish in a low-carb home version. The recipe has been rewritten to suit your own kitchen and serves four or the stated amount of servings.

## Description

The familiar taste in food is built from simple ingredients. Vegetables and creamy ingredients bring juiciness, and the portion size can be adjusted with the portion counter in the recipe.

## Preparation

1. Divide the cauliflower into florets and boil in salted water for 5 minutes. Drain well.
2. Fry the bacon until crispy and spread it in the pan with the cauliflower.
3. Pour the cream on top, season with pepper and sprinkle the cheese on top.
4. Bake at 200 °C for about 20 minutes until the surface takes on color.

## Notes

- Check for doneness and finally season according to your own taste.
