# Beef and root-vegetable soup without potatoes

## Description

Slowly simmered beef with swede, carrot and celeriac.

## Preparation

1. Put the beef in cold water in a saucepan. Bring to the boil and skim the foam.
2. Add onion, bay leaves and whole peppercorns. Cover and simmer gently for about 1.5 hours, until the meat is tender.
3. Lift the meat onto a board and strain the stock. Cut the meat into pieces.
4. Peel and dice the swede, carrot and celeriac. Cook them in the strained stock for 12–15 minutes.
5. Return the beef, heat through and season with salt. Finish with parsley.

## Notes

Root-vegetable cooking times depend on the size of the cubes.
