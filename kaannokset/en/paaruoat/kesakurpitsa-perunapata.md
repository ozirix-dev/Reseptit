# Layered courgette and potato stew

## Description

Thin layers of potato, courgette and tomato cook with a savoury pulled-oat filling in a covered pot.

## Instructions

1. Slice the potatoes and courgette about 3 mm thick. Dice half the tomatoes and slice the rest. Chop the onion, garlic and herbs.
2. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil in a wide pot. Stir in the diced tomato, pulled oats, tomato paste, pepper and herbs. Cook for 5 minutes, then transfer to a bowl.
3. Layer the potato, courgette and tomato slices and the pulled-oat mixture in the pot. Distribute the salt between the layers and pour the water down the side.
4. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook over low heat for 35–45 minutes until a knife slides easily through the potatoes. Rest for 5 minutes.

## Notes

- Check the ingredients and allergens on the pulled-oat package. Use sound potatoes and discard green ones.
