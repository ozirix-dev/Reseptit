# Flygande Jakob, Swedish chicken and banana bake

Chicken, bacon and banana bake in a mild chilli cream sauce.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 225 °C. Slice the chicken into strips. Brown them in oil in batches, season with salt and pepper and transfer to a clean baking dish.
2. Fry the bacon and add it to the dish. Peel and slice the banana and lay the slices over the chicken. Mix the cream with the chilli sauce and pour it over the chicken.
3. Bake on the middle shelf for about 20 minutes. Check that the thickest piece of chicken has reached at least 75 °C in the centre. If needed, bake longer and check again.
4. Scatter the peanuts over the finished bake. Serve with cooked rice if you like.

## Notes

- Contains milk and peanuts. Check the sauce and bacon ingredients for other allergens.
