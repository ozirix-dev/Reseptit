# Aubergine and beef bake – weighed version

## Description

Aubergine and beef bake – weighed version.

## Ingredients

The weighed ingredients are shown above.

## Instructions

1. Slice 600 g aubergine. Cook 500 g beef mince and 55 g onion in the meat’s own fat. Add 400 g crushed tomatoes, 1 g oregano and 1 g black pepper.
2. Layer the aubergine and all sauce in a dish, then add 150 g grated semi-hard cheese. Do not add unlisted frying oil.
3. Bake at 200 °C until the meat and aubergine are fully cooked. Divide the entire dish and all liquid into four portions.
