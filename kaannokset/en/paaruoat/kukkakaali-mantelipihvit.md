# Spiced cauliflower and almond fritters

Steamed cauliflower makes small pan-fried patties flavoured with mint, thyme and cumin.

## Preparation

1. Cut the cauliflower into florets and steam until tender but still holding their shape. Drain well and chop finely.
2. Mix the ground almonds, tapioca starch, cumin, fennel, turmeric, cayenne, salt and black pepper in a bowl. Add the lightly beaten eggs, chopped garlic, mint, thyme and cauliflower.
3. Heat some oil in a frying pan. Spoon in heaped tablespoons of batter and flatten lightly. Work in batches and fry for about 3 minutes on each side until golden, set in the centre and at least 71 °C. Adjust the heat as needed.

## Notes
