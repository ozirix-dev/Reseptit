# Carrot pancakes with oat drink

## Description

Carrot purée gives these pancakes an orange colour and soft texture. Use a small pancake pan so they are easy to flip.

## Preparation

1. Whisk the eggs, carrot purée and oat drink until smooth. Add the oat flour, wheat flour and salt and stir until smooth.
2. Stir in the margarine for the batter and let the batter rest for 30 minutes.
3. Heat a small pancake pan over medium heat. Brush the wells lightly with the margarine for frying and pour about 2 tbsp of batter into each well.
4. Flip when the edges have set and the underside has browned. Cook the second side until the centre is cooked through. Repeat with the remaining batter, adding more frying margarine between batches.

## Notes

- Contains egg, oats and wheat. Check the ingredients on the margarine label.
