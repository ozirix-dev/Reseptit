# Briam, Greek baked vegetables

Potato, aubergine and other vegetables bake in a tomato and herb sauce. Removing the cover near the end lets the vegetables brown.

## Preparation

1. Preheat a fan oven to 180 °C. Wash the vegetables and peel the potatoes and carrots. Cut the potatoes and aubergine into slices about 1 cm thick, the carrots and courgettes into half-moons and the peppers into bite-sized pieces. Quarter the onion and separate the layers. Chop the garlic finely.
2. Grate the tomatoes into a bowl and stir in the tomato purée, 80 g of the oil, salt, pepper, oregano, thyme and honey. Toss the vegetables with the sauce and garlic.
3. Spread the vegetables in a wide baking dish and drizzle over the remaining 20 g of oil. Cover with foil and bake for 30–40 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for 20–30 minutes more, until the vegetables are tender and a few edges are browned. Test the potatoes with a fork.

## Storage and safety

Discard green or sprouted potatoes. Do not give honey-containing food to children under one year. Cool leftovers quickly, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
