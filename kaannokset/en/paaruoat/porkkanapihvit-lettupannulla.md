# Carrot patties with cottage cheese sauce

Finely grated carrots make small pan-fried patties served with a chilled chive sauce.

## Method

1. Mix the milk and breadcrumbs. While they soak, wash, peel and finely grate the carrots.
2. Mix the carrot, eggs, flour, oregano, salt and the patties’ pepper into the soaked crumbs. In a separate bowl, stir the cottage cheese, yoghurt, chives, lemon juice and remaining pepper together. Refrigerate the sauce.
3. Heat a pancake pan over medium heat. Use the oil in batches and spread the mixture into thin patties in the wells. Fry on both sides until browned, firm and cooked in the centre. The egg mixture should reach at least 71°C in the centre. Serve with the sauce.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, milk and egg. The source recipe’s kale side is outside this serving.
