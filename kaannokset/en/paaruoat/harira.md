# Moroccan harira with lentils and chickpeas

## Description

Moroccan harira with lentils and chickpeas. Precisely weighed to make six servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredient quantities are stored in the structured serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Cook 200 g ground beef with 55 g onion and 80 g celery stalk in a saucepan without added fat. Keep all fat released by the beef.
2. Add 400 g crushed tomatoes, 1000 g ready-to-use meat stock, 150 g dry whole green or brown lentils, 2 g ground ginger, 2 g turmeric and 1 g ground cinnamon. Cook until the lentils are completely tender.
3. Add 400 g whole, cooked and drained chickpeas and 60 g dry durum wheat vermicelli. Cook until the noodles are done. Add 20 g fresh coriander and 30 g lemon juice.
4. Divide all the soup, including its liquid, into six servings. Do not purée the soup or drain away its cooking liquid.
