# Phuket-style soy-braised pork belly

Pork belly simmers with star anise, cinnamon and two soy sauces.

## Preparation

1. Wash coriander roots. Crush with garlic and peppercorns. Cut pork into roughly 3 cm pieces.
2. Warm a heavy pot on low heat. Add crushed spices, star anise and cinnamon; stir until aromatic, add palm sugar and heat carefully until it begins to melt. Add soy sauces and salt, toss pork in the mixture and brown.
3. Carefully add water, bring to a boil and simmer gently for about 3 h until fork-tender. Add water if needed to prevent drying. Remove cinnamon and star anise; divide into four.

## Notes

- Contains soy; check for wheat. Rice is separate. No additional frying oil; use a heavy pot over gentle heat so sugar does not burn.
