# Soto ayam – coconut chicken noodle soup

An Indonesian-Malaysian style spiced soup with coconut milk, chicken, rice noodles and egg.

## Instructions

1. Grind the coriander and cumin seeds. Blend peeled shallots, garlic, turmeric, galangal, ginger and lime juice with the seeds, using a splash of the measured water if needed.
2. Cube the chicken on a separate board; wash hands and utensils. Bruise the pale lemongrass and cut into three pieces. Heat oil in a pot and briefly sauté the spice paste.
3. Add stock, coconut milk, lemongrass and remaining water. Bring to a boil, add chicken and simmer covered for 30 minutes or until chicken reaches at least 75 °C throughout. Remove lemongrass and season with salt.
4. Cook noodles according to the package and boil sprouts separately until thoroughly cooked. Wash spring onion and limes, slice onion and cut limes and peeled eggs into wedges. Divide noodles, sprouts, chicken, soup and toppings among four bowls.

## Notes

- Contains egg. Check stock and fried shallot labels for allergens, especially celery and wheat. Weights for noodles, chicken, galangal, ginger, sprouts and crispy shallots are editorial adaptations of unspecified source quantities. This is not presented as a Thai recipe.
