# Mushroom stroganoff

Browned mushrooms simmer in paprika-seasoned stock.

## Preparation

1. Chop the onion, garlic and mushrooms. Soften the onion in the oil for about 5 minutes. Add the paprika and garlic and cook one minute more.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook over fairly high heat for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until tender and the liquid has evaporated.
3. Add the stock and Worcestershire sauce. Boil for about 5 minutes until slightly thickened. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the soured cream and most of the parsley and scatter the rest on top.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Use vegetarian Worcestershire sauce and check the sauce and stock labels for allergens.
