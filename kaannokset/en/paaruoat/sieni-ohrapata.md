# Mushroom and pearl barley stew

Button mushrooms and dried porcini give this spoonable barley stew a deep mushroom flavour.

## Preparation

1. Cover the dried porcini with hot water and soak for 20 minutes. Lift them out and chop them. Strain and reserve the soaking liquid through a fine sieve to remove any grit.
2. Heat the oil in a large pot. Soften the onion, chopped garlic and thyme for about 5 minutes. Add the sliced button mushrooms and cook for about 10 minutes until their liquid evaporates and they start to brown.
3. Stir in the flour and cook for a minute. Add the stock, bay leaves, barley, chopped porcini and strained soaking liquid. Bring to the boil, then simmer uncovered for about 45 minutes until the barley is tender. Stir occasionally. Add a little water if it gets too thick.
4. Remove the bay leaves. Season with salt and black pepper and serve hot.

## Notes

- Barley and wheat contain gluten. Check the stock label for allergens.
