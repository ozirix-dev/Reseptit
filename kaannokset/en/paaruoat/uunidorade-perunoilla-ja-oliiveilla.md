# Whole baked sea bream with potatoes and olives

Whole fish roasts over potatoes and olives, flavouring them as it cooks.

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 220 °C. Boil potatoes 10 min, drain and slice. Toss in a baking dish with olives, chopped garlic, half the parsley leaves, lemon zest and 1 tbsp oil.
2. Check fish is gutted and scaled. Pat dry, fill cavity with parsley stems, place on potatoes and drizzle remaining oil.
3. Roast 15 min, add stock and cook about 10 min more or until thickest flesh reaches 63 °C without probe touching bone. Remove flesh and bones, divide edible fish and potatoes in two; finish with parsley.

## Notes

- Contains fish; stock may contain celery. Remove bones carefully before serving.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Keep at 0–5 °C, use by the following day and reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
