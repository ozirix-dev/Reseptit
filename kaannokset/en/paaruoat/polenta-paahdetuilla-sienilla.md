# Oven polenta with roasted mushrooms

Spoon thyme-roasted mushrooms over soft polenta with cheese.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 165 °C. Cut the mushrooms into roughly 2–3 cm pieces. Toss them on a baking tray with thyme, crushed garlic cloves, oil, salt and pepper. Spread in one layer and put on the upper rack.
2. Bring the water to a simmer in a lidded ovenproof pot. Add the butter and a pinch of salt. Whisk in the polenta gradually. Bring to the boil, cover and transfer to the lower rack. Bake for 25–30 minutes or until the grains are tender.
3. Take out the polenta. Raise the oven to 225 °C and roast the mushrooms for another 5–10 minutes until browned at the edges. Carefully uncover the pot, whisk the polenta until smooth and stir in the cheese. Keep warm.
4. Remove the woody thyme stems. Toss the hot mushrooms with vinegar and spoon them over the polenta.

## Notes

- Contains milk. Check the vinegar label for sulphites.
