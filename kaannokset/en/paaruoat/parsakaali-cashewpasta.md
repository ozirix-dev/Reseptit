# Broccoli and cashew spaghetti

## Description

Roasted broccoli, toasted cashews and Parmesan dress wholemeal spaghetti.

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut off the florets. Peel the tough outside of the stalk and thinly slice the tender centre.
2. Spread the broccoli on a tray and toss with 2 tbsp oil and the salt. Roast for 15–18 minutes until the florets brown. Add thinly sliced garlic for the last 5 minutes.
3. Cook the spaghetti according to the packet. Reserve 1 dl cooking water before draining. Toast the cashews in a dry pan while the pasta cooks.
4. Toss the hot pasta with the broccoli, cashews, remaining oil, pepper and half the Parmesan. Add cooking water a little at a time until it mixes easily. Serve with the remaining Parmesan.
