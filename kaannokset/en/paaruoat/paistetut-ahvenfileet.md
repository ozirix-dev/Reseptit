# Pan-fried perch fillets

Thin perch fillets turn golden when fried in butter.

## Preparation

1. Run a finger along the fillets and use tweezers to pull out any bones. Pat dry with kitchen paper and season lightly with salt and pepper.
2. Melt the butter in a frying pan until it foams. Lay the fillets in a single layer and cook in batches if needed.
3. Turn the fillets once the first side has browned. Cook until the fish is opaque all the way through and flakes readily with a fork. Serve right away.
