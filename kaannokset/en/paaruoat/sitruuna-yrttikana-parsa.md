# Lemon herb chicken with roasted asparagus

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 210 °C. Place 600 g chicken breast and 400 g trimmed asparagus in a baking dish.
2. Mix 27.4 g olive oil, 30 g lemon juice, 2 g finely grated outer lemon zest, 2 g dried thyme, 3.1 g garlic powder and 0.5 g black pepper. Coat the chicken and asparagus, add 30 g butter in pieces and salt.
3. Roast for 20–25 min until the chicken is cooked through. Rest for 5 min. Divide all chicken, asparagus, fat and pan juices into four servings.
