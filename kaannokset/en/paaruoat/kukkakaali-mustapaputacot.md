# Cauliflower and black bean tacos

Roasted cauliflower and black beans fill warm corn tortillas. Pickled onion and lime brighten the filling.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 230 °C. Slice the red onion thinly. Stir together the vinegar, water, sugar and half the salt, then add the onion and let it sit while you make the filling.
2. Cut the cauliflower into small bite-sized pieces. Toss on a baking tray with the oil, cumin, chilli and remaining salt. Roast for about 25–30 minutes, stirring halfway through, until tender and browned at the edges.
3. Rinse and drain the cooked black beans. Add them to the tray for the last 5 minutes so they heat through. Squeeze half the lime over the cooked filling.
4. Warm the tortillas as directed on the pack. Fill them with cauliflower, beans, sliced avocado and drained onion. Cut the remaining lime half into wedges and serve with the tacos.

## Notes

- Check the tortilla label for allergens. Corn tortillas can also contain wheat.
