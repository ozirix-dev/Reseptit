# Plaice fillets with brown butter

A light flour coating protects the plaice, served with nutty brown butter, lemon and capers.

## Preparation

1. Check for bones and dry fillets. Season flour, dust fish and shake off excess. Discard flour that contacted raw fish.
2. Heat oil in a pan. Fry skin-side down for about 2 minutes, turn carefully and continue until the thickest part reaches 63 °C. Move to a clean warm plate.
3. Melt butter separately over medium heat. Once the milk solids are light nut-brown, remove from heat. Carefully add lemon juice and capers to avoid splashes.
4. Divide between four plates and finish with butter sauce and parsley.

## Notes

- Contains fish, wheat and milk.
- Keep raw seafood chilled, thaw in the refrigerator and use clean utensils for cooked food. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate within two hours at 0–5 °C; use by the next day and reheat once to 74 °C.
