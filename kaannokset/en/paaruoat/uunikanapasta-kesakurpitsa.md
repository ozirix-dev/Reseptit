# Baked chicken with courgette ribbons – weighed pasta-free version

## Description

Chicken baked with courgette ribbons instead of wheat pasta. A weighed recipe for four servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredient amounts appear in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. This version uses fresh garlic and basil **without wheat pasta**. Combine 150 g cream cheese, 100 g liquid cream, 200 g tomatoes, 6 g garlic and 6 g basil.
2. Add 500 g chicken to the baking dish and bake at **200 °C for approximately 20 minutes**. Add 500 g courgette ribbons, 100 g grated cheese and 0.5 g black pepper.
3. Continue baking until the chicken is thoroughly cooked throughout. Divide the entire bake, including all juices, into four servings.
