# Greek-style baked chicken with olives

## Description

A weighed Greek-style chicken bake with olives, making four servings.

## Ingredients

Ingredient amounts are shown in the serving calculator above.

## Instructions

1. Place 600 g boneless, skinless chicken thigh meat in a baking dish. Add 250 g cherry tomatoes and 100 g red onion.
2. Mix 27 g olive oil, 30 g lemon juice, 2 g dried oregano and 0.5 g ground black pepper. Add to the dish along with 100 g feta cheese and 60 g pitted olives.
3. Bake at 200 °C until the chicken is cooked all the way through. Divide the entire contents of the dish, including all juices, into four servings.
