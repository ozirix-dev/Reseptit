# Coconut and red lentil soup

## Description

Red lentils thicken this tomato and coconut soup as they cook. Lemon juice brightens it just before serving.

## Preparation

1. Finely chop the onion and garlic. Cook gently in the oil in a saucepan for about 4 minutes. Add the curry powder, cumin and ginger and stir briefly.
2. Rinse the lentils in a sieve. Add the lentils, coconut milk, tomatoes with their juice, water, tomato purée, salt and pepper. Break up the whole tomatoes with a spatula.
3. Cover and simmer gently for 20–25 minutes or until the lentils are completely soft. Stir a few times to prevent sticking.
4. Take the pan off the heat and blend with a stick blender. Stir in the lemon juice and sugar.

## Notes

- Check the curry powder label for allergens. Rinse the lentils and cook until soft. Keep the blender blade below the surface of the hot soup to avoid splashes.
