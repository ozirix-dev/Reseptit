# Prawn and fennel risotto

Fennel and lemon brighten creamy rice with tender prawns.

## Preparation

1. Keep stock hot. Chop onion, garlic and fennel; soften in oil 10 min. Stir in rice for 2 min. Add hot stock gradually, stirring until rice is nearly tender.
2. Add prawns and zest of half the lemon. Cook initially 3–4 min; continue until prawns are pearly white and opaque throughout and rice is tender.
3. Fold in rocket and 1 tbsp lemon juice. Rest 2 min and divide into four portions.

## Notes

- Contains crustaceans; stock may contain celery.
- Keep raw fish and seafood chilled, thaw frozen products in the fridge and separate utensils for raw and cooked food.
- Cool rice or risotto in shallow containers as quickly as possible, ideally within an hour. Refrigerate at 0–5 °C for no more than 24 hours; reheat once to 74 °C throughout.
