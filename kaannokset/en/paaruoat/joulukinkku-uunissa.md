# Traditional Finnish Christmas Ham

This is a traditional Finnish home-cooking recipe.

## Preparation

1. Thaw frozen ham completely in the refrigerator according to the manufacturer's instructions; never at room temperature. Use separate clean utensils for raw and cooked meat.
2. Preheat oven to 110–125°C. Put ham on a rack over a tray. Insert a meat thermometer into the thickest part, avoiding any bone.
3. Roast for an estimated 5–7 hours, but ensure the internal temperature reaches at least +75°C. The thermometer, not elapsed time, determines doneness.
4. Rest briefly. Remove netting and rind with clean utensils. Mix mustard and egg, brush over ham and sprinkle with breadcrumbs.
5. Brown at 225°C for about 5–10 minutes, watching carefully. Cool remaining ham promptly and refrigerate at +6°C or colder.
