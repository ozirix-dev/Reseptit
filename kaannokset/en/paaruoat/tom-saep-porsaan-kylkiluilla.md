# Thai hot and sour pork rib soup

Galangal, lemongrass and lime flavour a clear pork rib broth.

## Preparation

1. Slice galangal, bruise lemongrass, tear lime leaves and peel shallots. Simmer the aromatics in water for 5 min.
2. Add ribs, return to a boil and skim. Simmer for about 30 min or longer until the meat is cooked through (at least 70 °C) and pulls easily from the bone.
3. Season with fish sauce and lime, add basil, chilli and toasted rice powder. Divide between two bowls and garnish with parsley. Remove tough aromatics before eating.

## Notes

- Contains fish. Toasted rice powder amount is an editorial clarification; do not use raw rice flour. Watch for bones.
