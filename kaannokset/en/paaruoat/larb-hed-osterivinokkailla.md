# Thai oyster mushroom and pork larb

Grilled oyster mushrooms and browned ground pork are dressed with lime and fish sauce. Ground toasted rice adds a nutty taste and a little crunch.

## Preparation

1. Toast the dry rice in a skillet over medium-low heat for 10–15 minutes until golden, shaking the pan often. Cool the rice and crush it coarsely in a mortar.
2. Heat 30 ml oil in a skillet and fry the pork, breaking it up. Add 60 ml water and cook until the meat reaches at least 71 °C throughout and only a little liquid remains. Stir in 15 ml fish sauce.
3. Clean and dry the mushrooms. Separate large clusters as needed and brush with the remaining 30 ml oil. Use a grill or a hot grill pan and cook for 4–5 minutes per side, until tender and browned. Cut large mushrooms into bite-size pieces.
4. Mix the remaining 30 ml fish sauce with the lime juice and sugar. Add the mushrooms, pork, shallots, spring onions, lemongrass, chillies, mint and coriander. Fold in the ground toasted rice just before serving and divide into four portions.

## Storage and safety

Contains fish. Wash the herbs, onions and chillies before chopping. Keep utensils for raw pork separate from those used for the salad. Serve soon after cooking. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers and refrigerate.
