# Turkish menemen with tomatoes and paprika

In Menemen, eggs are cooked softly in a tomato and paprika mixture. Serve with fresh bread.

## Description

In Menemen, eggs are cooked softly in a tomato and paprika mixture. Serve with fresh bread.

## Preparation

1. Heat the olive oil in a pan. Sauté the onion and paprika until soft for about 6 minutes.
2. Add tomatoes and paprika powder. Simmer until the tomatoes have broken down and the liquid has evaporated a little.
3. Crack the eggs into the pan. Mix gently until the egg whites are just set but the mixture is still soft.
4. Season with salt and pepper. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Some versions do not use onion; you can leave it out.
