# Turkish menemen with tomatoes and peppers

## Instructions

1. Soften all 55 g onion and 170 g green pepper in 27 g olive oil. Add 375 g tomato and 1 g paprika; simmer until soft.
2. Add 220 g egg without shell and cook gently while stirring. Add 10 g parsley and 0.5 g black pepper and season with salt.
3. Divide the entire pan, including all liquid, into three portions. Onion is included in this exact version.
