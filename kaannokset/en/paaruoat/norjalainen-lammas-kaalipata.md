# Norwegian lamb and cabbage stew (fårikål)

## Description

Lamb and cabbage simmer together under a lid, with whole peppercorns seasoning the broth.

## Preparation

1. Cut the boneless leg of lamb into large chunks. Remove the tough cabbage core and cut the cabbage into wedges.
2. Layer lamb and cabbage in a pot, starting with the lamb. Season between the layers with salt and peppercorns. Pour in the water and bring to a boil.
3. Cover and simmer very gently for about 2½ hours, or until the meat pulls apart with a fork. Serve hot.
