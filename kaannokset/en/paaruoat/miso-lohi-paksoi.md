# Miso salmon with pan-fried bok choy

## Description

The miso and tamari glaze keeps the baked salmon succulent. Quickly pan-fried bok choy adds freshness.

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Mix the miso, tamari, rice vinegar and sesame oil.
2. Place the salmon on baking parchment and brush it with the miso mixture. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily.
3. Halve the bok choy lengthwise. Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the bok choy cut side down for 2–3 minutes.
4. Turn the bok choy and cook for another minute. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
5. Serve with the miso salmon.

## Notes

Miso and tamari are salty. Taste before adding extra salt.
