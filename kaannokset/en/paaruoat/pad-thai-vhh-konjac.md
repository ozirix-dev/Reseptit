# Low-carb pad thai with shrimp and konjac noodles

A tamarind, salty and slightly sweet variation of pad thai. konjac noodles replace rice noodles; shrimp, tofu and egg make the dish filling.

## Description

Rinse the noodles well and fry them dry before adding them to the wok. This way the excess liquid evaporates.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the noodles with cold water and dry-fry in a hot pan for 3–4 minutes. Move aside.
2. Mix tamarind paste, fish sauce, lime juice and erythritol.
3. Heat half the oil in a wok. Fry the tofu until golden and set aside.
4. Add the shrimps and fry until almost cooked. Move them aside, add the rest of the oil and stir-fry the eggs.
5. Add the noodles and sauce. Turn in a hot pan for a couple of minutes.
6. Return the prawns and tofu to the pan. Add the sprouts and half the peanuts. Stir-fry for about a minute, continuing as needed until the prawns are fully cooked.
7. Serve with spring onions, cilantro, remaining peanuts and lime wedges.

## Notes

- konjac noodles replace rice noodles. The carbohydrates in tamarind paste vary; check the package label.
- The nutritional values ​​are indicative.
