# Baked lentil and mushroom balls

Lentils, browned mushrooms, walnuts and oats make up the mixture.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the lentils. Cook as directed on the pack until tender but still firm, then drain well. Combine the ground flaxseed and measured water and let it thicken for 15 minutes. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Soften the onion in coconut oil. Add chopped garlic and mushrooms. Cook until the mushrooms brown and their moisture evaporates. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Grind the oats and walnuts in a food processor and set aside. Pulse the lentils, mushroom mixture, flax mixture, rinsed fresh greens and herbs, oregano, thyme and vinegar. Add the oat and walnut mixture and pulse until it holds together.
4. Shape about 18 balls and space them on a lined baking tray. Bake for 20–30 minutes until firm with a crisp surface.

## Notes

- Contains walnuts and oats. Check the vinegar label for sulphites.
