# Roasted sweet potatoes with feta

## Description

Top tender roasted sweet potato halves with feta, chilli and spring onions. Black sesame seeds add a little crunch.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Scrub the sweet potatoes and halve them lengthwise.
2. Prick the cut surfaces with a fork and brush with oil. Place the halves cut side up on a baking tray and roast for 30–50 minutes, depending on their size.
3. Slice the chilli and spring onions. When a fork goes easily into the flesh, roughen the surface with the fork and season with salt.
4. Crumble the feta over the hot halves. Scatter with chilli, spring onions and sesame seeds. Serve two halves per portion.

## Notes

- Contains milk and sesame. Larger sweet potatoes may take longer than the estimated time.
