# Polenta concia, cheesy polenta

Two cheeses melt into the slowly cooked corn porridge. Parmesan and melted butter go on top at serving.

## Preparation

1. Bring the water and salt to a boil in a heavy-based pan. Pour the cornmeal in a steady stream while whisking to avoid lumps.
2. Reduce to low heat. Cook for about 45 minutes or according to the packet, until the grains are tender. Stir frequently, scraping the bottom, and watch for splashes of hot polenta.
3. Dice the Fontina and Toma. Stir into the polenta over low heat until melted. Season with black pepper.
4. Melt the butter in a small pan. Divide the polenta into four servings, sprinkle with Parmesan and pour on the melted butter. Serve hot.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk. Check product labels and follow the cheese storage instructions. Cool leftovers quickly in small containers, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
