# Ropa vieja

## Description

Slow-cooked shredded beef with peppers, tomatoes and olives.

## Preparation

1. Brown the beef on all sides in a casserole. Set aside briefly.
2. Soften the onion and peppers in the same casserole. Add garlic, cumin and oregano.
3. Add crushed tomatoes, beef stock and bay leaf. Return the beef to the casserole.
4. Cover and simmer very gently for about 2.5 hours, until the meat pulls apart easily with a fork.
5. Lift the beef onto a board and shred with two forks. Remove the bay leaf.
6. Return the beef to the sauce. Add olives and wine vinegar and simmer for another 10 minutes.

## Notes

If the sauce is watery at the end, reduce it briefly uncovered. Suggested rice and black beans are separate side dishes.
