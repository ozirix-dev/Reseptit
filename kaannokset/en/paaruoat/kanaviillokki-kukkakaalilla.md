# Creamy Finnish chicken fricassee with cauliflower rice

## Description

This creamy Finnish chicken fricassee has its familiar curry flavour and is served with lightly steamed cauliflower rice in place of mashed potatoes.

## Preparation

1. Melt the butter in a saucepan and cook the flour in it for one minute, stirring. Gradually whisk in the warm stock.
2. Stir in the cream and curry powder. Simmer over low heat for 8–10 minutes.
3. Add the cooked chicken, cut into strips, and heat it through in the sauce. Season with lemon juice, salt and white pepper.
4. Grate the cauliflower to resemble rice. Steam or pan-fry for 4–5 minutes until just tender.
5. Serve the chicken fricassee over the cauliflower rice.

## Notes

This recipe is not gluten-free because the sauce is thickened with wheat flour. You can substitute cornflour if it suits your diet.
