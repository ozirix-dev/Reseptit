# Creamy salmon stew

Creamy salmon stew is an easy and filling everyday main dish.

## Description

Salmon, spinach and soft cream sauce cook in the oven at the same time. Serve the stew on its own or with a vegetable side dish.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200 degrees.
2. Chop the onion and garlic. Sauté them in butter in a pan.
3. Add cream cheese, whipped cream, lemon juice, mustard and spices. Mix into a smooth sauce.
4. Quickly fold the spinach into the sauce.
5. Place the salmon fillet in the baking dish and pour the sauce over it.
6. Cook in the oven for about 25 minutes until the salmon is just cooked.
7. Let it rest for a while before serving.

## Notes

- Goes well with, for example, fried zucchini or mashed cauliflower.
