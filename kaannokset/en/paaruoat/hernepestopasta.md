# Pasta with green pea pesto

Basil, cashews and lemon season the blended pea sauce. Half the peas are folded into the pasta whole.

## Preparation

1. Cook the pasta as directed on the pack. Save a little of the cooking water before draining it.
2. Heat the frozen peas in boiling water according to the pack directions and drain. Peel and chop the garlic and shallot.
3. Blend half the peas with the garlic, shallot, cashews, basil, lemon juice, salt and rapeseed oil. Stir in the crème fraîche. Thin with a splash of pasta water if needed.
4. Toss the drained pasta with the pesto and the remaining heated peas. Serve straight away.

## Notes

- Contains wheat, cashews and milk. Check the pasta label for egg.
