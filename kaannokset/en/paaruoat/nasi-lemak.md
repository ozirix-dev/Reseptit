# Malaysian nasi lemak

## Description

Fragrant rice cooked in coconut milk is served with spicy sambal, crisp anchovies, peanuts, cucumber and egg. This is a home-style version of the well-known Malaysian meal.

## Preparation

1. Put the rinsed rice in a saucepan with the coconut milk, water and pandan. Bring to a boil, cover and cook over very low heat according to the rice package directions.
2. Boil the eggs for 8–9 minutes. Cool, peel and halve them.
3. Rinse the anchovies briefly, dry them well and fry in the oil in a small saucepan until crisp. Transfer to kitchen paper.
4. Heat the shallot in a frying pan. Add the sambal, tamarind paste and a splash of water. Simmer for a few minutes until the sauce thickens.
5. Serve the coconut rice with the eggs, anchovies, peanuts, cucumber and sambal.

## Notes

Sambal varies in saltiness and heat. Taste before adding salt, as the anchovies are salty too.
