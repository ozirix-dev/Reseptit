# Lemon and white bean spaghetti

Cannellini beans blend into a creamy lemon sauce. Green beans and spinach add colour to the spaghetti.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the green beans and spinach. Trim the green beans and cut into pieces about 3 cm long. Cook the spaghetti as directed on the pack. In a separate pan, boil the green beans in plenty of water for at least 10 minutes, then drain.
2. Rinse and drain the cannellini beans. Wash, zest and juice the lemon. Blend the cannellini beans with the garlic, stock and half the lemon juice and zest. Taste and add more lemon juice if needed.
3. Reserve some pasta water before draining. Return the pasta to the pan with the spinach and a splash of pasta water. Stir over low heat for about one minute until the spinach wilts.
4. Add the green beans and white bean sauce. Stir until hot and loosen with pasta water as needed. Finish with the remaining lemon zest.

## Notes

- Contains wheat. Check the stock label for allergens and the pasta label for egg.
