# Tom yum goong – Thai shrimp soup

Sour, hot and herbal shrimp soup is seasoned with lemongrass, galangal and kaffir lime leaves. It is suitable as a low-carb meal.

## Description

Lime juice is added at the end to preserve its fresh taste. You can leave out the spices in the broth.

## Preparation

1. Heat the water and fish stock. Add crushed lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves and chili. Cook slowly for 8 minutes.
2. Add mushrooms and tomatoes. Cook for 3 minutes.
3. Add the prawns and cook for 2-3 minutes until they turn pink.
4. Remove the pot from the stove. Season with fish sauce and lime juice.
5. Check the taste, finish with cilantro and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Dispense the fish stock concentrate according to the instructions on the package; the saltiness of stock concentrates varies.
- The nutritional values ​​do not contain rice or coconut milk and are indicative.
