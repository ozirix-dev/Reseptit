# Sheet-pan chicken fajitas with avocado dressing – weighed recipe

## Description

Precisely weighed sheet-pan chicken with avocado dressing. Makes four servings.

## Ingredients

The quantities above are also shown in the serving calculator.

## Instructions

1. Combine 500 g raw chicken breast fillet, 240 g bell pepper, 55 g red onion, 27 g oil, 2 g ground cumin, 1 g smoked paprika and 0.3 g ground chilli.
2. Bake everything on a baking sheet at 200 °C until the chicken is completely cooked through. Retain all cooking juices and fat in the servings. The chicken's internal temperature must reach at least 75 °C.
3. Serve with 150 g edible avocado flesh, 100 g plain lactose-free Greek yogurt (7% fat) and 50 g peeled whole lime, including its flesh and juice. Divide all ingredients into four servings. This version contains no tortillas.
