# Lemon and asparagus fettuccine

## Description

Thin ribbons of green asparagus cook quickly. Lemon and toasted pine nuts finish the fettuccine.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the asparagus and snap off the woody ends before weighing. Use a vegetable peeler to shave the stalks into long ribbons. Halve the tips if needed.
2. Toast the pine nuts in a dry frying pan and set aside. Cook the pasta according to the packet and drain.
3. Heat 1 dl water in a wide saucepan. Add salt and asparagus and cook for 1–2 minutes. Lift the asparagus out with a slotted spoon if plenty of water remains.
4. Toss the pasta with the asparagus. Add the lemon juice, zest from a washed lemon, oil and pepper. Divide between plates and top with Parmesan and pine nuts.

## Notes

- Contains wheat and milk. Check the fresh pasta packaging for egg.
