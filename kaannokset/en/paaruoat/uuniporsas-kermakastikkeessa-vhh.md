# Roast pork with creamy pan sauce

Roast pork with creamy pan sauce is a familiar Finnish dish as a low-carb home version. The recipe has been rewritten to suit your own kitchen and serves four or the stated amount of servings.

## Description

The familiar taste in food is built from simple ingredients. Vegetables and creamy ingredients bring juiciness, and the portion size can be adjusted with the portion counter in the recipe.

## Preparation

1. Season the pork with salt, pepper and thyme. Place in a baking dish with the onion blocks.
2. Add water to the pan and bake at 175 °C for about 75 minutes or until the meat is cooked.
3. Lift the meat to rest. Strain the frying stock into the pot and add the cream.
4. Cook the sauce for a few minutes and check the taste. Slice the pork and serve with the sauce.

## Notes

- Check for doneness and finally season according to your own taste.
