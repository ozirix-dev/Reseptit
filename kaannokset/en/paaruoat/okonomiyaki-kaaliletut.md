# Okonomiyaki-style cabbage pancakes

## Description

These okonomiyaki-style cabbage pancakes are crisp around the edges and soft in the middle. Almond flour replaces wheat flour.

## Preparation

1. Shred the cabbage very finely and slice the spring onions.
2. Whisk together the eggs, almond flour and tamari. Fold the cabbage and half the spring onions into the batter.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan. Fry four small pancakes over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side.
4. Mix the mayonnaise and sriracha. Serve the sauce with the pancakes and finish with sesame seeds and the remaining spring onions.

## Notes

Wait until the underside is well browned and holds together before turning each pancake.
