# Thai steamed tofu with ginger and vegetables

Soft tofu is heated in steam and topped with shiitake, fresh ginger, chilli and greens. Soy sauce and sesame oil season the dish.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the chillies, spring onions and Chinese celery. Slice the shiitake and cut the ginger, chillies, spring onions and celery into thin strips. Put the drained tofu blocks on a heatproof plate.
2. Bring the water in a steamer to a boil and place the plate in the basket above the water. Cover and steam for about 3 minutes until the tofu is hot, extending the time if needed. Open the lid away from you and use oven gloves to handle the plate.
3. Warm the rapeseed oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the garlic and then cook the shiitake until tender and cooked through. Stir in the ginger, chillies, spring onions and celery and heat briefly. Spoon the mixture onto the tofu.
4. Heat the salt, soy sauce and sugar in a small saucepan over low heat until the sugar dissolves. Pour the sauce over the tofu, finish with sesame oil and divide into two portions.

## Storage and safety

Contains soy, celery and sesame. The soy sauce may contain wheat. Check the tofu and sauce packaging. Cool leftovers quickly, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly.
