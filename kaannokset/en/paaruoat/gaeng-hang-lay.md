# Gaeng hang lay – Northern Thai pork curry

Ginger, tamarind and pickled garlic flavour this slowly braised pork curry.

## Instructions

1. Soak dried chillies in hot water for 10–15 minutes and drain. Chop the pale lemongrass parts and other paste ingredients; grind in a mortar, adding kapi last. Measure 120 ml of paste for the curry; freeze the remainder promptly. Paste ingredients describe the full paste batch.
2. Cut pork into roughly 6–7 cm pieces. Heat oil in a pot of at least 5 litres, fry the measured paste briefly and brown the meat for a few minutes.
3. Add water and pickled garlic brine. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer gently for about an hour.
4. Halve the pickled garlic. Add it with fresh garlic, shallot, ginger, tamarind, soy and fish sauces, sugar and garam masala. Simmer for another 30–60 minutes or longer until the pork is cooked through and fork-tender. Divide into six servings.

## Notes

- Contains fish, crustaceans and soy; check soy sauce for wheat and pickled garlic for sulphites. Use Thai garlic in sweet-sour brine, not chilli-oil pickle. Actual paste yield and the mass of the 120 ml portion require measurement before nutrition calculation. Rice is not included.
