# Traditional Finnish Salmon and Potato Bake

A traditional Finnish oven dish.

## Preparation

1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Butter an ovenproof dish of about 2.5 litres, using part of the butter. Thinly slice potatoes and onion.
2. Check for bones, remove any skin still attached and cut the salmon into strips. Keep chilled while preparing the dish.
3. Layer half the potato slices in the dish, then onion, salmon and dill. Season with some salt and allspice. Add the remaining potatoes on top.
4. Whisk eggs with milk and the remaining seasoning. Pour over the dish and dot with the remaining butter.
5. Bake on a lower oven shelf at 200°C for about 65–80 minutes until potatoes are tender, salmon cooked and custard fully set. Rest for 10 minutes.
