# Ribeye steak and eggs

## Description

Pan-sear ribeye first and fry the eggs in the same pan while the steak rests. Makes two generous portions.

## Instructions

1. Pat the steak dry and season with salt. Get the eggs ready.
2. Heat 10 g butter over medium-high heat. Sear the steak about 3–4 minutes on each side, adjusting for thickness. Check for an internal temperature of at least 63 °C.
3. Transfer the steak to a plate and rest for at least 3 minutes.
4. Lower the heat, add the remaining butter and fry the eggs until both whites and yolks are firm.
5. Divide the steak and eggs between two plates. Pour the butter remaining in the pan over them.
