# Pork and mashed vegetable bake

Potatoes, cauliflower and carrots form a soft base for this bake. Mix cottage cheese, cream and browned ground pork into the mash before baking.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Boil the potatoes in unsalted water and drain them. Weigh out 400 g of cooked potatoes. Chop the cauliflower and carrots, boil them until tender and drain well. Wash and dice the tomatoes.
2. Grease a wide baking dish of about 2.5 litres with 5 g of butter. Heat 15 g of butter in a skillet and brown the ground pork, breaking it up as it cooks. Add the tomatoes and cook for a couple of minutes. Transfer everything, including the juices, to a bowl.
3. Mash the potatoes, cauliflower and carrots. Mix in the remaining 30 g of butter, cottage cheese, cream, lemon juice, salt, pepper and nutmeg. Stir in the pork and tomatoes. Let the mixture cool until lukewarm, then mix in the beaten eggs.
4. Spread the mixture in the baking dish. Bake for about 35–45 minutes, until the centre is set and the thickest part reaches at least 75 °C. Rest for 5 minutes and divide into four servings.

## Notes

- Keep raw ground pork separate from cooked vegetables. Cook the centre of the bake to at least 75 °C. Contains milk and egg. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate them.
