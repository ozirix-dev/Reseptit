# Kolokithokeftedes, Greek courgette and feta fritters

Squeeze the water from grated courgette before mixing it with feta. Mint, dill, basil and lime zest give the small fritters their fragrance.

## Preparation

1. Rinse the courgettes, herbs and limes. Trim the courgettes and grate them coarsely. Mix the salt into the grated courgette and squeeze out excess liquid in a clean tea towel.
2. Chop the spring onions and herbs. Grate only the green part of the lime peel. Add them to the courgette with the cumin, chilli, pepper and eggs. Crumble in the feta and fold in the flour.
3. Heat some of the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Shape 12 small fritters, patting them to about 1 cm thick, and fry in batches. Add the remaining oil as needed for later batches.
4. Fry for about 2–3 minutes per side. If needed, continue over lower heat until golden and the thickest fritters reach at least 71 °C in the centre. Drain on kitchen paper. Serve two fritters per portion.

## Storage and safety

Contains milk, egg and wheat. If the oil starts to smoke, turn off the heat and let it cool in the pan. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate.
