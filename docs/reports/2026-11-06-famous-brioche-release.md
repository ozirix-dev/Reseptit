## Evidence and constraints

- Original ingredient proportions come from the linked recipe sources, rounded only for measured grams and a nine-roll kitchen batch. Clever Carrot's flour-volume conversion is an assumption disclosed to readers.
- Creative recipe narrative and instructions are newly written. No original photos are copied; AI illustrations are marked as such.
- Fineli Rel20: wheat flour 110, milk 689, egg 858, yolk 851, butter 500, sugar 1; AFCD Rel3: sesame seeds F008214 and yeast F009606. See exact per-ingredient record and zero-contribution omissions in docs/nutrition/calculations.
- No public GK point values: available nearby GI records refer to different baked products (e.g. commercial wholegrain burger buns GI 62) or are category means. They do not meet current preparation-match and provenance gates for enriched white-flour home-baked buns. This does not stop publication of recipes or grounded ingredient nutrition.
- Ovens, baking-pan dimensions, dough times and egg-wash retention must be verified by an actual bake before claiming tested results. Nine large buns may need two pans.
- A future localization batch may add EN/SV/TH; the current scope is FI publication.
