# Herbed whipped feta with vegetable sticks

## Description

Salty feta and creamy Greek yogurt become a fresh herb dip, served with cucumber and red pepper. Makes four snack portions.

## Preparation

1. Drain the feta and weigh 120 g. Separately weigh 100 g of yogurt and 10 g of undiluted lemon juice.
2. Crumble the feta into a bowl. Blend with yogurt and lemon juice until smooth, using a hand blender or a small food processor.
3. Finely chop 5 g of fresh dill and stir into the whipped feta. Taste before adding seasoning; feta is already salty.
4. Wash the cucumber and red bell pepper. Remove the pepper stem and seeds; weigh 200 g of edible cucumber and 150 g of edible pepper, then cut into sticks.
5. Divide the whipped feta and weighed vegetable sticks evenly into four portions. Serve chilled.
