# Herb deviled eggs

## Description

A classic snack of boiled eggs filled with creamy yolk, mayonnaise, mustard and fresh chives. Each serving is two egg halves.

## Preparation

1. Hard-boil approximately six eggs (allow about 9–11 minutes once the water boils). Cool in cold water, peel and weigh 330 g of edible egg.
2. Halve the eggs lengthwise. Transfer the yolks to a bowl and arrange the 12 white halves on a serving platter.
3. Mash the yolks with 55 g of mayonnaise and 10 g of Dijon mustard until smooth. Finely chop and mix in 5 g of chives.
4. Spoon or pipe the filling evenly into all 12 egg halves. Sprinkle with a total of 1 g of smoked paprika.
5. Keep refrigerated until serving. Serve two halves per portion and refrigerate leftovers promptly.
