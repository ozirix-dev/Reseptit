# Crispy cheddar cheese crisps

## Description

Two ingredients turn into crunchy baked cheddar crisps. One snack serving contains three crisps.

## Preparation

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (conventional heat) and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Weigh 140 g of grated cheddar. Divide into 12 evenly sized, loosely spread mounds, leaving room for the cheese to melt.
3. Sprinkle the total 1 g of smoked paprika evenly over the mounds. Do not add oil or salt.
4. Bake for an initial 6–9 minutes, watching closely near the end. The edges should brown without burning.
5. Leave on the baking paper for at least 5 minutes, until crisp. Serve three crisps per portion.
