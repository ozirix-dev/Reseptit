**Repository:** `ozirix-dev/Reseptit`  | **Main:** `16a9c0444ff7b9d6ea61b20abd2c31b0d0ae9fa2`  | **Production baseline:** 326 recipes, 85 calculated, 241 incomplete, 45 GI records (26.1%).

## Decision summary

Neither category mean is acceptable as a generic individual-food GI match. They may remain as reference material, but direct bindings need food- and preparation-specific support. Do not remove all 33 GK values mechanically: the in-memory no-category scenario remains calculable for 8 and fails for 25. No recipe is cleared as scientifically acceptable by this diagnostic alone.

Atkinson et al. 2021 reports dairy GI 35 ± 11 (n=186) and fruits/fruit products GI 51 ± 11 (n=105). These are summary means calculated over heterogeneous food-specific entries in Supplemental Table 1, not individual human tests of cream cheese, cream, yogurt, berry mixes, lemon juice, figs or canned peaches. Food entries include human studies, but source validity and transfer apply only to the exact food and tested form. Do not interpret group SD as recipe uncertainty or LOW confidence as evidence of match.

### Source and metadata findings

- Dairy mean covers mixed milks, yogurts/kefir, flavored foods, ice creams, desserts and other dairy; it does not prove GI for cream, cream cheese, cottage cheese/quark, ricotta, feta or halloumi. Direct product studies in the table support only tested products/forms.
- Fruit mean pools raw, cooked, canned, dried, juice, puree and mixed products. It cannot be transferred indiscriminately between these forms.
- Fruit metadata describes a tamarind-paste analogue but live assignments include frozen berries, lemon juice, raw strawberries, fresh fig and canned peach: this is a material internal consistency defect. Dairy metadata describes cream cheese while bindings include eight-plus unlike dairy forms.
- Supplemental Table 1 entry 1174 lists raw fresh Australian strawberry GI 40 ± 7 (2001; n=10; glucose scale; 25 g available CHO; 925 g portion), but flags unpublished Sydney observations. This is a candidate source only; policy acceptance is unresolved. It does not validate strawberry cake as a whole.

Sources: [Atkinson et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/nqab233); [Supplemental Table 1](https://toitumisterapeudid.ee/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SupplementalTable1.pdf); [Supplemental Table 2](https://toitumisterapeudid.ee/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SupplementalTable2.pdf); [ISO 26642 preview](https://www.iso.org/standard/68248.html).
