Preparation and Cooking
No preparation necessary. French’s Dijon Mustard is ready to use to add bold flavor to sandwiches,
sauces and sides. French’s Honey Mustard is perfect as a plus-one ingredient and great for
blending with mayonnaise, horseradish sauce, Parmesan cheese and other premium seasonings.
Shake well before opening. To open, unscrew cap and peel off inner freshness seal.
Serving Suggestions
French’s Dijon Mustard provides a thick and creamy texture and robust flavor to: •Hickory-smoked
brisket with ham, Swiss cheese and sliced dill pickles served on a toasted house-made jalapeño
cheese bun •Lemon herb turkey breast marinated in Dijon mustard with chardonnay, honey, lemon
and herbs •Cedar plank salmon rubbed with Dijon mustard with chardonnay and glazed with a sweet
orange bourbon sauce served with grilled asparagus and potato gratin •Rotisserie chicken wrap with
Bibb lettuce, tomato and roasted red onion •Black Forest ham with Genoa salami, Swiss cheese
and lettuce on Asiago bread
Packaging and Storage
French’s Dijon Mustard is shipped and stored at ambient temperatures and has a shelf life of 9
months. Protect product from freezing and store with cap tightly closed to reduce the risk of
contamination and maintain maximum product quality. For best flavor, refrigerate after opening.
Recycling of cardboard cases is encouraged where acceptable.
